Conventions: sections 5, 32.3 and 33. A row is a corepresentation of 𝖣𝟧𝗁(𝖣𝟧)\mathsf{D_{5h}}(\mathsf{D_{5}}), named by its irreps (section 2.1). A column is a merged class of 𝖣𝟧\mathsf{D_{5}}, the union of a class and of its image under 𝘩↦𝘢𝘩−1𝘢−1\mathsfit{h} \mapsto \mathsfit{a} \mathsfit{h}^{-1} \mathsfit{a}^{-1}. The entry of a row is the character of its corepresentation on the class, the sum of the characters of its irreps. Exact values are rationals, square roots, cosines and sines of rational multiples of π\pi, and the constant
