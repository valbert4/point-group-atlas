3.1 Closed subgroups ¶

Data: subgroup_classes in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 15.2, 16.1, 36 and 37.5. Over a closed subgroup 𝖪\mathsf{K} of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} (the section Closed subgroups of the document of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}) lies one subgroup. It is 𝖪\mathsf{K}, of magnetic type I, for 𝖪\mathsf{K} in 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, and 𝖪(𝖪∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥))\mathsf{K}(\mathsf{K} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}), of magnetic type III, for any other 𝖪\mathsf{K}. In the diagram of the continuous closed subgroups, each group links to its document, and a line joins a group to a maximal closed subgroup among the groups of the diagram.

The table lists the closed subgroups other than 𝖮(𝟥)(𝖲𝖮(𝟥))\mathsf{O(3)}(\mathsf{SO(3)}) up to conjugacy in 𝖮(𝟥)(𝖲𝖮(𝟥))\mathsf{O(3)}(\mathsf{SO(3)}), one in each row: the continuous subgroups, then those over the members of each family, then those over the exceptional groups. A row with NN in its symbol stands for every NN of its range, and its entries are functions of NN. A row gives the representative of a class of subgroups (subgrp), its magnetic type (type), its order, the number of subgroups in the class (conjugates) and whether the representative is normal. It also gives its normalizer and its centralizer in 𝖮(𝟥)(𝖲𝖮(𝟥))\mathsf{O(3)}(\mathsf{SO(3)}). The record holds the members with N≤12N \le 12 (subgroup_classes), and the section Closed subgroups of the document of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} gives other names of some members with N=1N = 1.
