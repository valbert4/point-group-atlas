Conventions: sections 4, 9, 16, 32.2 and 37.2. A unitary element 𝘩\mathsfit{h} acts in a corepresentation 𝒟\mathcal{D} by the transposes DΛ(𝘩)⊤D^{\Lambda}(\mathsfit{h})^{\top} of the matrices of its irreps Λ\Lambda (the section Representation matrices of the document of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}). The representative 𝘢=Θ𝘪\mathsfit{a} = \varTheta\mathsfit{i} acts by a unitary matrix times the complex conjugation KK. In case (a), 𝒟(𝘢)=NK\mathcal{D}(\mathsfit{a}) = NK. Where a function moves with the points (Conventions, section 4.3), the corepresentation on the same kets is the complex conjugate of 𝒟\mathcal{D}: a unitary element 𝘩\mathsfit{h} acts by DΛ(𝘩)†D^{\Lambda}(\mathsfit{h})^{\dagger}, and N∗N^{*} replaces NN. The matrix NN has the rows and columns of the kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle, m=J,J−1,…,−Jm = J, J-1, \dots, -J, and the entries
