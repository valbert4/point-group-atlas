Data: subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 15 and 36. The diagram shows the conjugacy classes of subgroups, each joined to the classes of its maximal subgroups, and the orders increase upward. A normal subgroup is framed, and ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of conjugate subgroups. A gray letter after a symbol distinguishes the classes of subgroups with that symbol, which are not conjugate in 𝖣𝟣𝟤𝟣′\mathsf{D_{12}1'}. The table uses the same letters. The table lists the classes by order, then by magnetic type, those of one symbol together. A row gives the representative of a class of subgroups (subgrp), its magnetic type (type), its order, the number of subgroups in the class (conjugates) and whether the representative is normal. It also gives its normalizer and its centralizer in 𝖣𝟣𝟤𝟣′\mathsf{D_{12}1'}, by their classes.
