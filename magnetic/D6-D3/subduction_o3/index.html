Conventions: sections 11 and 33.1. The irrep D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} of 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} has angular momentum JJ and parity pp (g\mathrm{g} for p=+1p = +1, u\mathrm{u} for p=−1p = -1), and time reversal acts on it as on its kets (Conventions, section 31.1). A row is an angular momentum J≤6J \le 6. A row gives the multiplicity of each corepresentation of 𝖣𝟨(𝖣𝟥)\mathsf{D_{6}}(\mathsf{D_{3}}) in the restriction of D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} to 𝖣𝟨(𝖣𝟥)\mathsf{D_{6}}(\mathsf{D_{3}}). A corepresentation occurs as often as its first irrep occurs in the restriction to 𝖣𝟥\mathsf{D_{3}} (the section Subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} of the document of 𝖣𝟥\mathsf{D_{3}}), half as often in case (b).
