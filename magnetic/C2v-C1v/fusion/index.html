2.5 Products of corepresentations ¶

Data: corepresentations, fusion in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 10.1, 34 and 38. Every product 𝒟1⊗𝒟2\mathcal{D}_1\otimes\mathcal{D}_2, equal to 𝒟2⊗𝒟1\mathcal{D}_2\otimes\mathcal{D}_1, is one corepresentation, in row 𝒟1\mathcal{D}_1, column 𝒟2\mathcal{D}_2 for 𝒟1≤𝒟2\mathcal{D}_1 \le \mathcal{D}_2 in the order of the corepresentation table (section 2.2).
