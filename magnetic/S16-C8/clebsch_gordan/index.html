2.6 Clebsch–Gordan coefficients ¶

Data: clebsch_gordan, corepresentations in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 10.3, 10.4 and 38. A copy κ\kappa of a corepresentation 𝒟\mathcal{D} in 𝒟1⊗𝒟2\mathcal{D}_1\otimes\mathcal{D}_2 is a block CC of Clebsch–Gordan coefficients that satisfies two equations (Conventions, section 38). It has a row for each pair (μ1,μ2)(\mu_1, \mu_2) of partners of 𝒟1\mathcal{D}_1 and 𝒟2\mathcal{D}_2, in Kronecker order, and a column for each partner μ\mu of 𝒟\mathcal{D}. Exact values are rationals, square roots, cosines and sines of rational multiples of π\pi, and the constants

where i\mathrm{i} is the imaginary unit and e\mathrm{e} is the base of the natural logarithm. A copy whose columns of the first irrep of 𝒟\mathcal{D} are a block of the table of 𝖢𝟪\mathsf{C_{8}} times 1 or i\mathrm{i} is named by a pointer to that block (Conventions, section 38.2). That block is the copy κ\kappa of the first irrep of 𝒟\mathcal{D} in the product of the first irreps of 𝒟1\mathcal{D}_1 and 𝒟2\mathcal{D}_2, in the section Clebsch–Gordan coefficients of the document of 𝖢𝟪\mathsf{C_{8}}. Each such copy has the phase 1. The table below sets every other copy. Each block is headed 𝒟1⊗𝒟2→𝒟\mathcal{D}_1\otimes\mathcal{D}_2\to\mathcal{D}, then κ\kappa where 𝒟\mathcal{D} occurs more than once in 𝒟1⊗𝒟2\mathcal{D}_1\otimes\mathcal{D}_2, and a normalization 𝒩\mathcal{N}. Row μ\mu gives the nonzero entries of column μ\mu of 𝒩 C\sqrt{\mathcal{N}}\,C, so that their squared moduli sum to 𝒩\mathcal{N}. Each entry is written before ∣μ1 μ2⟩\lvert {\mu_1}\,{\mu_2}\rangle, the product of the partners μ1\mu_1 of 𝒟1\mathcal{D}_1 and μ2\mu_2 of 𝒟2\mathcal{D}_2. Only 𝒟1≤𝒟2\mathcal{D}_1 \le \mathcal{D}_2 is listed (𝒟2⊗𝒟1\mathcal{D}_2\otimes\mathcal{D}_1 exchanges μ1\mu_1 and μ2\mu_2). Where 𝒟\mathcal{D} occurs more than once, κ\kappa is followed by the rule that separates its copies (Conventions, sections 10.4 and 38.1). The copies labeled exch. sym. and exch. anti. are symmetric and antisymmetric under the exchange of the two factors. The copies labeled row order are selected by the projection rule, in row order.
