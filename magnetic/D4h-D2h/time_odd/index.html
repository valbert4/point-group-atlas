5.1 Tensors odd under time reversal ¶

Data: group in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 10, 13.2 and 35. A row is a tensor property odd under time reversal that 𝖣𝟦𝗁(𝖣𝟤𝗁)\mathsf{D_{4h}}(\mathsf{D_{2h}}) allows, by its Jahn symbol (name). A row gives the spaces of Cartesian tensors in which its components are read (spaces), by their Jahn symbols in the section Isotypic components of the document of 𝖣𝟦𝗁\mathsf{D_{4h}}. A row also gives the irrep of 𝖣𝟦𝗁\mathsf{D_{4h}} at which they are read and the properties in words. The irrep is χ\chi where the spaces are those of the tensor, and χ⊗det⁡\chi \otimes \det where they are those of the tensor without its axial factor e (Conventions, section 35). The components that 𝖣𝟦𝗁(𝖣𝟤𝗁)\mathsf{D_{4h}}(\mathsf{D_{2h}}) allows are the copies of that irrep in those spaces.
