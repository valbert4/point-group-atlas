The field magnetic_type is II, so 𝖢𝟦𝗁𝟣′\mathsf{C_{4h}1'} contains the elements of 𝖢𝟦𝗁\mathsf{C_{4h}} and their products with time reversal Θ\varTheta. The field G names 𝖢𝟦𝗁\mathsf{C_{4h}} by its record (Conventions, section 30.3). The four fields images name the groups by which the elements move a polar or an axial vector, even or odd under time reversal (Conventions, section 31.2). Each names the record of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class and the frame that carries that record onto the image. The fields normalizer and centralizer are taken in 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'}, and both groups are of magnetic type II. The field alternative_names gives the name of 𝖢𝟦𝗁𝟣′\mathsf{C_{4h}1'} in condensed-matter physics [CGLW13], where 𝖹𝟤𝖳\mathsf{Z_{2}^{T}} is the group of order 2 that time reversal Θ\varTheta generates.
