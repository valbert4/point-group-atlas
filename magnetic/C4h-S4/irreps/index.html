2.1 Irreducible representations ¶

Data: corepresentations, unitary_irreps in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 6, 7, 32.1 and 32.3. A row is an irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖲𝟦\mathsf{S_{4}}, the subgroup of the unitary elements of 𝖢𝟦𝗁(𝖲𝟦)\mathsf{C_{4h}}(\mathsf{S_{4}}), in the order of the section Irreducible representations of the document of 𝖲𝟦\mathsf{S_{4}}. A row gives its dimension dd, its Frobenius–Schur indicator νFS\nu_{\mathrm{FS}}, its reality and its complex conjugate (conjugate, a dash for the others), as in that section. Bilbao is the irrep’s symbol Γn\Gamma_n in the double point group tables of [EBW+17] (Conventions, section 6.5). A row also gives the indicator ι\iota of Λ\Lambda under the representative 𝘢\mathsfit{a}, its case and the corepresentation that contains it (corep), named by its irreps. In case (a), ι=+1\iota = +1 and Λ\Lambda alone forms the corepresentation. In case (b), ι=−1\iota = -1 and two copies of Λ\Lambda form it. In case (c), ι=0\iota = 0 and Λ\Lambda forms it with its partner, another irrep.
