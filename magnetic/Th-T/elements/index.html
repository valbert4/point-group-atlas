Conventions: sections 5, 30.1, 30.3 and 32.1. A row is a class of 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}. A row gives the indices kk of its elements, as in the section Symmetry operations of the document of 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}. As 𝖳𝗁(𝖳)\mathsf{T_{h}}(\mathsf{T}) is isomorphic to 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}, its classes are these, and its elements multiply as those of 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}} do, with their signs multiplied. The antiunitary representative 𝘢\mathsfit{a} of the corepresentations (representative) is Θ𝘪\varTheta\mathsfit{i}, over the element of index 12. The field frame is the identity, so the elements of the record of 𝖳\mathsf{T} are those of 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}} with the same matrices.
