37 Continuous groups ¶

The parent has 21 continuous closed subgroups up to conjugacy, seven of each magnetic type. Those of magnetic type I are the continuous point groups (section 16), and those of magnetic type II are their groups 𝖦𝟣′\mathsf{G1'}. The groups of magnetic type III are 𝖢∞𝗁(𝖢∞)\mathsf{C_{\infty h}}(\mathsf{C_{\infty}}), 𝖢∞𝗏(𝖢∞)\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}(\mathsf{C_{\infty}}), 𝖣∞(𝖢∞)\mathsf{D_{\infty}}(\mathsf{C_{\infty}}), 𝖣∞𝗁(𝖣∞)\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}(\mathsf{D_{\infty}}), 𝖣∞𝗁(𝖢∞𝗁)\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}(\mathsf{C_{\infty h}}), 𝖣∞𝗁(𝖢∞𝗏)\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}(\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}) and 𝖮(𝟥)(𝖲𝖮(𝟥))\mathsf{O(3)}(\mathsf{SO(3)}). The limiting symmetry groups of eight types of vectorlike physical quantities, one continuous group of the parent for each type, are listed in [Hli14]. The records of the groups of magnetic types II and III follow sections 30 to 36, with the changes of this section.

37.1 The record ¶

A continuous record has domain = "continuous", as in section 16. Its pointer fields name these values of the records of the point groups:

  1. unitary_irreps, class_space and subduction_o3: the families of irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}, its class space (section 16) and its subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in magnetic type II.
  2. subgroup (in group, magnetic type III): the entry of 𝖧\mathsf{H} among the closed subgroups of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 16.1). It takes the place of node.
  3. orbit_space (magnetic type III): the fields G, H and images, the orbit spaces (section 17.8) of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, of 𝖧\mathsf{H} and of the four images.
  4. record (in group.images), rings and bases: the values of sections 31.2 and 35.1.

The field antiunitary_character is the id of the family of χ\chi, or its family and index in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, as determinant_character is in section 2.2. The four images are continuous point groups in the frames of their records, so the matrix of each image and of frame is the identity. The field signs (magnetic type III) has one entry for each component of the class space of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, its component and the sign θ\theta of the elements of 𝖬\mathsf{M} over it.

37.2 Families of corepresentations ¶

The irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H} form families, and so do the corepresentations. In magnetic type III the representative is the antiunitary element over the element at angle 0 of the first component of 𝖦\mathsf{G} outside 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The field representative holds its component and its turn. The indicator of Λ\Lambda is the Haar integral of χΛ(𝘨2)\chi_{\Lambda}(\mathsfit{g}^2) over the elements 𝘨\mathsfit{g} of 𝖦∖𝖧\mathsf{G} \setminus \mathsf{H}, of mass 1, and over 𝖦\mathsf{G} in magnetic type II. It is the same for every index of a family, and no family is of case (b).

Each entry of corepresentations has these fields:

  1. family: the id of its first family of irreps, with the symbol of its index in index where it has one.
  2. case and, in case (c), partner: the case and the id of the partner family, of the same index.
  3. indicator (magnetic type III): value, ι\iota, and integral. The latter gives for each component of 𝖦\mathsf{G} outside 𝖧\mathsf{H} its mass and the character chi at the squares of its elements, a trigonometric polynomial in their angle (section 16).
  4. N (case (a)): the matrix NN of section 32.2, the same for every index. Its form is "diagonal" or "anti-diagonal", with the entries diag or antidiag from its first row to its last.

The basis of NN in a family of dimension 2 is the rotation-diagonal basis of section 9, where NN is the identity or exchanges the two vectors. For D(J)D^{(J)} and D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} the basis is the kets, and NN is the matrix by which the representative acts on them (section 31.1). This rule replaces that of section 32.2 for magnetic type II, which is the rule of the finite groups. In 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{SO(3)1'} and 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} the representative is Θ\varTheta, and antidiag is the formula "(-1)^m, m = J, ..., -J" of the entry in the row of mm and the column of −m-m. In 𝖮(𝟥)(𝖲𝖮(𝟥))\mathsf{O(3)}(\mathsf{SO(3)}) the representative is Θ𝘪\varTheta\mathsfit{i} and the kets of D(J)D^{(J)} have the parity (−1)J(-1)^J, so antidiag is "(-1)^(J-m), m = J, ..., -J".

37.3 The corepresentation table ¶

The field corepresentation_table holds classes, rows and characters, as in section 33. An entry of classes is a component of the class space of 𝖧\mathsf{H}, and its merged classes on an arc run over the coordinate from t0 to t1 (section 16). The characters of a row are a map from each component to a trigonometric polynomial in its angle.

37.4 Restrictions and symmetrized powers ¶

A term of a decomposition has corepresentation (the id of its first family), multiplicity and, for a family with an index, index. This index is a number or a function of the index of the entry, such as "2n". The entries of splitting and symmetrized_powers hold for every index. Those of splitting are the families of irreps of 𝖦\mathsf{G} over ℝ\mathbb{R}, with irrep and index. The field subduction_corepresentations lists in g and u the terms for each JJ up to J_max, and its rule states them for every JJ, as in section 11.

In 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{SO(3)1'}, 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} and 𝖮(𝟥)(𝖲𝖮(𝟥))\mathsf{O(3)}(\mathsf{SO(3)}), a term of a symmetrized power has index_range (symbol, from, to and step) in place of index. The multiplicity cKc_{K} of D(K)D^{(K)} in the symmetrized cube of D(J)D^{(J)} is the coefficient of t3J−Kt^{3J-K} in

(37.1)(1−t2J+1)(1−t2J+2)(1−t2J+3)(1−t2)(1−t3),\frac{(1-t^{2J+1})(1-t^{2J+2})(1-t^{2J+3})}{(1-t^{2})(1-t^{3})},

and cubes lists the decompositions for J≤4J \le 4.

37.5 Closed subgroups ¶

The closed subgroups of 𝖬\mathsf{M} are taken up to conjugation by its elements. They lie over the closed subgroups of 𝖦\mathsf{G} that its record holds (section 16.1), as the subgroups of section 36 lie over those of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. The field subgroup_classes has these fields:

  1. classes: the pointer to the closed subgroups of 𝖦\mathsf{G}.
  2. continuous and finite: the entries, other than 𝖬\mathsf{M}. In magnetic type III there is one for each entry of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, in its order. In magnetic type II the entries over each 𝖪\mathsf{K} are 𝖪\mathsf{K}, 𝖪𝟣′\mathsf{K1'} and the groups 𝖪(𝖪′)\mathsf{K}(\mathsf{K}').
  3. edges: the covering edges (section 15.1) of 𝖬\mathsf{M} and its continuous closed subgroups, by the names of their records.

Each entry has these fields:

  1. magnetic_type, symbol and record, as in section 36. In an axial group the symbol names the members of the families of section 16.1, which tells 𝖣𝟤(𝖣𝟣)\mathsf{D_{2}}(\mathsf{D_{1}}) from 𝖣𝟤(𝖢𝟤)\mathsf{D_{2}}(\mathsf{C_{2}}). In the documents it has 𝖢𝗌\mathsf{C_{s}} for 𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsf{C_{1h}} and 𝖢𝟣𝗏\mathsf{C_{1v}} and 𝖢𝗂\mathsf{C_{i}} for 𝖲𝟤\mathsf{S_{2}}, and gray letters distinguish the classes with one symbol that are not conjugate, as in section 36.
  2. unitary (magnetic type III): the family and the N of its unitary elements, where they form a member of a family.
  3. normalizer and centralizer: their symbols in 𝖬\mathsf{M}, with C_s and C_i as in section 15.1.
  4. order, class_size and normal (a group 𝖬\mathsf{M} of magnetic type II), as in section 16.1, and group, the family and the N of 𝖪\mathsf{K}.

37.6 Multipoles and invariants ¶

An entry of multipoles (section 35.1) holds its multiplicity as a number. The field every_rank gives it for every rank, for each kind, as "0", "1", "1 for J even", "1 for J odd" or "1 for J = 0". The field two_vectors is as in section 35.1, its counts by Haar integrals.

37.7 Products and Clebsch–Gordan coefficients ¶

The field fusion of a continuous group (kind "family-law") holds the family laws of the products, with corepresentations (the family ids), rules, and index_symbols, index_grammar and condition_grammar as in section 10.2. A rule has a and b, each a corepresentation with its index where it has one, and condition and terms, each term as in section 37.4.

The field clebsch_gordan of an axial group (kind "family-law") has group, corepresentations, products and rules, the couplings in words. A product has a, b, condition and couplings, and a coupling is a stored coupling of section 38.2 whose c is a corepresentation with its index. Its block holds at every pair of indices that meets the condition, in the bases of section 37.2. The rule of section 38.1 selects the copies, without inheritance from the rules of section 10.5.

In 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{SO(3)1'}, 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} and 𝖮(𝟥)(𝖲𝖮(𝟥))\mathsf{O(3)}(\mathsf{SO(3)}) the rules of fusion state D(J1)⊗D(J2)=⨁J=∣J1−J2∣J1+J2D(J)D^{(J_1)} \otimes D^{(J_2)} = \bigoplus_{J=\lvert J_1-J_2\rvert}^{J_1+J_2} D^{(J)}, and in 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} the terms have the parity p1p2p_1 p_2. Their clebsch_gordan (kind "table") has the layout of section 10.6, with corepresentations in the place of irreps. Each coupling is a pointer to the block of section 10.6 in the record SO3, or O3 in 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'}, with its phase. The second equation of section 38, with the matrix NN of section 37.2, makes that phase i\mathrm{i} for the couplings of odd J1+J2−JJ_1 + J_2 - J in 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{SO(3)1'} and 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'}, and 1 for every other coupling.

A document of an axial group sets the rules of fusion and the blocks of clebsch_gordan. A document of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{SO(3)1'}, 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} or 𝖮(𝟥)(𝖲𝖮(𝟥))\mathsf{O(3)}(\mathsf{SO(3)}) sets the rules of fusion, names the tables of section 10.6 that hold its blocks and states the rule of their phases.
