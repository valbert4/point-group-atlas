30 The parent and the records ¶

30.1 The parent ¶

The parent 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} is the group of the elements of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} and of their products with time reversal Θ\varTheta. It is the group of the record O3-1p, and section 30.2 gives its Hermann–Mauguin symbol. Its record lists every class of closed subgroups (section 37.5), and it stands at their top as 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} stands at the top of the lattice of the point groups (section 15.2). An element 𝘨\mathsfit{g} of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} is unitary, and Θ𝘨\varTheta\mathsfit{g} is antiunitary. The sign θ\theta of an element of the parent is +1+1 for a unitary element and −1-1 for an antiunitary one. Time reversal commutes with every element of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, because it acts on a function by complex conjugation (section 31.1) and an element of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} acts through its real matrix P(𝘨)P(\mathsfit{g}) (section 4.1). Its square is Θ2=𝘌\varTheta^2 = \mathsfit{E}, as on the vector representations of section 7. Two elements of the parent thus multiply as their elements of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} do, and their signs multiply.

A magnetic point group 𝖬\mathsf{M} is a closed subgroup of the parent, up to conjugacy. Its unitary elements form the subgroup 𝖧=𝖬∩𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{H} = \mathsf{M} \cap \mathsf{O(3)}, and the elements of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} that occur in 𝖬\mathsf{M}, alone or with Θ\varTheta, form the point group 𝖦\mathsf{G}. The magnetic type of 𝖬\mathsf{M} takes one of three values:

  1. Magnetic type I: 𝖬=𝖧=𝖦\mathsf{M} = \mathsf{H} = \mathsf{G}, a group without antiunitary elements.
  2. Magnetic type II: 𝖬=𝖦×{𝘌,Θ}\mathsf{M} = \mathsf{G} \times \{\mathsfit{E}, \varTheta\}, written 𝖦𝟣′\mathsf{G1'}, which contains Θ\varTheta.
  3. Magnetic type III: 𝖬=𝖧∪Θ(𝖦∖𝖧)\mathsf{M} = \mathsf{H} \cup \varTheta(\mathsf{G} \setminus \mathsf{H}), written 𝖦(𝖧)\mathsf{G}(\mathsf{H}), for a subgroup 𝖧\mathsf{H} of index 2 in 𝖦\mathsf{G}.

In magnetic type III the character χ\chi of 𝖦\mathsf{G} is the one-dimensional real irrep with kernel 𝖧\mathsf{H}, of value +1+1 on 𝖧\mathsf{H} and −1-1 on 𝖦∖𝖧\mathsf{G} \setminus \mathsf{H}. The element of 𝖬\mathsf{M} over 𝘨\mathsfit{g} then has the sign θ=χ(𝘨)\theta = \chi(\mathsfit{g}). In magnetic type I the character χ\chi is the trivial irrep, and in magnetic type II both signs occur over every element of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. The field signs (magnetic type III) lists the classes of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in the order of its record. Each entry has the class (its id), its elements (their indices) and the sign θ\theta of the elements of 𝖬\mathsf{M} over them.

30.2 Names ¶

The record of a group 𝖦(𝖧)\mathsf{G}(\mathsf{H}) of magnetic type III is named <G>-<H>, the names of the records of 𝖦\mathsf{G} and 𝖧\mathsf{H} (section 2.1) joined by a hyphen, such as C3v-C3. The record of a group 𝖦𝟣′\mathsf{G1'} of magnetic type II is named <G>-1p, such as C3v-1p. A group of magnetic type I is the point group 𝖦\mathsf{G}, and the record of 𝖦\mathsf{G} holds it. The records of the continuous groups (section 37) are named alike, such as Cinfv-Cinf and SO3-1p.

The magnetic Hermann–Mauguin symbol is the symbol of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 2.3) with a prime on each symbol of an axis or a mirror whose element lies in 𝖦∖𝖧\mathsf{G} \setminus \mathsf{H}. In magnetic type II it is the symbol of 𝖦\mathsf{G} with 1′1' appended. Thus 𝖢𝟥𝗏(𝖢𝟥)\mathsf{C_{3v}}(\mathsf{C_{3}}) is 3m′3m', 𝖢𝟦(𝖢𝟤)\mathsf{C_{4}}(\mathsf{C_{2}}) is 4′4', 𝖢𝟥𝗏𝟣′\mathsf{C_{3v}1'} is 3m1′3m1' and the parent 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} is ∞∞m1′\infty\infty m1'. When 𝖦\mathsf{G} holds several subgroups of index 2 in the 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class of 𝖧\mathsf{H}, the symbol is the one whose primes stand latest, such as 4′22′4'22' for 𝖣𝟦(𝖣𝟤)\mathsf{D_{4}}(\mathsf{D_{2}}) and 6′/mmm′6'/mmm' for 𝖣𝟨𝗁(𝖣𝟥𝗁)\mathsf{D_{6h}}(\mathsf{D_{3h}}). For 𝖢𝟤𝗏\mathsf{C_{2v}}, 𝖣𝟤\mathsf{D_{2}}, 𝖣𝟤𝗁\mathsf{D_{2h}}, 𝖢𝟦𝗏\mathsf{C_{4v}} and 𝖣𝟦𝗁\mathsf{D_{4h}} it is the one whose primes stand earliest, such as m′mmm'mm for 𝖣𝟤𝗁(𝖢𝟤𝗏)\mathsf{D_{2h}}(\mathsf{C_{2v}}) and 4′/mm′m4'/mm'm for 𝖣𝟦𝗁(𝖣𝟤𝗁)\mathsf{D_{4h}}(\mathsf{D_{2h}}). For the crystallographic groups, these are the symbols of the crystallographic tables.

In condensed-matter physics 𝖦𝟣′\mathsf{G1'} is written 𝖦×𝖹𝟤𝖳\mathsf{G} \times \mathsf{Z_{2}^{T}}, with 𝖹𝟤𝖳={𝘌,Θ}\mathsf{Z_{2}^{T}} = \{\mathsfit{E}, \varTheta\} [CGLW13].

30.3 Pointers ¶

Pointers are as in section 18.1. A record of this catalogue names each value that it takes from a record of the point groups by a pointer, such as "rotation/C3v#/character_table/irreps/0". A pointer to a record of this catalogue has the name of that record, such as "magnetic/C3v-C3#". The records of 𝖦\mathsf{G} and 𝖧\mathsf{H} are records of the point groups, and 𝖧\mathsf{H} is also a node of the subgroup lattice of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 15.1), with the monomorphism of its embedding (section 12.1). The pointer fields, named for what they replace, name these values of the records of the point groups:

  1. G and H (in group): the records of 𝖦\mathsf{G} and 𝖧\mathsf{H}.
  2. unitary_irreps and subduction_o3: the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H} and its subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in magnetic type II.
  3. permutation_representations: the permutation representations of 𝖦\mathsf{G}.
  4. orbifold (magnetic type III): the fields G, H and images, the orbifolds of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, of 𝖧\mathsf{H} and of the four images of section 31.2.
  5. record (in group.images), N (magnetic type II), rings, multiplicity and bases: the values of sections 31.2, 32.2 and 35.1.
  6. lattice (magnetic type III) and record (in subgroup_lattice): the values of section 36.

The field frame is an exact rotation FF that carries the elements of the record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} to their images in 𝖦\mathsf{G}. The matrix of the image of 𝘩\mathsfit{h} is F P(𝘩) F−1F\, P(\mathsfit{h})\, F^{-1}, with P(𝘩)P(\mathsfit{h}) the matrix in the record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (section 4.1). The field is the identity matrix when the two records share their frame. Of the rotations that carry these elements to their images, the field holds the one of least angle ω\omega, with F=P(𝘙𝐯(ω))F = P(\mathsfit{R_{\mathbf{v}}}(\omega)), 0≤ω≤π0 \le \omega \le \pi and 𝐯\mathbf{v} a unit vector (section 3). Among several of the least angle, it holds the one whose axis 𝐯\mathbf{v} comes first in the order of decreasing zz, then xx, then yy component. The field node of frame is the node of 𝖧\mathsf{H} in the subgroup lattice of 𝖦\mathsf{G}.

30.4 The group block ¶

The group block of a record of this catalogue has these fields:

  1. symbol: the Schoenflies symbol 𝖦(𝖧)\mathsf{G}(\mathsf{H}) or 𝖦𝟣′\mathsf{G1'}, written with the schoenflies of the records of 𝖦\mathsf{G} and 𝖧\mathsf{H} (section 2.2), such as "C_3v(C_3)".
  2. hermann_mauguin: the magnetic Hermann–Mauguin symbol of section 30.2, written as in section 2.3 with each prime as ', such as "4'/mm'm".
  3. magnetic_type: "II" or "III", the magnetic type of section 30.1.
  4. G and, in magnetic type III, H: the pointers to the records of 𝖦\mathsf{G} and 𝖧\mathsf{H}.
  5. node (magnetic type III): the id of the node of 𝖧\mathsf{H} in the subgroup lattice of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, the first node of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class.
  6. antiunitary_character (magnetic type III): the id of χ\chi among the irreps of 𝖦\mathsf{G}.
  7. abstract_type and gap_smallgroup_id: the isomorphism class of 𝖬\mathsf{M} and its identifier, as in section 2.2, the second the null continuous-group for a continuous group. A value equal to that of the record of 𝖦\mathsf{G} is the pointer to it. In magnetic type III both fields are such pointers, since the map that fixes the elements of 𝖧\mathsf{H} and takes each other element 𝘨\mathsfit{g} of 𝖦\mathsf{G} to Θ𝘨\varTheta\mathsfit{g} is an isomorphism of 𝖦\mathsf{G} onto 𝖬\mathsf{M}. In magnetic type II, 𝖬\mathsf{M} is isomorphic to 𝖦×𝖹𝟤\mathsf{G} \times \mathsf{Z_{2}}. A finite abelian 𝖬\mathsf{M} is written by its invariant factors ("Z_4 x Z_2" for 𝖢𝟦𝟣′\mathsf{C_{4}1'}). A finite 𝖬\mathsf{M} with an element of order ∣𝖬∣/2\lvert \mathsf{M}\rvert/2 is written "Dih(n)", with n=∣𝖬∣n = \lvert \mathsf{M}\rvert ("Dih(12)" for 𝖢𝟥𝗏𝟣′\mathsf{C_{3v}1'}). Any other 𝖬\mathsf{M} is written as the abstract_type of 𝖦\mathsf{G} followed by " x Z_2" ("Dih(8) x Z_2" for 𝖢𝟦𝗏𝟣′\mathsf{C_{4v}1'}, "SO(3) x Z_2" for 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{SO(3)1'}).
  8. alternative_names (magnetic type II): the name 𝖦×𝖹𝟤𝖳\mathsf{G} \times \mathsf{Z_{2}^{T}} of section 30.2, as an object with the symbol, such as "C_4v x Z_2^T", and the source, the key of the entry of references.bib.
  9. normalizer, centralizer: the normalizer and the centralizer of 𝖬\mathsf{M} in the parent, each with its symbol, and with index and normalizer_index as in section 2.2.
  10. images: the four images of section 31.2.
  11. allowed_properties (magnetic type III): the tensor properties odd under time reversal that 𝖬\mathsf{M} allows (section 35).

In magnetic type III the normalizer of 𝖬\mathsf{M} is the group of magnetic type II over the stabilizer of χ\chi in the normalizer of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}. The centralizer of 𝖬\mathsf{M} is the group of magnetic type II over the centralizer of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. In magnetic type II both groups are those of magnetic type II over the normalizer and the centralizer of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. Thus the symbol of each ends in 1'. The field index is the index of 𝖬\mathsf{M} in its normalizer, or the null continuous-group, and normalizer_index is the index of the centralizer in the normalizer.

30.5 Files ¶

The files of this catalogue are in the folder magnetic/, laid out and written as in section 18:

  1. data/json/<M>.json: the records.
  2. data/gap/, data/wolfram/, data/sympy/, data/latex/ and data/pdf/: the records in the other formats, the literal of each GAP, Wolfram and SymPy file bound to o3m.
  3. data/magnetic_groups.json: one entry for each record, with its id (the record's name), magnetic_type, symbol, hermann_mauguin and node (section 30.4, a finite group), and G and H as record names.
  4. CONVENTIONS.pdf.
  5. data/pdf/numbers.json: the numbers that the PDFs print (section 18).
  6. schema/<format>.schema.json: the JSON Schema of each format, derived from all its files.

The file data/magnetic_groups.json also holds magnetic_type_I, the pointer to the point groups, and aliases (section 15.2). Their schema is "point-group-atlas/magnetic/<format>/1". Here <format> is group for the record of a finite group, group-continuous for that of a continuous group, groups for that file and pdf-numbers for the numbers.
