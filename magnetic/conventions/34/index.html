34 Symmetrized powers ¶

The symmetrized square [𝒟2][\mathcal{D}^{2}] and the antisymmetrized square {𝒟2}\{\mathcal{D}^{2}\} of a corepresentation 𝒟\mathcal{D} are the symmetric and the antisymmetric part of 𝒟⊗𝒟\mathcal{D} \otimes \mathcal{D}. The symmetrized cube [𝒟3][\mathcal{D}^{3}] is the symmetric part of 𝒟⊗𝒟⊗𝒟\mathcal{D} \otimes \mathcal{D} \otimes \mathcal{D}. Each is a representation of 𝖬\mathsf{M}, of these characters on 𝖧\mathsf{H}:

(34.1)χ[𝒟2](𝘩)=12(χ𝒟(𝘩)2+χ𝒟(𝘩2)),χ{𝒟2}(𝘩)=12(χ𝒟(𝘩)2−χ𝒟(𝘩2)),χ[𝒟3](𝘩)=16(χ𝒟(𝘩)3+3χ𝒟(𝘩) χ𝒟(𝘩2)+2χ𝒟(𝘩3)).\begin{aligned} \chi_{[\mathcal{D}^{2}]}(\mathsfit{h}) &= \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\chi_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathsfit{h})^{2} + \chi_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathsfit{h}^{2})\bigr),\\ \chi_{\{\mathcal{D}^{2}\}}(\mathsfit{h}) &= \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\chi_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathsfit{h})^{2} - \chi_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathsfit{h}^{2})\bigr),\\ \chi_{[\mathcal{D}^{3}]}(\mathsfit{h}) &= \tfrac{1}{6}\bigl(\chi_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathsfit{h})^{3} + 3\chi_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathsfit{h})\,\chi_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathsfit{h}^{2}) + 2\chi_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathsfit{h}^{3})\bigr). \end{aligned}

The multiplicity of a corepresentation 𝒟\mathcal{D} in a representation 𝒟′\mathcal{D}' of 𝖬\mathsf{M} is

(34.2)b𝒟=∑𝘩∈𝖧χ𝒟′(𝘩) χ𝒟(𝘩)∗∑𝘩∈𝖧∣χ𝒟(𝘩)∣2,b_{\mathcal{D}} = \frac{\sum_{\mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{H}} \chi_{\mathcal{D}'}(\mathsfit{h})\, \chi_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathsfit{h})^{*}}{\sum_{\mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{H}} \lvert \chi_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathsfit{h})\rvert^{2}},

with the denominator ∣𝖧∣\lvert \mathsf{H}\rvert, 4∣𝖧∣4\lvert \mathsf{H}\rvert or 2∣𝖧∣2\lvert \mathsf{H}\rvert in the cases (a), (b) and (c) [BD68, eqs. 5.8 to 5.10]. The antisymmetrized square of a corepresentation of dimension 1 is zero.

The field symmetrized_powers lists the corepresentations in the order of corepresentations. Each entry has corepresentation, the id of its first irrep, and the decompositions of its powers in symmetrized_square, antisymmetrized_square and symmetrized_cube. A decomposition lists its terms as in splitting (section 33.1), and the list is empty for a power of dimension 0. The antisymmetrized squares and the symmetrized cubes of the irreducible corepresentations of the magnetic point groups are tabulated in [CS72].
