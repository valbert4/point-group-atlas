Conventions

This document defines the conventions of the records of the magnetic point groups, the closed subgroups of the parent 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} up to conjugacy (section 30.1). It continues the conventions of the point groups (rotation/CONVENTIONS.md). The files of conventions are numbered as one: sections 0 to 19 are in that file, sections 20 to 29 in double/CONVENTIONS.md and sections 30 to 39 in this one. It cites the sections of the conventions of the point groups by number and uses their notation, adding time reversal (section 30.1) and the corepresentations (section 32). A record of this catalogue names each value that it takes from a record of the point groups by a pointer (section 30.3). It stores the values that it derives from them by a rule, such as the characters of its corepresentations (section 33).
