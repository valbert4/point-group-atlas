33 The corepresentation table ¶

The character of a corepresentation is the trace of its matrices on 𝖧\mathsf{H}: χΛ\chi_{\Lambda}, 2χΛ2\chi_{\Lambda} or χΛ+χΛ𝘢\chi_{\Lambda} + \chi_{\Lambda^{\mathsfit{a}}} in the cases (a), (b) and (c). It takes one value on the class of 𝘩\mathsfit{h} and on that of 𝘢𝘩−1𝘢−1\mathsfit{a}\mathsfit{h}^{-1}\mathsfit{a}^{-1}. The columns of the corepresentation table are therefore the merged classes of 𝖧\mathsf{H}, each the union of a class and of its image under 𝘩↦𝘢𝘩−1𝘢−1\mathsfit{h} \mapsto \mathsfit{a}\mathsfit{h}^{-1}\mathsfit{a}^{-1}. Its rows are the corepresentations, and its entries are their characters. The field corepresentation_table holds classes, each merged class as a list of class ids of 𝖧\mathsf{H} in their order, and rows, the irreps of each corepresentation. Its characters give for each row the character on each merged class, as an exact value (section 8). The corepresentations of the 58 crystallographic groups of magnetic type III are tabulated in [Cra66], and their double-valued ones in [CW67], which also corrects Table VI of [Cra66].

33.1 Restrictions ¶

The splitting table restricts each corepresentation of 𝖦𝟣′\mathsf{G1'}, an irrep of 𝖦\mathsf{G} over ℝ\mathbb{R} (section 7), to 𝖬\mathsf{M} as a sum of corepresentations. A representation of 𝖬\mathsf{M} holds a corepresentation as many times as its restriction to 𝖧\mathsf{H} holds the first irrep of that corepresentation, half as many in case (b). The field subduction_corepresentations gives by this rule, from the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H} in subduction_o3, the multiplicity of each corepresentation in each D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} of the parent for JJ up to J_max. Its g and u list these by JJ for each parity (section 11), each entry a map from a corepresentation, by the id of its first irrep, to its multiplicity.

In magnetic type III the record also has the field splitting, one entry for each irrep of 𝖦\mathsf{G} over ℝ\mathbb{R}, a complex pair under its first irrep. Each entry has irrep and terms, each term a corepresentation (the id of its first irrep) and its multiplicity.

In an external magnetic field, the corepresentations of the group 𝖬\mathsf{M} that preserves it classify the orbital states of an ion or a molecule, with the spin a spectator, a rule stated in no reference. The field acts on the orbital motion, time reversal acts on the orbital functions by complex conjugation (section 31.1), and the spin's Zeeman term commutes with the action of 𝖬\mathsf{M} on these functions.
