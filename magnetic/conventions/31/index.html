31 Time reversal ¶

31.1 Kets ¶

Time reversal acts on a function by complex conjugation. On the kets of section 4.1, with their Condon–Shortley phases, it is therefore Θ∣ mJ⟩=(−1)m∣ −mJ⟩\varTheta \bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle = (-1)^m \bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle, and a real function such as zz is even under time reversal. Every block of the symmetry-adapted bases of section 14 carries the corepresentation of its irrep in 𝖦𝟣′\mathsf{G1'}. Time reversal acts on a block of a real irrep by the matrix NN (sections 32.2 and 37.2). It takes a block of the first irrep of a complex pair to the block of its partner.

31.2 The four images ¶

A vector is polar or axial, and even or odd under time reversal [BD68, section 2.6]. The element of the parent of sign θ\theta over 𝘨\mathsfit{g} moves a vector 𝐱\mathbf{x}, a row like a point (section 4.1), to one of four rows:

  1. 𝐱 P(𝘨)\mathbf{x}\, P(\mathsfit{g}) for a polar vector even under time reversal, such as a position or an electric dipole.
  2. (det⁡𝘨) 𝐱 P(𝘨)(\det \mathsfit{g})\, \mathbf{x}\, P(\mathsfit{g}) for an axial vector even under time reversal, such as a rotation or an electric toroidal dipole.
  3. θ 𝐱 P(𝘨)\theta\, \mathbf{x}\, P(\mathsfit{g}) for a polar vector odd under time reversal, such as a current or a toroidal moment.
  4. θ (det⁡𝘨) 𝐱 P(𝘨)\theta\, (\det \mathsfit{g})\, \mathbf{x}\, P(\mathsfit{g}) for an axial vector odd under time reversal, such as a magnetization or a spin.

The image of 𝖬\mathsf{M} of each kind is the group of the matrices by which its elements move that kind of vector. Each of the four images is a point group. The group 𝖬\mathsf{M} allows a nonzero vector of a kind, such as a spontaneous magnetization, exactly when its image of that kind fixes a nonzero vector.

The field group.images names the four images by their kinds, polar_even, axial_even, polar_odd and axial_odd in the order of the list above. The record of an image is a pointer to the record of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class. Its matrix is a frame FF that carries the matrices of that record onto the matrices of the image, as a set, the one that section 30.3 chooses for frame. An image whose class has no record, such as one of axial order above 12, has the null outside-the-catalogue as its record and its Schoenflies symbol as symbol, such as "C_22".

A magnetic moment 𝐦\mathbf{m} is an axial vector odd under time reversal, which the element moves to θ (det⁡𝘨) 𝐦 P(𝘨)\theta\, (\det \mathsfit{g})\, \mathbf{m}\, P(\mathsfit{g}). For a column vector this rule reads 𝐦↦θ (det⁡𝘨) P(𝘨)⊤𝐦\mathbf{m} \mapsto \theta\, (\det \mathsfit{g})\, P(\mathsfit{g})^{\top} \mathbf{m}, as in [STT23].
