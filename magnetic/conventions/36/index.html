36 Subgroup lattices ¶

The subgroups of 𝖬\mathsf{M} are taken up to conjugation by its elements. The magnetic type of a subgroup (section 30.1) is I without antiunitary elements, II when Θ\varTheta is among them, and III otherwise. An element of 𝖬\mathsf{M} over 𝘨\mathsfit{g} conjugates a subgroup as 𝘨\mathsfit{g} does, since time reversal commutes with every element (section 30.1).

In magnetic type III the projection of 𝖬\mathsf{M} onto 𝖦\mathsf{G} is an isomorphism. The subgroup over a subgroup 𝖪\mathsf{K} of 𝖦\mathsf{G} is 𝖪\mathsf{K} itself, of type I, when 𝖪≤𝖧\mathsf{K} \le \mathsf{H}, and 𝖪(𝖪∩𝖧)\mathsf{K}(\mathsf{K} \cap \mathsf{H}), of type III, otherwise. The subgroup lattice of 𝖬\mathsf{M} is thus that of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 15.1) with these groups at its nodes.

In magnetic type II the subgroups over 𝖪\mathsf{K} are 𝖪\mathsf{K}, of type I, 𝖪𝟣′\mathsf{K1'}, of type II, and 𝖪(𝖪′)\mathsf{K}(\mathsf{K}'), of type III, for each subgroup 𝖪′\mathsf{K}' of index 2 in 𝖪\mathsf{K}. Over the representative 𝖪\mathsf{K} of a node of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, two groups 𝖪(𝖪′)\mathsf{K}(\mathsf{K}') are conjugate exactly when the normalizer 𝖭𝖦(𝖪)\mathsf{N}_{\mathsf{G}}(\mathsf{K}) carries one 𝖪′\mathsf{K}' to the other.

The symbol of a subgroup of type I is the schoenflies of the record of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class. The symbol of a subgroup of type II or III is that of section 30.4, written with the schoenflies of the records of 𝖪\mathsf{K} and of its unitary elements, 𝖪∩𝖧\mathsf{K} \cap \mathsf{H} or 𝖪′\mathsf{K}'. In the documents, a node's symbol has 𝖢𝗌\mathsf{C_{s}} for 𝖢𝟣𝗏\mathsf{C_{1v}} and 𝖢𝗂\mathsf{C_{i}} for 𝖲𝟤\mathsf{S_{2}}, as in section 15.1, and gray letters distinguish the classes with one symbol that are not conjugate.

The field subgroup_lattice has nodes, one for each class. The representative of a class is the subgroup over the representative 𝖪\mathsf{K} of a node of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. Each node has these fields:

  1. node: the id of that node in the subgroup lattice of 𝖦\mathsf{G}.
  2. magnetic_type: "I", "II" or "III".
  3. symbol: the symbol of the subgroup.
  4. record: the pointer to the record of the subgroup, a record of the point groups in type I and of this catalogue otherwise, or the null outside-the-catalogue.
  5. unitary (type III): the subgroup of its unitary elements, as the id node of its class in the subgroup lattice of 𝖦\mathsf{G} and its index member (from 0) among the members of that class.

In magnetic type III the nodes follow those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in order, and the field lattice of subgroup_lattice is the pointer to the lattice of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. That lattice has the orders, the numbers of conjugates, the normalizers, the centralizers and the edges. In magnetic type II the nodes are sorted by order, magnetic type, node and unitary, and each also has these fields:

  1. id: n00, n01, ….
  2. order, class_size, normal: as in section 15.1.
  3. normalizer, centralizer: the ids of the classes of the normalizer and the centralizer of the representative in 𝖬\mathsf{M}, both of type II.
  4. covers: the ids of the classes that the class covers, the lower of its edges as in section 15.1.

A document draws the lattice when it has at most 60 classes.
