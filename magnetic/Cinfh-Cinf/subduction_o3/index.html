4.1 Subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} ¶

Data: subduction_corepresentations, subduction_o3 in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 11, 33.1 and 37.4. The irrep D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} of 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'} has angular momentum JJ and parity pp (g\mathrm{g} for p=+1p = +1, u\mathrm{u} for p=−1p = -1), and time reversal acts on it as on its kets (Conventions, section 31.1). A corepresentation occurs in its restriction to 𝖢∞𝗁(𝖢∞)\mathsf{C_{\infty h}}(\mathsf{C_{\infty}}) as often as its first irrep occurs in the restriction to 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} (the section Subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} of the document of 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}).
