2.6 Clebsch–Gordan coefficients ¶

Data: clebsch_gordan, corepresentations in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 10.3, 10.4 and 38. A copy κ\kappa of a corepresentation 𝒟\mathcal{D} in 𝒟1⊗𝒟2\mathcal{D}_1\otimes\mathcal{D}_2 is a block CC of Clebsch–Gordan coefficients that satisfies two equations (Conventions, section 38). It has a row for each pair (μ1,μ2)(\mu_1, \mu_2) of partners of 𝒟1\mathcal{D}_1 and 𝒟2\mathcal{D}_2, in Kronecker order, and a column for each partner μ\mu of 𝒟\mathcal{D}. A copy whose columns of the first irrep of 𝒟\mathcal{D} are a block of the table of 𝖳\mathsf{T} times 1 or i\mathrm{i} is named by a pointer to that block (Conventions, section 38.2). That block is the copy κ\kappa of the first irrep of 𝒟\mathcal{D} in the product of the first irreps of 𝒟1\mathcal{D}_1 and 𝒟2\mathcal{D}_2, in the section Clebsch–Gordan coefficients of the document of 𝖳\mathsf{T}. The phase is i\mathrm{i} for T⊗T→T\mathrm{\scriptsize T}\otimes \mathrm{\scriptsize T}\to \mathrm{\scriptsize T} (κ=1\kappa = 1), and 1 for the other copies named by a pointer. The table below sets every other copy. Each block is headed 𝒟1⊗𝒟2→𝒟\mathcal{D}_1\otimes\mathcal{D}_2\to\mathcal{D}, then κ\kappa where 𝒟\mathcal{D} occurs more than once in 𝒟1⊗𝒟2\mathcal{D}_1\otimes\mathcal{D}_2, and a normalization 𝒩\mathcal{N}. Row μ\mu gives the nonzero entries of column μ\mu of 𝒩 C\sqrt{\mathcal{N}}\,C, so that their squared moduli sum to 𝒩\mathcal{N}. Each entry is written before ∣μ1 μ2⟩\lvert {\mu_1}\,{\mu_2}\rangle, the product of the partners μ1\mu_1 of 𝒟1\mathcal{D}_1 and μ2\mu_2 of 𝒟2\mathcal{D}_2. Only 𝒟1≤𝒟2\mathcal{D}_1 \le \mathcal{D}_2 is listed (𝒟2⊗𝒟1\mathcal{D}_2\otimes\mathcal{D}_1 exchanges μ1\mu_1 and μ2\mu_2).
