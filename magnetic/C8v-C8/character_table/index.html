Data: corepresentation_table, corepresentations, unitary_irreps in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 5, 32.3 and 33. A row is a corepresentation of 𝖢𝟪𝗏(𝖢𝟪)\mathsf{C_{8v}}(\mathsf{C_{8}}), named by its irreps (section 2.1). A column is a merged class of 𝖢𝟪\mathsf{C_{8}}, the union of a class and of its image under 𝘩↦𝘢𝘩−1𝘢−1\mathsfit{h} \mapsto \mathsfit{a} \mathsfit{h}^{-1} \mathsfit{a}^{-1}. The entry of a row is the character of its corepresentation on the class, the sum of the characters of its irreps. The listed residues are the m mod 8m \bmod 8 of the kets of the irreps of a row whose character is not real (a dash for the other rows). These residues follow the section Multiplicities and allowed mm of the document of 𝖢𝟪\mathsf{C_{8}}. Where a function moves with the points (Conventions, section 4.3), two rows with complex-conjugate characters exchange, and these residues distinguish them. Exact values are rationals, square roots, cosines and sines of rational multiples of π\pi, and the constants
