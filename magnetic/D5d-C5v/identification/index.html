The field magnetic_type is III, so 𝖣𝟧𝖽(𝖢𝟧𝗏)\mathsf{D_{5d}}(\mathsf{C_{5v}}) contains the elements of 𝖢𝟧𝗏\mathsf{C_{5v}} and the products of time reversal Θ\varTheta with the other elements of 𝖣𝟧𝖽\mathsf{D_{5d}}. The fields G and H name these groups by their records (Conventions, section 30.3), and antiunitary_character names the irrep χ\chi of 𝖣𝟧𝖽\mathsf{D_{5d}} with kernel 𝖢𝟧𝗏\mathsf{C_{5v}}. The four fields images name the groups by which the elements move a polar or an axial vector, even or odd under time reversal (Conventions, section 31.2). Each names the record of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class and the frame that carries that record onto the image. The fields normalizer and centralizer are taken in 𝖮(𝟥)𝟣′\mathsf{O(3)1'}, and both groups are of magnetic type II. The field orbifold of the record gives the orbifold symbols of 𝖣𝟧𝖽\mathsf{D_{5d}} and of 𝖢𝟧𝗏\mathsf{C_{5v}}.
