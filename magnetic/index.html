Volume III: magnetic point groups

Conventions

Conventions, sections 30 to 39: the records of this volume, with its index of notation. Sections 0 to 19 are those of volume I.

Groups

A row holds the groups with one point group G of volume I: G1′, of magnetic type II, and then the groups G(H) of magnetic type III. The rows stand in the order of volume I. A group of magnetic type I is a point group, and its document is in volume I.

Data

The volume as one PDF is a file of this site. The other files are those of the repository at commit 119ff5dc: a PDF is a copy on this site and opens in a tab of its own, and any other file downloads from GitHub.

The volume as one PDF (1921 sheets) holds the conventions, the documents of its groups, the references and the index of notation. CONVENTIONS.pdf is CONVENTIONS.md typeset, the file magnetic_groups.json lists the groups of this volume with their magnetic types and symbols, and the schemas are those of this volume's records. The Data section of each group lists the group's own files.

The file index.json gives the size and SHA-256 digest of each file of the records, of each volume's conventions, lattice and record schemas, and of the README, the references and the license. For each record it also lists the records that the record names.
