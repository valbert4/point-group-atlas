2.1 Families of irreps ¶

Data: corepresentations in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 6, 7, 16, 32.1 and 37.2. A row is a family of irreps Λ\Lambda of 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, the subgroup of the unitary elements of 𝖣∞𝟣′\mathsf{D_{\infty}1'}, in the order of the section Families of irreps of the document of 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}. A row gives its dimension dd, its Frobenius–Schur indicator νFS\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} and the range of the index of its irreps, as in that section. A dash stands for none. A row also gives the case of Λ\Lambda and the corepresentation that contains it (corep), named by its irreps. In magnetic type II the indicator of Λ\Lambda is νFS\nu_{\mathrm{FS}}, the Haar integral of χΛ(𝘨2)\chi_{\Lambda}(\mathsfit{g}^2) over 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}. A real irrep alone is of case (a).
