2.4 Symmetrized powers ¶

Data: corepresentations, symmetrized_powers in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 32.3 and 34. A row is a corepresentation 𝒟\mathcal{D} of 𝖣𝟣𝟤𝖽(𝖢𝟣𝟤𝗏)\mathsf{D_{12d}}(\mathsf{C_{12v}}), named by its irreps. A row gives the decompositions into corepresentations of its symmetrized square [𝒟2][\mathcal{D}^{2}], its antisymmetrized square {𝒟2}\{\mathcal{D}^{2}\} and its symmetrized cube [𝒟3][\mathcal{D}^{3}]. A power of dimension 0 has the entry 0.
