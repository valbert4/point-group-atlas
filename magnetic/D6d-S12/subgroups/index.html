3.1 Subgroup lattice ¶

Data: subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 15 and 36. The diagram shows the conjugacy classes of subgroups, each joined to the classes of its maximal subgroups, and the orders increase upward. A normal subgroup is framed, and ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of conjugate subgroups. The table lists the classes by order, then by magnetic type, those of one symbol together. A row gives the representative of a class of subgroups (subgrp), its magnetic type (type), its order, the number of subgroups in the class (conjugates) and whether the representative is normal. It also gives its normalizer and its centralizer in 𝖣𝟨𝖽(𝖲𝟣𝟤)\mathsf{D_{6d}}(\mathsf{S_{12}}), by their classes.
