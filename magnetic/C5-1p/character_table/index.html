2.2 Corepresentation table ¶

Data: corepresentation_table, corepresentations, unitary_irreps in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 5, 32.3 and 33. A row is a corepresentation of 𝖢𝟧𝟣′\mathsf{C_{5}1'}, named by its irreps (section 2.1). A column is a merged class of 𝖢𝟧\mathsf{C_{5}}, the union of a class and of the class of the inverses of its elements. The entry of a row is the character of its corepresentation on the class, the sum of the characters of its irreps. Exact values are rationals, square roots, cosines and sines of rational multiples of π\pi, and the constant
