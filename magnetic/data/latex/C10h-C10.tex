The field \texttt{magnetic\symbol{95}type} is III, so $\grp{C_{10h}}(\grp{C_{10}})$ contains the elements of $\grp{C_{10}}$ and the products of time reversal $\varTheta$ with the other elements of $\grp{C_{10h}}$. The fields \texttt{G} and \texttt{H} name these groups by their records (\href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, section \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.30.3}{30.3}), and \texttt{antiunitary\symbol{95}character} names the irrep $\chi$ of $\grp{C_{10h}}$ with kernel $\grp{C_{10}}$. The four fields \texttt{images} name the groups by which the elements move a polar or an axial vector, even or odd under time reversal (\href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, section \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.31.2}{31.2}). Each names the record of its $\grp{O(3)}$-conjugacy class and the frame that carries that record onto the image. The fields \texttt{normalizer} and \texttt{centralizer} are taken in $\grp{O(3)1'}$, and both groups are of magnetic type II. The field \texttt{orbifold} of the record gives the orbifold symbols of $\grp{C_{10h}}$ and of $\grp{C_{10}}$.

Conventions: \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, sections \href{../../../rotation/CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.5}{5}, \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.30.1}{30.1}, \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.30.3}{30.3} and \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.32.1}{32.1}.
\leavevmode\label{n:head.class}A row is a class of $\grp{C_{10h}}$. \label{n:index.element}\label{n:element.g}A row gives the indices $k$ of its elements, as in the section Symmetry operations of the document of \href{../../../rotation/data/pdf/C10h.pdf}{$\grp{C_{10h}}$}. It also gives the sign $\theta = \chi(g)$ of the element of $\grp{C_{10h}}(\grp{C_{10}})$ over each element $g$ of the class. An element of sign $-1$ is antiunitary, the product $\varTheta g$ of time reversal and an element $g$ of $\grp{C_{10h}}$ outside $\grp{C_{10}}$. As $\grp{C_{10h}}(\grp{C_{10}})$ is isomorphic to $\grp{C_{10h}}$, its classes are these, and its elements multiply as those of $\grp{C_{10h}}$ do, with their signs multiplied. The antiunitary representative $\elt{a}$ of the corepresentations (\texttt{representative}) is $\varTheta\elt{i}$, over the element of index 10. The field \texttt{frame} is the identity, so the elements of the record of $\grp{C_{10}}$ are those of $\grp{C_{10h}}$ with the same matrices.
