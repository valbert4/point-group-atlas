The field \texttt{magnetic\symbol{95}type} is II, so $\grp{S_{24}1'}$ contains the elements of $\grp{S_{24}}$ and their products with time reversal $\varTheta$. The field \texttt{G} names $\grp{S_{24}}$ by its record (\href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, section \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.30.3}{30.3}). The four fields \texttt{images} name the groups by which the elements move a polar or an axial vector, even or odd under time reversal (\href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, section \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.31.2}{31.2}). Each names the record of its $\grp{O(3)}$-conjugacy class and the frame that carries that record onto the image. The fields \texttt{normalizer} and \texttt{centralizer} are taken in $\grp{O(3)1'}$, and both groups are of magnetic type II. \label{n:group.timereversal}The field \texttt{alternative\symbol{95}names} gives the name of $\grp{S_{24}1'}$ in condensed-matter physics [\href{../../../REFERENCES.pdf\#ref.CGLW13}{CGLW13}], where $\grp{Z_{2}^{T}}$ is the group of order 2 that time reversal $\varTheta$ generates.
