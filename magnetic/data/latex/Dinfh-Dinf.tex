The field \texttt{magnetic\symbol{95}type} is III, so $\grp{D_{\infty h}}(\grp{D_{\infty}})$ contains the elements of $\grp{D_{\infty}}$ and the products of time reversal $\varTheta$ with the other elements of $\grp{D_{\infty h}}$. The fields \texttt{G} and \texttt{H} name these groups by their records (\href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, section \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.30.3}{30.3}), and \texttt{antiunitary\symbol{95}character} names the irrep $\chi$ of $\grp{D_{\infty h}}$ with kernel $\grp{D_{\infty}}$. The four fields \texttt{images} name the groups by which the elements move a polar or an axial vector, even or odd under time reversal (\href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, section \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.31.2}{31.2}). Each names the record of its $\grp{O(3)}$-conjugacy class, and its frame is the identity. The fields \texttt{normalizer} and \texttt{centralizer} are taken in $\grp{O(3)1'}$, and both groups are of magnetic type II. The field \texttt{orbit\symbol{95}space} names the orbit spaces of the directions under $\grp{D_{\infty h}}$ and under $\grp{D_{\infty}}$, each an arc of polar angles.

\section{Corepresentation table}\label{s:character_table}
Conventions: \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, sections \href{../../../rotation/CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.16}{16}, \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.32.3}{32.3}, \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.33}{33} and \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.37.3}{37.3}.
A row is a family of corepresentations of $\grp{D_{\infty h}}(\grp{D_{\infty}})$, named by its irreps (section~\ref{s:families}), with the range of its index (index, a dash for none). A column is a component of the class space of $\grp{D_{\infty}}$ (the section Class space of the document of \href{../../../rotation/data/pdf/Dinf.pdf}{$\grp{D_{\infty}}$}).
