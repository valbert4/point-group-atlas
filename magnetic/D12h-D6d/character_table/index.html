A column is a merged class of 𝖣𝟨𝖽\mathsf{D_{6d}}, the union of a class and of its image under 𝘩↦𝘢𝘩−1𝘢−1\mathsfit{h} \mapsto \mathsfit{a} \mathsfit{h}^{-1} \mathsfit{a}^{-1}. The entry of a row is the character of its corepresentation on the class, the sum of the characters of its irreps.
