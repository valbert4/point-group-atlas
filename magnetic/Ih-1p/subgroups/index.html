3.1 Subgroup lattice ¶

Data: subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 15 and 36. The lattice has more than 60 classes and no diagram. A gray letter after a symbol distinguishes the classes of subgroups with that symbol, which are not conjugate in 𝖨𝗁𝟣′\mathsf{I_{h}1'}. The table lists the classes by order, then by magnetic type, those of one symbol together. A row gives the representative of a class of subgroups (subgrp), its magnetic type (type), its order, the number of subgroups in the class (conjugates) and whether the representative is normal. It also gives its normalizer and its centralizer in 𝖨𝗁𝟣′\mathsf{I_{h}1'}, by their classes.
