Conventions: sections 9 and 32.2. A unitary element 𝘩\mathsfit{h} acts in a corepresentation 𝒟\mathcal{D} by the transposes DΛ(𝘩)⊤D^{\Lambda}(\mathsfit{h})^{\top} of the matrices of its irreps Λ\Lambda (the section Representation matrices of the document of 𝖣𝟣𝟣\mathsf{D_{11}}). The representative 𝘢=Θ𝘪\mathsfit{a} = \varTheta\mathsfit{i} acts by a unitary matrix times the complex conjugation KK. In case (a), 𝒟(𝘢)=NK\mathcal{D}(\mathsfit{a}) = NK. In case (b), 𝒟(𝘢)=(0N−N0)K\mathcal{D}(\mathsfit{a}) = \left(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & N \\ -N & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\right) K. In case (c), 𝒟(𝘢)=(0DΛ(𝘢2)⊤10)K\mathcal{D}(\mathsfit{a}) = \left(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & D^{\Lambda}(\mathsfit{a}^2)^{\top} \\ 1 & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\right) K, with no matrix NN. Where a function moves with the points (Conventions, section 4.3), the corepresentation on the same kets is the complex conjugate of 𝒟\mathcal{D}: a unitary element 𝘩\mathsfit{h} acts by DΛ(𝘩)†D^{\Lambda}(\mathsfit{h})^{\dagger}, and N∗N^{*} replaces NN. A row is a corepresentation, by its irreps. A row gives its case and its matrix NN (a dash in case (c)).
