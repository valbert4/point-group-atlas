3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: corepresentations, splitting in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 12 and 33.1. A row is an irrep Γ\Gamma of 𝖣𝟪\mathsf{D_{8}} over ℝ\mathbb{R}, which is a corepresentation of 𝖣𝟪𝟣′\mathsf{D_{8}1'} (Conventions, section 32.1). A row gives the restriction of Γ\Gamma to 𝖣𝟪(𝖢𝟪)\mathsf{D_{8}}(\mathsf{C_{8}}) as a sum of corepresentations (section 2.1). A corepresentation of 𝖣𝟪𝟣′\mathsf{D_{8}1'} splits in 𝖣𝟪(𝖢𝟪)\mathsf{D_{8}}(\mathsf{C_{8}}) exactly when its row has more than one term.
