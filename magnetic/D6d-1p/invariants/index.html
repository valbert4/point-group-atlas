5.2 Invariants ¶

Data: invariants in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 31.2 and 35.1. The invariant polynomials of 𝖣𝟨𝖽𝟣′\mathsf{D_{6d}1'} in one vector of a kind are those of its image of that kind (section 1.1), in the section Invariant polynomials of the image’s document (rings). A row is a degree aa in a polar vector even under time reversal. A row gives the number of linearly independent invariants of degree aa in it and bb in an axial vector odd under time reversal, for a+b≤6a + b \le 6.
