The field type is I when 𝖣𝟧𝖽\mathsf{D_{5d}} contains only rotations, II when 𝖣𝟧𝖽\mathsf{D_{5d}} contains the inversion 𝘪\mathsfit{i} and III otherwise. The fields center and commutator_subgroup are classes of the table of section 3.1. The field polar is yes when 𝖣𝟧𝖽\mathsf{D_{5d}} fixes a nonzero vector. Crystallographers call a group chiral, or enantiomorphic, when its type is I, and centrosymmetric when it contains 𝘪\mathsfit{i}. The field laue_class is the group generated by 𝖣𝟧𝖽\mathsf{D_{5d}} and 𝘪\mathsfit{i}. In product_form, after a semidirect product, ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of subgroups conjugate to its second factor.
