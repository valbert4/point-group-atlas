3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: group, subduction_embeddings, subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 12.2 and 12.4. A column is a class of subgroups 𝖧\mathsf{H}, in the order of the table of section 3.1. The row subgrp names it by its symbol, as in that table. The symbol links to the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H}, the restriction of Γ\Gamma along a monomorphism from the record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} onto the subgroup, as a sum of the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The monomorphism identifies the subgroup with its record, and 𝖧\mathsf{H} names both.

Read down a column, a table gives induction (Frobenius reciprocity): Γ\Gamma occurs in Ind⁡𝖧𝖣𝟧𝖽Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{D_{5d}}} \Lambda as often as Λ\Lambda occurs in the entry of row Γ\Gamma. For every entry, the record holds the unitary matrix that carries Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H} into the direct sum of the matrices of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (subduction[].blocks). For every class 𝖧\mathsf{H} it holds the left coset representatives (transversal). From both, Ind⁡𝖧𝖣𝟧𝖽Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{D_{5d}}} \Lambda and its intertwiners follow as in Conventions, section 12.4.
