2.4 Clebsch–Gordan coefficients ¶

Conventions: sections 10.3 and 10.6. These coupling rules cover every pair of irreps:

  1. Every irrep in the product of two irreps (section 2.3) occurs once: 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} are simply reducible.

  2. ⟨ m1J1  m2J2∣ MJ⟩\bigl\langle{}^{J_1}_{\,m_1}\,{}^{J_2}_{\,m_2}\bigm\vert{}^{J}_{\,M}\bigr\rangle in the Condon–Shortley convention [VMK88, section 8.1.1]: real, zero unless M=m1+m2M = m_1 + m_2, and ⟨ J1J1  J−J1J2∣ JJ⟩>0\bigl\langle{}^{J_1}_{\,J_1}\,{}^{J_2}_{\,J-J_1}\bigm\vert{}^{J}_{\,J}\bigr\rangle > 0.

  3. C[(μ1,μ2),μ]=⟨ m1J1  m2J2∣ MJ⟩C[(\mu_1,\mu_2),\mu] = \bigl\langle{}^{J_1}_{\,m_1}\,{}^{J_2}_{\,m_2}\bigm\vert{}^{J}_{\,M}\bigr\rangle with μ1=J1−m1+1\mu_1 = J_1 - m_1 + 1, μ2=J2−m2+1\mu_2 = J_2 - m_2 + 1, μ=J−M+1\mu = J - M + 1 (mm descending), so the phase rule (Conventions, section 10.3) is the Condon–Shortley condition.

  4. The closed form of van der Waerden and Racah [VMK88, section 8.2.1, eq. 3] sums over the zz for which every factorial argument is ≥0\ge 0:

    (2.4.1)⟨ m1J1  m2J2∣ MJ⟩=δM,m1+m2(2J+1) (J1+J2−J)! (J1−J2+J)! (−J1+J2+J)!(J1+J2+J+1)!×(J1+m1)! (J1−m1)! (J2+m2)! (J2−m2)! (J+M)! (J−M)!×∑z(−1)zz! (J1+J2−J−z)! (J1−m1−z)! (J2+m2−z)! (J−J2+m1+z)! (J−J1−m2+z)!.\begin{aligned}&\bigl\langle{}^{J_1}_{\,m_1}\,{}^{J_2}_{\,m_2}\bigm\vert{}^{J}_{\,M}\bigr\rangle = \delta_{M,m_1+m_2}\sqrt{\frac{(2J+1)\,(J_1+J_2-J)!\,(J_1-J_2+J)!\,(-J_1+J_2+J)!}{(J_1+J_2+J+1)!}}\\ &\qquad\times\sqrt{(J_1+m_1)!\,(J_1-m_1)!\,(J_2+m_2)!\,(J_2-m_2)!\,(J+M)!\,(J-M)!}\\ &\qquad\times\sum_z\frac{(-1)^z}{z!\,(J_1+J_2-J-z)!\,(J_1-m_1-z)!\,(J_2+m_2-z)!\,(J-J_2+m_1+z)!\,(J-J_1-m_2+z)!}.\end{aligned}

  5. Symmetries [VMK88, section 8.4]: ⟨ m1J1  m2J2∣ MJ⟩=(−1)J1+J2−J⟨ m2J2  m1J1∣ MJ⟩=(−1)J1+J2−J⟨ −m1J1  −m2J2∣ −MJ⟩\bigl\langle{}^{J_1}_{\,m_1}\,{}^{J_2}_{\,m_2}\bigm\vert{}^{J}_{\,M}\bigr\rangle = (-1)^{J_1+J_2-J}\bigl\langle{}^{J_2}_{\,m_2}\,{}^{J_1}_{\,m_1}\bigm\vert{}^{J}_{\,M}\bigr\rangle = (-1)^{J_1+J_2-J}\bigl\langle{}^{J_1}_{\,{-m_1}}\,{}^{J_2}_{\,{-m_2}}\bigm\vert{}^{J}_{\,{-M}}\bigr\rangle.
