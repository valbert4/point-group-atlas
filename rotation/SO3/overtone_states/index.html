6.3 Overtone states for v≤4v \le 4 ¶

Data: subduction_unitary in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 19. The table gives the highest-weight states of v≤4v \le 4 quanta of D(1)D^{(1)} and D(2)D^{(2)} by the rule of Conventions, section 19. These states are partner 1 (m=J′m = J') of each copy of D(J′)D^{(J')} in each rv−ℓHℓr^{v-\ell}H_\ell. The state ∣v1…vN⟩\lvert v_1 \dots v_N\rangle has v1,…,vNv_1, \dots, v_N quanta in the partners ∣J,J⟩,…,∣J,−J⟩\lvert J, J\rangle, \dots, \lvert J, -J\rangle in turn. A coefficient is that of the monomial in the creation operators, a rational times a square root. A state with vJ−J′vJ - J' odd carries the factor i\mathrm{i}, so that its copy is adapted to time reversal (Conventions, section 19).
