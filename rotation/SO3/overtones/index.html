6.2 Vibrational overtone levels ¶

Data: subduction_unitary in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 19. The states of vv quanta of a degenerate mode Γ=D(J)\Gamma = D^{(J)}, a level of section 6.1, carry Sym⁡vΓ\operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma. They split by harmonic degree ℓ=v,v−2,…\ell = v, v - 2, \dots into rv−ℓHℓr^{v-\ell}H_\ell, where r2r^2 is the invariant quadratic form of the level and HℓH_\ell consists of the states of ℓ\ell quanta annihilated by the invariant Laplacian. The symmetric power Sym⁡vD(J)\operatorname{Sym}^{v}D^{(J)} contains D(J′)D^{(J')} as many times as there are monomials of degree vv in the partners ∣J,m⟩\lvert J, m\rangle of weight J′J', minus those of weight J′+1J' + 1. By Cayley and Sylvester’s rule, this number is that of the partitions of vJ−J′vJ - J' into at most vv parts of size at most 2J2J, minus those of vJ−J′−1vJ - J' - 1. The tables give Sym⁡vΓ\operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma for J≤3J \le 3 and, in the same row, HℓH_\ell at ℓ=v\ell = v: Sym⁡vΓ=Hℓ⊕r2Sym⁡v−2Γ\operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma = H_\ell \oplus r^2\operatorname{Sym}^{v-2}\Gamma at ℓ=v\ell = v.
