6.1 Subduction from 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} ¶

Data: subduction_unitary in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 19. A level of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} is a real irrep Γ\Gamma of dimension N≥2N \ge 2: a degenerate mode of vibration, or a set of degenerate orbitals. Its matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) lie in 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})}. The branching rule 𝖴(N)⊃𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} \supset \mathsf{SO(3)} gives the multiplicity bΛ(λ)b_\Lambda(\lambda) of the irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} in the irrep 𝕊λ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda} of 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} of highest weight λ=[λ1,λ2,… ]\lambda = [\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots], restricted along DΓD^{\Gamma}. The quanta of a degenerate mode fill the one-row shapes 𝕊[v]Γ=Sym⁡vΓ\mathbb{S}_{[v]}\Gamma = \operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma (section 6.2), and nn electrons of spin SS fill the two-column shapes λ=[2n/2−S,12S]\lambda = [2^{n/2-S}, 1^{2S}] (section 6.4). Each level here is a member of a family of the table. A row gives the family (level) and the subgroups through which 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} restricts to 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} (chain). The chain ends at the image of Γ\Gamma, the group formed by the level’s matrices, isomorphic to 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{SO(3)}/\ker\Gamma. The image leaves a quadratic form invariant, and 𝖮(N)\mathsf{O(\mathnormal{N})} is the orthogonal group of that form.
