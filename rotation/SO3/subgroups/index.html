Conventions: section 15.2. The closed subgroups of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} are, up to conjugacy in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, 𝖳\mathsf{T}, 𝖮\mathsf{O}, 𝖨\mathsf{I} and, for every N≥1N \ge 1, the members of the families 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}}. For N=1N = 1: 𝖣𝟣\mathsf{D_{1}} ≅\cong 𝖢𝟤\mathsf{C_{2}}. In the diagram, where each group links to its document, a line joins a group to a maximal closed subgroup among the groups of the diagram; the groups that do not lie in 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} are gray. Section 3.2 gives the restriction of D(J)D^{(J)} to each of these groups; the document of each group except the members with N>12N > 12 lists it JJ by JJ, in its section Subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}.
