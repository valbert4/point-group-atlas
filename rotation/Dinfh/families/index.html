2.1 Families of irreps ¶

Data: character_functions, group in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 6, 7 and 16. A row is a family of irreps, by its label, with its dimension dd, its Frobenius–Schur indicator νFS\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} (real for +1+1) and the range of the index of its irreps. It also gives its other names (aliases). A dash stands for none. The kernel ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma is the subgroup of the elements that an irrep Γ\Gamma of the family maps to the identity. The image is the group formed by the irrep’s matrices, isomorphic to 𝖣∞𝗁/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}/\ker\Gamma and named by a point group. For d=1d = 1 it is the group of the characters, turning the complex plane. For d=2d = 2 it is the group of the matrices in a real basis, in 𝖮(𝟤)\mathsf{O(2)} acting on the plane. A row also gives the family’s character on each component, in the angle ϕ\phi of the rotation. The irreps are irreducible over ℂ\mathbb{C}. Each is real. Over ℝ\mathbb{R} (Conventions, section 7), a real irrep is also an irrep in a basis with real matrices. The boxed family, of one irrep, is the determinant character (determinant_character), of value det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g}.
