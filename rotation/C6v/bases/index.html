Conventions: section 14.1. The kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle are the spherical harmonics (the inversion acts as (−1)J(-1)^J). A vector is listed by its coefficients eme_m:

Defines
  • ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle the ket of angular momentum JJ and zz component mm, a spherical harmonic
  • eme_m the coefficient of the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle in a basis vector, its amplitude being emFm/tote_m\sqrt{F_m/\mathrm{tot}}
  • FmF_m the weight (J+m)! (J−m)!(J + m)!\,(J - m)! of the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle
  • tot\mathrm{tot} the normalization ∑m∣em∣2Fm\sum_m \lvert e_m\rvert^2 F_m of a basis vector
  • vv a symmetry-adapted vector, a combination of kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle, with vμv_\mu the partner μ\mu of a block
Symbols
  • mm a summation index
  • JJ the angular momentum, the degree of the spherical harmonics ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle

so a single ket has amplitude ±1\pm1, and from J=3J = 3 on a pair ∣ mJ⟩+s ∣ −mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle + s\,\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle, s=±1s = \pm1, has amplitudes 122\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2} and 12s2\tfrac{1}{2}s\sqrt{2}, or these times i\mathrm{i}. Each eme_m is ±1\pm1 or ±i\pm\mathrm{i}. The subgroup 𝖠=𝖢𝟨𝗏∩𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{A} = \mathsf{C_{6v}}\cap\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} acts diagonally on the kets, on ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle by the character χ𝖠(m)\chi_{\mathsf{A}}(m), and [𝖢𝟨𝗏:𝖠]=2[\mathsf{C_{6v}} : \mathsf{A}] = 2. The flip σ𝘷(0)\mathsfit{\sigma_v}(0), the mirror in the xzxz plane, sends mm to −m-m. By the rule of Conventions, section 14.1, a two-dimensional block has e=+1e = +1 on partner 1 and e=c=±1e = c = \pm1 on partner 2. Its first partner is v1=∣ mJ⟩v_1 = \bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle with m≡−ℓ(mod6)m \equiv -\ell \pmod{6}, and the second v2=c ∣ −mJ⟩v_2 = c\,\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle with c=η (−1)J+mc = \eta\,(-1)^{J+m}, where ℓ\ell is the first of the parameters (ℓ,δ)(\ell, \delta) of the block’s irrep (section 2.3.2) and η=(−1)J\eta = (-1)^J. Kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle and ∣ −mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle of one character of 𝖠\mathsf{A} give two one-dimensional blocks ∣ mJ⟩+s ∣ −mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle + s\,\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle, s=±1s = \pm1, the one with s=−(−1)ms = -(-1)^m times i\mathrm{i}. The factor i\mathrm{i} makes each block adapted to time reversal (Conventions, section 14). Blocks are ordered by JJ, then irrep, then ∣m∣|m|.
