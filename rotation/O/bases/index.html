in the order of their lowest mm. The index κ\kappa follows this order, and each partner-1 vector is its basis vector minus its projections on the copies before it (Gram–Schmidt). Partner μ\mu is Pμ,1ΓP^\Gamma_{\mu,1} applied to partner 1. The copies are adapted to time reversal (Conventions, section 14.2). For a real irrep the basis is that over ℝ\mathbb{R} of the vectors of the range that time reversal keeps with the matrix of the irrep, in the real and imaginary parts of the eme_m. Each copy then has the sign that gives its first coefficient a positive real part, or makes it i\mathrm{i} times a positive number. The copies of the second irrep of a complex pair are those of the first under time reversal. The bases have 51 blocks and 121 vectors at J=0,1,…,10J = 0, 1, \dots, 10. A row gives a vector of a block at JJ: the block’s irrep and copy index κ\kappa, the partner index μ\mu and the vector. The full set J=0,1,…,60J = 0, 1, \dots, 60 (1551 blocks, 3721 vectors) is in data/symmetry_adapted_bases/O/. The repository holds J≤10J \le 10, and the release archive O_J011-J060.tar holds J=11,12,…,60J = 11, 12, \dots, 60 and unpacks in the folder rotation/ (README.md).
