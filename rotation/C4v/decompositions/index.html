Data: character_table, elements, group, permutation_representations, subduction_embeddings, subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 12.4 and 12.5. The kernel of the permutation representation of a class of subgroups is the subgroup of the elements that leave every copy in place. The decomposition is that of DD, whose character at 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is the number of copies that 𝘨\mathsfit{g} leaves in place. An irrep occurs in DD as often as the trivial irrep of 𝖧\mathsf{H} occurs in its restriction to 𝖧\mathsf{H} (section 3.2).
