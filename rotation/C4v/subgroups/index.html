3.1 Subgroup lattice ¶

Data: subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 15. The diagram shows the conjugacy classes of subgroups, each joined to the classes of its maximal subgroups, and the orders increase upward. A normal subgroup is framed, and ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of conjugate subgroups. A gray letter after a symbol distinguishes the classes of subgroups with that symbol, which are not conjugate in 𝖢𝟦𝗏\mathsf{C_{4v}} (they are conjugate in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}). The table and the subduction tables of section 3.2 use the same letters. The table lists the classes by order, those of one symbol together. A row gives the representative 𝖧\mathsf{H} of a class of subgroups (subgrp), its order, the number of subgroups in the class (conjugates) and whether 𝖧\mathsf{H} is normal. It also gives the normalizer 𝖭 𝖢𝟦𝗏(𝖧)\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{C_{4v}}}(\mathsf{H}) and the centralizer 𝖢 𝖢𝟦𝗏(𝖧)\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{C_{4v}}}(\mathsf{H}) in 𝖢𝟦𝗏\mathsf{C_{4v}}, by their classes (normalizer, centralizer), and the classes of 𝖢𝟦𝗏\mathsf{C_{4v}} other than 𝘌\mathsfit{E} met by 𝖧\mathsf{H} (classes, a dash for none). A subgroup’s other names in its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class follow ≅\cong (Conventions, section 2.1).
