where μ1\mu_1, μ2\mu_2 and μ\mu are counted from 1. Each block is headed Γ1⊗Γ2→Γ\Gamma_1\otimes\Gamma_2\to\Gamma, κ\kappa (trivial when Γ\Gamma occurs once in Γ1⊗Γ2\Gamma_1\otimes\Gamma_2) and a normalization 𝒩\mathcal{N}. Row μ\mu gives the nonzero terms of 𝒩 ∣ μΓ;κ⟩\sqrt{\mathcal{N}}\,\bigl\lvert{}^{\Gamma}_{\,\mu};\kappa\bigr\rangle, each coefficient times 𝒩\sqrt{\mathcal{N}}, with ∣μ1 μ2⟩\lvert {\mu_1}\,{\mu_2}\rangle for ∣ μ1Γ1⟩∣ μ2Γ2⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{\Gamma_1}_{\,\mu_1}\bigr\rangle\bigl\lvert{}^{\Gamma_2}_{\,\mu_2}\bigr\rangle, so that the squared moduli of a row sum to 𝒩\mathcal{N}. Only Γ1≤Γ2\Gamma_1 \le \Gamma_2 is listed (Γ2⊗Γ1\Gamma_2\otimes\Gamma_1 exchanges μ1\mu_1 and μ2\mu_2), and a block whose two factors are one-dimensional is left out: it is ∣Γ⟩=∣Γ1⟩∣Γ2⟩\lvert \Gamma\rangle = \lvert \Gamma_1\rangle\lvert \Gamma_2\rangle, ⟨Γ1 Γ2∣Γ⟩=1\langle \Gamma_1\,\Gamma_2\mid \Gamma\rangle = 1. Where Γ\Gamma occurs more than once, κ\kappa is followed by the rule that separates its copies (Conventions, section 10.4). The copies labeled exch. sym. and exch. anti. are symmetric and antisymmetric under the exchange of the two factors.
