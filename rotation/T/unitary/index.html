7.1 Subduction from 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} ¶

Data: subduction_unitary in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 19. A level of 𝖳\mathsf{T} is a real irrep Γ\Gamma of dimension N≥2N \ge 2: a degenerate mode of vibration, or a set of degenerate orbitals. Its matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) lie in 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})}. The branching rule 𝖴(N)⊃𝖳\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} \supset \mathsf{T} gives the multiplicity bΛ(λ)b_\Lambda(\lambda) of the irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖳\mathsf{T} in the irrep 𝕊λ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda} of 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} of highest weight λ=[λ1,λ2,… ]\lambda = [\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots], restricted along DΓD^{\Gamma}. The quanta of a degenerate mode fill the one-row shapes 𝕊[v]Γ=Sym⁡vΓ\mathbb{S}_{[v]}\Gamma = \operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma (section 7.2), and nn electrons of spin SS fill the two-column shapes λ=[2n/2−S,12S]\lambda = [2^{n/2-S}, 1^{2S}] (section 7.4). The table lists the levels by dimension. A row gives the level Γ\Gamma, the denominator ∏k(1−tak)\prod_k (1 - t^{a_k}) of the generating functions (7.2.1) of its overtones and the subgroups through which 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} restricts to 𝖳\mathsf{T} (chain). The chain ends at the image of Γ\Gamma, the group formed by the matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}), isomorphic to 𝖳/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{T}/\ker\Gamma (the column kernel of the table of irreps, section 2.1). The image leaves a quadratic form invariant, and 𝖮(N)\mathsf{O(\mathnormal{N})} is the orthogonal group of that form.

The matrices of a level of dimension 3 are conjugate by a matrix uu to those of the elements of the record of its image: u DΓ(𝘨) u−1=P(k)u\,D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g})\,u^{-1} = P(k). The level’s chain.image.element_map gives the element kk for 𝘨\mathsfit{g}. The level’s states of harmonic degree ℓ\ell carry D(ℓ,(−1)ℓ)D^{(\ell,(-1)^{\ell})} of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} restricted to the image. This restriction is the row J=ℓJ = \ell, p=(−1)ℓp = (-1)^{\ell}, of the image’s subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, pulled back by 𝘨↦k\mathsfit{g} \mapsto k. Every level of dimension 3 has u=1u = 1.
