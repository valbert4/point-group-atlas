Data: character_table, permutation_representations, subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 12.5. The entry in the row of a class of subgroups and the column of a class of elements is the cycle type of j↦j′j \mapsto j' for these elements, written ℓc\ell^{c} for cc cycles of length ℓ\ell. The cycles of length 1 are the copies that the element leaves in place, whose number is the character of DD. With c(𝘨)c(\mathsfit{g}) cycles in all, 𝘨\mathsfit{g} has the character qc(𝘨)q^{c(\mathsfit{g})} on the qNq^N products of one of qq states of each copy (Conventions, section 12.5).
