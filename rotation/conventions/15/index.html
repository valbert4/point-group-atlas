15 Subgroup lattices ¶

15.1 subgroup_lattice (finite groups) ¶

The field subgroup_lattice of a finite record is the lattice of the subgroups of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. Elements are the indices of the record's elements.items, multiplied by its multiplication_table, and every list of indices is ascending. A subgroup is a set of indices, and its conjugates are 𝖧𝘨={𝘨−1𝘩𝘨:𝘩∈𝖧}\mathsf{H}^{\mathsfit{g}} = \{\mathsfit{g}^{-1} \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g} : \mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{H}\}.

  1. nodes[]: the 𝖦\mathsf{G}-conjugacy classes of subgroups, sorted by (order, element list of the representative) and named n00, n01, … (the ids of section 12.1):

    1. order: ∣𝖧∣\lvert \mathsf{H}\rvert.
    2. index: [𝖦:𝖧][\mathsf{G} : \mathsf{H}].
    3. class_size: the number of subgroups in the class.
    4. normal: whether class_size = 1.
    5. normalizer, centralizer: the subgroups 𝖭𝖦(𝖧)={𝘨:𝖧𝘨=𝖧}\mathsf{N}_{\mathsf{G}}(\mathsf{H}) = \{\mathsfit{g} : \mathsf{H}^{\mathsfit{g}} = \mathsf{H}\} and 𝖢𝖦(𝖧)={𝘨:𝘨𝘩=𝘩𝘨 for every 𝘩∈𝖧}\mathsf{C}_{\mathsf{G}}(\mathsf{H}) = \{\mathsfit{g} : \mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} = \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g} \text{ for every } \mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{H}\} of the representative, each with node, the id of its class, and elements.
    6. normalizer_order: ∣𝖭𝖦(𝖧)∣\lvert \mathsf{N}_{\mathsf{G}}(\mathsf{H})\rvert.
    7. o3_class: as in section 12.1, the record whose elements have the same multiset of (det⁡\det, turn).
    8. o3_schoenflies: that record's schoenflies, with C_s for 𝖢𝟣𝗏\mathsf{C_{1v}} and C_i for 𝖲𝟤\mathsf{S_{2}}.
    9. embedding: kind (always "G-conjugacy class of subgroups"), members (every subgroup of the class sorted by element list) and representative (the first member). Each member has elements, conjugator (the smallest index 𝘨\mathsfit{g} with member=representative𝘨\text{member} = \text{representative}^{\mathsfit{g}}) and conjugator_symbol (the symbol of 𝘨\mathsfit{g}). The field representative has elements, the symbols and poles of those elements (as in the record) and class_ids (the sorted ids of the classes of 𝖦\mathsf{G} met by it).

  2. edges[]: the covering pairs of the order lower≤upper  ⟺  \text{lower} \le \text{upper} \iff some member of the lower class lies in the representative of the upper class, sorted by (index of the lower node, index of the upper node). Each has these fields:

    1. lower, upper: node ids.
    2. index: the order of the upper node divided by that of the lower node.
    3. embeddings_in_upper: the number of orbits of the conjugation by the representative of the upper class on the members of the lower class that lie in that representative.
    4. witness: the first member of the lower class (in members order) that lies in the representative of the upper class. It has lower_elements, lower_symbols, lower_poles, conjugator, conjugator_symbol (as in members) and upper_elements (that representative).
  3. counts: subgroups (∑\sum class_size), g_classes (the number of nodes), o3_classes (the number of distinct o3_class), g_classes_by_order and o3_classes_by_order (keyed by the order as a decimal string).

15.2 data/point_groups.json ¶

The containment lattice of the 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy classes of this catalogue's groups has these fields:

  1. nodes[]: one for each class, named by the group name of a record of that class (94: 87 finite, 7 continuous). The four groups whose class is that of another record are not nodes, and aliases.finite maps each to its node: C1h →\to C1v, D1 →\to C2, D1d →\to C2h, D1h →\to C2v. Sorted by (order, id) with the continuous nodes last, the nodes have these fields:

    1. id.
    2. kind: "finite" or "continuous".
    3. order: the order, or null for the seven continuous nodes.
    4. type: the record's.
    5. family, N: the record's family, or null for the seven exceptional and seven continuous nodes.
    6. schoenflies: the record's, with C_s and C_i as in section 15.1.

  2. edges[]: the covering pairs of the containment order, sorted by (order of lower, lower, order of upper, upper) like the nodes. Each has covering true, lower, upper and a witness:

    1. {"kind": "explicit subgroup of the record", "in", "elements", "symbols"} for a finite upper: the subgroup of the record in = upper (in the frame of section 3) with the smallest element list in class lower, by its element indices and symbols in that record.
    2. {"kind": "argument", "reason"} for a continuous upper, with a one-sentence reason.
  3. counts: nodes, covering_edges and relations, the number of ordered pairs (a,b)(a, b), a≠ba \ne b, such that a group of class aa is contained in a group of class bb.

  4. family_rules.rules: 49 entries, one for each (upper_family, lower_family) over the axial families in the order Cn, S2n, Cnh, Cnv, Dn, Dnh, Dnd (upper family outer).

    Each rule is the condition on (N,M)(N, \texttt{M}) under which the group of upper_family with parameter NN has a subgroup 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugate to the group of lower_family with parameter M, after the N=1N = 1 identifications (𝖣𝟣≅𝖢𝟤\mathsf{D_{1}} \cong \mathsf{C_{2}}, 𝖢𝟣𝗁≅𝖢𝟣𝗏\mathsf{C_{1h}} \cong \mathsf{C_{1v}}, 𝖣𝟣𝗁≅𝖢𝟤𝗏\mathsf{D_{1h}} \cong \mathsf{C_{2v}}, 𝖣𝟣𝖽≅𝖢𝟤𝗁\mathsf{D_{1d}} \cong \mathsf{C_{2h}}). It is a short formula ("M | N", "M >= 2 and 2M | N", …) or "never". For the lower families Cnh, Dn, Dnh and Dnd, a rule covers the members with M≥2\texttt{M} \ge 2. Their members with M=1\texttt{M} = 1 are read under the family of their node: 𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsf{C_{1h}} as Cnv with M=1\texttt{M} = 1, 𝖣𝟣\mathsf{D_{1}} as Cn with M=2\texttt{M} = 2, 𝖣𝟣𝗁\mathsf{D_{1h}} as Cnv with M=2\texttt{M} = 2 and 𝖣𝟣𝖽\mathsf{D_{1d}} as Cnh with M=2\texttt{M} = 2.
