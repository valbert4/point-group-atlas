11 Subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} ¶

The irrep D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, of angular momentum JJ and parity p=χ(𝘪)/dim⁡p = \chi(\mathsfit{i})/\dim, is p[det⁡𝘨=−1]D(J)((det⁡𝘨) 𝘨)p^{[\det \mathsfit{g} = -1]} D^{(J)}((\det \mathsfit{g})\,\mathsfit{g}) on 𝘨\mathsfit{g}, with the character p[det⁡𝘨=−1]sin⁡((2J+1)ϕ/2)/sin⁡(ϕ/2)p^{[\det \mathsfit{g} = -1]} \sin((2J+1)\phi/2)/\sin(\phi/2), where ϕ\phi is the rotation angle of (det⁡𝘨) 𝘨(\det \mathsfit{g})\,\mathsfit{g}. The spherical harmonics of degree JJ carry p=(−1)Jp = (-1)^J. In the record of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} is the instance JJ of the family Dg (label D^(J,+), p=+1p = +1) or Du (D^(J,-), p=−1p = -1). In the record of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, D(J)D^{(J)} is the instance JJ of D (label D^(J)).

A finite record's subduction_o3 has domain "integer J" and embedding.id "<schoenflies>/standard", the group in the frame of section 3. Continuous groups. In a continuous record, instances lists D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} restricted to 𝖦\mathsf{G} for JJ in J_range and p=±1p = \pm 1. An entry has J, parity and terms with multiplicity and irrep, the irrep's label (its family's label_template at nn, for any nn). The field rule states the result for every JJ. Restricted to 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} is D(J)D^{(J)} for either pp ("D^(J,p) | SO(3) = D^(J) [independent of p]"), and restricted to 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} it is D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} itself ("D^(J,p) | O(3) = D^(J,p)").
