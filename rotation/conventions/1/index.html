1 Scope ¶

The data are the single-valued (vector) irreducible representations over ℂ\mathbb{C} of 98 closed subgroups of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}: the 91 finite groups 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}}, 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}, 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}}, 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} (N=1,2,…,12N = 1, 2, \dots, 12), 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} (2N=2,4,…,242N = 2, 4, \dots, 24), 𝖳\mathsf{T}, 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}, 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, 𝖮\mathsf{O}, 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}}, 𝖨\mathsf{I}, 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}, and the seven continuous groups 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} (also written 𝖲∞\mathsf{S_{\infty}}), 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}, 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, and the permutation representations of each finite group on the orbits of points of space (section 12.5). Angular momenta JJ are integers.

Each group has one record, data/json/<G>.json. A finite record holds group (identification, section 2; product forms, section 12.3), elements (section 4), character_table (classes, section 5; irreps, sections 6, 7, 9), fusion and clebsch_gordan (section 10), subduction_o3 (section 11), subduction_embeddings (section 12), permutation_representations (section 12.5), cartesian_tensors (section 13), invariants (section 13.1), subduction_basis (section 14), subgroup_lattice (section 15.1), fundamental_domain (section 17) and, when the group has a level (a real irrep of dimension at least 2), subduction_unitary (section 19). A continuous record has domain = "continuous" and holds group, character_functions (section 16), fusion, clebsch_gordan, subduction_o3, cartesian_tensors, invariants, subduction_basis and fundamental_domain, those of the five axial continuous groups subduction_subgroups and those of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} subgroup_classes and subduction_rules (section 16.1), and those of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}, 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, which have a level, subduction_unitary (section 19). The records of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} also hold the Clebsch–Gordan coefficients (section 10.6) and the Wigner dd matrices (section 9) of their irreps for J≤10J \le 10.

Symmetry-adapted bases are given in closed form for every JJ and enumerated for J≤10J \le 10 in the axial and continuous records (section 14.1); for the seven exceptional groups they are enumerated for J≤10J \le 10 in the records (section 14.4) and for J≤60J \le 60 in data/symmetry_adapted_bases/ (sections 14.2, 14.3). The subgroup lattice of a finite group is its record's subgroup_lattice, and the lattice of the point-group classes in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} is data/point_groups.json (section 15). The overtone levels and the terms of the levels, and their states, are in subduction_unitary, those of the levels of dimension 4 and 5 and of the terms of the f shell in data/subduction_unitary/ (section 19); the files are listed in section 18.
