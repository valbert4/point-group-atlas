1 Scope ¶

This catalogue holds the single-valued (vector) irreducible representations over ℂ\mathbb{C} of 98 closed subgroups of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, and the permutation representations of each finite group on the orbits of points of space (section 12.5). The 91 finite groups are the 84 axial groups, which map the zz axis to itself, and the seven exceptional groups. The axial groups are 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}}, 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}, 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} and 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} (N=1,2,…,12N = 1, 2, \dots, 12), and the exceptional groups are 𝖳\mathsf{T}, 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}, 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, 𝖮\mathsf{O}, 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}}, 𝖨\mathsf{I} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}. The seven continuous groups are the five axial continuous groups 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} (also written 𝖲∞\mathsf{S_{\infty}}), 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} and 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}, then 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}. Angular momenta JJ are integers.

Each group has one record, data/json/<G>.json. A finite record holds these fields:

  1. group: identification (section 2) and product forms (section 12.3).
  2. elements (section 4).
  3. character_table: classes (section 5) and irreps (sections 6, 7 and 9).
  4. fusion and clebsch_gordan (section 10).
  5. subduction_o3 (section 11).
  6. subduction_embeddings (section 12).
  7. permutation_representations (section 12.5).
  8. cartesian_tensors (section 13).
  9. invariants (section 13.1).
  10. subduction_basis (section 14).
  11. subgroup_lattice (section 15.1).
  12. fundamental_domain (section 17).
  13. orbifold (sections 17.6 to 17.8).
  14. subduction_unitary (section 19), when the group has a level (a real irrep of dimension at least 2).

Continuous groups. A continuous record has domain = "continuous" and holds group, character_functions (section 16), fusion, clebsch_gordan, subduction_o3, cartesian_tensors, invariants, subduction_basis, fundamental_domain, orbifold and orbit_space (section 17.8). The records of the five axial continuous groups also hold subduction_subgroups, and the records of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} hold subgroup_classes and subduction_rules (section 16.1). These two records also hold the Clebsch–Gordan coefficients (section 10.6) and the Wigner dd matrices (section 9) of their irreps for J≤10J \le 10. The records of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}, 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, which have a level, hold subduction_unitary (section 19).

Symmetry-adapted bases are given in closed form for every JJ and enumerated for J≤10J \le 10 in the axial and continuous records (section 14.1). For the seven exceptional groups they are enumerated for J≤10J \le 10 in the records (section 14.4) and for J≤60J \le 60 in data/symmetry_adapted_bases/ (sections 14.2 and 14.3). The subgroup lattice of a finite group is its record's subgroup_lattice, and the lattice of the 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy classes of the 98 groups is data/point_groups.json (section 15). The overtone levels and the terms of the levels are in subduction_unitary with their states. However, the states of the levels of dimension 4 and 5 and of the terms of the f shell are in data/subduction_unitary/ (section 19). The files are listed in section 18.
