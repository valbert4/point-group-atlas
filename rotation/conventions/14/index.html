The kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle are the spherical harmonics, with the action of section 4: the inversion acts as (−1)J(-1)^J, and an element 𝘨\mathsfit{g} as (det⁡𝘨)J(\det \mathsfit{g})^J times the rotation (det⁡𝘨) 𝘨(\det \mathsfit{g})\, \mathsfit{g}.
