19 Vibrational and electronic levels ¶

A level of a finite group 𝖦\mathsf{G} is a real irrep Γ\Gamma of it (section 7) of dimension N≥2N \ge 2: a degenerate mode of vibration, or a set of degenerate orbitals. Its matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}) (section 9) are unitary, so the level is a representation of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})}, and every irrep of 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} restricts along it. The irrep 𝕊λ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda} of 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} has the highest weight λ=[λ1,λ2,… ]\lambda = [\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots], a partition of at most NN rows. The irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖦\mathsf{G} has the multiplicity bΛ(λ)b_\Lambda(\lambda) in the restriction 𝕊λΓ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda}\Gamma, the branching rule 𝖴(N)⊃𝖦\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} \supset \mathsf{G}. The character of 𝕊λΓ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda}\Gamma at 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is a symmetric polynomial in the eigenvalues of DΓ(𝘨)D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}), computed exactly from the power sums χΓ(𝘨k)\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}^k).

The vv quanta of a degenerate mode fill the one-row shapes, 𝕊[v]Γ=Sym⁡vΓ\mathbb{S}_{[v]}\Gamma = \operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma. Without spin–orbit coupling, nn electrons of spin SS fill the two-column shapes λ=[2n/2−S,12S]\lambda = [2^{n/2-S}, 1^{2S}], whose restrictions are the terms 2S+1Λ{}^{2S+1}\Lambda of the configuration Γn\Gamma^{n}. For n≤Nn \le N the one column [1n][1^{n}], ΛnΓ\Lambda^{n}\Gamma, contains the terms of the highest spin, S=n/2S = n/2. A shape of electrons has at most two columns: three or more would need three or more states for each orbital. The work [Gri61, section 6.6.2] writes one-electron irreps in lowercase, the configuration Γn\Gamma^{n} of T2\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_2 as t_2^n and a configuration of two levels as t_2^m e^n [Gri61, section 9.1].

The level has one invariant quadratic form r2r^2, and HℓH_{\ell} consists of the states of ℓ\ell quanta that the invariant Laplacian annihilates, the harmonic polynomials of degree ℓ\ell in the partners of the level. The states of vv quanta split by their harmonic degree: Sym⁡vΓ\operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma is the sum of rv−ℓHℓr^{v-\ell} H_{\ell} over ℓ=v,v−2,…\ell = v, v - 2, \dots (the branching 𝖴(N)⊃𝖮(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} \supset \mathsf{O(\mathnormal{N})}, [DGS77, Theorem 3.1]). The multiplicity hΛ(ℓ)\mathnormal{h}_{\Lambda}(\ell) of Λ\Lambda in HℓH_{\ell} is therefore the same at every vv. With HΛΓ(t)=∑ℓ≥0hΛ(ℓ) tℓH^{\Gamma}_\Lambda(t) = \sum_{\ell \ge 0} \mathnormal{h}_{\Lambda}(\ell)\, t^{\ell}, Molien's formula reads

(19.1)HΛΓ(t)1−t2=∑v≥0bΛ(v) tv=1∣𝖦∣∑𝘨∈𝖦χΛ(𝘨)∗det⁡(1−t DΓ(𝘨))=PΛ(t)∏k(1−tak),bΛ(v)=bΛ([v]),\frac{H^{\Gamma}_\Lambda(t)}{1 - t^2} = \sum_{v \ge 0} b_\Lambda(v)\, t^v = \frac{1}{\lvert\mathsf{G}\rvert} \sum_{\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G}} \frac{\chi_\Lambda(\mathsfit{g})^{*}}{\det\bigl(1 - t\,D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g})\bigr)} = \frac{P_\Lambda(t)}{\prod_k (1 - t^{a_k})}, \qquad b_\Lambda(v) = b_\Lambda([v]),

the factor 1/(1−t2)1/(1 - t^2) counting the powers of r2r^2, which raise vv by 2 and keep the irrep. The denominator a1≤⋯≤aNa_1 \le \dots \le a_N, 1≤ak≤∣𝖦∣1 \le a_k \le \lvert\mathsf{G}\rvert, is that of least ∏kak\prod_k a_k and then lexicographically least for which every PΛ(t)P_\Lambda(t) is a polynomial with nonnegative integer coefficients. For every level a1=2a_1 = 2, the degree of r2r^2, so that HΛΓ(t)=PΛ(t)H^{\Gamma}_\Lambda(t) = P_\Lambda(t) over the product of the factors 1−tak1 - t^{a_k} with k≥2k \ge 2. For a level whose matrices are the elements' own, DΓ(𝘨)=P(𝘨)D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}) = P(\mathsfit{g}) (the partners xx, yy, zz), HΛΓ(t)H^{\Gamma}_\Lambda(t) is the generating function HΛ(t)H_\Lambda(t) of section 14. The series of the trivial irrep is the Hilbert series of the polynomials in the partners of the level that 𝖦\mathsf{G} leaves invariant. For N=2N = 2 they are the polynomials in r2r^2 and in one invariant of degree a2a_2. For N=3N = 3 the series is that of the invariant polynomials of the image of the level (chain, below), with the same denominator, and the aka_k are the degrees of its primary invariants (section 13.1). For a level of dimension 2, ℓ\ell is the vibrational angular momentum, and for the levels of dimension 5 it is the seniority of the five-dimensional oscillator.

In a finite record with a level, subduction_unitary holds levels, one entry for each level in irrep order, with these fields:

  1. irrep, irrep_id: the label and the id of the irrep.
  2. dim: NN.

  3. symmetric: the one-row shapes, with these fields:

    1. generating_function: denominator and numerators, each with irrep, irrep_id and coefficients (those of PΛP_\Lambda from t0t^0).
    2. v_max_tabulated: 12.
    3. table: bΛ(v)b_\Lambda(v) for v≤12v \le 12.
    4. harmonic: l_max_tabulated, 12, and table, hΛ(ℓ)\mathnormal{h}_{\Lambda}(\ell) for ℓ≤12\ell \le 12.
    5. period (for N=2N = 2): LL, the least common multiple of the aka_k.
    6. increment (for N=2N = 2): ΔΛ=bΛ(v+L)−bΛ(v)\Delta_\Lambda = b_\Lambda(v + L) - b_\Lambda(v), one number at the vv of the parity at which Λ\Lambda occurs, as in section 11.
  4. two_column: the shapes of at most two columns and NN rows, by n=0,…,2Nn = 0, \dots, 2N and then increasing SS: n, twoS, partition (the parts of λ\lambda), dim (that of 𝕊λ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda}) and table (the bΛ(λ)b_\Lambda(\lambda)).

  5. chain: the subgroups through which 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} restricts to 𝖦\mathsf{G} along the level, with kind, image and rule. Its kind, such as "U(2) > O(2) > image", lists them from 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} down to the image, the group formed by the matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}), isomorphic to 𝖦/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{G}/\ker\Gamma. Its rule states the branching along the chain in a line. The documents set ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma for every irrep in the table of section 7.

    The matrices are unitary, real for N=3N = 3 and complex for the other levels, whose partners are complex. The image leaves the quadratic form r2r^2 invariant, and the 𝖮(N)\mathsf{O(\mathnormal{N})} of the chain is the orthogonal group of that form, conjugate in 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} to the real orthogonal matrices. The chain depends on NN:

    1. For N=2N = 2, image is the Schoenflies symbol of the axial group that acts on the xyxy plane as the matrices act on the level. The states of harmonic degree ℓ\ell carry the irrep Eℓ\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{\ell} (ℓ>0\ell > 0) or A1\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_{1} (ℓ=0\ell = 0) of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} restricted to the axial group (section 16.1).
    2. For N=3N = 3, image holds record (the exceptional record of the image), conjugator (a matrix uu) and element_map: u DΓ(𝘨) u−1=P(k)u\,D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g})\,u^{-1} = P(k), the matrix of section 4 of the element kk = element_map[g] of that record. Here uu is 1 when the matrices of the level are those of the record's elements. Otherwise uu realizes the first assignment of images to the generators of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (in the record's element order) that a conjugation realizes, and 1 is its first nonzero entry (row-major). The states of harmonic degree ℓ\ell carry D(ℓ,(−1)ℓ)D^{(\ell,(-1)^{\ell})} restricted to the image (section 11), pulled back by element_map.
    3. For N≥4N \ge 4 (the G\mathrm{\scriptsize G} and H\mathrm{\scriptsize H} of 𝖨\mathsf{I} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}), image holds abstract_type, the abstract type (section 2.2) of the image. This abstract type is that of 𝖦\mathsf{G} when the level is faithful and that of 𝖨\mathsf{I} when its kernel is {𝘌,𝘪}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\}. The table of irreps names the image 𝖨\mathsf{I} or 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} (section 7). The generator asserts both rules below against the harmonic table.
    4. A level of dimension 4 is the product of the irrep of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} of dimension 2 and its Galois twin (5↦−5\sqrt{5} \mapsto -\sqrt{5}). Its matrices have determinant 1, and kind is "U(4) > O(4) > SO(4) > image". At an element whose matrix turns two orthogonal planes by θ1\theta_{1} and θ2\theta_{2}, the harmonic polynomials of degree ℓ\ell have the character χℓ/2(θ1+θ2) χℓ/2(θ1−θ2)\chi_{\ell/2}(\theta_{1} + \theta_{2})\, \chi_{\ell/2}(\theta_{1} - \theta_{2}), with χj(ω)=sin⁡((2j+1)ω/2)/sin⁡(ω/2)\chi_j(\omega) = \sin\bigl((2j + 1)\omega/2\bigr)/\sin(\omega/2). At a rotation by ω≤π\omega \le \pi, θ1+θ2\theta_{1} + \theta_{2} and θ1−θ2\theta_{1} - \theta_{2} are ω\omega and ω′\omega' up to sign, ω′=3ω\omega' = 3\omega when the rotation has order 5 and ω′=ω\omega' = \omega otherwise. At an improper element of 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} it is pℓp^{\ell} times the character at the rotation part, where the level's matrix of 𝘪\mathsfit{i} is pp times the identity.
    5. A level of dimension 5 is D(2,p)D^{(2,p)} restricted, and image.o3_irrep holds J 2 and parity pp. Its kind runs through the image of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} under D(2)D^{(2)} ("U(5) > O(5) > SO(3) > image") or, for p=−1p = -1, through that of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} under D(2,−)D^{(2,-)} ("U(5) > O(5) > O(3) > image"). The image of the level is that of 𝖨\mathsf{I} in the first and that of 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} in the second. Its harmonic polynomials of degree ℓ\ell in five variables, an irrep of 𝖲𝖮(𝟧)\mathsf{SO(5)}, restrict to 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} as the D(J)D^{(J)} with J=2k,2k−2,2k−3,…,kJ = 2k, 2k - 2, 2k - 3, \dots, k for k=ℓ,ℓ−3,ℓ−6,⋯≥0k = \ell, \ell - 3, \ell - 6, \dots \ge 0 [RTR04, eq. 21]. Thus the irreps in HℓH_{\ell} are the sum of those rows JJ of section 11, at the parity pℓp^{\ell}.
  6. partner_functions (a level whose irrep's construction names them, section 9): the functions as which the partners of the level transform, such as "x-iy" and "x+iy", or "(x-iy)^2 z" and "(x+iy)^2 z", for an axial-monomial irrep. For a defining irrep they are "x", "y", "z".

  7. symmetric.bases: the states of vv quanta, with these fields:

    1. v_max: 6 for N=2N = 2 and N=3N = 3, 3 above.
    2. blocks: one for each copy of an irrep in each rv−ℓHℓr^{v-\ell} H_{\ell}, v≤v \le v_max, by vv, then ℓ\ell descending, then irrep order, with v, l (ℓ\ell), irrep, irrep_id, kappa, dim and vectors. Each vector has mu and components, each with quanta, the numbers v1,…,vNv_1, \dots, v_N of quanta in the partners of the level, and e.

    The vector is

    (19.2)1tot∑eF ∣v1,…,vN⟩,F=v1!…vN!,tot=∑∣e∣2F,\frac{1}{\sqrt{\mathrm{tot}}} \sum e \sqrt{F}\, \lvert v_1, \dots, v_N\rangle, \qquad F = v_1! \dots v_N!, \qquad \mathrm{tot} = \sum \lvert e\rvert^2 F,

    where ∣v1,…,vN⟩\lvert v_1, \dots, v_N\rangle is the state of v1v_1 quanta in partner 1, …, vNv_N in partner NN. The partners transform by the matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}) of the level, and so do the creation operators of the quanta. Thus ee is the coefficient of their product, as eme_m in section 14. The tables depend only on the character of the level, and the states depend on its partners as well.

  8. two_column[].bases: the states of the terms of the shape, each at the spin projection MS=SM_S = S, as blocks with irrep, irrep_id, kappa, dim and vectors (mu, components), each component with determinant and e. A determinant lists the spin–orbitals of a Slater determinant as pairs of the partner of the level and twice the spin projection ([1, 1] partner 1 with spin up, [1, -1] with spin down). Their order is 1 up, 1 down, 2 up, 2 down, …, and the empty list is the vacuum. The vector is tot−1/2∑e ∣…∣\mathrm{tot}^{-1/2} \sum e\, \lvert \dots\rvert with tot=∑∣e∣2\mathrm{tot} = \sum \lvert e\rvert^2, and unlike the spins, the orbitals transform by DΓ(𝘨)D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}). The other components of a term follow by lowering the spin.
  9. bases_location (the levels of dimension N≥4N \ge 4, the G\mathrm{\scriptsize G} and H\mathrm{\scriptsize H} of 𝖨\mathsf{I} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}), in place of symmetric.bases and two_column[].bases: the file data/subduction_unitary/<G>/<irrep id>.json.gz that holds them. The file {schema, group, irrep, irrep_id, symmetric, two_column} has v_max and blocks in symmetric and one entry for each shape in two_column, in the record's order, with n, twoS and blocks. The states of 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} are those of 𝖨\mathsf{I}, as in section 14.3, and the level has the sign pp at 𝘪\mathsfit{i}. A block of the irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖨\mathsf{I} at vv quanta (nn electrons) is the block of the irrep of 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} that has the matrices of Λ\Lambda on the rotations and pvp^v (pnp^n) at 𝘪\mathsfit{i}.

In both bases the states are symmetry-adapted linear combinations (SALCs) of the occupation states and of the determinants. The copies of Λ\Lambda are orthonormal in the inner product that makes the occupation states and the determinants orthonormal. Each copy lies in one rv−ℓHℓr^{v-\ell} H_{\ell} for vv quanta, and for nn electrons in the states of spin SS among the determinants of spin projection SS, the kernel of the spin raising operator. Time reversal acts on the partners of the level as on a block of its irrep (section 14), and so on the creation operators of the quanta and of the electrons. On a state it also conjugates the coefficients, and on a determinant it acts on the orbitals only, the spin a spectator (section 33.1). The coordinates of a space are ordered with the (v1,…,vN)(v_1, \dots, v_N) descending lexicographically (∣v,0,…,0⟩\lvert v, 0, \dots, 0\rangle first) and the determinants by their lists ascending. In these coordinates the range of P1,1Λ=∑𝘨∈𝖦D1,1Λ(𝘨)∗ 𝘨P^\Lambda_{1,1} = \sum_{\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G}} D^\Lambda_{1,1}(\mathsfit{g})^{*}\, \mathsfit{g} on each such space has a reduced row echelon basis, over ℂ\mathbb{C} for the first irrep of a complex pair. For a real irrep it is the basis over ℝ\mathbb{R} of the real form of this range, as in section 14.2. The real form holds the vectors that are partner 1 of a copy adapted to time reversal, and its coordinates are the real and imaginary parts of those of the space. Up to a positive rational factor, partner 1 of copy κ\kappa is vector κ\kappa of this basis (κ\kappa ascending with the pivot) minus its projections on the copies before it. This subtraction, the Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization of section 14.2, leaves copy 1 unchanged. Each copy is then multiplied by ±1\pm1, the sign that puts its first nonzero coefficient in the phase form of section 14. Partner μ\mu is Pμ,1ΛP^\Lambda_{\mu,1} applied to partner 1, and the copies of the second irrep of a complex pair are those of the first under time reversal. Each vector is then divided by the positive rational that makes the power-basis coordinates of its coefficients coprime integers, as in section 14.4. These coordinates are those of the cyclotomic field of the irrep matrices of the group, with i\mathrm{i} adjoined for a vector that holds a coefficient outside it. The copies in different rv−ℓHℓr^{v-\ell} H_{\ell} are orthogonal already, HℓH_{\ell} being orthogonal to r2r^2 times the states of ℓ−2\ell - 2 quanta.

The states of vv quanta apply to any coordinates q1,…,qNq_1, \dots, q_N of a degenerate mode that the group maps linearly among themselves: normal coordinates, and symmetrized internal coordinates. The group permutes these internal coordinates (bond lengths, bond angles) although they are not linear in the positions of the nuclei. The creation operators of an oscillator in these coordinates transform as the coordinates. If they transform by W DΓ(𝘨) W−1W\,D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g})\,W^{-1} with WW unitary (for example, real Cartesian components of a mode of dimension 2), the states are the stored states transformed by Sym⁡v\operatorname{Sym}^{v} of WW. By Schur's lemma WW is unique up to a factor.

A coordinate that the group maps affinely, a torsion angle τ↦±τ+c\tau \mapsto \pm\tau + c, enters through the circle. The group maps (cos⁡τ,sin⁡τ)(\cos\tau, \sin\tau) linearly, by the matrices of 𝖮(𝟤)\mathsf{O(2)} that turn by cc or reflect, and the free-rotor states e±iℓτ\mathrm{e}^{\pm\mathrm{i}\ell\tau}, ℓ>0\ell > 0, carry Eℓ\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{\ell} of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} restricted to the group of those matrices (section 16.1). When this group is not abelian, e∓iτ\mathrm{e}^{\mp\mathrm{i}\tau} carry a level of dimension 2. When they transform as its partners, e∓iℓτ\mathrm{e}^{\mp\mathrm{i}\ell\tau} are its states ∣ℓ0⟩\lvert \ell 0\rangle and ∣0ℓ⟩\lvert 0 \ell\rangle of harmonic degree ℓ\ell, the polynomials of degree ℓ\ell in the partners on the circle r=1r = 1. The angles of several torsions lie on a torus, held by no level.

A complex pair 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q, 2Eq{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q (section 6.4) is not a level and needs no table. The irrep 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q has the character χ\chi. When the two partners of a degenerate mode or of two degenerate orbitals transform as 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q and 2Eq{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q, every occupation state ∣v1v2⟩\lvert v_1 v_2\rangle belongs to the irrep of dimension 1 with the character χ(𝘨)v1−v2\chi(\mathsfit{g})^{v_1 - v_2}. The state ∣v1v2⟩\lvert v_1 v_2\rangle lies in r2min⁡(v1,v2)Hℓr^{2\min(v_1, v_2)} H_\ell with ℓ=∣v1−v2∣\ell = \lvert v_1 - v_2\rvert (r2=∣11⟩r^2 = \lvert 11\rangle). A determinant with v1v_1 electrons in the first partner and v2v_2 in the second also belongs to this irrep.

Continuous groups. The records of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}, 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} hold subduction_unitary for their families of levels. The records of 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} and 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} have none: their En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n are complex pairs. Each entry of levels is a family, with family, index ("n" or "J"), dim (2 or "2J+1"), chain (kind, image, rule), symmetric and two_column:

  1. The axial families En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n, En,g\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}}, En,u\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}}, whose matrices form 𝖮(𝟤)\mathsf{O(2)}, 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} acting on the plane, for every nn:

    1. symmetric.table (v≤12v \le 12) and symmetric.harmonic.table (ℓ≤12\ell \le 12): family laws giving Sym⁡vEn=Evn⊕E(v−2)n⊕…\operatorname{Sym}^{v}\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n = \mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{vn} \oplus \mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{(v-2)n} \oplus \dots, down to En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n or A1\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_{1}, and HℓH_\ell as Eℓn\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{\ell n} (ℓ>0\ell > 0) or A1\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_{1} (ℓ=0\ell = 0), at the parity pℓp^{\ell} in 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}. Their terms {family, index, mult} have an expression of nn as index ("n", "2n", …).
    2. symmetric.bases.rule: the rule that the occupation state ∣v1v2⟩\lvert v_1 v_2\rangle is partner 1 of E(v1−v2)n\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{(v_1 - v_2)n} when v1>v2v_1 > v_2, partner 2 of E(v2−v1)n\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{(v_2 - v_1)n} when v2>v1v_2 > v_1 and A1\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_{1} when v1=v2v_1 = v_2, in r2min⁡(v1,v2)Hℓr^{2\min(v_1, v_2)} H_\ell with ℓ=∣v1−v2∣\ell = \lvert v_1 - v_2\rvert.
    3. two_column: the six shapes of n≤4n \le 4 electrons, with table and bases as in the finite records, the blocks naming family and index. Time reversal exchanges the two partners of the level, and the state of A2\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_{2} carries the factor i\mathrm{i}.

  2. 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, the families D(J)D^{(J)} (D(J,+)D^{(J,+)}, D(J,−)D^{(J,-)}), J≥1J \ge 1, whose image is 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} (𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} for D(J,−)D^{(J,-)}):

    1. symmetric.rule: Cayley and Sylvester's rule, Sym⁡vD(J)\operatorname{Sym}^{v}D^{(J)} containing D(J′)D^{(J')} as many times as there are partitions of vJ−J′vJ - J' into at most vv parts of size at most 2J2J, minus those of vJ−J′−1vJ - J' - 1.
    2. symmetric.instances: for J=1,2,3J = 1, 2, 3, table (v≤12v \le 12) and harmonic.table (ℓ≤12\ell \le 12) of terms {J, mult}, with parity pvp^{v} (pℓp^{\ell}) in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}. The harmonic table of J=2J = 2 is the rule J′=2k,2k−2,2k−3,…,kJ' = 2k, 2k - 2, 2k - 3, \dots, k above.
    3. symmetric.bases: for J=1J = 1 and 2 and v≤4v \le 4, the highest-weight states, partner 1 (m=J′m = J') of each copy of D(J′)D^{(J')} in each rv−ℓHℓr^{v-\ell} H_{\ell}. Each has v, l, J, kappa and one vector of quanta and e, with factor when vJ−J′vJ - J' is odd.
    4. two_column.shells: for l=1,2,3l = 1, 2, 3 (the p, d and f shells), the shapes of every nn with table and bases, the highest-weight states (ML=J′M_L = J', MS=SM_S = S) of each copy of each term. Each state has J, kappa and one vector of determinant and e, with factor when nl−J′nl - J' is odd, the orbital of partner μ\mu being ∣l,l−μ+1⟩\lvert l, l - \mu + 1\rangle. For the f shell, bases_location replaces bases and names the file data/subduction_unitary/SO3/f.json.gz that holds its states, {schema, group, l, shapes}, each shape with n, twoS and bases. The record of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} shares it, its blocks at the parity pnp^{n} of the family, the product of the parities of the electrons, whatever the J′J' of the term [Lax74, section 2.11].
