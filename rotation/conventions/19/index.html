19 Vibrational and electronic levels ¶

A level of a finite group 𝖦\mathsf{G} is a real irrep Γ\Gamma of it (section 7) of dimension N≥2N \ge 2: a degenerate mode of vibration, or a set of degenerate orbitals. Its matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}) (section 9) are unitary, so the level is a representation of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})}, and every irrep of 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} restricts along it. The irrep 𝕊λ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda} of 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} has the highest weight λ=[λ1,λ2,… ]\lambda = [\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots], a partition of at most NN rows, and bΛ(λ)b_\Lambda(\lambda) is the multiplicity of the irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in 𝕊λΓ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda}\Gamma, the restriction: the branching rule 𝖴(N)⊃𝖦\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} \supset \mathsf{G}. Its character at 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is a symmetric polynomial in the eigenvalues of DΓ(𝘨)D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}), computed exactly from the power sums χΓ(𝘨k)\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}^k). The vv quanta of a degenerate mode fill the one-row shapes, 𝕊[v]Γ=Sym⁡vΓ\mathbb{S}_{[v]}\Gamma = \operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma; nn electrons of spin SS without spin–orbit coupling fill the two-column shapes λ=[2n/2−S,12S]\lambda = [2^{n/2-S}, 1^{2S}], whose restrictions are the terms 2S+1Λ{}^{2S+1}\Lambda of the configuration Γn\Gamma^{n}, and for n≤Nn \le N the one column [1n][1^{n}], ΛnΓ\Lambda^{n}\Gamma, holds the terms of the highest spin, S=n/2S = n/2. A shape of electrons has at most two columns: one of three or more would need three or more states per orbital.

The states of vv quanta split by their harmonic degree: the level has one invariant quadratic form r2r^2, and Sym⁡vΓ\operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma is the sum of rv−ℓHℓr^{v-\ell} H_{\ell} over ℓ=v,v−2,…\ell = v, v - 2, \dots, where HℓH_{\ell} holds the states of ℓ\ell quanta that the invariant Laplacian annihilates, the harmonic polynomials of degree ℓ\ell in the partners of the level (the branching 𝖴(N)⊃𝖮(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} \supset \mathsf{O(\mathnormal{N})}; [DGS77, Theorem 3.1]). The multiplicity hΛ(ℓ)\mathnormal{h}_{\Lambda}(\ell) of Λ\Lambda in HℓH_{\ell} is therefore the same at every vv, and with HΛΓ(t)=∑ℓ≥0hΛ(ℓ) tℓH^{\Gamma}_\Lambda(t) = \sum_{\ell \ge 0} \mathnormal{h}_{\Lambda}(\ell)\, t^{\ell} Molien's formula reads

(19.1)HΛΓ(t)1−t2=∑v≥0bΛ(v) tv=1∣𝖦∣∑𝘨∈𝖦χΛ(𝘨)∗det⁡(1−t DΓ(𝘨))=PΛ(t)∏k(1−tak),bΛ(v)=bΛ([v]),\frac{H^{\Gamma}_\Lambda(t)}{1 - t^2} = \sum_{v \ge 0} b_\Lambda(v)\, t^v = \frac{1}{\lvert\mathsf{G}\rvert} \sum_{\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G}} \frac{\chi_\Lambda(\mathsfit{g})^{*}}{\det\bigl(1 - t\,D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g})\bigr)} = \frac{P_\Lambda(t)}{\prod_k (1 - t^{a_k})}, \qquad b_\Lambda(v) = b_\Lambda([v]),

the factor 1/(1−t2)1/(1 - t^2) counting the powers of r2r^2, which raise vv by 2 and leave the species alone. The denominator a1≤⋯≤aNa_1 \le \dots \le a_N, 1≤ak≤∣𝖦∣1 \le a_k \le \lvert\mathsf{G}\rvert, is that of least ∏kak\prod_k a_k and then lexicographically least for which every PΛ(t)P_\Lambda(t) is a polynomial with nonnegative integer coefficients. a1=2a_1 = 2 for every level, the degree of r2r^2, so that HΛΓ(t)=PΛ(t)H^{\Gamma}_\Lambda(t) = P_\Lambda(t) over the product of the factors 1−tak1 - t^{a_k} with k≥2k \ge 2. For a level whose matrices are the elements' own, DΓ(𝘨)=P(𝘨)D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}) = P(\mathsfit{g}) (the partners xx, yy, zz), HΛΓ(t)H^{\Gamma}_\Lambda(t) is the generating function HΛ(t)H_\Lambda(t) of section 14. The series of the trivial irrep is the Hilbert series of the polynomials in the partners of the level that 𝖦\mathsf{G} leaves invariant. For N=2N = 2 they are the polynomials in r2r^2 and in one invariant of degree a2a_2; for N=3N = 3 the series is that of the invariant polynomials of the image of the level (chain, below), with the same denominator, the aka_k the degrees of its primary invariants (section 13.1). For a level of dimension 2, ℓ\ell is the vibrational angular momentum. For the levels of dimension 5, ℓ\ell is the seniority of the five-dimensional oscillator.

For N≥4N \ge 4 (the G\mathrm{\scriptsize G} and H\mathrm{\scriptsize H} of 𝖨\mathsf{I} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}), image holds abstract_type, the abstract type (section 2.2) of the group that the matrices form: that of 𝖦\mathsf{G} when the level is faithful, that of 𝖨\mathsf{I} when its kernel is {𝘌,𝘪}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\}; the table of irreps names the image 𝖨\mathsf{I} or 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} (section 7). A level of dimension 5 is D(2,p)D^{(2,p)} restricted, image.o3_irrep holding J 2 and parity pp; kind runs through the image of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} under D(2)D^{(2)}, in which the image of the level is that of 𝖨\mathsf{I} ("U(5) > O(5) > SO(3) > image"), or, for p=−1p = -1, through that of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} under D(2,−)D^{(2,-)}, in which it is that of 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} ("U(5) > O(5) > O(3) > image"). Its harmonic polynomials of degree ℓ\ell in five variables, an irrep of 𝖲𝖮(𝟧)\mathsf{SO(5)}, restrict to 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} as the D(J)D^{(J)} with J=2k,2k−2,2k−3,…,kJ = 2k, 2k - 2, 2k - 3, \dots, k for k=ℓ,ℓ−3,ℓ−6,⋯≥0k = \ell, \ell - 3, \ell - 6, \dots \ge 0 [RTR04, eq. 21], so that the species of degree ℓ\ell are the sum of those rows JJ, parity pℓp^{\ell}, of section 11. A level of dimension 4 is the product of the irrep of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} of dimension 2 and its Galois twin (5↦−5\sqrt{5} \mapsto -\sqrt{5}); its matrices have determinant 1, and kind is "U(4) > O(4) > SO(4) > image". At an element whose matrix turns two orthogonal planes by θ1\theta_{1} and θ2\theta_{2}, its harmonic polynomials of degree ℓ\ell have the character χℓ/2(θ1+θ2) χℓ/2(θ1−θ2)\chi_{\ell/2}(\theta_{1} + \theta_{2})\, \chi_{\ell/2}(\theta_{1} - \theta_{2}), with χj(ω)=sin⁡((2j+1)ω/2)/sin⁡(ω/2)\chi_j(\omega) = \sin\bigl((2j + 1)\omega/2\bigr)/\sin(\omega/2); at a rotation by ω≤π\omega \le \pi, θ1+θ2\theta_{1} + \theta_{2} and θ1−θ2\theta_{1} - \theta_{2} are ω\omega and ω′\omega' up to sign, ω′=3ω\omega' = 3\omega when the rotation has order 5 and ω′=ω\omega' = \omega otherwise. The tables depend only on the character of the level; the states depend on its partners as well. The states hold for any coordinates q1,…,qNq_1, \dots, q_N of a degenerate mode that the group maps linearly among themselves: normal coordinates, and symmetrized internal coordinates, which the group permutes although they are not linear in the positions of the nuclei (bond lengths, bond angles); the creation operators of an oscillator in these coordinates transform as the coordinates. If they transform by W DΓ(𝘨) W−1W\,D^\Gamma(\mathsfit{g})\,W^{-1}, WW unitary (real Cartesian components of a mode of dimension 2, say), the states are those of Sym⁡v\operatorname{Sym}^{v} of WW applied to these; by Schur's lemma WW is unique up to a factor. A coordinate that the group maps affinely, a torsion angle τ↦±τ+c\tau \mapsto \pm\tau + c, enters through the circle: the group maps (cos⁡τ,sin⁡τ)(\cos\tau, \sin\tau) linearly, by the matrices of 𝖮(𝟤)\mathsf{O(2)} that turn by cc or reflect, and the free-rotor states e±iℓτ\mathrm{e}^{\pm\mathrm{i}\ell\tau}, ℓ>0\ell > 0, carry Eℓ\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{\ell} of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} restricted to the group of those matrices (section 16.1). When that group is not abelian, e∓iτ\mathrm{e}^{\mp\mathrm{i}\tau} carry a level of dimension 2, and when they transform as its partners, e∓iℓτ\mathrm{e}^{\mp\mathrm{i}\ell\tau} are its states ∣ℓ0⟩\lvert \ell 0\rangle and ∣0ℓ⟩\lvert 0 \ell\rangle of harmonic degree ℓ\ell: the polynomials of degree ℓ\ell in the partners on the circle r=1r = 1. The angles of several torsions lie on a torus, which no level holds;
