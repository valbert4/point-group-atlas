8 Exact values and nulls ¶

No value is a floating-point number. A number is an integer or one of these exact values:

  1. {"f": "q", "v": [n, d]}: the rational n/dn/d, d≥1d \ge 1, in lowest terms.
  2. {"f": "cyc", "M": M, "c": [ [n0,d0],…,[ndeg⁡Φℳ−1,ddeg⁡Φℳ−1][n_0, d_0], \dots, [n_{\deg \Phi_{\mathcal{M}}-1}, d_{\deg \Phi_{\mathcal{M}}-1}] ]}: the element ∑k(nk/dk) ζℳk\sum_k (n_k/d_k)\, \zeta_{\mathcal{M}}^k of ℚ(ζℳ)\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{\mathcal{M}}), in the power basis reduced modulo the cyclotomic polynomial Φℳ\Phi_{\mathcal{M}}, so len(c) =deg⁡Φℳ= \deg \Phi_{\mathcal{M}} and k<deg⁡Φℳk < \deg \Phi_{\mathcal{M}}.
  3. {"f": "sqrt", "v": [p, q]}: the real number (sign⁡p)∣p∣/q(\operatorname{sign} p) \sqrt{\lvert p\rvert/q}, p≠0p \ne 0, q≥1q \ge 1, with ∣p∣/q\lvert p\rvert/q in lowest terms and not the square of a rational.

A turn (angle, turn, azimuth_turn, t0, t1), a Haar mass or density (haar_mass, mass, density_dt, total_mass), the qmq_m of section 14.4 and the coefficient of a volume (section 17) are rationals. They are written as the pair [n,d][n, d] of integers, d≥1d \ge 1, in lowest terms. However, the density_dt of an arc of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} is a trigonometric polynomial (section 16).

In the records every q and cyc value is written in its minimal field: a rational is a q, and a cyc has the smallest ℳ\mathcal{M} with the value in ℚ(ζℳ)\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{\mathcal{M}}). Equal values of these kinds have equal encodings in the records, but the bases files do not reduce ℳ\mathcal{M} (section 14.2).

Two kinds of data keep a square root implicit, as a pair of exact values. A coefficient of a bases file is the pair (em,rm)(e_m, r_m) of the amplitude emrme_m \sqrt{r_m} (section 14.2), and a Clebsch–Gordan block is the pair (ℰ,𝒩)(\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{N}) with C=ℰ/𝒩C = \mathcal{E}/\sqrt{\mathcal{N}} (section 10.3).

The sqrt kind writes a square root explicitly. The Clebsch–Gordan coefficients of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} (blocks with 𝒩=1\mathcal{N} = 1, section 10.6) and the coefficients of their Wigner dd matrices (section 9) are q or sqrt values. A sqrt value occurs only in these coefficients and in the states of the levels (section 19) of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, including those of the f shell. It may denote the same number as a cyc value elsewhere (2\sqrt{2} is {"f": "sqrt", "v": [2, 1]} and also a cyc with ℳ=8\mathcal{M} = 8).

An absent datum is the structured null {"reason": R, "value": null}, with reason among these values:

  1. continuous-group: the datum does not exist for a continuous group:

    1. order, gap_smallgroup_id, irrep_count and the class_size of an atom.
    2. the order of an infinite product-form factor and the class_size of a factor with a continuum of conjugates.
    3. the class_size of an arc of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} and of their distinguished points C2 and sh.
    4. the index of a continuous normalizer over a finite group and the normalizer_index of a centralizer of infinite index.
    5. the order and the index of a center or a commutator_subgroup when infinite (section 2.2).
    6. the class_size of an entry of subduction_subgroups.finite or subgroup_classes with a continuum of conjugates, and the order of a continuous normalizer or centralizer in the subduction_subgroups or subgroup_classes of section 16.1.
    7. orbifold (section 17.8).
  2. depends-on-the-point: the shape of an orbit of points whose shape depends on its point (section 12.5).
  3. no-mirror: the mirror of the orbifold of a group without a mirror (section 17.8).
  4. not-a-named-shape: the shape or the vertex_shape of a figure whose points form none of the shapes of section 12.6.
  5. not-a-rational-turn: an Euler angle that is not a rational turn (section 4.2).
  6. not-a-regular-compound: the compound of a figure whose copies form no regular compound (section 12.6).

  7. not-defined-for-family: the field is not defined for this group or object:

    1. family, principal_axis, hk, limit_of, closed_form.flip, the lens of a space that is not a lens space (section 17) and the index of a one-member continuous family.
    2. closed_form.abelian_part, abelian_index and self_conjugacy_condition of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}.
    3. crystal_family, crystal_system and lattices of a group that is not crystallographic.
    4. the theta of an orbit space that is a point (section 17.8).
  8. not-in-source: altmann_T, conway_smith and orbifold of a group, and bethe and bilbao of an irrep of a group, when not tabulated in the cited work.
  9. one-class-under-the-rotations: the hands of a figure whose copies the rotations of the group keep in one class (section 12.6).

A bare null states a fact and occurs only in conjugate_of (the irrep is self-conjugate), pair_label (not half of a complex pair), operation.sense (no sense), operation.axis_index (no axis), alternative_names[].source (a name of the Schoenflies system, section 2.2) and, in point_groups.json, order, family and N (section 15.2). In the subgroup_classes of O3 (section 16.1), order, family and N of a subgroup are bare nulls as in point_groups.json, and so is the class_size of a class that is not normal.
