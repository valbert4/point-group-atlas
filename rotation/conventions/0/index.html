0.1 Common notation ¶

The symbols of this section have one meaning in every document of the catalogue; a document defines each of its other symbols where it first uses it. The elementary signs have their usual meaning: ++ −- ±\pm ⋅\cdot // ab\frac{a}{b} x\sqrt{x} n!n! ∑\sum == ≠\ne << >> ≥\ge ∉\notin ⊃\supset   ⟺  \iff cos⁡\cos sin⁡\sin tan⁡\tan min⁡\min ∞\infty, as do parentheses for grouping, the punctuation of a formula and words in a formula. Element indices count from 0; partner indices μ\mu, copy indices κ\kappa and Clebsch–Gordan indices μ1\mu_1, μ2\mu_2 count from 1. A turn is an angle in units of 2π2\pi, and 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(3)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO}(3) is the subgroup of the elements with det⁡=+1\det = +1.

0.2 Typesetting ¶

The group documents (data/latex, data/pdf; section 18) set groups in sans-serif, also the letters that stand for groups (𝖦\mathsf{G}, 𝖧\mathsf{H}, 𝖭\mathsf{N}, 𝖢\mathsf{C}, …), the abstract groups of abstract_type and the groups in the names of other schemes; irrep labels in small capitals, those of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} as in section 11 (D(J)D^{(J)} and D(J,p)D^{(J,p)}); elements in a sans-serif oblique, named or not: element names, digits and class counts included, as 𝟤𝘊𝟥\mathsfit{2C_{3}} and 𝘚𝟤𝟦𝟩\mathsfit{S_{24}^{7}} (section 5), a variable in a name in italic, as 𝘊nk\mathsfit{C_{\mathnormal{n}}^{\mathnormal{k}}}; the letters that stand for elements, as 𝘨\mathsfit{g}, 𝘩\mathsfit{h}, 𝘙\mathsfit{R} (section 4), ξj\mathsfit{\xi}_{j} (section 9) and 𝘘\mathsfit{Q} (section 17), an exponent or index after one set as usual, as in 𝘨−1\mathsfit{g}^{-1}; and the rotations, rotoreflections and mirrors written by their axis and angle, 𝘙𝐯(ω)\mathsfit{R_{\mathbf{v}}}(\omega) (section 3), 𝘚𝐯(ω)\mathsfit{S_{\mathbf{v}}}(\omega) and σ𝐯\mathsfit{\sigma_{\mathbf{v}}} (section 4.2); but not the angle after an element, as in 𝘊∞(2π/3)\mathsfit{C_{\infty}}(2\pi/3) (section 16); the kets of the spherical harmonics as ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle (JJ above, mm below, a negative mm with a minus sign; also in a matrix element), with κ\kappa the copy index and μ\mu the partner index; angles as rational multiples of π\pi, and the angle xx of a class-space component (section 16) as its element writes it: ϕ\phi, the rotation angle, on the arcs 𝘊(ϕ)\mathsfit{C}(\phi) and 𝘪 𝘊(ϕ)\mathsfit{i}\,\mathsfit{C}(\phi), and ψ\psi, the azimuth of an element of an atom, on σ𝘷(ψ)\mathsfit{\sigma_{v}}(\psi) and 𝘊𝟤′(ψ)\mathsfit{C_{2}'}(\psi); and exact values as rationals, square roots, cosines and sines of rational multiples of π\pi, the golden ratio φ=12(1+5)\varphi = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(1 + \sqrt{5}\bigr) and roots of unity ζn=e2πi/n\zeta_n = \mathrm{e}^{2\pi\mathrm{i}/n}. A list may be set in two or three copies side by side: it then reads down each copy within a band of rows, and the bands, between heavier rules, follow one another. A document defines the symbols and named constants that section 0.1 does not, each where it first uses it, and refers to this document by section number. A citation names an entry of REFERENCES.pdf by its label, the initials of its authors (or the first letters of one author's name) and its year, as [Lax74, section 2.10], and links to that entry.
