6 Irrep labels ¶

Irreps are labeled in the Mulliken notation, computed from the characters. For an irrep Γ\Gamma of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, χΓ(𝘨)\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}) is its character at the element 𝘨\mathsfit{g} (χ(𝘨)\chi(\mathsfit{g}) when the irrep is understood).

Continuous groups. The irrep families of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} and 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}} also carry Greek aliases (section 16). The irreps of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} are labeled D(J)D^{(J)} and D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} as in section 11 (D^(J), D^(J,+), D^(J,-)), with spectroscopic aliases (section 16).

6.1 Rules ¶

The label follows these rules:

  1. If 𝘪∈𝖦\mathsfit{i} \in \mathsf{G}, then 𝖦=(𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥))×{𝘌,𝘪}\mathsf{G} = (\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}) \times \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\}: the label is that of the restriction of Γ\Gamma to 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}, followed by g when χΓ(𝘪)=+dim⁡Γ\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{i}) = +\dim\Gamma and u when χΓ(𝘪)=−dim⁡Γ\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{i}) = -\dim\Gamma. The restriction is labeled by these rules in 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} without the renames of section 6.3.
  2. Otherwise, if σ𝘻∈𝖦\mathsfit{\sigma_{z}} \in \mathsf{G}, then 𝖦=(𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥))×{𝘌,σ𝘩}\mathsf{G} = (\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}) \times \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{\sigma_{h}}\}: as in rule 1, with ' (χΓ(σ𝘩)=+dim⁡Γ\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{\sigma_{h}}) = +\dim\Gamma) and '' (χΓ(σ𝘩)=−dim⁡Γ\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{\sigma_{h}}) = -\dim\Gamma).

  3. Otherwise 𝖦\mathsf{G} is 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} or 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}, is 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} or 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} with NN even, or is 𝖳\mathsf{T}, 𝖮\mathsf{O}, 𝖨\mathsf{I} or 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}.

    1. Axial 𝖦\mathsf{G}. The principal generator 𝘨p\mathsfit{g}_p is the operation about zz with the smallest positive turn 1/Np1/N_p: 𝘙𝘻(2π/N)\mathsfit{R_{z}}(2\pi/N) with Np=NN_p = N, except σ𝘩𝘙𝘻(π/N)\mathsfit{\sigma_{h}} \mathsfit{R_{z}}(\pi/N) with Np=2NN_p = 2N in 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}}. The flip is the class of 𝘊𝟤𝘹\mathsfit{C_{2x}} when 𝖦\mathsf{G} has binary rotations about horizontal axes, otherwise the class of σ𝘷(0)\mathsfit{\sigma_{v}}(0) (𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} have none). A one-dimensional irrep is A if χ(𝘨p)=1\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = 1 and B if χ(𝘨p)=−1\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = -1, with the subscript 1 if χ(flip)=+1\chi(\text{flip}) = +1 and 2 if χ(flip)=−1\chi(\text{flip}) = -1 (none without a flip). Otherwise such an irrep has χ(𝘨p)=ζNpq\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = \zeta_{N_p}^q with 0<q<Np0 < q < N_p and is 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q if q<Np/2q < N_p/2 and 2ENp−q{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{N_p-q} if q>Np/2q > N_p/2 (section 6.4). A two-dimensional irrep is E_q with χ(𝘨p)=2cos⁡(2πq/Np)\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = 2\cos(2\pi q/N_p).
    2. 𝖳\mathsf{T}, 𝖮\mathsf{O}, 𝖨\mathsf{I}, 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}. The letter depends on the dimension: A (1), E (2), T (3), G (4), H (5). The two complex one-dimensional irreps of 𝖳\mathsf{T} are 1E{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E} and 2E{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E} (section 6.4). The letters A and T carry the subscript 1 if χ>0\chi > 0 on the anchor class and 2 otherwise: 𝟨𝘊𝟦\mathsfit{6C_{4}} in 𝖮\mathsf{O}, 𝟨𝘚𝟦\mathsfit{6S_{4}} in 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, 𝟣𝟤𝘊𝟧\mathsfit{12C_{5}} in 𝖨\mathsf{I} and none in 𝖳\mathsf{T}.
  4. The renames of section 6.3 are applied last, to the group itself.

Thus g\mathrm{g}/u\mathrm{u} takes precedence over primes: rule 2 applies only when σ𝘻∈𝖦\mathsfit{\sigma_{z}} \in \mathsf{G} and 𝘪∉𝖦\mathsfit{i} \notin \mathsf{G} (𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}} and 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} with NN odd). By rule 1, 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} inherit the anchors of 𝖮\mathsf{O} and 𝖨\mathsf{I}. The vector (x,y,z)(x, y, z) spans T1u\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_{1\mathrm{u}} in 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} and T2\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_2 in 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}.

6.2 The stored labels ¶

  1. mulliken: the label of section 6.1, with every index.

  2. label: mulliken with two omissions:

    1. the E\mathrm{\scriptsize E} index when all E\mathrm{\scriptsize E}-type irreps of 𝖦\mathsf{G} have the same index (E in 𝖢𝟦𝗏\mathsf{C_{4v}} and E_1, E_2 in 𝖢𝟨𝗏\mathsf{C_{6v}}).
    2. a subscript 1 or 2 determined by the flip or the anchor when no other irrep of 𝖦\mathsf{G} has the same prefix, letter and tag (A, T_1, T_2 in 𝖨\mathsf{I} and A_g, A_u in 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}).
  3. display: label in LaTeX ("{}^{1}E_{1}", "A_{2g}"): the prefix as a left superscript, the numeric subscript with g\mathrm{g} or u\mathrm{u} as a subscript, primes after.
  4. id: mulliken with every ' written p and every character that is not a letter or digit removed (A_1g →\to A1g, E_1'' →\to E1pp, 1E_2 →\to 1E2). Elsewhere the record names irreps by id. In subduction_basis and subduction_unitary the id (irrep_id) has the label or the mulliken of the irrep beside it (irrep). The blocks of subduction_basis.enumerated (section 14.4) and the terms of a continuous group's subduction_o3.instances (section 11) name irreps by label alone.
  5. pair_label: its label without the leading 1 or 2 when its mulliken begins with 1E or 2E, null otherwise. It names the irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} formed by the two irreps of a complex-conjugate pair (section 6.4).
  6. conjugate_of: the id of the irrep whose character is the complex conjugate of χΓ\chi_\Gamma, or null when χΓ\chi_\Gamma is real.
  7. dim: the dimension.
  8. chi: the characters, one for each class in the class order.
  9. kernel: the id of the node of subgroup_lattice (section 15.1) that is ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma, the elements on which χΓ\chi_\Gamma is the dimension.

  10. image: the Schoenflies symbol of the group formed by the matrices:

    1. 𝖢n\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{n}}} for dimension 1 (n=∣𝖦∣/∣ker⁡Γ∣n = \lvert\mathsf{G}\rvert/\lvert\ker\Gamma\rvert).
    2. the image of the level's chain for dimension 2 and 3 (section 19).
    3. the group isomorphic to it for dimension 4 and 5 (section 19).

The order of character_table.irreps is the record's irrep order. It sorts the mulliken labels by these keys in turn, as in C4h (A_g, B_g, 1E_1g, 2E_1g, A_u, B_u, 1E_1u, 2E_1u):

  1. the tag, g, ' or none before u and ''.
  2. the letter, in the order A, B, E, T, G, H.
  3. the numeric subscripts, as numbers.
  4. the prefix, none before 1 before 2.

Continuous groups. The irrep families of a continuous group (section 16) are in the same order, with the family index in place of the subscript, and D^(J,+) before D^(J,-).

6.4 Complex pairs ¶

A complex-conjugate pair is two irreps, each the conjugate_of of the other, with the same pair_label. The irrep with χ(𝘨p)=ζNp+q\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = \zeta_{N_p}^{+q} is 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q, and its complex conjugate (χ(𝘨p)=ζNp−q\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = \zeta_{N_p}^{-q}) is 2Eq{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q. In 𝖳\mathsf{T} and 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}, 1E{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E} is the irrep with χ(𝟦𝘊𝟥)=ζ3\chi(\mathsfit{4C_{3}}) = \zeta_3, where 𝟦𝘊𝟥\mathsfit{4C_{3}} is the class of rotations by 1/31/3 turn about the poles with xyz>0xyz > 0.

Over ℝ\mathbb{R} the two irreps of a pair form one irrep, of dimension 2 and character 2Re⁡χ2\operatorname{Re}\chi, where χ\chi is the character of either member. This irrep is named by the pair_label (Eq\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q for 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q and 2Eq{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q), which the documents set in the column over ℝ\mathbb{R}. An element acts on functions by a real operation (section 4.1), so the complex conjugate v∗v^* of a vector vv of 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q (1E{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E} in 𝖳\mathsf{T} and 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}) is a vector of 2Eq{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q. On kets, the conjugate of ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle is (−1)m∣ −mJ⟩(-1)^m \bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle. The vectors 122 (v+v∗)\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}\,(v + v^*) and −12i2 (v−v∗)-\tfrac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\sqrt{2}\,(v - v^*) are real, and on them an element 𝘨\mathsfit{g} with χ(𝘨)=eiθ\chi(\mathsfit{g}) = \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}\theta} on 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q acts by the matrix (cos⁡θsin⁡θ−sin⁡θcos⁡θ)\left(\begin{smallmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{smallmatrix}\right) in the row-vector convention of section 9.

In the bases (section 14), copy κ\kappa of 2Eq{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q at JJ is (−1)m(-1)^m times the conjugate of copy κ\kappa of 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q in the axial groups, finite and continuous, where that copy is ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle. In 𝖳\mathsf{T} and 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}} it is (−1)J(-1)^J times this conjugate.

Continuous groups. The families 1En{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n, 2En{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n of 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} and 1En,g{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}}, 2En,g{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}}, 1En,u{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}}, 2En,u{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}} of 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} have no pair_label. By the same rule, the documents name the families over ℝ\mathbb{R} formed by these pairs: En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n, En,g\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}} and En,u\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}}. These families are the restrictions of the families En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} and 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} and of En,g\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}}, En,u\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}} of 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}, whose real basis is 122 (v+v∗)\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}\,(v + v^*) and +12i2 (v−v∗)+\tfrac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\sqrt{2}\,(v - v^*). Here v=(x+iy)nzδv = (x + \mathrm{i}y)^n z^\delta (section 9) is a vector of 1En{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n (1En,g{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}}, 1En,u{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}}), and this basis differs from the real vectors above in the sign of the second vector.

6.5 bethe and bilbao ¶

Two fields name an irrep in an outside numbering, for the 32 crystallographic point groups and the records C1v, D1, D1h, D1d of their classes. Every other irrep has null not-in-source (section 8).

  1. bethe (in the records only): its number in the tables of [KDWS63], "Gamma_4", with the sign as a superscript in a group with 𝘪\mathsfit{i}, "Gamma_6^+" for g and "Gamma_6^-" for u.
  2. bilbao (the documents' column Bilbao): its label in the double point group tables of the Bilbao Crystallographic Server [Bil26, EBW+17] (the notation of [CDML79] at the zone center, "Gamma_2^+"). The server's point group tables [AKC+06] number as bethe.

A work's symbol names the irrep whose characters equal the work's on its operations. Each operation of the work is the element with the same matrix in the frame of section 3. An operation of another frame is carried by a rotation that takes the axis it names, with its sign, to the pole of the element that names its class (section 5.2). This rotation also takes the principal axis to zz (the axis b of the monoclinic tables of bilbao). In a work that lets a function move with the points (section 4.3), the functions with the symbol of a complex irrep are those of its conjugate.

The records C1v, D1, D1h and D1d take the symbols of C1h, C2, C2v and C2h through the cyclic permutation x→y→z→xx \to y \to z \to x (its inverse for C1v). This permutation is the setting of the elements of D1h in the lattice of D2h (section 12.1).

The tables of bethe place the binary axes of 𝖣𝟥\mathsf{D_{3}}, 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}} and 𝖣𝟥𝗁\mathsf{D_{3h}} along yy, read through 𝘙𝘻(−π/2)\mathsfit{R_{z}}(-\pi/2). The groups tabulated together in [KDWS63] correspond through the map that takes 𝘨\mathsfit{g} to (det⁡𝘨) 𝘨(\det \mathsfit{g})\,\mathsfit{g}. In the frame of section 3, bethe has these values:

  1. Γ2=B2\Gamma_{2} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_2, Γ3=B1\Gamma_{3} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_1, Γ4=B3\Gamma_{4} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_3 in 𝖣𝟤\mathsf{D_{2}}.
  2. Γ3=B2\Gamma_{3} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_2, Γ4=B1\Gamma_{4} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_1 in 𝖣𝟨\mathsf{D_{6}}.
  3. Γ3=B1\Gamma_{3} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_1 in 𝖢𝟨𝗏\mathsf{C_{6v}}.
  4. Γ3=2E\Gamma_{3} = {}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E} in 𝖲𝟦\mathsf{S_{4}}.
  5. Γ5=E′′\Gamma_{5} = \mathrm{\scriptsize E}'' and Γ6=E′\Gamma_{6} = \mathrm{\scriptsize E}' in 𝖣𝟥𝗁\mathsf{D_{3h}}.
  6. Γ2=1E\Gamma_{2} = {}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E} in 𝖳\mathsf{T}, whose 𝟦𝘊𝟥\mathsfit{4C_{3}} in the tables of bethe is the class of section 6.4.

A label of bilbao follows the orientation of its table: bilbao reads 𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{D_{2d}} and 𝖣𝟥𝗁\mathsf{D_{3h}} in the orientations -42m and -62m, binary axes along xx.

No field holds the labels of [BSW36] for the groups of a wave vector of the cubic lattices. Of the cubic groups here, only 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}}, at Γ\Gamma, R and H, and 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, at the point P of the body-centered lattice, are such groups. As [AC65] gives them:
