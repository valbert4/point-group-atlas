6 Irrep labels ¶

Irreps are labeled in the Mulliken notation, computed from the characters. Γ\Gamma is an irrep of 𝖦\mathsf{G} and χΓ(𝘨)\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{g}) its character at the element 𝘨\mathsfit{g} (χ(𝘨)\chi(\mathsfit{g}) when the irrep is understood). The irrep families of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} and 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}} also carry Greek aliases (section 16). The irreps of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} are labeled D(J)D^{(J)} and D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} as in section 11 (D^(J), D^(J,+), D^(J,-)), with spectroscopic aliases (section 16).

6.1 Rules ¶

  1. If 𝘪∈𝖦\mathsfit{i} \in \mathsf{G}, then 𝖦=(𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥))×{𝘌,𝘪}\mathsf{G} = (\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}) \times \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\}: the label is that of the restriction of Γ\Gamma to 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}, built by these rules in 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} without the renames of section 6.3, followed by g when χΓ(𝘪)=+dim⁡Γ\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{i}) = +\dim\Gamma and u when χΓ(𝘪)=−dim⁡Γ\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{i}) = -\dim\Gamma.
  2. Else, if σ𝘻∈𝖦\mathsfit{\sigma_{z}} \in \mathsf{G}, then 𝖦=(𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥))×{𝘌,σ𝘩}\mathsf{G} = (\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}) \times \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{\sigma_{h}}\}: the same with ' (χΓ(σ𝘩)=+dim⁡Γ\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{\sigma_{h}}) = +\dim\Gamma) and '' (χΓ(σ𝘩)=−dim⁡Γ\chi_\Gamma(\mathsfit{\sigma_{h}}) = -\dim\Gamma).

  3. Otherwise 𝖦\mathsf{G} is 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} or 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}; 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} or 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} with NN even; or 𝖳\mathsf{T}, 𝖮\mathsf{O}, 𝖨\mathsf{I} or 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}.

    1. Axial 𝖦\mathsf{G}. The principal generator 𝘨p\mathsfit{g}_p is the operation about zz with the smallest positive turn 1/Np1/N_p: 𝘙𝘻(2π/N)\mathsfit{R_{z}}(2\pi/N) with Np=NN_p = N, except σ𝘩𝘙𝘻(π/N)\mathsfit{\sigma_{h}} \mathsfit{R_{z}}(\pi/N) with Np=2NN_p = 2N in 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}}. The flip is the class of 𝘊𝟤𝘹\mathsfit{C_{2x}} when 𝖦\mathsf{G} has binary rotations about horizontal axes, else the class of σ𝘷(0)\mathsfit{\sigma_{v}}(0) (𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} have none). Dimension 1: A if χ(𝘨p)=1\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = 1 and B if χ(𝘨p)=−1\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = -1, with the subscript 1 if χ(flip)=+1\chi(\text{flip}) = +1 and 2 if χ(flip)=−1\chi(\text{flip}) = -1 (none without a flip); otherwise χ(𝘨p)=ζNpq\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = \zeta_{N_p}^q with 0<q<Np0 < q < N_p, and the irrep is 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q if q<Np/2q < N_p/2 and 2ENp−q{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{N_p-q} if q>Np/2q > N_p/2 (section 6.4). Dimension 2: E_q with χ(𝘨p)=2cos⁡(2πq/Np)\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = 2\cos(2\pi q/N_p).
    2. 𝖳\mathsf{T}, 𝖮\mathsf{O}, 𝖨\mathsf{I}, 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}. The letter is set by the dimension: A (1), E (2), T (3), G (4), H (5); the two complex one-dimensional irreps of 𝖳\mathsf{T} are 1E{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E} and 2E{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E} (section 6.4). A and T carry the subscript 1 if χ>0\chi > 0 on the anchor class and 2 otherwise: 𝟨𝘊𝟦\mathsfit{6C_{4}} in 𝖮\mathsf{O}, 𝟨𝘚𝟦\mathsfit{6S_{4}} in 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, 𝟣𝟤𝘊𝟧\mathsfit{12C_{5}} in 𝖨\mathsf{I}; 𝖳\mathsf{T} has no anchor.
  4. The renames of section 6.3 are applied last, to the group itself.

So g\mathrm{g}/u\mathrm{u} takes precedence over primes: rule 2 applies only when σ𝘻∈𝖦\mathsfit{\sigma_{z}} \in \mathsf{G} and 𝘪∉𝖦\mathsfit{i} \notin \mathsf{G} (𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}} and 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} with NN odd). By rule 1, 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} inherit the anchors of 𝖮\mathsf{O} and 𝖨\mathsf{I}. The vector (x,y,z)(x, y, z) spans T1u\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_{1\mathrm{u}} in 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} and T2\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_2 in 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}.

6.2 The stored labels ¶

  1. mulliken: the label of section 6.1, with every index.
  2. label: mulliken with two omissions: the E\mathrm{\scriptsize E} index when all E\mathrm{\scriptsize E}-type irreps of 𝖦\mathsf{G} have the same index (𝖢𝟦𝗏\mathsf{C_{4v}}: E; 𝖢𝟨𝗏\mathsf{C_{6v}}: E_1, E_2), and a subscript 1 or 2 set by the flip or the anchor when no other irrep of 𝖦\mathsf{G} has the same prefix, letter and tag (𝖨\mathsf{I}: A, T_1, T_2; 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}: A_g, A_u).
  3. display: label in LaTeX ("{}^{1}E_{1}", "A_{2g}"): the prefix as a left superscript, the numeric subscript with g\mathrm{g} or u\mathrm{u} as a subscript, primes after.
  4. id: mulliken with every ' written p and every character that is not a letter or a digit removed (A_1g →\to A1g, E_1'' →\to E1pp, 1E_2 →\to 1E2). Every other block names irreps by id, but for the blocks of subduction_basis.enumerated (section 14.4) and the terms of a continuous group's subduction_o3.instances (section 11), which name them by label.
  5. pair_label: for an irrep whose mulliken begins with 1E or 2E, its label without the leading 1 or 2; null otherwise. It names the irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} that the two irreps of a complex-conjugate pair form (section 6.4).
  6. conjugate_of: the id of the irrep whose character is the complex conjugate of χΓ\chi_\Gamma; null when χΓ\chi_\Gamma is real.
  7. dim, chi: the dimension and the characters, one per class in the class order.
  8. kernel: the id of the node of subgroup_lattice (section 15.1) that is ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma, the elements on which χΓ\chi_\Gamma is the dimension; image: the Schoenflies symbol of the group that the matrices form, 𝖢n\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{n}}} for dimension 1 (n=∣𝖦∣/∣ker⁡Γ∣n = \lvert\mathsf{G}\rvert/\lvert\ker\Gamma\rvert), the image of the level's chain for dimension 2 and 3, and the group isomorphic to it for dimension 4 and 5 (section 19).

The order of character_table.irreps is the record's irrep order. It sorts the mulliken labels by the tag, g, ' or none before u and ''; then by the letter, in the order A, B, E, T, G, H; then by the numeric subscripts, as numbers; then by the prefix, none before 1 before 2 (C4h: A_g, B_g, 1E_1g, 2E_1g, A_u, B_u, 1E_1u, 2E_1u). The irrep families of a continuous group (section 16) are in the same order, with the family index in place of the subscript, and D^(J,+) before D^(J,-).

6.4 Complex pairs ¶

1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q is the irrep with χ(𝘨p)=ζNp+q\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = \zeta_{N_p}^{+q} and 2Eq{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q its complex conjugate (χ(𝘨p)=ζNp−q\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = \zeta_{N_p}^{-q}). In 𝖳\mathsf{T} and 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}, 1E{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E} is the irrep with χ(𝟦𝘊𝟥)=ζ3\chi(\mathsfit{4C_{3}}) = \zeta_3, where 𝟦𝘊𝟥\mathsfit{4C_{3}} is the class of rotations by 1/31/3 turn about the poles with xyz>0xyz > 0. A complex-conjugate pair is two irreps, each the conjugate_of of the other, with the same pair_label.

Over ℝ\mathbb{R} the two irreps of a pair form one irrep, of dimension 2 and character 2Re⁡χ2\operatorname{Re}\chi, with χ\chi the character of either member; it is named by the pair_label (Eq\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q for 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q and 2Eq{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q), which the documents print in the column over ℝ\mathbb{R}. An element acts on functions by a real operation (section 4.1), so the complex conjugate v∗v^* of a vector vv of 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q (1E{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E} in 𝖳\mathsf{T} and 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}) is a vector of 2Eq{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q; on kets, the conjugate of ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle is (−1)m∣ −mJ⟩(-1)^m \bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle. The vectors 122 (v+v∗)\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}\,(v + v^*) and −12i2 (v−v∗)-\tfrac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\sqrt{2}\,(v - v^*) are real, and on them an element 𝘨\mathsfit{g} with χ(𝘨)=eiθ\chi(\mathsfit{g}) = \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}\theta} on 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q acts by the matrix (cos⁡θsin⁡θ−sin⁡θcos⁡θ)\left(\begin{smallmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{smallmatrix}\right) in the row-vector convention of section 9.

The families 1En{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n, 2En{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n of 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} and 1En,g{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}}, 2En,g{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}}, 1En,u{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}}, 2En,u{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}} of 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} store no pair_label; the documents name the families over ℝ\mathbb{R} that they form by the same rule, En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n, En,g\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}} and En,u\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}}. These are the restrictions of the families En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} and 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} and of En,g\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}}, En,u\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}} of 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}, whose real basis is 122 (v+v∗)\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}\,(v + v^*) and +12i2 (v−v∗)+\tfrac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\sqrt{2}\,(v - v^*) with v=(x+iy)nzδv = (x + \mathrm{i}y)^n z^\delta (section 9), a vector of 1En{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n (1En,g{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}}, 1En,u{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}}): it differs from the real vectors above in the sign of the second vector. In the bases of section 14, copy κ\kappa of 2Eq{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q at JJ is (−1)m(-1)^m times the conjugate of copy κ\kappa of 1Eq{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_q in the axial groups, finite and continuous, where that copy is the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle, and (−1)J(-1)^J times it in 𝖳\mathsf{T} and 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}.

6.5 bethe and bilbao ¶

Two fields name an irrep in an outside numbering, for the 32 crystallographic point groups and the records C1v, D1, D1h, D1d of their classes; every other irrep has null not-in-source (section 8).

  1. bethe, held in the records only: its number in the tables of [KDWS63], "Gamma_4", in a group with 𝘪\mathsfit{i} with the sign as a superscript, "Gamma_6^+" for g and "Gamma_6^-" for u.
  2. bilbao (the documents' column Bilbao): its label in the double point group tables of the Bilbao Crystallographic Server [Bil26, EBW+17], the notation of [CDML79] at the zone center, "Gamma_2^+"; the server's point group tables [AKC+06] number as bethe.

A work's symbol names the irrep whose characters equal the work's on its operations, each the element of the same matrix in the frame of section 3. An operation of another frame is carried by the rotation that takes the axis it names, with its sign, to the pole of the element that names its class (section 5.1), and the principal axis to zz (the axis b of the monoclinic tables of bilbao): the tables of bethe set the binary axes of 𝖣𝟥\mathsf{D_{3}}, 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}} and 𝖣𝟥𝗁\mathsf{D_{3h}} along yy, read through 𝘙𝘻(−π/2)\mathsfit{R_{z}}(-\pi/2); C1v, D1, D1h and D1d take the symbols of C1h, C2, C2v and C2h through the cyclic permutation x→y→z→xx \to y \to z \to x (its inverse for C1v), the setting of D1h's elements in the lattice of D2h (section 12.1). The groups that [KDWS63] tabulates together correspond through the map that takes 𝘨\mathsfit{g} to (det⁡𝘨) 𝘨(\det \mathsfit{g})\,\mathsfit{g}. In this frame bethe has Γ2=B2\Gamma_{2} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_2, Γ3=B1\Gamma_{3} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_1, Γ4=B3\Gamma_{4} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_3 in 𝖣𝟤\mathsf{D_{2}}; Γ3=B2\Gamma_{3} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_2, Γ4=B1\Gamma_{4} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_1 in 𝖣𝟨\mathsf{D_{6}}; Γ3=B1\Gamma_{3} = \mathrm{\scriptsize B}_1 in 𝖢𝟨𝗏\mathsf{C_{6v}}; Γ3=2E\Gamma_{3} = {}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize E} in 𝖲𝟦\mathsf{S_{4}}; Γ5=E′′\Gamma_{5} = \mathrm{\scriptsize E}'' and Γ6=E′\Gamma_{6} = \mathrm{\scriptsize E}' in 𝖣𝟥𝗁\mathsf{D_{3h}}; and in 𝖳\mathsf{T}, whose 𝟦𝘊𝟥\mathsfit{4C_{3}} in the tables of bethe is the class of section 6.4, Γ2=1E\Gamma_{2} = {}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize E}.
