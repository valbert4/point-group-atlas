2 Names and identification ¶

2.1 Names ¶

The name of a group is its Schoenflies symbol group.schoenflies with every _ and every parenthesis removed: C1 … C12, C1h … C12h, C1v … C12v, D1 … D12, D1h … D12h, D1d … D12d, S2, S4, …, S24; T, Th, Td, O, Oh, I, Ih; Cinf, Cinfh, Cinfv, Dinf, Dinfh; SO3 and O3 ("SO(3)", "O(3)"). The 98 names are distinct, also ignoring case. They name every file and are the value of every field that refers to another group (o3_class, computed_from.group, the node ids and aliases of point_groups.json, the group of a bases file).

The N=1N = 1 groups keep their systematic names: C1h ={𝘌,σ𝘩}= \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{\sigma_{h}}\} and C1v ={𝘌,σ𝘷(0)}= \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{\sigma_{v}}(0)\} are both 𝖢𝗌\mathsf{C_{s}}; D1 ={𝘌,𝘊𝟤𝘹}= \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{C_{2x}}\} is 𝖢𝟤\mathsf{C_{2}} with its axis along xx; D1h is 𝖢𝟤𝗏\mathsf{C_{2v}} and D1d is 𝖢𝟤𝗁\mathsf{C_{2h}}, each with its 2-fold axis along xx; S2 ={𝘌,𝘪}= \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\} is 𝖢𝗂\mathsf{C_{i}}. The records of one 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class name each other in the field o3_conjugates, and each lists the other names of the class in the field alternative_names (section 2.2). The documents write these other names after ≅\cong, which joins two names of one class (𝖢𝟤𝗏≅𝖣𝟣𝗁\mathsf{C_{2v}} \cong \mathsf{D_{1h}}).

2.2 The group block ¶

Six fields name 𝖦\mathsf{G} in an outside scheme, gap_smallgroup_id, olive, ihrig_golubitsky, altmann_T, conway_smith and orbifold, and two name its irreps, bethe and bilbao (section 6.5); their citations are entries of REFERENCES.pdf. Every other field follows from the record by the rule given.

  1. schoenflies: the Schoenflies symbol in ASCII, _ before the subscript: "C_3v", "S_4", "D_1h", "T_d", "C_inf", "D_infh"; "T", "O", "I" have no subscript; 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} are "SO(3)" and "O(3)" (their Schoenflies symbols 𝖪\mathsf{K} and 𝖪𝗁\mathsf{K_{h}} are in alternative_names).
  2. schoenflies_unicode (continuous groups): schoenflies with inf written ∞ ("C_∞v").
  3. o3_conjugates: the names of the other records whose groups are conjugate to 𝖦\mathsf{G} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, the same group in another orientation (section 2.1; the classes of section 15.2): C1h ["C1v"] and C1v ["C1h"], D1 ["C2"] and C2 ["D1"], D1h ["C2v"] and C2v ["D1h"], D1d ["C2h"] and C2h ["D1d"]; [] for every other group.
  4. hermann_mauguin: the short Hermann–Mauguin symbol in ASCII (section 2.3).
  5. type: "I" if 𝖦⊂𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \subset \mathsf{SO(3)}; else "II" if 𝘪∈𝖦\mathsfit{i} \in \mathsf{G}; else "III".
  6. order: ∣𝖦∣\lvert \mathsf{G}\rvert; continuous groups: null continuous-group.
  7. family: axial groups {"name": F, "N": N} with FF one of Cn, Cnh, Cnv, Dn, Dnh, Dnd, S2n read off the name (NN is half the subscript for 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}}: S4 is {"name": "S2n", "N": 2}); the exceptional and continuous groups: null not-defined-for-family.
  8. principal_axis: [0, 0, 1] for the axial groups and the continuous groups other than 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}; the exceptional groups, 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}: null not-defined-for-family.
  9. contains_inversion: whether 𝘪∈𝖦\mathsfit{i} \in \mathsf{G}.
  10. contains_sigma_h: whether σ𝘻∈𝖦\mathsfit{\sigma_{z}} \in \mathsf{G}, in the frame of section 3. For an axial group σ𝘻\mathsfit{\sigma_{z}} is the σ𝘩\mathsfit{\sigma_{h}} of the principal axis. For the exceptional groups it is a statement about the frame: true for 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}, 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}, whose coordinate planes are mirror planes, false for 𝖳\mathsf{T}, 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, 𝖮\mathsf{O} and 𝖨\mathsf{I}.
  11. crystallographic: whether every element has order 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6; false for the continuous groups.
  12. polar: whether 𝖦\mathsf{G} fixes a nonzero vector, so that polar-1 (section 13) carries its trivial irrep: the groups 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}, 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} and 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}. Crystallographers call a group chiral, or enantiomorphic, when its type is I, and centrosymmetric when contains_inversion is true.
  13. crystal_family, crystal_system and lattices, for a crystallographic group (otherwise null not-defined-for-family), by its Laue class [Aro16]: the family "triclinic", "monoclinic", "orthorhombic", "tetragonal", "hexagonal" or "cubic"; the system, the family with "hexagonal" split into "trigonal" (the Laue classes 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}} and 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}}) and "hexagonal"; lattices[], the Bravais types of the lattices of its crystals, each with bravais, the symbol of the type ("aP", "mP", "mS", "oP", "oS", "oI", "oF", "tP", "tI", "hR", "hP", "cP", "cI", "cF"), and holohedry, the Schoenflies symbol of the point group of the lattice: the types of its system, and for a trigonal group "hR" (holohedry 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}}) and "hP" (𝖣𝟨𝗁\mathsf{D_{6h}}).
  14. laue_class: the group that 𝖦\mathsf{G} and 𝘪\mathsfit{i} generate, written as normalizer is: schoenflies, and index, 1 when 𝘪∈𝖦\mathsfit{i} \in \mathsf{G} and 2 otherwise. It may have no record (𝖢𝟤𝟤𝗁\mathsf{C_{22h}}, that of 𝖢𝟣𝟣𝗁\mathsf{C_{11h}}).
  15. allowed_properties[]: the tensor properties of section 13.2 that 𝖦\mathsf{G} allows, in the order of its table, each with jahn, the Jahn symbol of the tensor ("V", "eV", "e[V2]", "{V2}V", "V[V2]", "[V3]", "V3", "e[V2]V", "e[V3]"), spaces, the ids of the spaces of cartesian_tensors whose sum the tensor is, and properties, their names; empty when 𝖦\mathsf{G} allows none of them (𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}}, 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}).
  16. abstract_type: the isomorphism type. Finite abelian 𝖦\mathsf{G} (every class has one element): its invariant factors, largest first, "Z_a x Z_b" (each factor divides the one before; "Z_1" for the trivial group; D2h is "Z_2 x Z_2 x Z_2"). Finite non-abelian 𝖦\mathsf{G}: "Dih(n)", the dihedral group of order n=∣𝖦∣n = \lvert \mathsf{G}\rvert, if some element has order ∣𝖦∣/2\lvert \mathsf{G}\rvert/2; else, if 𝘪∈𝖦\mathsfit{i} \in \mathsf{G}, "X x Z_2" with X=X = "Dih(n/2)" if some element of 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} has order ∣𝖦∣/4\lvert \mathsf{G}\rvert/4, else X=X = "A_4", "S_4", "A_5" for ∣𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)∣=12,24,60\lvert \mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}\rvert = 12, 24, 60; else "A_4", "S_4", "A_5" for ∣𝖦∣=12,24,60\lvert \mathsf{G}\rvert = 12, 24, 60 (𝖳\mathsf{T}; 𝖮\mathsf{O} and 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}; 𝖨\mathsf{I}). Continuous groups: the table of 12.3. The documents set "Z_n" as 𝖹n\mathsf{Z_{\mathnormal{n}}}, the cyclic group of order nn, "Dih(n)" as 𝖣𝗂𝗁n\mathsf{Dih_{\mathnormal{n}}}, "A_4" and "A_5" as 𝖠𝗅𝗍𝟦\mathsf{Alt_{4}} and 𝖠𝗅𝗍𝟧\mathsf{Alt_{5}}, the alternating groups of degree 4 and 5, "S_4" as 𝖲𝗒𝗆𝟦\mathsf{Sym_{4}}, the symmetric group of degree 4, and x as ×\times.
  17. hk (type III only; otherwise null not-defined-for-family): K =𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)= \mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} and H =(𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥))∪{−𝘨:𝘨∈𝖦, det⁡𝘨=−1}= (\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}) \cup \{-\mathsfit{g} : \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G},\ \det \mathsfit{g} = -1\}, the rotation group isomorphic to 𝖦\mathsf{G}, as Schoenflies symbols chosen by size. Axial 𝖦\mathsf{G}: K =𝖣∣𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)∣/2= \mathsf{D}_{\lvert\mathsf{G}\cap\mathsf{SO(3)}\rvert/2} if 𝖦\mathsf{G} has a class with axis_type "perpendicular", else 𝖢∣𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)∣\mathsf{C}_{\lvert\mathsf{G}\cap\mathsf{SO(3)}\rvert}; H =𝖣∣𝖦∣/2= \mathsf{D}_{\lvert\mathsf{G}\rvert/2} if 𝖦\mathsf{G} has a class with axis_type "perpendicular" or "vertical-mirror", else 𝖢∣𝖦∣\mathsf{C}_{\lvert\mathsf{G}\rvert}. 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}: H "O", K "T". 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}: H "D_inf", K "C_inf". These are systematic symbols (K of D1h is "D_1"), not group names.
  18. determinant_character: the id of the first one-dimensional irrep (continuous groups: family), in the record's irrep order, whose character equals det⁡\det on every class; the trivial irrep when 𝖦\mathsf{G} is of type I. In 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} it is the instance reference {"family": id, "index": n}, the instance nn of a family (section 16): {"family": "D", "index": 0} and {"family": "Du", "index": 0}.
  19. gap_smallgroup_id: [order, k], the SmallGroups identifier of 𝖦\mathsf{G} [BEOH24, GAP25]; continuous groups: null continuous-group.
  20. olive: the name of the 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in the classification of Olive [Oli19] of the closed subgroups of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, e.g. "O^-", "Z_10 + Z_2^c". In these names Z_n is the cyclic group of the nn rotations about an axis, Z_2^c the group {𝘌,𝘪}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\} of the central inversion, + the direct product with it ("D_3 + Z_2^c" is 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}}), and a twisted group D_n^v, D_2n^h, Z_2n^-, O^- or O(2)^- the group K∪{−𝘩:𝘩∈H, 𝘩∉K}\texttt{K} \cup \{-\mathsfit{h} : \mathsfit{h} \in \texttt{H},\ \mathsfit{h} \notin \texttt{K}\} of type III, named by its H (hk) with a superscript for its K ("D_3^v" is 𝖢𝟥𝗏\mathsf{C_{3v}}).
  21. ihrig_golubitsky: the name of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in the notation of Ihrig and Golubitsky [IG84], e.g. "O^-", "D_10^z". The parts of these names are those of olive, with (+) (⊕\oplus in the documents) for + and D_n^z, D_2n^d for D_n^v, D_2n^h ("D_3 (+) Z_2^c" is 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}}, "D_3^z" is 𝖢𝟥𝗏\mathsf{C_{3v}}).
  22. altmann_T: the number of the table of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in Altmann and Herzig [AH11]; null not-in-source for the 19 groups that work does not tabulate (C11, C12, C11h, C12h, C11v, C12v, D11, D12, D11h, D12h, D11d, D12d, S22, S24, Cinf, Cinfh, Dinf, SO3, O3).
  23. conway_smith: the algebraic name of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in Conway and Smith [CS03, Table 3.1], in ASCII: +G for a group of rotations, +-G (±𝖦\pm \mathsf{G}) for a group with 𝘪\mathsfit{i}, and the hybrids CC(2n) =+𝖢n−𝖢𝟤n= +\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{n}}} - \mathsf{C_{2\mathnormal{n}}}, CD(n) =+𝖢n−𝖣𝟤n= +\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{n}}} - \mathsf{D_{2\mathnormal{n}}}, DD(2n) =+𝖣𝟤n−𝖣𝟦n= +\mathsf{D_{2\mathnormal{n}}} - \mathsf{D_{4\mathnormal{n}}} and TO =+𝖳−𝖮= +\mathsf{T} - \mathsf{O}, each subscript an order. The table's even and odd entries follow the parity of NN: 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} "+C_N", 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} "+D_2N", 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}} "CD(N)"; 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}} "+-C_N" (NN even) or "CC(2N)" (NN odd); 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} "CC(2N)" (NN even) or "+-C_N" (NN odd); 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} "+-D_2N" (NN even) or "DD(2N)" (NN odd); 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} "DD(2N)" (NN even) or "+-D_2N" (NN odd); with NN in decimal (D3d is "+-D_6", C3v "CD(3)"); T "+T", Th "+-T", Td "TO", O "+O", Oh "+-O", I "+I", Ih "+-I". D1, C1h, D1h and D1d have the names of C2, C1v, C2v and C2h, whose classes they share (section 2.1): "+C_2", "CD(1)", "CD(2)", "+-C_2". Continuous groups: null not-in-source.
  24. orbifold: the orbifold symbol of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in Conway and Smith [CS03, Table 3.1], in ASCII, x for the cross and NN in decimal without separators: 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} "NN", 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} "22N", 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}} "*NN", 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}} "N*", 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} "Nx", 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} "*22N", 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} "2*N" (C3v is "*33", D12d "2*12"); T "332", Th "3*2", Td "*332", O "432", Oh "*432", I "532", Ih "*532". D1, C1h, D1h and D1d have the symbols of C2, C1v, C2v and C2h, and at N=1N = 1 a point of order 1 is left out but in C1's "11": D1 "22", C1v and C1h "*", D1h "*22", D1d "2*", S2 "x". In a symbol * is a mirror and x a cross-cap; a number after * is the order of a corner where mirrors meet and any other number the order of a rotation center off the mirrors ("2*3" of 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}}). Continuous groups: null not-in-source.
  25. product_form: section 12.3.
  26. rotation_subgroup: the Schoenflies symbol of the 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class of 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}, that of product_form.rotation_subgroup (section 12.3): "D_6" for D6h, "C_1" for C1h, "C_2" for D1h and D1d; for a group of type I the symbol of the class of 𝖦\mathsf{G} itself ("C_2" for D1); continuous groups: "C_inf", "D_inf" or "SO(3)".
  27. normalizer: the normalizer 𝖭={𝘨∈𝖮(𝟥):𝘨𝖦𝘨−1=𝖦}\mathsf{N} = \{\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{O(3)} : \mathsfit{g} \mathsf{G} \mathsfit{g}^{-1} = \mathsf{G}\} of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, with schoenflies, the Schoenflies symbol of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class, and index [𝖭:𝖦][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{G}] (null continuous-group for a finite 𝖦\mathsf{G} in a continuous 𝖭\mathsf{N}). In 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, 𝖭(𝖢n)=𝖮(𝟤)\mathsf{N}(\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{n}}}) = \mathsf{O(2)} for n≥2n \ge 2, 𝖭(𝖣n)=𝖣𝟤n\mathsf{N}(\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{n}}}) = \mathsf{D_{2\mathnormal{n}}} for n≥3n \ge 3, 𝖭(𝖣𝟤)=𝖭(𝖳)=𝖭(𝖮)=𝖮\mathsf{N}(\mathsf{D_{2}}) = \mathsf{N}(\mathsf{T}) = \mathsf{N}(\mathsf{O}) = \mathsf{O} and 𝖭(𝖨)=𝖨\mathsf{N}(\mathsf{I}) = \mathsf{I}; in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} the normalizer of a group of type I or II is that of its rotation group times {𝘌,𝘪}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\}, and that of a group of type III the intersection of the normalizers of 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} and of {(det⁡𝘨) 𝘨:𝘨∈𝖦}\{(\det \mathsfit{g})\,\mathsfit{g} : \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G}\} times {𝘌,𝘪}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\}. So: "O(3)" for C1, S2, SO3 and O3; "D_infh" for 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} (N≥2N \ge 2), 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}}, 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} (N≥2N \ge 2), C1v, D1, D1d and the other continuous groups; "D_4h" for D1h; "O_h" for D2, D2h and the cubic groups; "I_h" for I and Ih; "D_2Nh" for 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}} (N≥2N \ge 2), 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} (N≥3N \ge 3), 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} (N≥3N \ge 3) and 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} (N≥2N \ge 2), also where 𝖣𝟤N𝗁\mathsf{D_{2\mathnormal{N}h}} has no record (N>6N > 6).
  28. centralizer: the centralizer 𝖢={𝘨∈𝖮(𝟥):𝘨𝘩=𝘩𝘨 for every 𝘩∈𝖦}\mathsf{C} = \{\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{O(3)} : \mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} = \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g} \text{ for every } \mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{G}\} of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, with schoenflies, the Schoenflies symbol of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class, and normalizer_index [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}], 𝖭\mathsf{N} the normalizer (null continuous-group when it is infinite). The conjugations by the elements of 𝖭\mathsf{N} are the automorphisms of 𝖦\mathsf{G} that conjugation in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} induces, a group isomorphic to 𝖭/𝖢\mathsf{N}/\mathsf{C}: [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] is their number, and the monomorphism.count of every embedding whose subgroup is 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 12.1). As 𝘪\mathsfit{i} commutes with every element, 𝖢\mathsf{C} is the centralizer in 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} of the rotation group {(det⁡𝘨) 𝘨:𝘨∈𝖦}\{(\det \mathsfit{g})\,\mathsfit{g} : \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G}\} times {𝘌,𝘪}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\}. 2.3 Hermann–Mauguin symbols ¶

In a symbol a number nn is an nn-fold rotation axis, a bar over it an nn-fold rotoinversion axis (of the rotation by 2π/n2\pi/n combined with 𝘪\mathsfit{i}), m a mirror plane and nn/m an nn-fold axis with a mirror perpendicular to it. Axial groups, by rule. The axis orders NN and 2N2N are written in decimal. A bar is a leading - and covers the axis order that follows it: the whole number (-10m2 is 10‾m2\overline{10}m2), except in -42m, -2N2N2m and -43m, where the final 2 or 3 is a symbol of its own (-42m is 4ˉ2m\bar{4}2m, -122m is 12‾2m\overline{12}2m). Multi-digit axis orders are written without separators, so a symbol is read together with family.N.
