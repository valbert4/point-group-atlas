2 Names and identification ¶

2.1 Names ¶

The name of a group is its Schoenflies symbol group.schoenflies without any _ or parenthesis:

  1. C1 … C12, C1h … C12h, C1v … C12v, D1 … D12, D1h … D12h, D1d … D12d, S2, S4, …, S24.
  2. T, Th, Td, O, Oh, I, Ih.
  3. Cinf, Cinfh, Cinfv, Dinf, Dinfh.
  4. SO3 and O3 ("SO(3)", "O(3)").

The 98 names are distinct, also ignoring case. They name every file and are the value of every field that names another group (o3_class, computed_from.group, the node ids and aliases of point_groups.json, the group of a bases file).

The N=1N = 1 groups keep their systematic names:

  1. C1h ={𝘌,σ𝘩}= \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{\sigma_{h}}\} and C1v ={𝘌,σ𝘷(0)}= \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{\sigma_{v}}(0)\} are both 𝖢𝗌\mathsf{C_{s}}.
  2. D1 ={𝘌,𝘊𝟤𝘹}= \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{C_{2x}}\} is 𝖢𝟤\mathsf{C_{2}} with its axis along xx.
  3. D1h is 𝖢𝟤𝗏\mathsf{C_{2v}} and D1d is 𝖢𝟤𝗁\mathsf{C_{2h}}, each with its 2-fold axis along xx.
  4. S2 ={𝘌,𝘪}= \{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\} is 𝖢𝗂\mathsf{C_{i}}.

The records of one 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class name each other in the field o3_conjugates, and each lists the other names of the class in the field alternative_names (section 2.2). The documents write these other names after ≅\cong, which joins two names of one class (𝖢𝟤𝗏≅𝖣𝟣𝗁\mathsf{C_{2v}} \cong \mathsf{D_{1h}}).

2.2 The group block ¶

Six fields name 𝖦\mathsf{G} in an outside scheme (gap_smallgroup_id, olive, ihrig_golubitsky, altmann_T, conway_smith and orbifold), and two name its irreps (bethe and bilbao, section 6.5). Their citations are entries of REFERENCES.pdf. Every other field follows from the record by the rule given.

  1. schoenflies: the Schoenflies symbol in ASCII, _ before the subscript ("C_3v", "S_4", "D_1h", "T_d", "C_inf", "D_infh"). The symbols "T", "O", "I" have no subscript, and 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} have "SO(3)" and "O(3)" (their Schoenflies symbols 𝖪\mathsf{K} and 𝖪𝗁\mathsf{K_{h}} are in alternative_names).
  2. schoenflies_unicode (continuous groups): schoenflies with inf written ∞ ("C_∞v").

  3. o3_conjugates: the names of the other records whose groups are conjugate to 𝖦\mathsf{G} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, the same group in another orientation (section 2.1 and the classes of section 15.2):

    1. C1h ["C1v"] and C1v ["C1h"].
    2. D1 ["C2"] and C2 ["D1"].
    3. D1h ["C2v"] and C2v ["D1h"].
    4. D1d ["C2h"] and C2h ["D1d"].
    5. [] for every other group.
  4. hermann_mauguin: the short Hermann–Mauguin symbol in ASCII (section 2.3). For the 32 crystallographic classes the symbol is the string that spglib returns for the point group of a crystal [TST24].

  5. alternative_names: the other symbols of the 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, each an object {"schoenflies": S, "source": null}. Its source is null for a name of the Schoenflies system and the key of the entry of references.bib for an author's own symbol (no record has one). No alternative_names lists the symbol D_nv that [Gri61, section 6.3] writes for 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}}. For odd NN the group 𝖲𝟤N=𝖢N×𝖢𝗂\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} = \mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} \times \mathsf{C_{i}} (section 3) is also written 𝖢N𝗂\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}i}}. The symbols are the conventional name of the class when it is not schoenflies (section 2.1), 𝖢N𝗂\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}i}} for odd N≥3N \ge 3, then, without repeats, the schoenflies of the records of o3_conjugates:

    1. C1h: "C_s", "C_1v".
    2. C1v: "C_s", "C_1h".
    3. D1: "C_2".
    4. C2: "D_1".
    5. D1h: "C_2v".
    6. C2v: "D_1h".
    7. D1d: "C_2h".
    8. C2h: "D_1d".
    9. S2: "C_i".
    10. S6: "C_3i".
    11. S10: "C_5i".
    12. S14: "C_7i".
    13. S18: "C_9i".
    14. S22: "C_11i".
    15. 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}}, 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}: one entry each, given below.
    16. [] for every other group.

    The entry of a continuous group also has schoenflies_unicode. Since 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} is also written 𝖲∞\mathsf{S_{\infty}}, the closure of 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} as N→∞N \to \infty (𝖢∞∪σ𝘩𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} \cup \mathsfit{\sigma_{h}} \mathsf{C_{\infty}}, as for 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}}), its entry also has limit_of. The entries of 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}}, 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} are, in this order:

    {"schoenflies": "S_inf", "source": null, "schoenflies_unicode": "S_\u221e",
 "limit_of": {"family": "S2n"}}
{"schoenflies": "K", "source": null, "schoenflies_unicode": "K"}
{"schoenflies": "K_h", "source": null, "schoenflies_unicode": "K_h"}
  6. type: "I" if 𝖦⊂𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \subset \mathsf{SO(3)}, "II" if 𝘪∈𝖦\mathsfit{i} \in \mathsf{G} and "III" otherwise.
  7. order: ∣𝖦∣\lvert \mathsf{G}\rvert, or null continuous-group for a continuous group.
  8. family: {"name": F, "N": N} for an axial group, or null not-defined-for-family for the exceptional and continuous groups. The name gives FF (one of Cn, Cnh, Cnv, Dn, Dnh, Dnd, S2n) and NN. For 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}}, NN is half the subscript (S4 is {"name": "S2n", "N": 2}).
  9. limit_of (continuous groups): {"family": F}, the axial family whose closure as N→∞N \to \infty is 𝖦\mathsf{G} (𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} Cn, 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} Cnh, 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} Cnv, 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} Dn, 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}} Dnh), or null not-defined-for-family otherwise.
  10. principal_axis: [0, 0, 1] for the axial groups and the continuous groups other than 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, or null not-defined-for-family otherwise.
  11. contains_inversion: whether 𝘪∈𝖦\mathsfit{i} \in \mathsf{G}.
  12. contains_sigma_h: whether σ𝘻∈𝖦\mathsfit{\sigma_{z}} \in \mathsf{G}, in the frame of section 3. For an axial group σ𝘻\mathsfit{\sigma_{z}} is the σ𝘩\mathsfit{\sigma_{h}} of the principal axis. For the exceptional groups it is a statement about the frame: true for 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}, 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}, whose coordinate planes are mirror planes, false for 𝖳\mathsf{T}, 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, 𝖮\mathsf{O} and 𝖨\mathsf{I}.

  13. abstract_type: the isomorphism class (for a continuous group, the table of section 12.3):

    1. Finite abelian 𝖦\mathsf{G} (every class has one element): its invariant factors, largest first, "Z_a x Z_b". Each factor divides the one before, the trivial group is "Z_1" and D2h is "Z_2 x Z_2 x Z_2".
    2. Otherwise, if some element has order ∣𝖦∣/2\lvert \mathsf{G}\rvert/2: "Dih(n)", the dihedral group of order n=∣𝖦∣n = \lvert \mathsf{G}\rvert.
    3. Otherwise, if 𝘪∈𝖦\mathsfit{i} \in \mathsf{G}: "X x Z_2". Here X=X = "Dih(n/2)" if some element of 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} has order ∣𝖦∣/4\lvert \mathsf{G}\rvert/4, otherwise X=X = "A_4", "S_4", "A_5" for ∣𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)∣=12,24,60\lvert \mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}\rvert = 12, 24, 60.
    4. Otherwise: "A_4" (𝖳\mathsf{T}), "S_4" (𝖮\mathsf{O} and 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}) or "A_5" (𝖨\mathsf{I}) for ∣𝖦∣=12,24,60\lvert \mathsf{G}\rvert = 12, 24, 60.

    The documents set "Z_n" as 𝖹n\mathsf{Z_{\mathnormal{n}}} (the cyclic group of order nn) and "Dih(n)" as 𝖣𝗂𝗁n\mathsf{Dih_{\mathnormal{n}}}. They set "A_4" and "A_5" as 𝖠𝗅𝗍𝟦\mathsf{Alt_{4}} and 𝖠𝗅𝗍𝟧\mathsf{Alt_{5}} (the alternating groups of degree 4 and 5), "S_4" as 𝖲𝗒𝗆𝟦\mathsf{Sym_{4}} (the symmetric group of degree 4) and x as ×\times.

  14. hk (type III only, otherwise null not-defined-for-family): K =𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)= \mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} and H =(𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥))∪{−𝘨:𝘨∈𝖦, det⁡𝘨=−1}= (\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}) \cup \{-\mathsfit{g} : \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G},\ \det \mathsfit{g} = -1\}, the rotation group isomorphic to 𝖦\mathsf{G}, as Schoenflies symbols chosen by size. For an axial 𝖦\mathsf{G}, K =𝖣∣𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)∣/2= \mathsf{D}_{\lvert\mathsf{G}\cap\mathsf{SO(3)}\rvert/2} if 𝖦\mathsf{G} has a class with axis_type "perpendicular" and 𝖢∣𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)∣\mathsf{C}_{\lvert\mathsf{G}\cap\mathsf{SO(3)}\rvert} otherwise, and H =𝖣∣𝖦∣/2= \mathsf{D}_{\lvert\mathsf{G}\rvert/2} if 𝖦\mathsf{G} has a class with axis_type "perpendicular" or "vertical-mirror" and 𝖢∣𝖦∣\mathsf{C}_{\lvert\mathsf{G}\rvert} otherwise. In 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}} H and K are "O" and "T", and in 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} they are "D_inf" and "C_inf". These systematic symbols (K of D1h is "D_1") are not group names.
  15. determinant_character: the id of the first one-dimensional irrep (for a continuous group, family), in the record's irrep order, whose character equals det⁡\det on every class (the trivial irrep when 𝖦\mathsf{G} is of type I). In 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} it is the instance reference {"family": id, "index": n}, the instance nn of a family (section 16): {"family": "D", "index": 0} and {"family": "Du", "index": 0}.
  16. product_form: section 12.3.
  17. rotation_subgroup: the Schoenflies symbol of the 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class of 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}, that of product_form.rotation_subgroup (section 12.3): "D_6" for D6h, "C_1" for C1h and "C_2" for D1h and D1d. For a group of type I it is the symbol of the class of 𝖦\mathsf{G} itself ("C_2" for D1). A continuous group has "C_inf", "D_inf" or "SO(3)".

  18. normalizer: the normalizer 𝖭={𝘨∈𝖮(𝟥):𝘨𝖦𝘨−1=𝖦}\mathsf{N} = \{\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{O(3)} : \mathsfit{g} \mathsf{G} \mathsfit{g}^{-1} = \mathsf{G}\}, with schoenflies, the Schoenflies symbol of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class, and index [𝖭:𝖦][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{G}] (null continuous-group for a finite 𝖦\mathsf{G} in a continuous 𝖭\mathsf{N}). In 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, 𝖭(𝖢n)=𝖮(𝟤)\mathsf{N}(\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{n}}}) = \mathsf{O(2)} for n≥2n \ge 2, 𝖭(𝖣n)=𝖣𝟤n\mathsf{N}(\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{n}}}) = \mathsf{D_{2\mathnormal{n}}} for n≥3n \ge 3, 𝖭(𝖣𝟤)=𝖭(𝖳)=𝖭(𝖮)=𝖮\mathsf{N}(\mathsf{D_{2}}) = \mathsf{N}(\mathsf{T}) = \mathsf{N}(\mathsf{O}) = \mathsf{O} and 𝖭(𝖨)=𝖨\mathsf{N}(\mathsf{I}) = \mathsf{I}. In 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} the normalizer of a group of type I or II is that of its rotation group times {𝘌,𝘪}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\}. For a group of type III it is the intersection of the normalizers of 𝖦∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} and of {(det⁡𝘨) 𝘨:𝘨∈𝖦}\{(\det \mathsfit{g})\,\mathsfit{g} : \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G}\} times {𝘌,𝘪}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\}. Thus schoenflies takes these values:

    1. "O(3)" for C1, S2, SO3 and O3.
    2. "D_infh" for 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} (N≥2N \ge 2), 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}}, 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} (N≥2N \ge 2), C1v, D1, D1d and the other continuous groups.
    3. "D_4h" for D1h.
    4. "O_h" for D2, D2h and the cubic groups.
    5. "I_h" for I and Ih.
    6. "D_2Nh" for 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}} (N≥2N \ge 2), 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} (N≥3N \ge 3), 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} (N≥3N \ge 3) and 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} (N≥2N \ge 2), also where 𝖣𝟤N𝗁\mathsf{D_{2\mathnormal{N}h}} has no record (N>6N > 6).

  19. centralizer: the centralizer 𝖢={𝘨∈𝖮(𝟥):𝘨𝘩=𝘩𝘨 for every 𝘩∈𝖦}\mathsf{C} = \{\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{O(3)} : \mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} = \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g} \text{ for every } \mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{G}\}, with schoenflies, the Schoenflies symbol of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class, and normalizer_index [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] (null continuous-group when infinite). The conjugations by the elements of 𝖭\mathsf{N} are the automorphisms of 𝖦\mathsf{G} induced by conjugation in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, a group isomorphic to 𝖭/𝖢\mathsf{N}/\mathsf{C}. Their number is [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}], also the monomorphism.count of every embedding whose subgroup is 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 12.1). As 𝘪\mathsfit{i} commutes with every element, 𝖢\mathsf{C} is the centralizer in 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} of the rotation group {(det⁡𝘨) 𝘨:𝘨∈𝖦}\{(\det \mathsfit{g})\,\mathsfit{g} : \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G}\} times {𝘌,𝘪}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\}. Thus the fields take these values:

    1. "O(3)", index 1, for C1 and S2.
    2. "D_infh", index 1, for C2, C1h, C1v, C2h, D1 and D1d.
    3. "C_infh", index 2, for 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}} (N≥3N \ge 3), 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} (N≥2N \ge 2), Cinf and Cinfh.
    4. "D_2h", index 2 for C2v and D1h and 6 for D2 and D2h.
    5. "C_2h", index 2N2N, for 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}, 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} (N≥3N \ge 3) and 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} (N≥2N \ge 2), and of infinite index for Cinfv, Dinf and Dinfh.
    6. "C_i", index 24 for the cubic groups and 60 for I and Ih, and of infinite index for SO3 and O3.

  20. center: the center 𝖹(𝖦)={𝘩∈𝖦:𝘩𝘨=𝘨𝘩 for every 𝘨∈𝖦}\mathsf{Z}(\mathsf{G}) = \{\mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{G} : \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g} = \mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} \text{ for every } \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G}\}, with these fields:

    1. schoenflies: the Schoenflies symbol of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class (C_s for 𝖢𝟣𝗏\mathsf{C_{1v}} and C_i for 𝖲𝟤\mathsf{S_{2}}).
    2. o3_class: the name of the record of its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class (section 12.1).
    3. order and index [𝖦:𝖹(𝖦)][\mathsf{G} : \mathsf{Z}(\mathsf{G})], null continuous-group when infinite.
    4. node (a finite 𝖦\mathsf{G}): its node of subgroup_lattice (section 15.1).

    An abelian group (𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}}, 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖢𝟤𝗏\mathsf{C_{2v}}, 𝖣𝟤\mathsf{D_{2}}, 𝖣𝟤𝗁\mathsf{D_{2h}}, the N=1N = 1 groups, 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} and 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}}) is its own center. Other groups have these centers:

    1. 𝖢𝟣\mathsf{C_{1}} for 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}} and 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} with NN odd, 𝖳\mathsf{T}, 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, 𝖮\mathsf{O}, 𝖨\mathsf{I} and 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}.
    2. 𝖢𝟤\mathsf{C_{2}}, the binary rotation about the principal axis, for 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}} and 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} with N≥4N \ge 4 even, 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} with NN even, 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} and 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}.
    3. 𝖢𝗌\mathsf{C_{s}}, {𝘌,σ𝘩}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{\sigma_{h}}\}, for 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} with NN odd.
    4. 𝖢𝟤𝗁\mathsf{C_{2h}} for 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} with N≥4N \ge 4 even and 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}.
    5. 𝖢𝗂\mathsf{C_{i}} for 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} with NN odd, 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}, 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}}, 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}.

  21. commutator_subgroup: the commutator subgroup 𝖦′=[𝖦,𝖦]\mathsf{G}' = [\mathsf{G}, \mathsf{G}], generated by the commutators 𝘨𝘩𝘨−1𝘩−1\mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g}^{-1} \mathsfit{h}^{-1}, with the same fields. It takes these values:

    1. 𝖢𝟣\mathsf{C_{1}} for an abelian group.
    2. 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} for 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}, 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} with NN odd and for 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}}.
    3. 𝖢N/𝟤\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}/2}} for 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}, 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} with NN even.
    4. 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} for 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} and 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}.
    5. 𝖣𝟤\mathsf{D_{2}} for 𝖳\mathsf{T} and 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}.
    6. 𝖳\mathsf{T} for 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, 𝖮\mathsf{O} and 𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}}.
    7. 𝖨\mathsf{I} for 𝖨\mathsf{I} and 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}.
    8. 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} for 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}.

    The quotient 𝖦/𝖦′\mathsf{G}/\mathsf{G}' is abelian, and for a finite 𝖦\mathsf{G} the index [𝖦:𝖦′][\mathsf{G} : \mathsf{G}'] is the number of one-dimensional irreps.

  22. schur_multiplier (finite groups): the Schur multiplier of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, the abelian group of the classes of factor systems of its projective representations, with these fields:

    1. invariants: its invariant factors, largest first, each dividing the one before ([] when it is trivial).
    2. order: their product, the number of classes.
    3. spinor_class: the class of the spinor irreps of the preimage of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 23.1), "trivial" when they are the irreps of 𝖦\mathsf{G} times one spinor irrep of dimension 1 and "nontrivial" otherwise.
    4. other_classes: the number of classes other than the class of the irreps and the class of the spinor irreps.

  23. automorphisms (finite groups): the automorphisms of 𝖦\mathsf{G} as an abstract group, with these fields:

    1. order: their number.
    2. inner_order: the number [𝖦:𝖹(𝖦)][\mathsf{G} : \mathsf{Z}(\mathsf{G})] of inner automorphisms, the conjugations by the elements of 𝖦\mathsf{G}.
    3. outer_order: order divided by inner_order.
    4. induced_order: the number [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] of automorphisms that conjugation in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} induces (centralizer).
    5. class_orbits and irrep_orbits: the orbits of the automorphisms on the class ids and on the irrep ids. An automorphism carries the irrep of character χ\chi (section 6) to the irrep whose character at the image of 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is χ(𝘨)\chi(\mathsfit{g}). Each orbit lists its ids in the record's order, and the orbits come in the order of their first ids.
    6. induced_class_orbits and induced_irrep_orbits: the orbits of the induced automorphisms, in the same form.
  24. laue_class: the group generated by 𝖦\mathsf{G} and 𝘪\mathsfit{i}, written as in normalizer: schoenflies, and index, 1 when 𝘪∈𝖦\mathsfit{i} \in \mathsf{G} and 2 otherwise. It may have no record (𝖢𝟤𝟤𝗁\mathsf{C_{22h}}, that of 𝖢𝟣𝟣𝗁\mathsf{C_{11h}}).
  25. crystallographic: whether every element has order 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6 (false for the continuous groups).
  26. polar: whether 𝖦\mathsf{G} fixes a nonzero vector, so that polar-1 (section 13) carries its trivial irrep: the groups 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}, 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} and 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, and C1h, D1 and D1h (section 2.1). Crystallographers call a group chiral, or enantiomorphic, when its type is I, and centrosymmetric when contains_inversion is true.

  27. crystal_family, crystal_system and lattices, for a crystallographic group (otherwise null not-defined-for-family), by its Laue class [Aro16]:

    1. crystal_family: "triclinic", "monoclinic", "orthorhombic", "tetragonal", "hexagonal" or "cubic".
    2. crystal_system: the family with "hexagonal" split into "trigonal" (the Laue classes 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}} and 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}}) and "hexagonal".
    3. lattices[]: the Bravais types of the lattices of its crystals. Each type has bravais, its symbol ("aP", "mP", "mS", "oP", "oS", "oI", "oF", "tP", "tI", "hR", "hP", "cP", "cI", "cF"), and holohedry, the Schoenflies symbol of the point group of the lattice. The types are those of the group's system, and for a trigonal group "hR" (holohedry 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}}) and "hP" (𝖣𝟨𝗁\mathsf{D_{6h}}).

  28. allowed_properties[]: the tensor properties of section 13.2 allowed by 𝖦\mathsf{G}, in the order of its table, or empty when 𝖦\mathsf{G} allows none of them (𝖮𝗁\mathsf{O_{h}}, 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}). Each has these fields:

    1. jahn: the Jahn symbol of the tensor ("V", "eV", "e[V2]", "{V2}V", "V[V2]", "[V3]", "V3", "e[V2]V", "e[V3]").
    2. spaces: the ids of the spaces of cartesian_tensors whose sum is the tensor.
    3. properties: its names.
  29. gap_smallgroup_id: [order, k], the SmallGroups identifier of 𝖦\mathsf{G} [BEOH24, GAP25], or null continuous-group for a continuous group.
  30. olive: the name of the 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in the classification of Olive [Oli19] of the closed subgroups of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, for example "O^-", "Z_10 + Z_2^c". In these names Z_n is the cyclic group of the nn rotations about an axis, Z_2^c is the group {𝘌,𝘪}\{\mathsfit{E}, \mathsfit{i}\} of the central inversion, and + is the direct product with it ("D_3 + Z_2^c" is 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}}). A twisted group D_n^v, D_2n^h, Z_2n^-, O^- or O(2)^- is the group K∪{−𝘩:𝘩∈H, 𝘩∉K}\texttt{K} \cup \{-\mathsfit{h} : \mathsfit{h} \in \texttt{H},\ \mathsfit{h} \notin \texttt{K}\} of type III, named by its H (hk) with a superscript for its K ("D_3^v" is 𝖢𝟥𝗏\mathsf{C_{3v}}).
  31. ihrig_golubitsky: the name of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in the notation of Ihrig and Golubitsky [IG84], for example "O^-", "D_10^z". The parts of these names are those of olive, with (+) (⊕\oplus in the documents) for +, D_n^z for D_n^v and D_2n^d for D_2n^h ("D_3 (+) Z_2^c" is 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}}, "D_3^z" is 𝖢𝟥𝗏\mathsf{C_{3v}}).
  32. altmann_T: the number of the table of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in Altmann and Herzig [AH11], or null not-in-source for the 19 groups not tabulated in that work (C11, C12, C11h, C12h, C11v, C12v, D11, D12, D11h, D12h, D11d, D12d, S22, S24, Cinf, Cinfh, Dinf, SO3, O3).

  33. conway_smith: the algebraic name of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in Conway and Smith [CS03, Table 3.1], in ASCII. Its forms are +G for a group of type I, +-G (±𝖦\pm \mathsf{G}) for a group with 𝘪\mathsfit{i}, and the hybrids CC(2n) =+𝖢n−𝖢𝟤n= +\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{n}}} - \mathsf{C_{2\mathnormal{n}}}, CD(n) =+𝖢n−𝖣𝟤n= +\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{n}}} - \mathsf{D_{2\mathnormal{n}}}, DD(2n) =+𝖣𝟤n−𝖣𝟦n= +\mathsf{D_{2\mathnormal{n}}} - \mathsf{D_{4\mathnormal{n}}} and TO =+𝖳−𝖮= +\mathsf{T} - \mathsf{O}, each subscript an order. The table's even and odd entries follow the parity of NN, which is in decimal (D3d is "+-D_6", C3v "CD(3)"):

    1. 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} "+C_N", 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}} "+D_2N", 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}} "CD(N)".
    2. 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}} "+-C_N" (NN even) or "CC(2N)" (NN odd).
    3. 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} "CC(2N)" (NN even) or "+-C_N" (NN odd).
    4. 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} "+-D_2N" (NN even) or "DD(2N)" (NN odd).
    5. 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} "DD(2N)" (NN even) or "+-D_2N" (NN odd).

  34. orbifold: the orbifold symbol of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in Conway and Smith [CS03, Table 3.1], in ASCII, x for the cross and NN in decimal without separators:

    1. 2.3 Hermann–Mauguin symbols ¶

The Hermann–Mauguin symbol of 𝖦\mathsf{G} depends only on the 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, whatever the orientation of 𝖦\mathsf{G} in the frame of section 3.

In a symbol a number nn is an nn-fold rotation axis, and a bar over it is an nn-fold rotoinversion axis (of the rotation by 2π/n2\pi/n combined with 𝘪\mathsfit{i}). Here m is a mirror plane, and nn/m is an nn-fold axis with a mirror perpendicular to it.

The axial groups have symbols by rule, with the axis orders NN and 2N2N in decimal. A bar is a leading - and covers the whole axis order that follows it (-10m2 is 10‾m2\overline{10}m2). However, in -42m, -2N2N2m and -43m the final 2 or 3 is a symbol of its own (-42m is 4ˉ2m\bar{4}2m, -122m is 12‾2m\overline{12}2m).
