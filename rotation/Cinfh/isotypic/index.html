5.3 Isotypic components ¶

Data: cartesian_tensors in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 13. A row is an isotypic component, with the Jahn symbol of its space (name, as in the tensor spaces), its irrep, the multiplicity of the irrep in the space (mult.) and the component’s dimension (dim). It also gives the field of the coefficients of its basis, rational or cyclotomic, and its reduced row echelon basis. A basis tensor of the elastic, piezoelectric, electrogyration or photoelastic tensors or of the third-harmonic susceptibilities is a combination of their coordinates, in this order, c11,…,c66c_{11}, \dots, c_{66}, d11,…,d36d_{11}, \dots, d_{36}, g11,…,g63\mathnormal{g}_{11}, \dots, \mathnormal{g}_{63}, p11,…,p66p_{11}, \dots, p_{66} or χ1111(3),…,χ3333(3)\chi^{(3)}_{1111}, \dots, \chi^{(3)}_{3333} (Conventions, section 13). Each coordinate stands for the tensor with that coordinate 1 and every other coordinate 0. The symmetric axial tensors of ranks 2 and 3, the electrogyration and photoelastic tensors and the third-harmonic susceptibilities have only the trivial irrep’s row, where they contain that irrep. The general polar tensors of rank 4 have none.
