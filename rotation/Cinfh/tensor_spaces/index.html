5.2 Tensor spaces ¶

Data: cartesian_tensors, character_functions in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: section 13. A row is a space of Cartesian tensors: name is its Jahn symbol (V a polar vector, e an axial factor, a power the number of factors, brackets their symmetrized product), which names the space in the isotypic components too, and description the space in words: the symmetric polar tensors of a rank, the homogeneous polynomials of that degree; the axial vectors and the symmetric axial tensors of ranks 2 (optical activity) and 3; the elastic stiffness tensors, symmetric in their first two indices, in their last two and under the exchange of the two pairs; the piezoelectric tensors, symmetric in their last two indices; the electrogyration tensors, axial, symmetric in their first two indices; the photoelastic tensors, symmetric in their first two and in their last two indices (also electrostriction and the quadratic electro-optic effect); the third-harmonic susceptibilities, symmetric in their last three indices; or the general polar tensors of rank 4, without symmetry; dim is its dimension and decomposition the irreps of 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} that it carries, with their multiplicities.
