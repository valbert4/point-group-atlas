Defines
  • ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle the ket of angular momentum JJ and zz component mm, a spherical harmonic
  • eme_m the coefficient of the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle in a basis vector, its amplitude being emFm/tote_m\sqrt{F_m/\mathrm{tot}}
  • FmF_m the weight (J+m)! (J−m)!(J + m)!\,(J - m)! of the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle
  • tot\mathrm{tot} the normalization ∑m∣em∣2Fm\sum_m \lvert e_m\rvert^2 F_m of a basis vector
  • vv a symmetry-adapted vector, a combination of kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle; vμv_\mu, the partner μ\mu of a block

so a single ket has amplitude ±1\pm1; eme_m is +1+1, except that it may be −1-1 on the second partner of a two-dimensional block. The subgroup 𝖠=𝖣∞∩𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{A} = \mathsf{D_{\infty}}\cap\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} acts diagonally on the kets, on ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle by the character χ𝖠(m)\chi_{\mathsf{A}}(m); [𝖣∞:𝖠]=2[\mathsf{D_{\infty}} : \mathsf{A}] = 2, and the flip 𝘊𝟤𝘹\mathsfit{C_{2x}} sends mm to −m-m. A two-dimensional block has e=+1e = +1 on partner 1 and e=c=±1e = c = \pm1 on partner 2, by the rule of Conventions, section 14.1; blocks are ordered by JJ, then ∣m∣|m|. The first partner of a two-dimensional block is v1=∣ mJ⟩v_1 = \bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle with m=−nm = -n and the second v2=c ∣ −mJ⟩v_2 = c\,\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle with c=(−1)J+δc = (-1)^{J+\delta} and δ\delta the exponent of zz in the rotation-diagonal basis of the block’s family (section 2.2.2).
