Conventions: section 16.1. The closed subgroups of 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} other than 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, up to conjugacy in 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, are its continuous subgroups and, for every N≥1N \ge 1, one subgroup per member of each family of section 3.1: the group of that member’s document, in its own frame. Members of different families are not conjugate in 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, also where they lie in one 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} class, such as 𝖣𝟣\mathsf{D_{1}} and 𝖢𝟤\mathsf{C_{2}} (a binary axis perpendicular to zz and along zz). The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H} as a sum of the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}.

Continuous subgroups. The row subgrp gives each continuous subgroup 𝖧\mathsf{H} and the row index its index [𝖣∞:𝖧][\mathsf{D_{\infty}} : \mathsf{H}]. The intertwiners, in the rotation-diagonal basis (∣e1⟩,∣e2⟩)(\lvert e_1\rangle, \lvert e_2\rangle) of a family of dimension 2, are the identity, except: to 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, the exchange of ∣e1⟩\lvert e_1\rangle and ∣e2⟩\lvert e_2\rangle of En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n} (subduction_subgroups.continuous[]).

For each family, the rule gives the restriction for every NN as the label of Conventions, section 6.1, of the irrep with χ(𝘨p)=ζNpq\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = \zeta_{N_p}^{q} (the pair ζNp±q\zeta_{N_p}^{\pm q} for an En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n), ζNp=e2πi/Np\zeta_{N_p} = \mathrm{e}^{2\pi\mathrm{i}/N_p}, where 𝘨p=𝘊∞(2π/N)\mathsfit{g}_p = \mathsfit{C_\infty}(2\pi/N) and Np=NN_p = N; for N=1N = 1 and 22 the labels take the names of Conventions, section 6.3. The tables list N≤12N \le 12 and n≤6n \le 6; the restriction of the instance nn depends on nn only modulo the period LL of the member, and the JSON lists n=1,2,…,Ln = 1, 2, \dots, L with the intertwiners (subduction_subgroups.finite[]). The record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} maps onto the subgroup in [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] ways, 𝘩↦𝘨𝘩𝘨−1\mathsfit{h} \mapsto \mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g}^{-1} (𝘩∈𝖧\mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{H}) for the 𝘨∈𝖮(𝟥)\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{O(3)} that carry 𝖧\mathsf{H} to it, 𝖭\mathsf{N} and 𝖢\mathsf{C} the normalizer and the centralizer of 𝖧\mathsf{H} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}; the row centralizer of section Names and structure of the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H} gives 𝖢\mathsf{C} and this index (the index in the row normalizer is [𝖭:𝖧][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{H}]). Two maps related by a conjugation in 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} give the same restriction, so the maps fall into [𝖭:𝖢]/[𝖭 𝖣∞:𝖢 𝖣∞][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}]/[\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{D_{\infty}}} : \mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{D_{\infty}}}] classes, 𝖭 𝖣∞\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{D_{\infty}}} and 𝖢 𝖣∞\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{D_{\infty}}} the normalizer and the centralizer of the subgroup in 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} (the table of section 3.1). A subgroup whose classes give different tables has one column per table, its settings: 1 for the stored monomorphism (monomorphism) and 2, 3, … for the alternative_settings, each with the images of the generators of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (setting). The row images gives, under each setting, each generator of 𝖧\mathsf{H} whose image differs between the settings, in their order, by the symbol of its class (Conventions, section 5.1), mapped to its image, the element of its component of the class space at its angle; where two generators share a symbol, a superscript number in brackets gives the generator’s index in the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. Each other column under a subgroup is column 1 with each irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖧\mathsf{H} replaced by Λ\Lambda composed with one automorphism of 𝖧\mathsf{H} that a conjugation in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} induces and none in 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} does (Conventions, section 12.1).
