Conventions: section 15. The diagram shows the conjugacy classes of subgroups, each joined to the classes of its maximal subgroups, the orders increasing upward; a normal subgroup is framed, and ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of conjugate subgroups. A gray letter after a symbol tells apart the classes of subgroups with that symbol, which are not conjugate in 𝖣𝟣𝟣𝗁\mathsf{D_{11h}}; the table and the subduction tables of section 3.2 use the same letters. The table lists the classes by order, those of one symbol together; subgrp is the representative 𝖧\mathsf{H} of a class of subgroups, order its order, conjugates the number of subgroups in the class and normal whether 𝖧\mathsf{H} is normal; classes lists the classes of 𝖣𝟣𝟣𝗁\mathsf{D_{11h}}, other than 𝘌\mathsfit{E}, that 𝖧\mathsf{H} meets (a dash for none), and 𝖭 𝖣𝟣𝟣𝗁(𝖧)\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{D_{11h}}}(\mathsf{H}) and 𝖢 𝖣𝟣𝟣𝗁(𝖧)\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{D_{11h}}}(\mathsf{H}) are the classes of its normalizer and its centralizer in 𝖣𝟣𝟣𝗁\mathsf{D_{11h}} (normalizer, centralizer); a subgroup’s other names in its class of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} follow ≅\cong (Conventions, section 2.1).
