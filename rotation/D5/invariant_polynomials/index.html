6.4 Invariant polynomials ¶

Data: invariants in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 13.1. The invariants are the polynomials in xx, yy, zz that take the same value at 𝐫\mathbf{r} and at 𝐫 P(𝘨)\mathbf{r}\,P(\mathsfit{g}) for every point 𝐫\mathbf{r} and every element 𝘨\mathsfit{g}. They form a ring with the Hilbert series HA1(t)/(1−t2)=(1+t6)/((1−t2)(1−t2)(1−t5))H_{\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_{1}}(t)/(1-t^{2}) = (1 + t^{6})/((1-t^{2})(1-t^{2})(1-t^{5})), whose coefficient of a power of tt is the number of linearly independent invariants of that degree. A row is an invariant of a minimal generating set of the ring (Conventions, section 13.1), with its degree, the invariant (polynomial) and its role, primary or secondary. Every invariant is in one way a sum of 1 and the secondary invariants, each times a polynomial in the primary invariants.
