7.1 Subduction from 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} ¶

Data: subduction_unitary in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: section 19. A level of 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}} is a real irrep Γ\Gamma of dimension N≥2N \ge 2: a degenerate mode of vibration, or a set of degenerate orbitals. Its matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) lie in 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})}, and bΛ(λ)b_\Lambda(\lambda) is the multiplicity of the irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}} in the irrep 𝕊λ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda} of 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} of highest weight λ=[λ1,λ2,… ]\lambda = [\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots], restricted along DΓD^{\Gamma}: the branching rule 𝖴(N)⊃𝖳𝗁\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} \supset \mathsf{T_{h}}. The quanta of a degenerate mode fill the one-row shapes 𝕊[v]Γ=Sym⁡vΓ\mathbb{S}_{[v]}\Gamma = \operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma (section 7.2); nn electrons of spin SS fill the two-column shapes λ=[2n/2−S,12S]\lambda = [2^{n/2-S}, 1^{2S}] (section 7.4). In the table, level is the irrep Γ\Gamma, denominator the product ∏k(1−tak)\prod_k (1 - t^{a_k}) under the generating functions of its overtones, (7.2.1), and chain the subgroups through which 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} restricts to 𝖳𝗁\mathsf{T_{h}}, down to the image of Γ\Gamma: the group that the matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) form, isomorphic to 𝖳𝗁/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{T_{h}}/\ker\Gamma (the column kernel of the table of irreps, section 2.1). The image leaves a quadratic form invariant, and 𝖮(N)\mathsf{O(\mathnormal{N})} is the orthogonal group of that form. The levels go by their dimension.
