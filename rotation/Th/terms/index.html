Conventions: section 19. Without spin–orbit coupling, the orbital states of nn electrons (0≤n≤2N0 \le n \le 2N) of spin SS in a level Γ\Gamma of section 7.1 lie in 𝕊λΓ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda}\Gamma when λ=[2n/2−S,12S]\lambda = [2^{n/2-S}, 1^{2S}] has at most NN rows. A term 2S+1Λ{}^{2S+1}\Lambda of the configuration Γn\Gamma^{n} is a summand Λ\Lambda of the entry in row nn, column 2S+12S + 1. An empty entry has no shape, and row 2N−n2N - n is row nn (the holes of the filled level).
