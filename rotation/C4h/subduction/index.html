3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: group, subduction_embeddings, subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 12.2 and 12.4. A column is a class of subgroups 𝖧\mathsf{H}, in the order of the table of section 3.1. The row subgrp names it by its symbol, as in that table. The symbol links to the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H}, the restriction of Γ\Gamma along a monomorphism from the record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} onto the subgroup, as a sum of the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The monomorphism identifies the subgroup with its record, and 𝖧\mathsf{H} names both. The record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} maps onto the subgroup in [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] ways, 𝘩↦𝘨𝘩𝘨−1\mathsfit{h} \mapsto \mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g}^{-1} (𝘩∈𝖧\mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{H}) for the 𝘨∈𝖮(𝟥)\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{O(3)} that carry 𝖧\mathsf{H} to it. Here 𝖭\mathsf{N} and 𝖢\mathsf{C} are the normalizer and centralizer of 𝖧\mathsf{H} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}. In the section Names and structure of the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}, the row centralizer gives 𝖢\mathsf{C} and the index [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] (the index in the row normalizer is [𝖭:𝖧][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{H}]). Two maps related by a conjugation in 𝖢𝟦𝗁\mathsf{C_{4h}} give the same restriction. Thus the maps fall into [𝖭:𝖢]/[𝖭 𝖢𝟦𝗁:𝖢 𝖢𝟦𝗁][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}]/[\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{C_{4h}}} : \mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{C_{4h}}}] classes, where 𝖭 𝖢𝟦𝗁\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{C_{4h}}} and 𝖢 𝖢𝟦𝗁\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{C_{4h}}} are the normalizer and centralizer of the subgroup in 𝖢𝟦𝗁\mathsf{C_{4h}} (the table of section 3.1). A subgroup whose classes of maps give different tables has one column for each table: setting 1 for the stored monomorphism (monomorphism) and settings 2, 3, … for the alternative_settings. Each setting has the images of the generators of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (setting). The row images gives, under each setting, each generator whose image differs between the settings, in their order, mapped to the class of 𝖢𝟦𝗁\mathsf{C_{4h}} that contains its image. Each generator is written by the symbol of its class (Conventions, section 5.1), and where two share a symbol, a superscript number in brackets gives the generator’s index in the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. Each other setting is setting 1 with each irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖧\mathsf{H} replaced by Λ\Lambda composed with one automorphism of 𝖧\mathsf{H} induced by a conjugation in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} and by none in 𝖢𝟦𝗁\mathsf{C_{4h}} (Conventions, section 12.1).

Read down a column, a table gives induction (Frobenius reciprocity): Γ\Gamma occurs in Ind⁡𝖧𝖢𝟦𝗁Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{C_{4h}}} \Lambda as often as Λ\Lambda occurs in the entry of row Γ\Gamma. For every entry, the record holds the unitary matrix that carries Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H} into the direct sum of the matrices of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (subduction[].blocks). For every class 𝖧\mathsf{H} it holds the left coset representatives (transversal). From both, Ind⁡𝖧𝖢𝟦𝗁Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{C_{4h}}} \Lambda and its intertwiners follow as in Conventions, section 12.4.
