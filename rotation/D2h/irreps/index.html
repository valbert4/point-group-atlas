2.1 Irreducible representations ¶

Data: character_table, elements, group, subgroup_lattice in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: sections 6 and 7. χΓ(𝘨)\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) is the character of the irrep Γ\Gamma on the class of 𝘨\mathsfit{g}, which section 2.2 gives. Bilbao is the irrep’s symbol Γn\Gamma_n in the double point group tables of [EBW+17] (Conventions, section 6.5); dd is the dimension of the irrep and νFS=∣𝖣𝟤𝗁∣−1∑𝘨∈𝖣𝟤𝗁χΓ(𝘨2)\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = |\mathsf{D_{2h}}|^{-1}\allowbreak\sum_{\mathsfit{g}\in\mathsf{D_{2h}}}\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}^2) its Frobenius–Schur indicator, which gives its reality: real (νFS=+1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = +1), pseudoreal (νFS=−1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = -1) or complex (νFS=0\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = 0, paired with its conjugate); conjugate is the conjugate of a complex irrep (a dash for the others); kernel the subgroup ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma of the elements that it maps to the identity, named as in section 3.1; and image the group that its matrices form, isomorphic to 𝖣𝟤𝗁/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{D_{2h}}/\ker\Gamma. The image of an irrep of dimension 1 is the cyclic group of the characters, roots of unity that turn the complex plane. The irreps are irreducible over ℂ\mathbb{C}. Each is real, and in a basis with real matrices it is also an irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} (Conventions, section 7). The boxed irrep is the determinant character (determinant_character), whose character is det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g}.
