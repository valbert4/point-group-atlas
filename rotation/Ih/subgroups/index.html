Data: subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 15. The diagram shows the conjugacy classes of subgroups, each joined to the classes of its maximal subgroups, and the orders increase upward. A normal subgroup is framed, and ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of conjugate subgroups. The table lists the classes by order, those of one symbol together. A row gives the representative 𝖧\mathsf{H} of a class of subgroups (subgrp), its order, the number of subgroups in the class (conjugates) and whether 𝖧\mathsf{H} is normal. It also gives the normalizer 𝖭 𝖨𝗁(𝖧)\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{I_{h}}}(\mathsf{H}) and the centralizer 𝖢 𝖨𝗁(𝖧)\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{I_{h}}}(\mathsf{H}) in 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}, by their classes (normalizer, centralizer), and the classes of 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} other than 𝘌\mathsfit{E} met by 𝖧\mathsf{H} (classes, a dash for none). A subgroup’s other names in its 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class follow ≅\cong (Conventions, section 2.1).
