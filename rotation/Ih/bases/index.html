5.3 Symmetry-adapted bases ¶

Data: group, subduction_basis in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: sections 14, 14.2 to 14.4. The kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle are the spherical harmonics (the inversion acts as (−1)J(-1)^J), and a vector is listed by its coefficients eme_m:

(5.3.1)v=1tot∑memFm  ∣ mJ⟩,Fm=(J+m)! (J−m)!,tot=∑m∣em∣2Fm,v = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mathrm{tot}}}\sum_m e_m\sqrt{F_m}\;\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle,\qquad F_m = (J+m)!\,(J-m)!,\qquad \mathrm{tot} = \sum_m |e_m|^2F_m,
(5.3.1) notation and links
Defines
  • ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle the ket of angular momentum JJ and zz component mm, a spherical harmonic
  • eme_m the coefficient of the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle in a basis vector, its amplitude being emFm/tote_m\sqrt{F_m/\mathrm{tot}}
  • FmF_m the weight (J+m)! (J−m)!(J + m)!\,(J - m)! of the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle
  • tot\mathrm{tot} the normalization ∑m∣em∣2Fm\sum_m \lvert e_m\rvert^2 F_m of a basis vector
  • vv a symmetry-adapted vector, a combination of kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle; vμv_\mu, the partner μ\mu of a block
Symbols
  • mm a summation index
  • JJ the angular momentum, the degree of the spherical harmonics ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle
