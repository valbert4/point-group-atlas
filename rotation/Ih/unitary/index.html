7.1 Subduction from 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} ¶

Data: group, subduction_unitary in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: section 19. A level of 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} is a real irrep Γ\Gamma of dimension N≥2N \ge 2: a degenerate mode of vibration, or a set of degenerate orbitals. Its matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) lie in 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})}, and bΛ(λ)b_\Lambda(\lambda) is the multiplicity of the irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}} in the irrep 𝕊λ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda} of 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} of highest weight λ=[λ1,λ2,… ]\lambda = [\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots], restricted along DΓD^{\Gamma}: the branching rule 𝖴(N)⊃𝖨𝗁\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} \supset \mathsf{I_{h}}. The quanta of a degenerate mode fill the one-row shapes 𝕊[v]Γ=Sym⁡vΓ\mathbb{S}_{[v]}\Gamma = \operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma (section 7.2); nn electrons of spin SS fill the two-column shapes λ=[2n/2−S,12S]\lambda = [2^{n/2-S}, 1^{2S}] (section 7.4). In the table, level is the irrep Γ\Gamma, denominator the product ∏k(1−tak)\prod_k (1 - t^{a_k}) under the generating functions of its overtones, (7.2.1), and chain the subgroups through which 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} restricts to 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}, down to the image of Γ\Gamma: the group that the matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) form, isomorphic to 𝖨𝗁/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{I_{h}}/\ker\Gamma (the column kernel of the table of irreps, section 2.1). The image leaves a quadratic form invariant, and 𝖮(N)\mathsf{O(\mathnormal{N})} is the orthogonal group of that form. The levels go by their dimension.

The matrices of a level of dimension 3, conjugated by a matrix uu, are those of the elements of the record of its image: u DΓ(𝘨) u−1=P(k)u\,D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g})\,u^{-1} = P(k), kk the element that the record’s chain.image.element_map gives for 𝘨\mathsfit{g}. Its states of harmonic degree ℓ\ell carry D(ℓ,(−1)ℓ)D^{(\ell,(-1)^{\ell})} of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} restricted to the image: the row J=ℓJ = \ell, p=(−1)ℓp = (-1)^{\ell}, of the image’s subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, pulled back by 𝘨↦k\mathsfit{g} \mapsto k. u=1u = 1 for every level but T2g\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_{2\mathrm{g}} and T2u\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_{2\mathrm{u}}:

The levels Γ=Hg\Gamma = \mathrm{\scriptsize H}_{\mathrm{g}} and Γ=Hu\Gamma = \mathrm{\scriptsize H}_{\mathrm{u}} are D(2,+)D^{(2,+)} and D(2,−)D^{(2,-)} restricted to 𝖨𝗁\mathsf{I_{h}}, and the 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} of their chains are the images of those irreps. The harmonic polynomials of degree ℓ\ell in five variables, an irrep of 𝖲𝖮(𝟧)\mathsf{SO(5)}, restrict to 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} as the D(J)D^{(J)} with J=2k,2k−2,2k−3,…,kJ = 2k, 2k - 2, 2k - 3, \dots, k for k=ℓ,ℓ−3,ℓ−6,⋯≥0k = \ell, \ell - 3, \ell - 6, \dots \ge 0 (Conventions, section 19; their table by ℓ\ell is in the document of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, under Subduction from 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})}). So the species of the states of harmonic degree ℓ\ell (section 7.2) are the sum of those rows JJ of the subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} (section 5.1), at the parity pℓp^{\ell} for the level of parity pp. The levels Γ=Gg\Gamma = \mathrm{\scriptsize G}_{\mathrm{g}} and Γ=Gu\Gamma = \mathrm{\scriptsize G}_{\mathrm{u}} are the product of the irrep of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} of dimension 2 and its Galois twin. Each image lies in 𝖲𝖮(𝟦)\mathsf{SO(4)}, and the table names it by its abstract type. At an element whose matrix turns two orthogonal planes by θ1\theta_{1} and θ2\theta_{2}, the harmonic polynomials of degree ℓ\ell in four variables have the character χℓ/2(θ1+θ2) χℓ/2(θ1−θ2)\chi_{\ell/2}(\theta_{1} + \theta_{2})\,\chi_{\ell/2}(\theta_{1} - \theta_{2}) (Conventions, section 19).
