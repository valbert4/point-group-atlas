7.3 Overtone states for v≤4v \le 4 ¶

Data: subduction_unitary in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 19. The occupation state ∣v1,…,vN⟩\lvert v_1, \dots, v_N\rangle is the state of v1v_1 quanta in partner 1 of the level, …, vNv_N in partner NN, with v=v1+⋯+vNv = v_1 + \dots + v_N. The tables set occupation states such as ∣03⟩\lvert 03\rangle without commas. The partners and the creation operators of the quanta transform by the matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) of the level. A vector is listed by its coefficients ee:

(7.3.1)1tot∑eF  ∣v1,…,vN⟩,F=v1!…vN!,tot=∑∣e∣2F,\frac{1}{\sqrt{\mathrm{tot}}}\sum e\sqrt{F}\;\lvert v_1, \dots, v_N\rangle,\qquad F = v_1!\dots v_N!,\qquad \mathrm{tot} = \sum |e|^2F,

The table sets the states of a level for v≤2v \le 2 and for each further vv at which every state fills at most four lines, up to v=4v = 4 for a level of dimension 3.
