2.1 Families of irreps ¶

Data: character_functions, group in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: sections 6, 7 and 16. A family is a family of irreps, by its label, dd the dimension of its irreps and νFS\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} their Frobenius–Schur indicator (real: +1+1; complex: 00), index the range of the index of its instances, conjugate the family of the complex-conjugate irreps, over ℝ\mathbb{R} the family of the irreps over ℝ\mathbb{R} in which its irreps lie, kernel the subgroup ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma of the elements that an irrep Γ\Gamma of the family maps to the identity, image the group that its matrices form, isomorphic to 𝖢∞/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{C_{\infty}}/\ker\Gamma, and aliases its other names; a dash stands for none. The image is named by a group of the catalogue: for d=1d = 1 the group of the characters, turning the complex plane. The irreps are irreducible over ℂ\mathbb{C}. A real irrep, in a basis with real matrices, is also an irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R}; the two irreps 1E{}^{1}\mathrm{\scriptsize E}, 2E{}^{2}\mathrm{\scriptsize E} of a complex-conjugate pair together form one irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} of dimension 2d2d and character 2Re⁡χ1E2\operatorname{Re}\chi_{{}^{1}\mathrm{\scriptsize E}}, χΓ(𝘨)\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) being the character of Γ\Gamma at 𝘨\mathsfit{g}, labeled E\mathrm{\scriptsize E} (the label without its prefix; Conventions, sections 6.4 and 7). The boxed family, of one irrep, is the determinant character (determinant_character), whose character is det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g}: in a group of rotations, the trivial irrep.
