3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: character_functions, subduction_subgroups in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 16.1. The closed subgroups of 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} other than 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, up to conjugacy in 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, are, for every N≥1N \ge 1, one subgroup for each member of the family of section 3.1. The subgroup for a member is the group of that member’s document, in its own frame. The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H} as a sum of the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}.

Finite subgroups. For each family, the rule gives the restriction for every NN as the irrep with χ(𝘨p)=ζNpq\chi(\mathsfit{g}_p) = \zeta_{N_p}^{q} (the pair ζNp±q\zeta_{N_p}^{\pm q} for an En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n), labeled as in Conventions, section 6.1. Here ζNp=e2πi/Np\zeta_{N_p} = \mathrm{e}^{2\pi\mathrm{i}/N_p}, where 𝘨p=𝘊∞(2π/N)\mathsfit{g}_p = \mathsfit{C_\infty}(2\pi/N) and Np=NN_p = N. For N=1N = 1 and 22 the irreps are named as in Conventions, section 6.3. The restriction of the instance nn depends on nn only modulo the period LL of the member. The tables list N≤12N \le 12 and n≤6n \le 6, and the record holds n=1,2,…,Ln = 1, 2, \dots, L with the intertwiners (subduction_subgroups.finite[]). The record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} maps onto the subgroup in [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] ways, 𝘩↦𝘨𝘩𝘨−1\mathsfit{h} \mapsto \mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g}^{-1} (𝘩∈𝖧\mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{H}) for the 𝘨∈𝖮(𝟥)\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{O(3)} that carry 𝖧\mathsf{H} to it. Here 𝖭\mathsf{N} and 𝖢\mathsf{C} are the normalizer and centralizer of 𝖧\mathsf{H} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}. In the section Names and structure of the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}, the row centralizer gives 𝖢\mathsf{C} and the index [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] (the index in the row normalizer is [𝖭:𝖧][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{H}]). Two maps related by a conjugation in 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} give the same restriction. Thus the maps fall into [𝖭:𝖢]/[𝖭 𝖢∞:𝖢 𝖢∞][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}]/[\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{C_{\infty}}} : \mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{C_{\infty}}}] classes, where 𝖭 𝖢∞\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{C_{\infty}}} and 𝖢 𝖢∞\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{C_{\infty}}} are the normalizer and centralizer of the subgroup in 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} (the table of section 3.1). A subgroup whose classes of maps give different tables has one column for each table: setting 1 for the stored monomorphism (monomorphism) and settings 2, 3, … for the alternative_settings. Each setting has the images of the generators of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (setting). The row images gives, under each setting, each generator whose image differs between the settings, in their order, mapped to its image. Each image is the element of its component of the class space at its angle. Each generator is written by the symbol of its class (Conventions, section 5.1), and where two share a symbol, a superscript number in brackets gives the generator’s index in the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. Each other setting is setting 1 with each irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖧\mathsf{H} replaced by Λ\Lambda composed with one automorphism of 𝖧\mathsf{H} induced by a conjugation in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} and by none in 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} (Conventions, section 12.1).
