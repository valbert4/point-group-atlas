2.1 Irreducible representations ¶

Data: character_table, elements, group, subduction_unitary, subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 6 and 7. The character χΓ(𝘨)\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) of the irrep Γ\Gamma on the class of 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is given in section 2.2. A row is an irrep of dimension dd, with its Frobenius–Schur indicator νFS=∣𝖣𝟣𝟤𝖽∣−1∑𝘨∈𝖣𝟣𝟤𝖽χΓ(𝘨2)\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = |\mathsf{D_{12d}}|^{-1}\allowbreak\sum_{\mathsfit{g}\in\mathsf{D_{12d}}}\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}^2), which gives its reality: real (νFS=+1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = +1), pseudoreal (νFS=−1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = -1) or complex (νFS=0\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = 0, paired with its conjugate). The column conjugate gives that conjugate (a dash for the others). The kernel ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma is the subgroup of the elements mapped to the identity, named as in section 3.1. The image is the group formed by the irrep’s matrices, isomorphic to 𝖣𝟣𝟤𝖽/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{D_{12d}}/\ker\Gamma and named by a point group (section 7.1). For d=1d = 1 it is the cyclic group of the characters, roots of unity that turn the complex plane. For d=2d = 2 it is the group of the matrices in a real basis, in 𝖮(𝟤)\mathsf{O(2)} acting on the plane. The irreps are irreducible over ℂ\mathbb{C}. Each is real. Over ℝ\mathbb{R} (Conventions, section 7), a real irrep is also an irrep in a basis with real matrices. The boxed irrep is the determinant character (determinant_character), of value det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g}.
