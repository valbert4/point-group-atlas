7.3 Overtone states for v≤6v \le 6 ¶

Data: subduction_unitary in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: section 19. ∣v1,…,vN⟩\lvert v_1, \dots, v_N\rangle is the state of v1v_1 quanta in partner 1 of the level, …, vNv_N in partner NN, v=v1+⋯+vNv = v_1 + \dots + v_N, written without commas in the tables, as ∣03⟩\lvert 03\rangle: the partners transform by the matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) of the level, and so do the creation operators of the quanta. A vector is listed by its coefficients ee, the vector being

so that ee is the coefficient of the product of the creation operators, as eme_m is in section 5.3. The partners of the level T1u\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_{1\mathrm{u}} transform as xx, yy and zz. A row gives one partner of one copy of an irrep (the copies as in Conventions, section 19): for a level of dimension 2, its harmonic degree ℓ\ell, the irrep, the copy index κ\kappa, the partner index μ\mu and the state at v=ℓ,ℓ+2,…v = \ell, \ell + 2, \dots, every number of quanta raised by 1 from one column to the next, since r2=∣11⟩r^2 = \lvert 11\rangle; for the others, vv, ℓ\ell, the irrep, κ\kappa, μ\mu and the vector. The table sets the states of a level for v≤2v \le 2 and for each further vv at which every state fills at most four lines, up to v=4v = 4 for a level of dimension 3, so it stops the states of the levels T1g\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_{1\mathrm{g}}, T2g\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_{2\mathrm{g}}, T1u\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_{1\mathrm{u}} and T2u\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_{2\mathrm{u}} at v=4v = 4 (the record holds v≤6v \le 6).
