A row gives the index kk of 𝘨\mathsfit{g}, its symbol (sym), its order (ord) and the index k−1k^{-1} of 𝘨−1\mathsfit{g}^{-1}. The class of 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is set with its size before its symbol (section 1.4). Exact values are rationals, square roots, cosines and sines of rational multiples of π\pi, and the constants
