Conventions: section 19. The states are the occupation states themselves. The state ∣v1v2⟩\lvert v_1 v_2\rangle has v1v_1 quanta in the partner (x−iy)n(x - \mathrm{i}y)^n and v2v_2 in (x+iy)n(x + \mathrm{i}y)^n (times zδz^{\delta} of section 2.2). It is partner 1 of E(v1−v2)n\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{(v_1 - v_2)n} when v1>v2v_1 > v_2, partner 2 of E(v2−v1)n\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{(v_2 - v_1)n} when v2>v1v_2 > v_1 and A1\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_{1} when v1=v2v_1 = v_2. This state lies in r2min⁡(v1,v2)Hℓr^{2\min(v_1, v_2)} H_\ell with ℓ=∣v1−v2∣\ell = \lvert v_1 - v_2\rvert (r2=∣11⟩r^2 = \lvert 11\rangle). A row gives one partner: its harmonic degree ℓ\ell, the irrep, the partner index μ\mu and the state at v=ℓ,ℓ+2,…v = \ell, \ell + 2, \dots. Every number of quanta rises by 1 from one column to the next.
