2.4 Clebsch–Gordan coefficients ¶

Data: clebsch_gordan in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 10. The two factors of a product have the rotation-diagonal bases ∣e1⟩,∣e2⟩\lvert e_1\rangle, \lvert e_2\rangle and ∣f1⟩,∣f2⟩\lvert f_1\rangle, \lvert f_2\rangle (section 2.2.2), and the product has the partners ∣1⟩\lvert 1\rangle, ∣2⟩\lvert 2\rangle. These coupling rules cover every pair of irreps:

  1. Two one-dimensional irreps: the characters multiply, and the coefficient has the value 1.

  2. λ⊗En=En\lambda\otimes\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n = \mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n for a one-dimensional λ\lambda: ∣1⟩=∣e1⟩\lvert 1\rangle = \lvert e_1\rangle, ∣2⟩=λ(σ𝘷(0))∣e2⟩\lvert 2\rangle = \lambda(\mathsfit{\sigma_v}(0))\lvert e_2\rangle in 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} and 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}, and ∣2⟩=λ(𝘊𝟤𝘹)∣e2⟩\lvert 2\rangle = \lambda(\mathsfit{C_{2x}})\lvert e_2\rangle in 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}. The product En⊗λ\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n\otimes\lambda has the same coefficients with the factors in the other order.

  3. En⊗En′=En+n′⊕E∣n−n′∣\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n\otimes\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n'} = \mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n+n'}\oplus\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{\lvert n-n'\rvert} for n≠n′n \ne n', every coefficient +1+1: En+n′\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n+n'} has ∣1⟩=∣e1⟩∣f1⟩\lvert 1\rangle = \lvert e_1\rangle\lvert f_1\rangle, ∣2⟩=∣e2⟩∣f2⟩\lvert 2\rangle = \lvert e_2\rangle\lvert f_2\rangle. The irrep E∣n−n′∣\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{\lvert n-n'\rvert} has ∣1⟩=∣e1⟩∣f2⟩\lvert 1\rangle = \lvert e_1\rangle\lvert f_2\rangle, ∣2⟩=∣e2⟩∣f1⟩\lvert 2\rangle = \lvert e_2\rangle\lvert f_1\rangle if n>n′n > n', and ∣1⟩=∣e2⟩∣f1⟩\lvert 1\rangle = \lvert e_2\rangle\lvert f_1\rangle, ∣2⟩=∣e1⟩∣f2⟩\lvert 2\rangle = \lvert e_1\rangle\lvert f_2\rangle if n<n′n < n'.

  4. En⊗En=A1⊕A2⊕E2n\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n\otimes\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n = \mathrm{\scriptsize A}_1\oplus\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_2\oplus\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{2n}: A1\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_1, A2=122 (∣e1⟩∣f2⟩±ε∣e2⟩∣f1⟩)\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_2 = \tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}\,(\lvert e_1\rangle\lvert f_2\rangle \pm \varepsilon \lvert e_2\rangle\lvert f_1\rangle) (𝒩=2\mathcal{N} = 2), where ε=−1\varepsilon = -1 only for En,g⊗En,u\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{g}}\otimes\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n,\mathrm{u}} of 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}} and +1+1 otherwise. The irrep E2n\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{2n} is coupled as En+n′\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n+n'}.
