Conventions: section 16.1. The closed subgroups of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} other than 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, up to conjugacy in 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, are its continuous subgroups and, for every N≥1N \ge 1, one subgroup for each member of each family of section 3.1. The subgroup for a member is the group of that member’s document, in its own frame. Members of different families are not conjugate in 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}. The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H} as a sum of the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}.

Continuous subgroups. The row subgrp gives each continuous subgroup 𝖧\mathsf{H}, and the row index gives its index [𝖢∞𝗏:𝖧][\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} : \mathsf{H}]. The intertwiners (subduction_subgroups.continuous[]) are the identity in the rotation-diagonal basis (∣e1⟩,∣e2⟩)(\lvert e_1\rangle, \lvert e_2\rangle) of a family of dimension 2, except to 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, where they exchange ∣e1⟩\lvert e_1\rangle and ∣e2⟩\lvert e_2\rangle of En\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n}.

Induction. For a finite subgroup 𝖧\mathsf{H}, Ind⁡𝖧𝖢∞𝗏Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}} \Lambda has infinite dimension: by Frobenius reciprocity it contains each irrep of 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}} as often as its restriction to 𝖧\mathsf{H} contains Λ\Lambda. A continuous subgroup has finite index, and the record holds its coset representatives (transversal) and the intertwiners in closed form, from which Ind⁡𝖧𝖢∞𝗏Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}} \Lambda follows as in Conventions, section 12.4.
