6.4 Invariant polynomials ¶

Data: invariants in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: section 13.1. The polynomials in xx, yy, zz that take the same value at 𝐫\mathbf{r} and at 𝐫 P(𝘨)\mathbf{r}\,P(\mathsfit{g}) for every point 𝐫\mathbf{r} and every element 𝘨\mathsfit{g}, the invariants, form a ring with the Hilbert series HA1g(t)/(1−t2)=1/((1−t2)(1−t2)(1−t6))H_{\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_{1\mathrm{g}}}(t)/(1-t^{2}) = 1/((1-t^{2})(1-t^{2})(1-t^{6})), in which the coefficient of a power of tt is the number of linearly independent invariants of that degree. A row is an invariant of a minimal generating set of the ring (Conventions, section 13.1): degree is its degree, polynomial the invariant and role whether it is a primary or a secondary invariant; every row is primary, and the ring is the polynomial ring in them.
