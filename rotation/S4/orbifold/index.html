1.6 Orbifold ¶

Data: elements, group, orbifold, permutation_representations, subgroup_lattice in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 17.6 to 17.8. The orbifold 𝖲2/𝖲𝟦\mathsf{S}^{2}/\mathsf{S_{4}} is the set of the orbits of 𝖲𝟦\mathsf{S_{4}} on the sphere 𝖲2\mathsf{S}^{2} of the directions, each orbit marked by the class of the stabilizer of its directions. Its symbol is 2×2{\times}, and it lies on a projective plane (the cross-cap ×{\times} of the symbol), of area π\pi and of Euler characteristic 12\tfrac{1}{2}. A row is a stratum of the orbifold: a cone point, a corner, an arc of the mirror edge or the generic points. A row gives the order of its points, the direction of a point or the side of the region along an arc, and the stabilizer of its directions. Its orbit has the size and the shape of the figure of the stabilizer in section 4.1. A dash marks a shape that depends on the direction.

The region meets every orbit, and an orbit meets it more than once only on its boundary. A row is a vertex of the region together with the side that runs from it to the next vertex. A row gives the direction of the vertex, its angle and the inward normal of the side’s plane. It also gives the element that carries the side onto its partner, by its index kk and its symbol (sym), then the partner and the sense of the gluing. A side on a mirror is its own partner, and its element is the reflection in that mirror.

Forgetting the turn about a direction sends the space 𝖢𝟤\𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{C_{2}}\backslash\mathsf{SO(3)} of section 1.5 onto the orbifold of 𝖢𝟤\mathsf{C_{2}}, which covers this one with 2 sheets. In the orientation of Conventions, section 17.6, the space has the Seifert invariants (−2; (2,1),(2,1))(-2;\ (2, 1), (2, 1)) over the sphere, the Euler number 11 and a first homology of order 4.
