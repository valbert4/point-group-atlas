4.1 Figures ¶

Data: permutation_representations, subgroup_lattice in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: sections 12.4 to 12.6. Every class of subgroups 𝖧\mathsf{H} is the stabilizer of a figure in space, the subgroup of the elements that carry it into itself, and 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}} permutes the N=[𝖲𝟨:𝖧]N = [\mathsf{S_{6}} : \mathsf{H}] copies of the figure as it permutes the right cosets 𝖧ξj\mathsf{H}\mathsfit{\xi}_j, j=0,1,…,N−1j = 0, 1, \dots, N - 1, ξj\mathsfit{\xi}_j the element of least index of its coset, in the order of these indices (ξ0=𝘌\mathsfit{\xi}_0 = \mathsfit{E}): the copy jj is the figure moved by ξj\mathsfit{\xi}_j. The element 𝘨\mathsfit{g} moves the copy jj to the copy j′j', where 𝖧ξj𝘨=𝖧ξj′\mathsf{H}\mathsfit{\xi}_j \mathsfit{g} = \mathsf{H}\mathsfit{\xi}_{j'}, and the permutation matrix D(𝘨)D(\mathsfit{g}), whose row jj has its one nonzero entry in column j′j',

The copies are generated by the defining representation of 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}}, its matrices P(𝘨)P(\mathsfit{g}) (section 1.2), acting on the figure and its root point 𝐫0\mathbf{r}_0: 𝐫j=𝐫0 P(ξj)\mathbf{r}_j = \mathbf{r}_0\, P(\mathsfit{\xi}_j) is the point of the copy jj that 𝐫0\mathbf{r}_0 moves to. The JSON record stores the coordinates of the points of every copy, the images j′j' of every copy under every element and the table of marks (Conventions, sections 12.5 and 12.6). The figure of a class is the first of these whose stabilizer is 𝖧\mathsf{H}: the root point 𝐫0\mathbf{r}_0; the line through the origin along 𝐫0\mathbf{r}_0; a spin, the axial vector 𝐫0\mathbf{r}_0, a sense of rotation about that line; a spinning arrow, the arrow 𝐫0\mathbf{r}_0 with a sense of rotation about itself; a helix, the line along 𝐫0\mathbf{r}_0 with a handedness (these two only when 𝖧\mathsf{H} has no improper element and 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}} has one); and otherwise the least orbit of 𝐫0\mathbf{r}_0 under 𝖧\mathsf{H} whose stabilizer is 𝖧\mathsf{H}, a segment, a polygon or a polyhedron. The table has a row per class of subgroups, in the order of the table of section 3.1 and named as there: NN; the figure, by its shape or its number of points; site, the stabilizer of the point 𝐫0\mathbf{r}_0, which tells the symmetry elements of 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}} that the figure lies on; 𝐫0\mathbf{r}_0; vertices, what the points of all the copies form (the tips of the arrows and spins, both ends of the lines and helices), with ×k{\times}k when k>1k > 1 copies pass through each; rank, the number of classes of pairs of copies under 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}}, a copy with itself one of them; cos, for a point, a line or a spin of rank at most 5, the cosine of the angle between two copies, one per class of pairs other than a copy with itself (of the acute angle, for lines); and hand, 2 when 𝖧\mathsf{H} has no improper element and 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}} has one, so that the rotations of 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}} leave the copies in two classes, mirror images of each other: the left- and the right-handed copies of a chiral figure.
