The determinant ∣1 2ˉ∣\lvert 1\,\bar{2}\rvert is the Slater determinant of the spin–orbitals in it, written in the order 1,1ˉ,2,2ˉ,…1, \bar{1}, 2, \bar{2}, \dots, and ∣ ∣\lvert \,\rvert is the vacuum. The spin–orbital μ\mu is partner μ\mu of the level with spin up, and μˉ\bar{\mu} with spin down. Unlike the spins, the orbitals transform by the matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) of the level.
