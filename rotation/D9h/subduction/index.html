3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: group, subduction_embeddings, subgroup_lattice in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: sections 12.2 and 12.4. A column is a class of subgroups 𝖧\mathsf{H}, in the order of the table of section 3.1, by its symbol in the row subgrp as there, with the letter that tells apart classes with the same symbol, which are not conjugate in 𝖣𝟫𝗁\mathsf{D_{9h}}; the symbol links to the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H}, the restriction of Γ\Gamma along a monomorphism from the record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} onto the subgroup, as a sum of the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}; the monomorphism identifies the subgroup with its record, and 𝖧\mathsf{H} names both. The record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} maps onto the subgroup in [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] ways, 𝘩↦𝘨𝘩𝘨−1\mathsfit{h} \mapsto \mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g}^{-1} (𝘩∈𝖧\mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{H}) for the 𝘨∈𝖮(𝟥)\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{O(3)} that carry 𝖧\mathsf{H} to it, 𝖭\mathsf{N} and 𝖢\mathsf{C} the normalizer and the centralizer of 𝖧\mathsf{H} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}; the row centralizer of section Names and structure of the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H} gives 𝖢\mathsf{C} and this index (the index in the row normalizer is [𝖭:𝖧][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{H}]). Two maps related by a conjugation in 𝖣𝟫𝗁\mathsf{D_{9h}} give the same restriction, so the maps fall into [𝖭:𝖢]/[𝖭 𝖣𝟫𝗁:𝖢 𝖣𝟫𝗁][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}]/[\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{D_{9h}}} : \mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{D_{9h}}}] classes, 𝖭 𝖣𝟫𝗁\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{D_{9h}}} and 𝖢 𝖣𝟫𝗁\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{D_{9h}}} the normalizer and the centralizer of the subgroup in 𝖣𝟫𝗁\mathsf{D_{9h}} (the table of section 3.1). A subgroup whose classes give different tables has one column per table, its settings: 1 for the stored monomorphism (monomorphism) and 2, 3, … for the alternative_settings, each with the images of the generators of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (setting). The row images gives, under each setting, each generator of 𝖧\mathsf{H} whose image differs between the settings, in their order, by the symbol of its class (Conventions, section 5.1), mapped to the class of 𝖣𝟫𝗁\mathsf{D_{9h}} that holds its image; where two generators share a symbol, a superscript number in brackets gives the generator’s index in the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. Each other column under a subgroup is column 1 with each irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖧\mathsf{H} replaced by Λ\Lambda composed with one automorphism of 𝖧\mathsf{H} that a conjugation in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} induces and none in 𝖣𝟫𝗁\mathsf{D_{9h}} does (Conventions, section 12.1).

Read down a column, a table gives induction (Frobenius reciprocity): Γ\Gamma occurs in Ind⁡𝖧𝖣𝟫𝗁Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{D_{9h}}} \Lambda as often as Λ\Lambda occurs in the entry of row Γ\Gamma. The JSON gives, for every entry, the unitary matrix that carries Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H} into the direct sum of the matrices of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (subduction[].blocks), and for every class the left coset representatives of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (transversal), from which Conventions, section 12.4, builds Ind⁡𝖧𝖣𝟫𝗁Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{D_{9h}}} \Lambda and its intertwiners.
