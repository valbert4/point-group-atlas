Conventions: sections 12.4 and 12.5. The kernel of the permutation representation of a class is the subgroup of the elements that leave every copy in place, and the decomposition is that of DD, whose character at 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is the number of copies that 𝘨\mathsfit{g} leaves in place: an irrep occurs in DD as often as the trivial irrep of 𝖧\mathsf{H} occurs in its restriction to 𝖧\mathsf{H} (section 3.2).
