6.1 s\mathrm{s}, p\mathrm{p}, d\mathrm{d}, f\mathrm{f} functions ¶

Data: cartesian_tensors in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 13. A function is a real solid harmonic s\mathrm{s}, p\mathrm{p}, d\mathrm{d} or f\mathrm{f}, of degree 0 to 3 with coprime integer coefficients, or a component Rx\mathrm{R}_{x}, Ry\mathrm{R}_{y}, Rz\mathrm{R}_{z} of an axial vector. A row gives the function, its polynomial in xx, yy and zz, and the irreps of its nonzero isotypic components.
