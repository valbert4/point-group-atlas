6.2 Tensor spaces ¶

Data: cartesian_tensors in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 13. A row is a space of Cartesian tensors, with its Jahn symbol (name), its description in words, its dimension (dim) and the irreps of 𝖢𝟣𝟣\mathsf{C_{11}} that it carries, with their multiplicities (decomposition). The same symbol names the space in the isotypic components (V a polar vector, e an axial factor, a power the number of factors, brackets their symmetrized product). The descriptions name the symmetric polar tensors of a rank (the homogeneous polynomials of that degree), the axial vectors and the symmetric axial tensors of ranks 2 (optical activity) and 3. The elastic stiffness tensors are symmetric in their first two indices, in their last two and under the exchange of the two pairs. The photoelastic tensors are symmetric in their first two and in their last two indices, as are the electrostriction and the quadratic electro-optic tensors. The piezoelectric tensors are symmetric in their last two indices, the third-harmonic susceptibilities in their last three and the electrogyration tensors, which are axial, in their first two. The general polar tensors of rank 4 have no symmetry.
