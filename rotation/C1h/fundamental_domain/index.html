1.5 Fundamental domain ¶

Data: elements, fundamental_domain, group, orbifold in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 17. A molecule with symmetry 𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsf{C_{1h}} placed by 𝘘∈𝖮(𝟥)\mathsfit{Q} \in \mathsf{O(3)} is the same set of atoms when placed by 𝘨𝘘\mathsfit{g}\mathsfit{Q} with 𝘨∈𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{C_{1h}}, since P(𝘨𝘘)=P(𝘨) P(𝘘)P(\mathsfit{g}\mathsfit{Q}) = P(\mathsfit{g})\,P(\mathsfit{Q}). Thus its orientations are the cosets 𝖢𝟣𝗁𝘘={𝘨𝘘:𝘨∈𝖢𝟣𝗁}\mathsf{C_{1h}}\mathsfit{Q} = \{\mathsfit{g}\mathsfit{Q} : \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{C_{1h}}\}, the points of the space 𝖢𝟣𝗁\𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{C_{1h}}\backslash\mathsf{O(3)}. On column vectors a coset is the left coset of the transposes P(𝘘)⊤P(𝘨)⊤P(\mathsfit{Q})^{\top}P(\mathsfit{g})^{\top}, 𝘨∈𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{C_{1h}}, a point of 𝖮(𝟥)/𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsf{O(3)}/\mathsf{C_{1h}}. Volumes are those of the invariant metric of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} in which the distance from 𝘌\mathsfit{E} to a rotation by ω≤π\omega \le \pi is ω\omega, so that vol⁡𝖲𝖮(𝟥)=8π2\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{SO(3)} = 8\pi^2 and vol⁡𝖮(𝟥)=16π2\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{O(3)} = 16\pi^2. A finite group has the counting measure, vol⁡𝖢𝟣𝗁=∣𝖢𝟣𝗁∣=2\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{C_{1h}} = |\mathsf{C_{1h}}| = 2.

As 𝖢𝟣𝗁∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)={𝘌}\mathsf{C_{1h}} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} = \{\mathsfit{E}\}, the cell 𝒟\mathcal{D} of the rotations nearer to 𝘌\mathsfit{E} than to any other element of 𝖢𝟣𝗁∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{C_{1h}} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} is all of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}.

As 𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsf{C_{1h}} contains an improper element, every coset 𝖢𝟣𝗁𝘘\mathsf{C_{1h}}\mathsfit{Q} meets 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} in one coset of 𝖢𝟣𝗁∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{C_{1h}} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)}, so that ℱ=𝒟\mathcal{F} = \mathcal{D} is a fundamental domain. It contains one element of every coset, more only on its boundary. The space 𝖢𝟣𝗁\𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{C_{1h}}\backslash\mathsf{O(3)} is 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, the lens space 𝖫(2,1)\mathsf{L}(2, 1), and
