6.3 Isotypic components ¶

Data: cartesian_tensors in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: section 13. A row is an isotypic component: name (the Jahn symbol of the space, as in the tensor spaces) and irrep give the space and the irrep, mult. the multiplicity of the irrep in the space, dim the dimension of the component, coefficients the field of the coefficients of its basis, rational or cyclotomic, and basis its reduced row echelon basis. A basis tensor of the elastic, piezoelectric, electrogyration or photoelastic tensors or of the third-harmonic susceptibilities is a combination of their coordinates c11,…,c66c_{11}, \dots, c_{66}, d11,…,d36d_{11}, \dots, d_{36}, g11,…,g63\mathnormal{g}_{11}, \dots, \mathnormal{g}_{63}, p11,…,p66p_{11}, \dots, p_{66} or χ1111(3),…,χ3333(3)\chi^{(3)}_{1111}, \dots, \chi^{(3)}_{3333} (Conventions, section 13), each standing for the tensor with that coordinate 1 and every other coordinate 0; the symmetric axial tensors of ranks 2 and 3, the electrogyration and photoelastic tensors and the third-harmonic susceptibilities have only the row of the trivial irrep, the general polar tensors of rank 4 none.
