In an atomic shell of angular momentum ll, nn electrons in the configurations pn\mathrm{p}^{n}, dn\mathrm{d}^{n} and fn\mathrm{f}^{n} occupy the level D(l)D^{(l)}, which in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} is D(l,(−1)l)D^{(l,(-1)^l)}, with the parity of the orbitals. Their terms are the two-column shapes of 𝖴(𝟤l+𝟣)\mathsf{U(2\mathnormal{l}+1)} restricted to 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, each written with its spin multiplicity 2S+12S + 1 and the spectroscopic letter of its D(J′)D^{(J')} (S,P,D,…\mathrm{S}, \mathrm{P}, \mathrm{D}, \dots for J′=0,1,2,…J' = 0, 1, 2, \dots), with the parity (−1)nl(-1)^{nl}. The configurations of nn and of 4l+2−n4l + 2 - n electrons have the same terms. A row gives the terms of nn electrons in the shell named by the centered row above it, and a number before a term is its multiplicity.
