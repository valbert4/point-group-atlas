3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: subduction_rules in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 16.1. Below is the restriction of D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} to each closed subgroup 𝖧\mathsf{H} of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} (section 3.1) for every JJ and both parities (subduction_rules). For an axial 𝖧\mathsf{H} it is the direct sum, over the kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle, of the terms of the first true case of its rule. For 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}} and the families 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}, 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} and 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}}, the sum runs over ∣ 0J⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,0}\bigr\rangle and the pairs ∣ ±mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,\pm m}\bigr\rangle, m>0m > 0. In the rules, q=m mod Nq = m \bmod N, and ζNq\zeta_{N}^{q} is the character of ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle on the rotation by 2π/N2\pi/N about zz, where ζN=e2πi/N\zeta_{N} = \mathrm{e}^{2\pi\mathrm{i}/N}. However, in 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} with NN even, the rotoreflection σ𝘩𝘊∞(π/N)\mathsfit{\sigma_h C_\infty}(\pi/N) labels their irreps, and ζ2Nq\zeta_{2N}^{q} is the character of ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle on it. In the rules of a family, the terms are irreps of the member, labeled as in Conventions, section 6.1, and for N≤2N \le 2 named as in Conventions, section 6.3. The terms of a rule whose subgroup contains 𝘪\mathsfit{i}, which acts on D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} as pp, are written at p=+1p = +1. In 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}} and 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} with NN odd, σ𝘩\mathsfit{\sigma_h} acts on ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle as p(−1)mp(-1)^m, and the terms are written where this sign is +1+1. The multiplicity bΛ(J)b_\Lambda(J) of an irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖧\mathsf{H} at the parity (−1)J(-1)^J is the coefficient of tJt^J in its generating function HΛ(t)=∑J≥0bΛ(J) tJH_\Lambda(t) = \sum_{J\ge0} b_\Lambda(J)\, t^J. The documents of the subgroups use this definition in their section Multiplicities and allowed mm. At the other parity the multiplicity of Λ\Lambda is that of the product of Λ\Lambda and the determinant character of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. In the generating functions, kk is the subscript of an irrep Ek\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{k}, and nn the index of a family of irreps of a continuous subgroup. The floor ⌊x⌋\lfloor x\rfloor is the greatest integer ≤x\le x, and the ceiling ⌈x⌉\lceil x\rceil is the least integer ≥x\ge x. The document of each subgroup except the members with N>12N > 12 lists the restrictions JJ by JJ at both parities, in its section Subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}.
