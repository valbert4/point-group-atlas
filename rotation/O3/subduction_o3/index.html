4.1 Subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} ¶

Data: subduction_o3 in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: section 11. D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} is the irrep of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} of angular momentum JJ and parity pp (p=+1p = +1: g\mathrm{g}, p=−1p = -1: u\mathrm{u}). For every JJ:

(4.1.1)D(J,p) ⁣↓𝖮(𝟥)=D(J,p).D^{(J,p)}\!\downarrow\mathsf{O(3)} = D^{(J,p)}.
