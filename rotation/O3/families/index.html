2.1 Families of irreps ¶

Data: character_functions, group in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 6, 7 and 16. A row is a family of irreps, by its label, with its dimension dd, its Frobenius–Schur indicator νFS\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} (real for +1+1) and the range of the index of its irreps. It also gives its other names (aliases). The kernel ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma is the subgroup of the elements that an irrep Γ\Gamma of the family maps to the identity. The image is the group formed by the irrep’s matrices, isomorphic to 𝖮(𝟥)/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{O(3)}/\ker\Gamma and named by a point group. For J=0J = 0 it is the group of the characters, turning the complex plane. For J≥1J \ge 1 it is named by the point group isomorphic to it, and in a real basis its matrices lie in 𝖮(𝟤J+𝟣)\mathsf{O(2\mathnormal{J} + 1)}. A row also gives the family’s character on each component, in the angle ϕ\phi of the rotation. The irreps are irreducible over ℂ\mathbb{C}. Each is real. Over ℝ\mathbb{R} (Conventions, section 7), a real irrep is also an irrep in a basis with real matrices. The determinant character (determinant_character), of value det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g}, is D(0,−)D^{(0,-)}, the instance J=0J = 0 of D(J,−)D^{(J,-)}. The constant i\mathrm{i} is the imaginary unit, and e\mathrm{e} is the base of the natural logarithm.
