Data: subgroup_classes in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy, and the subgroups of O(3) in point_groups.json

Conventions: sections 15.2 and 16.1. The closed subgroups of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} up to conjugacy in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} are 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}}, 𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{C_{\infty v}}, 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, 𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{D_{\infty h}}, 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, the exceptional groups of the table and, for every N≥1N \ge 1, the finite groups of its families (family). The last row of the table gives the exceptional groups, and each number or symbol under documents links to the document of that group. Some members with N=1N = 1 have other names: 𝖲𝟤\mathsf{S_{2}} ≅\cong 𝖢𝗂\mathsf{C_{i}}, 𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsf{C_{1h}} ≅\cong 𝖢𝗌\mathsf{C_{s}}, 𝖢𝟣𝗏\mathsf{C_{1v}} ≅\cong 𝖢𝗌\mathsf{C_{s}}, 𝖣𝟣\mathsf{D_{1}} ≅\cong 𝖢𝟤\mathsf{C_{2}}, 𝖣𝟣𝗁\mathsf{D_{1h}} ≅\cong 𝖢𝟤𝗏\mathsf{C_{2v}} and 𝖣𝟣𝖽\mathsf{D_{1d}} ≅\cong 𝖢𝟤𝗁\mathsf{C_{2h}}. In the diagram, each group links to its document, and a line joins a group to a maximal closed subgroup among the groups of the diagram. Section 3.2 gives the restriction of D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} to each closed subgroup, and the document of each group except the members with N>12N > 12 lists it JJ by JJ, in its section Subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}.
