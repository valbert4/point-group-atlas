Data: elements, fundamental_domain, group in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: section 17. A molecule with symmetry 𝖢𝟣\mathsf{C_{1}} placed by 𝘘∈𝖮(𝟥)\mathsfit{Q} \in \mathsf{O(3)} is the same set of atoms when placed by 𝘨𝘘\mathsfit{g}\mathsfit{Q} with 𝘨∈𝖢𝟣\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{C_{1}}, as P(𝘨𝘘)=P(𝘨) P(𝘘)P(\mathsfit{g}\mathsfit{Q}) = P(\mathsfit{g})\,P(\mathsfit{Q}): its orientations are the cosets 𝖢𝟣𝘘={𝘨𝘘:𝘨∈𝖢𝟣}\mathsf{C_{1}}\mathsfit{Q} = \{\mathsfit{g}\mathsfit{Q} : \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{C_{1}}\}, the points of the space 𝖢𝟣\𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{C_{1}}\backslash\mathsf{O(3)}. On column vectors the matrices of a coset are P(𝘘)⊤P(𝘨)⊤P(\mathsfit{Q})^{\top}P(\mathsfit{g})^{\top}, a left coset of the transposes and a point of 𝖮(𝟥)/𝖢𝟣\mathsf{O(3)}/\mathsf{C_{1}}. Volumes are those of the invariant metric of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} in which the distance from 𝘌\mathsfit{E} to a rotation by ω≤π\omega \le \pi is ω\omega, so that vol⁡𝖲𝖮(𝟥)=8π2\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{SO(3)} = 8\pi^2 and vol⁡𝖮(𝟥)=16π2\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{O(3)} = 16\pi^2; a finite group has the counting measure, vol⁡𝖢𝟣=∣𝖢𝟣∣=1\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{C_{1}} = |\mathsf{C_{1}}| = 1.

As 𝖢𝟣∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)={𝘌}\mathsf{C_{1}} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} = \{\mathsfit{E}\}, the cell 𝒟\mathcal{D} of the rotations nearer to 𝘌\mathsfit{E} than to any other element of 𝖢𝟣∩𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{C_{1}} \cap \mathsf{SO(3)} is all of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}.

Every coset 𝖢𝟣𝘘\mathsf{C_{1}}\mathsfit{Q} lies in 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} or in 𝘪 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsfit{i}\,\mathsf{SO(3)}, so that ℱ=𝒟∪𝘪 𝒟\mathcal{F} = \mathcal{D} \cup \mathsfit{i}\,\mathcal{D} is a fundamental domain: it holds one element of every coset, more only on its boundary. 𝖢𝟣\𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{C_{1}}\backslash\mathsf{O(3)} is two copies of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, the lens space 𝖫(2,1)\mathsf{L}(2, 1), and
