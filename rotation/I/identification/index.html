The field type is I when 𝖨\mathsf{I} contains only rotations, II when 𝖨\mathsf{I} contains the inversion 𝘪\mathsfit{i} and III otherwise. The fields center and commutator_subgroup are classes of the table of section 3.1. The field polar is yes when 𝖨\mathsf{I} fixes a nonzero vector. Crystallographers call a group chiral, or enantiomorphic, when its type is I, and centrosymmetric when it contains 𝘪\mathsfit{i}. The field laue_class is the group generated by 𝖨\mathsf{I} and 𝘪\mathsfit{i}.
