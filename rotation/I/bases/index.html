5.3 Symmetry-adapted bases ¶

Data: subduction_basis in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 14 and 14.2 to 14.4. The kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle are the spherical harmonics (the inversion acts as (−1)J(-1)^J). A vector is listed by its coefficients eme_m:

(5.3.1) notation and links
Defines

  • eme_m the coefficient of the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle in a basis vector, its amplitude being emFm/tote_m\sqrt{F_m/\mathrm{tot}}
  • FmF_m the weight (J+m)! (J−m)!(J + m)!\,(J - m)! of the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle
  • tot\mathrm{tot} the normalization ∑m∣em∣2Fm\sum_m \lvert e_m\rvert^2 F_m of a basis vector
  • vv a symmetry-adapted vector, a combination of kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle, with vμv_\mu the partner μ\mu of a block

The copies of Γ\Gamma at JJ are orthonormal. In the coordinates eme_m, m=−J,−J+1,…,Jm = -J, -J+1, \dots, J, their partner-1 vectors come from the vectors of the reduced row echelon basis of the range of

TeX P^\Gamma_{1,1} = \sum_{\mathsfit{g}} {D^{\Gamma}_{1,1}(\mathsfit{g})}^{*}\,D^{(J)}(\mathsfit{g}),

in the order of their lowest mm. The index κ\kappa follows this order, and each partner-1 vector is its basis vector minus its projections on the copies before it (Gram–Schmidt). Partner μ\mu is Pμ,1ΓP^\Gamma_{\mu,1} applied to partner 1. The copies are adapted to time reversal (Conventions, section 14.2). For a real irrep the basis is that over ℝ\mathbb{R} of the vectors of the range that time reversal keeps with the matrix of the irrep, in the real and imaginary parts of the eme_m. Each copy then has the sign that gives its first coefficient a positive real part, or makes it i\mathrm{i} times a positive number. The copies of the second irrep of a complex pair are those of the first under time reversal. The bases have 33 blocks and 121 vectors at J=0,1,…,10J = 0, 1, \dots, 10. A row gives a vector of a block at JJ: the block’s irrep and copy index κ\kappa, the partner index μ\mu and the vector. The full set J=0,1,…,60J = 0, 1, \dots, 60 (993 blocks, 3721 vectors) is in data/symmetry_adapted_bases/I/. The repository holds J≤10J \le 10, and the release archive I_J011-J060.tar holds J=11,12,…,60J = 11, 12, \dots, 60 and unpacks in the folder rotation/ (README.md). A vector at J=4J = 4 fills more than four lines, so the table stops at J=3J = 3 (5 blocks, 16 vectors). The record (subduction_basis.enumerated) and the files also hold J=4,5,…,10J = 4, 5, \dots, 10.
