7.1 Subduction from 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} ¶

Data: group, subduction_unitary in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 19. A level of 𝖨\mathsf{I} is a real irrep Γ\Gamma of dimension N≥2N \ge 2: a degenerate mode of vibration, or a set of degenerate orbitals. Its matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) lie in 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})}. The branching rule 𝖴(N)⊃𝖨\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} \supset \mathsf{I} gives the multiplicity bΛ(λ)b_\Lambda(\lambda) of the irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖨\mathsf{I} in the irrep 𝕊λ\mathbb{S}_{\lambda} of 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} of highest weight λ=[λ1,λ2,… ]\lambda = [\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots], restricted along DΓD^{\Gamma}. The quanta of a degenerate mode fill the one-row shapes 𝕊[v]Γ=Sym⁡vΓ\mathbb{S}_{[v]}\Gamma = \operatorname{Sym}^{v}\Gamma (section 7.2), and nn electrons of spin SS fill the two-column shapes λ=[2n/2−S,12S]\lambda = [2^{n/2-S}, 1^{2S}] (section 7.4). The table lists the levels by dimension. A row gives the level Γ\Gamma, the denominator ∏k(1−tak)\prod_k (1 - t^{a_k}) of the generating functions (7.2.1) of its overtones and the subgroups through which 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})} restricts to 𝖨\mathsf{I} (chain). The chain ends at the image of Γ\Gamma, the group formed by the matrices DΓ(𝘨)D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}), isomorphic to 𝖨/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{I}/\ker\Gamma (the column kernel of the table of irreps, section 2.1). The image leaves a quadratic form invariant, and 𝖮(N)\mathsf{O(\mathnormal{N})} is the orthogonal group of that form.

The matrices of a level of dimension 3 are conjugate by a matrix uu to those of the elements of the record of its image: u DΓ(𝘨) u−1=P(k)u\,D^{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g})\,u^{-1} = P(k). The level’s chain.image.element_map gives the element kk for 𝘨\mathsfit{g}. The level’s states of harmonic degree ℓ\ell carry D(ℓ,(−1)ℓ)D^{(\ell,(-1)^{\ell})} of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} restricted to the image. This restriction is the row J=ℓJ = \ell, p=(−1)ℓp = (-1)^{\ell}, of the image’s subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, pulled back by 𝘨↦k\mathsfit{g} \mapsto k. Every level of dimension 3 has u=1u = 1 except T2\mathrm{\scriptsize T}_{2}:

The level Γ=H\Gamma = \mathrm{\scriptsize H} is D(2)D^{(2)} restricted to 𝖨\mathsf{I}, and the 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} of its chain is the image of that irrep. The harmonic polynomials of degree ℓ\ell in five variables, an irrep of 𝖲𝖮(𝟧)\mathsf{SO(5)}, restrict to 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} as the D(J)D^{(J)} with J=2k,2k−2,2k−3,…,kJ = 2k, 2k - 2, 2k - 3, \dots, k for k=ℓ,ℓ−3,ℓ−6,⋯≥0k = \ell, \ell - 3, \ell - 6, \dots \ge 0 (Conventions, section 19). Thus the states of harmonic degree ℓ\ell (section 7.2) carry the sum of those rows JJ of the subduction from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} (section 5.1). The table of this rule by ℓ\ell is in the document of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, in its section Subduction from 𝖴(N)\mathsf{U(\mathnormal{N})}. The level Γ=G\Gamma = \mathrm{\scriptsize G} is the product of the irrep of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} of dimension 2 and its Galois twin. Its image lies in 𝖲𝖮(𝟦)\mathsf{SO(4)}, and the table names it by its abstract type. At an element whose matrix turns two orthogonal planes by θ1\theta_{1} and θ2\theta_{2}, the harmonic polynomials of degree ℓ\ell in four variables have the character χℓ/2(θ1+θ2) χℓ/2(θ1−θ2)\chi_{\ell/2}(\theta_{1} + \theta_{2})\,\chi_{\ell/2}(\theta_{1} - \theta_{2}) (Conventions, section 19).
