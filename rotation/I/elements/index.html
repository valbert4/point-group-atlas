On column vectors its matrix is the transpose P(𝘨)⊤P(\mathsfit{g})^{\top}, and products compose in the reverse order. The frame is icosahedral, with 2-fold axes along xx, yy, zz, a 3-fold axis along (1,1,1)(1, 1, 1) and a 5-fold axis along (0,1,φ)(0, 1, \varphi), where φ=12(1+5)\varphi = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(1+\sqrt{5}\bigr). A proper element is the rotation 𝘙𝐯(ω)\mathsfit{R_{\mathbf{v}}}(\omega) by the angle ω\omega about the axis 𝐯\mathbf{v}, counterclockwise as seen from the tip of 𝐯\mathbf{v}. The Euler angles of the rotation part (det⁡𝘨) 𝘨(\det \mathsfit{g})\,\mathsfit{g} are (α,β,γ)(\alpha,\beta,\gamma): by α\alpha about zz, then by β\beta about yy, then by γ\gamma about zz (fixed axes). An en dash marks an Euler angle that is not a rational multiple of π\pi. A row gives the index kk of 𝘨\mathsfit{g}, its symbol (sym), its order (ord) and the index k−1k^{-1} of 𝘨−1\mathsfit{g}^{-1}. The class of 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is set with its size before its symbol (section 1.4). Exact values are rationals, square roots, cosines and sines of rational multiples of π\pi, and the constants φ\varphi and
