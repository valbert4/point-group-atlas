An object with symmetry 𝖦\mathsf{G} placed by 𝘘~∈𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\tilde{\mathsfit{Q}} \in \mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} is the same when placed by 𝘨𝘘~\mathsfit{g}\tilde{\mathsfit{Q}} with 𝘨∈𝖦\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G}: its orientations are the cosets 𝖦𝘘~={𝘨𝘘~:𝘨∈𝖦}\mathsf{G}\tilde{\mathsfit{Q}} = \{\mathsfit{g}\tilde{\mathsfit{Q}} : \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{G}\}, the points of the space 𝖦\𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{G}\backslash\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}. Volumes are those of the metric that the covering map carries to that of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} (Conventions, section 17), so that vol⁡𝖲𝖴(𝟤)=16π2\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{SU(2)} = 16\pi^2 and vol⁡𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)=32π2\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} = 32\pi^2, twice those of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}; a finite group has the counting measure, vol⁡𝖦=∣𝖦∣=18\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{G} = |\mathsf{G}| = 18. As 𝖦\mathsf{G} does not hold 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}}, a coset 𝖦𝘘~\mathsf{G}\tilde{\mathsfit{Q}} holds one lift of each element of a coset of 𝖲𝟣𝟪\mathsf{S_{18}} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, and 𝖦\𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{G}\backslash\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} covers 𝖲𝟣𝟪\𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{S_{18}}\backslash\mathsf{O(3)} twice: the two lifts of every rotation of the cell of 𝖲𝟣𝟪\mathsf{S_{18}}, in its section Fundamental domain, form the cell 𝒟\mathcal{D} here, one element of every coset of 𝖦∩𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SU(2)} in 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)}, and ℱ=𝒟\mathcal{F} = \mathcal{D} is a fundamental domain. 𝖦∩𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SU(2)} has 9 elements, so that the space (𝖦∩𝖲𝖴(𝟤))\𝖲𝖴(𝟤)(\mathsf{G} \cap \mathsf{SU(2)})\backslash\mathsf{SU(2)} is the lens space 𝖫(9,1)\mathsf{L}(9, 1). Hence
