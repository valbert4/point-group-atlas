A binary form f=∑ici uivn−if = \sum_i c_i\, u^i v^{n-i} of degree nn in the spin kets u=∣ 1/21/2⟩u = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,1/2}\bigr\rangle and v=∣ −1/21/2⟩v = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,-1/2}\bigr\rangle is invariant when kf=fk f = f for every element kk, the elements acting at the parity +1+1 (Conventions, section 26.4). The spinor irreps of 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O} have dimension 2 and 4, as those of the binary octahedral group, the group of its matrices on uu, vv. The invariant polynomials in the partners of one are those of the group of its matrices, counted in each degree by its Molien series HV(t)H_V(t) below. In dimension 2 the matrices form the binary octahedral group, with the binary forms below. In dimension 4 the invariants are those below. Among the finite groups of this catalogue, only 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O}, 𝟤𝖳𝖽\mathsf{2T_{d}}, 𝟤𝖮𝗁\mathsf{2O_{h}}, 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I} and 𝟤𝖨𝗁\mathsf{2I_{h}} have spinor irreps of dimension above 2 [McK80].

Binary forms. The matrices of the elements on uu, vv form the binary octahedral group, of type E7, whose invariant forms are those of [Kle84]. In degree 2J2J these forms are the kets at JJ that 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O} leaves invariant [Vil68, chapter III, section 2]. Molien series. For a spinor irrep VV, or a spinor level of section 6.2 that is not one irrep, the Molien series is HV(t)=∣𝟤𝖮∣−1∑k1/det⁡(1−t DV(k))H_V(t) = \lvert\mathsf{2O}\rvert^{-1}\sum_k 1/\det\bigl(1 - t\,D^V(k)\bigr) [Mol97]. Its coefficient of tnt^n is the number of linearly independent invariant polynomials of degree nn in the partners. This number is the multiplicity of the trivial irrep in the nn-th symmetric power of VV, the counterpart of the exterior powers of section 6.2. Spinor irreps of dimension 4. The invariant polynomials in the partners of a spinor irrep VV of dimension 4, V=D(J,±1)V = D^{(J,\pm 1)} restricted, are written in the kets Xi=uiv2J−iX_i = u^i v^{2J-i} (Conventions, section 26.4). A row gives the degrees of a minimal set of generators of degree at most 12, with their number in parentheses when it exceeds one. It also gives the degrees of the primary invariants (they need not be the factors of the denominator of HV(t)H_V(t) above) and whether those generators generate the ring (complete). The primary invariants are a homogeneous system of parameters, over whose polynomials the ring is a free module. The matrices have determinant 1, so the ring is Gorenstein [Ben93, section 4.6] and the numerator of its HV(t)H_V(t) is a palindrome. The record holds the forms (invariants.higher).
