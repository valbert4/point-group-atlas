5.1 Spin–orbitals ¶

Data: spin_orbitals in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 25.4. The states of an electron of orbital angular momentum ll and spin 1 ⁣/2\nicefrac{1}{2} are each given up to a positive factor. They are written in the functions ff of the s\mathrm{s}, p\mathrm{p} and d\mathrm{d} shells of 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}} (Conventions, section 13) times the spin kets u=∣ 1/21/2⟩u = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,1/2}\bigr\rangle and v=∣ −1/21/2⟩v = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,-1/2}\bigr\rangle. The record also holds the f\mathrm{f} shell (spin_orbitals). An orbital level consists of the functions of degree ll that carry the irrep Γ\Gamma of 𝖲𝟨\mathsf{S_{6}}, bb times when it occurs bb times. With the spin, it splits into the spinor irreps Λ\Lambda of 𝟤𝖲𝟨\mathsf{2S_{6}}, those of the doublet of Γ\Gamma in the spin–orbit levels (section 6.1, first column). Under the heading of its shell, a row gives Γ\Gamma, a copy κ\kappa of Λ\Lambda (when there are several), its partner μ\mu and the state. The normalized state (Conventions, section 25.4) follows the partner law with the matrices of section 2.3. The copies are adapted to time reversal, which sends fuf u to fvf v and fvf v to −fu-f u (Conventions, section 25.4).
