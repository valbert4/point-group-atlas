3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: group, subduction_embeddings in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: sections 12.2, 12.4 and 28. A column is a class of subgroups 𝖧\mathsf{H}, in the order of the table of section 3.1, by its symbol in the row subgrp as there, with the letter that tells apart classes with the same symbol, which are not conjugate in 𝟤𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsf{2C_{1h}}; the symbol links to the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H}, the restriction of Γ\Gamma along a monomorphism from the record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} onto the subgroup, as a sum of the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}; the monomorphism identifies the subgroup with its record, and 𝖧\mathsf{H} names both. The rows of the vector irreps of a preimage are those of 𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsf{C_{1h}} (vector_subduction); the column of a section is that of its preimage with the spinor irreps renamed (Conventions, section 27), which the record does not store.

A′′\mathrm{\scriptsize A}''A\mathrm{\scriptsize A}
E1/2′\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{1/2}'A1/2\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_{1/2}
E1/2′′\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{1/2}''A1/2\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_{1/2}

Read down a column, a table gives induction (Frobenius reciprocity): Γ\Gamma occurs in Ind⁡𝖧𝟤𝖢𝟣𝗁Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{2C_{1h}}} \Lambda as often as Λ\Lambda occurs in the entry of row Γ\Gamma. The JSON gives, for every entry of a spinor irrep, the unitary matrix that carries Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H} into the direct sum of the matrices of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (subduction[].blocks), and for every class the left coset representatives of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (transversal), from which Conventions, section 12.4, builds Ind⁡𝖧𝟤𝖢𝟣𝗁Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{2C_{1h}}} \Lambda and its intertwiners.
