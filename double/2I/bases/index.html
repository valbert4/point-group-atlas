in the order of their lowest mm. The index κ\kappa follows this order, and each partner-1 vector is its basis vector minus its projections on the copies before it (Gram–Schmidt). For a pseudoreal irrep the basis is that over ℝ\mathbb{R} of the vectors of the range that time reversal keeps with the matrix of section 26.1, in the real and imaginary parts of the eme_m. Each copy then has the sign that gives its first coefficient a positive real part, or makes it i\mathrm{i} times a positive number.
