1.5 Conjugacy classes ¶

Data: character_table in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 5 and 22. A class of 𝖢𝟪𝗏\mathsf{C_{8v}} lifts to one class of 𝟤𝖢𝟪𝗏\mathsf{2C_{8v}} that contains both lifts of its elements (its size doubled, no bar) or to two classes listed next to each other (section 1.2). The two are the class of the canonical lifts (𝘨,0)(\mathsfit{g}, 0) of these elements and that of their barred partners (𝘨,1)(\mathsfit{g}, 1). A row is a class of elements 𝘨\mathsfit{g}, with their number (size), their order (ord) and the classes of 𝘨−1\mathsfit{g}^{-1} and 𝘨2\mathsfit{g}^{2}. It also gives the angle ω\omega of the lift of the rotation part of 𝘨\mathsfit{g} (section 1.3) and the axis 𝐯\mathbf{v} of a member (a dash for none).
