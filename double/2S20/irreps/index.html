Conventions: sections 6, 7 and 23. χΓ(𝘨)\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) is the character of the irrep Γ\Gamma on the class of the element 𝘨\mathsfit{g}, which section 2.2 gives. The vector irreps (χΓ(𝘌ˉ)=+d\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{\bar{E}}) = +d, dd the dimension) are those of 𝖲𝟤𝟢\mathsf{S_{20}}, on both lifts (vector_irreps); the spinor irreps (χΓ(𝘌ˉ)=−d\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{\bar{E}}) = -d) follow below the rule. A spinor irrep is labeled by its dimension, A\mathrm{\scriptsize A} (1), E\mathrm{\scriptsize E} (2), G\mathrm{\scriptsize G} (4) or I\mathrm{\scriptsize I} (6), a complex one by that of its complex-conjugate pair (E\mathrm{\scriptsize E} for dimension 1, G\mathrm{\scriptsize G} for dimension 2), and the least half-integer JJ at which it occurs in the irreps D(J,+1)D^{(J,+1)} of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} (section 4.1). Two that would share a label carry the prefixes 1 and 2: 1 has the character of the smaller argument in [0,2π)[0, 2\pi) on the first class where they differ. dd is the dimension of the irrep and νFS=∣𝟤𝖲𝟤𝟢∣−1∑𝘨∈𝟤𝖲𝟤𝟢χΓ(𝘨2)\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = |\mathsf{2S_{20}}|^{-1}\allowbreak\sum_{\mathsfit{g}\in\mathsf{2S_{20}}}\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}^2) its Frobenius–Schur indicator, which gives its reality: real (νFS=+1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = +1), pseudoreal (νFS=−1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = -1) or complex (νFS=0\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = 0, paired with its conjugate); conjugate is the conjugate of a complex irrep (a dash for the others), over ℝ\mathbb{R} the irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} in which it lies; kernel the subgroup ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma of the elements that it maps to the identity, named as in section 3.1; and image the group that its matrices form, isomorphic to 𝟤𝖲𝟤𝟢/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{2S_{20}}/\ker\Gamma: for a vector irrep that of the irrep of 𝖲𝟤𝟢\mathsf{S_{20}}; for a spinor irrep of dimension 1 the cyclic group of its characters, of dimension 2 with its matrices in 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} the preimage 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H} that they form, and otherwise the group of the catalogue isomorphic to it. The irreps are irreducible over ℂ\mathbb{C}. A real irrep, in a basis with real matrices, is also an irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R}; the two irreps 1E{}^{1}\mathrm{\scriptsize E}, 2E{}^{2}\mathrm{\scriptsize E} of a complex-conjugate pair together form one irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} of dimension 2d2d and character 2Re⁡χ1E2\operatorname{Re}\chi_{{}^{1}\mathrm{\scriptsize E}}, labeled without the prefix when they are vector irreps; a complex-conjugate pair of spinor irreps forms one irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} of dimension 2d2d, named by the labels of its two members side by side, in the order of the irreps (Conventions, sections 6.4, 7 and 23.2). The boxed irrep is the determinant character of 𝖲𝟤𝟢\mathsf{S_{20}}, whose character is det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g} on the element (𝘨,b)(\mathsfit{g}, b).
