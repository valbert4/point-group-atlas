Conventions: sections 16.1 and 29.5.

Vector families. The vector families of 𝟤𝖣∞\mathsf{2D_{\infty }} are those of 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}. They restrict to the preimage 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H} of a closed subgroup 𝖧\mathsf{H} of 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} as 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}} restricts them to 𝖧\mathsf{H} (vector_entry, vector_rules), whose irreps are the vector irreps of 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H}. The families restrict to a section as to its preimage. The tables are those of the section Descent of symmetry of the document of 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}, with its settings. They are headed by the preimages and give the images of the generators of 𝖧\mathsf{H} at the points of the class space (section 1.2). Members of different families are not conjugate in 𝟤𝖣∞\mathsf{2D_{\infty }}, also where they lie in one 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}-conjugacy class, such as 𝟤𝖣𝟣\mathsf{2D_{1}} and 𝟤𝖢𝟤\mathsf{2C_{2}} (a binary axis perpendicular to zz and along zz). The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H} as a sum of the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}.
