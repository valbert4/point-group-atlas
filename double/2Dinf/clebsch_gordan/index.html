2.4 Clebsch–Gordan coefficients ¶

Data: clebsch_gordan in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: sections 10.3 and 29.3. The coupling rules of the products with a spinor factor, with ∣e1⟩,∣e2⟩\lvert e_1\rangle, \lvert e_2\rangle and ∣f1⟩,∣f2⟩\lvert f_1\rangle, \lvert f_2\rangle the bases of the two factors (a spinor’s those of section 1.2, a vector’s those of 𝖣∞\mathsf{D_{\infty}}) and ∣1⟩\lvert 1\rangle, ∣2⟩\lvert 2\rangle the partners of the product; ∣2⟩\lvert 2\rangle carries the sign ss that the flip 𝘊~𝟤′(0)\mathsfit{\tilde{C}_2'}(0) requires, and every coefficient written is 1 but ss:

  1. λ⊗En=En\lambda\otimes\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n = \mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n for a one-dimensional λ\lambda: ∣1⟩=∣f1⟩\lvert 1\rangle = \lvert f_1\rangle, ∣2⟩=λ(𝘊~𝟤′(0))∣f2⟩\lvert 2\rangle = \lambda(\mathsfit{\tilde{C}_2'}(0))\lvert f_2\rangle.

  2. En⊗En′=En+n′⊕E∣n−n′∣\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n\otimes\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n'} = \mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n+n'}\oplus\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{\lvert n-n'\rvert}, nn a vector and n′n' a spinor index: En+n′\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n+n'} has ∣1⟩=∣e1⟩∣f1⟩\lvert 1\rangle = \lvert e_1\rangle\lvert f_1\rangle, ∣2⟩=s∣e2⟩∣f2⟩\lvert 2\rangle = s\lvert e_2\rangle\lvert f_2\rangle, s=s = (−1)n(-1)^{n}; En−n′\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n-n'} (n>n′n > n') has ∣1⟩=∣e1⟩∣f2⟩\lvert 1\rangle = \lvert e_1\rangle\lvert f_2\rangle, ∣2⟩=s∣e2⟩∣f1⟩\lvert 2\rangle = s\lvert e_2\rangle\lvert f_1\rangle, s=s = (−1)n+1(-1)^{n+1}; En′−n\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n'-n} (n<n′n < n') has ∣1⟩=∣e2⟩∣f1⟩\lvert 1\rangle = \lvert e_2\rangle\lvert f_1\rangle, ∣2⟩=s∣e1⟩∣f2⟩\lvert 2\rangle = s\lvert e_1\rangle\lvert f_2\rangle, s=s = (−1)n(-1)^{n}.

  3. En⊗En′\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n\otimes\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n'}, n≠n′n \ne n' both spinor indices, the same partners with s=s = (−1)n+n′(-1)^{n+n'} for En+n′\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n+n'}, (−1)n+n′(-1)^{n+n'} for n>n′n > n' and (−1)n+n′(-1)^{n+n'} for n<n′n < n'.

  4. En⊗En=A1⊕A2⊕E2n\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n\otimes\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_n = \mathrm{\scriptsize A}_1\oplus\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_2\oplus\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{2n} for a spinor family: A1\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_1, A2=122 (∣e1⟩∣f2⟩∓∣e2⟩∣f1⟩)\mathrm{\scriptsize A}_2 = \tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}\,(\lvert e_1\rangle\lvert f_2\rangle \mp \lvert e_2\rangle\lvert f_1\rangle) (𝒩=2\mathcal{N} = 2); E2n\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{2n} as En+n′\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{n+n'}.
