Conventions: sections 15 and 28. The diagram shows the conjugacy classes of subgroups, each joined to the classes of its maximal subgroups, the orders increasing upward; a normal subgroup is framed, and ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of conjugate subgroups.
