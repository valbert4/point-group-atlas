the spin being even under the inversion. Row Γ\Gamma, column 2S+12S + 1 of a table gives the levels of 2S+1Γ{}^{2S+1}\Gamma, for every vector irrep Γ\Gamma and S=1 ⁣/2,1,…,7 ⁣/2S = \nicefrac{1}{2}, 1, \dots, \nicefrac{7}{2}, the nonzero spins of a d or f configuration: spinor irreps at half-integer SS, vector irreps at integer SS. They follow from the products of section 2.4 and the multiplicities of section 4.1, and the record holds them (spin_orbit_levels). For an orbital level of the shells of section 5.1, column 2S+1=22S + 1 = 2 gives the levels of one electron, whose states that section writes out.
