The volume as one PDF (1994 sheets) holds the conventions, the documents of its groups, the references and the index of notation. CONVENTIONS.pdf is CONVENTIONS.md typeset, the file double_groups.json holds the lattice of the 112 Pin−(3)-conjugacy classes of the double point groups, and the schemas are those of this volume's records. The Data section of each group lists the group's own files.

The symmetry-adapted bases for 2J = 23, 25, …, 121, too large for the repository, are attached to its release double-bases, one archive for each group. The file index.json gives the size and SHA-256 digest of each file of the records, of each volume's conventions, lattice and record schemas, and of the README, the references and the license. For each record it also lists the records that the record names.
