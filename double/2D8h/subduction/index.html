3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: group, subduction_embeddings in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: sections 12.2, 12.4 and 28. A column is a class of subgroups 𝖧\mathsf{H}, in the order of the table of section 3.1, by its symbol in the row subgrp as there, with the letter that tells apart classes with the same symbol, which are not conjugate in 𝟤𝖣𝟪𝗁\mathsf{2D_{8h}}; the symbol links to the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H}, the restriction of Γ\Gamma along a monomorphism from the record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} onto the subgroup, as a sum of the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}; the monomorphism identifies the subgroup with its record, and 𝖧\mathsf{H} names both. The rows of the vector irreps of a preimage are those of 𝖣𝟪𝗁\mathsf{D_{8h}} (vector_subduction); the column of a section is that of its preimage with the spinor irreps renamed (Conventions, section 27), which the record does not store. The record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} maps onto the subgroup in [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] ways, 𝘩↦𝘨𝘩𝘨−1\mathsfit{h} \mapsto \mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g}^{-1} (𝘩∈𝖧\mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{H}) for the 𝘨∈𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} that carry 𝖧\mathsf{H} to it, 𝖭\mathsf{N} and 𝖢\mathsf{C} the normalizer and the centralizer of 𝖧\mathsf{H} in 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}; the row centralizer of section Names and structure of the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H} gives 𝖢\mathsf{C} and this index. Two maps related by a conjugation in 𝟤𝖣𝟪𝗁\mathsf{2D_{8h}} give the same restriction, so the maps fall into [𝖭:𝖢]/[𝖭 𝟤𝖣𝟪𝗁:𝖢 𝟤𝖣𝟪𝗁][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}]/[\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{8h}}} : \mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{8h}}}] classes, 𝖭 𝟤𝖣𝟪𝗁\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{8h}}} and 𝖢 𝟤𝖣𝟪𝗁\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{8h}}} the normalizer and the centralizer of the subgroup in 𝟤𝖣𝟪𝗁\mathsf{2D_{8h}} (the table of section 3.1). A subgroup whose classes give different tables has one column per table, its settings: 1 for the stored monomorphism (monomorphism) and 2, 3, … for the alternative_settings, each with the images of the generators of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (setting). The row images gives, under each setting, each generator of 𝖧\mathsf{H} whose image differs between the settings, by the symbol of its class (Conventions, section 5.1), mapped to the class of 𝟤𝖣𝟪𝗁\mathsf{2D_{8h}} that holds its image; where two generators share a symbol, a superscript number in brackets gives the generator’s index in the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. Each other column under a subgroup is column 1 with each irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖧\mathsf{H} replaced by Λ\Lambda composed with one automorphism of 𝖧\mathsf{H} that a conjugation in 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} induces and none in 𝟤𝖣𝟪𝗁\mathsf{2D_{8h}} does (Conventions, section 12.1).
