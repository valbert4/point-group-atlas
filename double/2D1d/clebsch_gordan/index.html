2.5 Clebsch–Gordan coefficients ¶

Data: character_table, clebsch_gordan in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 10. Every irrep is one-dimensional, so each product Γ1⊗Γ2\Gamma_1\otimes\Gamma_2 is one irrep Γ\Gamma (the fusion rules of section 2.4) and its coefficient is ⟨Γ1 Γ2∣Γ⟩=1\langle \Gamma_1\,\Gamma_2\mid \Gamma\rangle = 1: ∣Γ⟩=∣Γ1⟩∣Γ2⟩\lvert \Gamma\rangle = \lvert \Gamma_1\rangle\lvert \Gamma_2\rangle (clebsch_gordan.products[]). The products of two vector irreps have the coefficients of 𝖣𝟣𝖽\mathsf{D_{1d}} (vector_products).
