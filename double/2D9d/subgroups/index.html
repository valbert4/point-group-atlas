3.1 Subgroup lattice ¶

Conventions: sections 15 and 28. The diagram shows the conjugacy classes of subgroups, each joined to the classes of its maximal subgroups, the orders increasing upward; a normal subgroup is framed, and ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of conjugate subgroups. A subgroup that contains 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} is the preimage of its image in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, named 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H} for the image 𝖧\mathsf{H}; one that does not is a section over its image, named 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The table lists the classes by order, then by the type of the image, a preimage before a section; subgrp is the representative 𝖧\mathsf{H} of a class of subgroups, order its order, conjugates the number of subgroups in the class and normal whether 𝖧\mathsf{H} is normal; classes lists the classes of 𝟤𝖣𝟫𝖽\mathsf{2D_{9d}}, other than 𝘌\mathsfit{E}, that 𝖧\mathsf{H} meets (a dash for none), and 𝖭 𝟤𝖣𝟫𝖽(𝖧)\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{9d}}}(\mathsf{H}) and 𝖢 𝟤𝖣𝟫𝖽(𝖧)\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{9d}}}(\mathsf{H}) are the classes of its normalizer and its centralizer in 𝟤𝖣𝟫𝖽\mathsf{2D_{9d}} (normalizer, centralizer); a subgroup’s other names in its class of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} follow ≅\cong (Conventions, section 20.2).
