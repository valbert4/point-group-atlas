2.1 Families of irreps ¶

Data: character_functions, subduction_basis in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 7, 16, 23 and 29.2. A row is a family of irreps, by its label, with its dimension dd, its Frobenius–Schur indicator νFS\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} (real for +1+1 and complex for 00) and the range of the index of its irreps. It also gives the family of the irreps over ℝ\mathbb{R} in which its irreps lie and the family of the complex-conjugate irreps. The kernel ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma is the subgroup of the elements that an irrep Γ\Gamma of the family maps to the identity, and the character of Γ\Gamma at 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is χΓ(𝘨)\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}). The image is the group formed by the irrep’s matrices, isomorphic to 𝟤𝖢∞𝗁/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{2C_{\infty h}}/\ker\Gamma. A row also gives the family’s character on each component, in the angle ω\omega of the lift. Where a function moves with the points (Conventions, section 4.3), the two rows of a pair exchange, and the mm of their kets (section 4.2, and at integer JJ the document of 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}}) distinguishes them. A dash stands for none. The vector families, on which 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} is the identity, are those of 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}} (vector_families), read on these classes through the covering map. A point ω\omega of an arc covers the rotation angle ϕ=ω\phi = \omega modulo 2π2\pi. The image and matrices of a vector family are those of 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}}, and its kernel is the preimage of its kernel in that group. The spinor families, on which 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} is −1-1, follow below the rule, with an instance for every half-integer n=12,32,…n = \tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{3}{2}, \dots. The image of a spinor family is 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, the group of the characters, turning the complex plane. As 2n2n is odd, the kernel of a spinor instance nn is a section (Conventions, section 27). The irreps are irreducible over ℂ\mathbb{C}. Over ℝ\mathbb{R} (Conventions, sections 6.4, 7 and 23.2), a real irrep is also an irrep in a basis with real matrices. The two irreps 1E{}^{1}\mathrm{\scriptsize E}, 2E{}^{2}\mathrm{\scriptsize E} of a complex-conjugate pair together form one irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} of dimension 2d2d and character 2Re⁡χ1E2\operatorname{Re}\chi_{{}^{1}\mathrm{\scriptsize E}}, labeled without the prefix when they are vector irreps. A complex-conjugate pair of spinor irreps forms one irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} of dimension 2d2d, named by the labels of its two members side by side, in irrep order. The boxed family, of one irrep, is the determinant character of 𝖢∞𝗁\mathsf{C_{\infty h}}, of value det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g} on the elements that cover 𝘨\mathsfit{g}.
