6.1 Spin–orbit levels ¶

Data: character_table, fusion, spin_orbit_levels, subduction_parent in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 19 and 25.2. A term 2S+1Γ{}^{2S+1}\Gamma is the vector irrep Γ\Gamma with the spin SS, a spin even under the inversion, as in the terms of the degenerate levels of 𝖣𝟣𝟣𝖽\mathsf{D_{11d}} (Conventions, section 19). Under spin–orbit coupling it splits into the irreps of 𝟤𝖣𝟣𝟣𝖽\mathsf{2D_{11d}} in

(6.1.1)(Γ⊗D(S,+1)) ⁣↓𝟤𝖣𝟣𝟣𝖽,(\Gamma \otimes D^{(S,+1)})\!\downarrow\mathsf{2D_{11d}},
(6.1.1) notation and links
Symbols
  • Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H} the restriction of the irrep Γ\Gamma to the subgroup 𝖧\mathsf{H}
  • Γ\Gamma an irrep of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, with Γ′\Gamma' an irrep of the parent record 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T}, 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O} or 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I} whose bases a derived record relabels
  • ⊗\otimes the tensor product of irreps, of representations and of their matrices
  • D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} the irrep of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} of angular momentum JJ and parity pp, pulled back from 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} at integer JJ
  • SS the spin of the electrons of a term
  • 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} the preimage of a point group 𝖦\mathsf{G} in 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}, its double group, such as 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T} and 𝟤𝖢𝟥𝗏\mathsf{2C_{3v}}

and they are spinor irreps at half-integer SS and vector irreps at integer SS. They follow from the products of section 2.4 and the multiplicities of section 4.1. Row Γ\Gamma, column 2S+12S + 1 of a table gives the levels of 2S+1Γ{}^{2S+1}\Gamma for every vector irrep Γ\Gamma and S=1 ⁣/2,1,…,7 ⁣/2S = \nicefrac{1}{2}, 1, \dots, \nicefrac{7}{2}, the nonzero spins of a d or f configuration, and the record holds them (spin_orbit_levels). The section Electronic terms of the document of 𝖣𝟣𝟣𝖽\mathsf{D_{11d}} gives the terms in the same way. For an orbital level of section 5.1, column 2S+1=22S + 1 = 2 gives the levels of one electron, and that section gives their states.
