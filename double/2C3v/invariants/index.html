The spinor irreps of 𝟤𝖢𝟥𝗏\mathsf{2C_{3v}} have dimension 1 and 2, as those of the binary dihedral group of order 12, the group of its matrices on uu, vv; among the finite groups of this catalogue only 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O}, 𝟤𝖳𝖽\mathsf{2T_{d}}, 𝟤𝖮𝗁\mathsf{2O_{h}}, 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I} and 𝟤𝖨𝗁\mathsf{2I_{h}} have spinor irreps of dimension above 2 [McK80]. The invariant polynomials in the partners of one are those of the group of its matrices, whose number in each degree its Molien series HV(t)H_V(t) below gives: in dimension 1 they follow the rule below; and in dimension 2 the matrices form a binary dihedral group, whose invariants are Klein’s forms [Kle84] of the degrees of its series.
