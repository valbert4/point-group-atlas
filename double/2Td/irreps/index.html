2.1 Irreducible representations ¶

Data: character_table, elements, group in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 6, 7 and 23. The character χΓ(𝘨)\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) of the irrep Γ\Gamma on the class of 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is given in section 2.2. The vector irreps (χΓ(𝘌ˉ)=+d\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{\bar{E}}) = +d, dd the dimension) are those of 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, on both lifts (vector_irreps), and the spinor irreps (χΓ(𝘌ˉ)=−d\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{\bar{E}}) = -d) follow below the rule. A spinor irrep is labeled by its dimension, A\mathrm{\scriptsize A} (1), E\mathrm{\scriptsize E} (2), G\mathrm{\scriptsize G} (4) or I\mathrm{\scriptsize I} (6), and a complex one by that of its complex-conjugate pair (E\mathrm{\scriptsize E} for dimension 1, G\mathrm{\scriptsize G} for dimension 2). The label carries the least half-integer JJ at which the irrep occurs in D(J,+1)D^{(J,+1)} of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} (section 4.1). A row is an irrep of dimension dd, with its Frobenius–Schur indicator νFS=∣𝟤𝖳𝖽∣−1∑𝘨∈𝟤𝖳𝖽χΓ(𝘨2)\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = |\mathsf{2T_{d}}|^{-1}\allowbreak\sum_{\mathsfit{g}\in\mathsf{2T_{d}}}\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}^2), which gives its reality: real (νFS=+1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = +1), pseudoreal (νFS=−1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = -1) or complex (νFS=0\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = 0, paired with its conjugate). The column conjugate gives that conjugate (a dash for the others). Each irrep lies in the irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} of its row. Bilbao is the irrep’s symbol Γn\Gamma_n in the double point group tables of [EBW+17], Γˉn\bar{\Gamma}_n for a spinor irrep (Conventions, sections 6.5 and 23.4). The kernel ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma is the subgroup of the elements mapped to the identity, named as in section 3.1. The image is the group formed by the irrep’s matrices, isomorphic to 𝟤𝖳𝖽/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{2T_{d}}/\ker\Gamma. For a vector irrep it is that of the irrep of 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}. For a spinor irrep of dimension 1 it is the cyclic group of the characters. For a spinor irrep of dimension 2 with its matrices in 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} it is the preimage 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H} formed by them. Otherwise it is the group of this catalogue isomorphic to it. The irreps are irreducible over ℂ\mathbb{C}. Over ℝ\mathbb{R} (Conventions, section 7), a real irrep is also an irrep in a basis with real matrices. Read as a real space of dimension 2d2d, a pseudoreal irrep is one irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} of character 2χΓ2\chi_{\Gamma} and keeps its own label. The boxed irrep is the determinant character of 𝖳𝖽\mathsf{T_{d}}, of value det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g} on the element (𝘨,b)(\mathsfit{g}, b).
