An object with symmetry 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{2D_{2d}} placed by 𝘘~∈𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\tilde{\mathsfit{Q}} \in \mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} is the same when placed by 𝘨𝘘~\mathsfit{g}\tilde{\mathsfit{Q}} with 𝘨∈𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{2D_{2d}}. Thus its orientations are the cosets 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽𝘘~={𝘨𝘘~:𝘨∈𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽}\mathsf{2D_{2d}}\tilde{\mathsfit{Q}} = \{\mathsfit{g}\tilde{\mathsfit{Q}} : \mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{2D_{2d}}\}, the points of the space 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{2D_{2d}}\backslash\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}. Volumes are those of the metric that the covering map carries to that of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} (Conventions, section 17), so that vol⁡𝖲𝖴(𝟤)=16π2\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{SU(2)} = 16\pi^2 and vol⁡𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)=32π2\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} = 32\pi^2, twice those of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}. A finite group has the counting measure, vol⁡𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽=∣𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽∣=16\operatorname{vol}\mathsf{2D_{2d}} = |\mathsf{2D_{2d}}| = 16. As 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{2D_{2d}} contains 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}}, a coset 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽𝘘~\mathsf{2D_{2d}}\tilde{\mathsfit{Q}} contains both lifts of every element of a coset of 𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{D_{2d}} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, and 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{2D_{2d}}\backslash\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} is 𝖣𝟤𝖽\𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{D_{2d}}\backslash\mathsf{O(3)}, 𝖣𝟤\𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{D_{2}}\backslash\mathsf{SO(3)}, a prism space. A cell’s rotations stand here for their canonical lifts (Conventions, section 21.1). Thus the cell 𝒟\mathcal{D}, with its facets and vertices, and the fundamental domain ℱ\mathcal{F} are those of the document of 𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{D_{2d}}, in its section Fundamental domain. Hence

The group 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{2D_{2d}} acts on the directions through the covering map, so its orbifold is that of the document of 𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{D_{2d}} (orbifold). Forgetting the turn about a direction sends the orientation space 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{2D_{2d}}\backslash\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} onto that orbifold. The harmonics on 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{2D_{2d}}\backslash\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} are those on 𝖣𝟤𝖽\𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{D_{2d}}\backslash\mathsf{O(3)} of the document of 𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{D_{2d}} (harmonics), of integer JJ since 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} acts on D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} (Conventions, section 25.1) by (−1)2J(-1)^{2J}. The invariant forms of section 5.2 are the functions on the singularity of type D6, the quotient of the spin kets by the matrices of the elements.
