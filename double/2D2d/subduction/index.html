3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: group, subduction_embeddings in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 12.2, 12.4 and 28. A column is a class of subgroups 𝖧\mathsf{H}, in the order of the table of section 3.1. The row subgrp names it by its symbol, as in that table, with the letter that distinguishes classes with the same symbol, which are not conjugate in 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{2D_{2d}}. The symbol links to the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H}, the restriction of Γ\Gamma along a monomorphism from the record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} onto the subgroup, as a sum of the irreps of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The monomorphism identifies the subgroup with its record, and 𝖧\mathsf{H} names both. The rows of the vector irreps of a preimage are those of 𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{D_{2d}} (vector_subduction). The column of a section, which the record does not store, is that of its preimage with the spinor irreps renamed (Conventions, section 27). The record of 𝖧\mathsf{H} maps onto the subgroup in [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] ways, 𝘩↦𝘨𝘩𝘨−1\mathsfit{h} \mapsto \mathsfit{g} \mathsfit{h} \mathsfit{g}^{-1} (𝘩∈𝖧\mathsfit{h} \in \mathsf{H}) for the 𝘨∈𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsfit{g} \in \mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} that carry 𝖧\mathsf{H} to it. Here 𝖭\mathsf{N} and 𝖢\mathsf{C} are the normalizer and centralizer of 𝖧\mathsf{H} in 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}. In the section Names and structure of the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}, the row centralizer gives 𝖢\mathsf{C} and the index [𝖭:𝖢][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}] (the index in the row normalizer is [𝖭:𝖧][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{H}]). Two maps related by a conjugation in 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{2D_{2d}} give the same restriction. Thus the maps fall into [𝖭:𝖢]/[𝖭 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽:𝖢 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽][\mathsf{N} : \mathsf{C}]/[\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{2d}}} : \mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{2d}}}] classes, where 𝖭 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{2d}}} and 𝖢 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{2d}}} are the normalizer and centralizer of the subgroup in 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{2D_{2d}} (the table of section 3.1). A subgroup whose classes of maps give different tables has one column for each table: setting 1 for the stored monomorphism (monomorphism) and settings 2, 3, … for the alternative_settings. Each setting has the images of the generators of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (setting). The row images gives, under each setting, each generator whose image differs between the settings, in their order, mapped to the class of 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{2D_{2d}} that contains its image. Each generator is written by the symbol of its class (Conventions, section 5.1), and where two share a symbol, a superscript number in brackets gives the generator’s index in the document of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. Each other setting is setting 1 with each irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖧\mathsf{H} replaced by Λ\Lambda composed with one automorphism of 𝖧\mathsf{H} induced by a conjugation in 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} and by none in 𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽\mathsf{2D_{2d}} (Conventions, section 12.1).

Read down a column, a table gives induction (Frobenius reciprocity): Γ\Gamma occurs in Ind⁡𝖧𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{2D_{2d}}} \Lambda as often as Λ\Lambda occurs in the entry of row Γ\Gamma. For every entry of a spinor irrep, the record holds the unitary matrix that carries Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H} into the direct sum of the matrices of 𝖧\mathsf{H} (subduction[].blocks). For every class 𝖧\mathsf{H} it holds the left coset representatives (transversal). From both, Ind⁡𝖧𝟤𝖣𝟤𝖽Λ\operatorname{Ind}_{\mathsf{H}}^{\mathsf{2D_{2d}}} \Lambda and its intertwiners follow as in Conventions, section 12.4.
