Vector families. The entry of row Γ\Gamma is Γ ⁣↓𝖧\Gamma\!\downarrow\mathsf{H}: the vector families of 𝟤𝖢∞\mathsf{2C_{\infty }}, those of 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, restrict to the preimage 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H} of a closed subgroup 𝖧\mathsf{H} of 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} as 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}} restricts them to 𝖧\mathsf{H}, whose irreps are the vector irreps of 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H}, and to a section as to its preimage (vector_entry, vector_rules).
