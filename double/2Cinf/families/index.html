2.1 Families of irreps ¶

Data: character_functions in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: sections 7, 16, 23 and 29.2. A row is a family of irreps, by its label: its dimension dd, its Frobenius–Schur indicator νFS\nu_{\mathrm{FS}}, index the range of the index of its instances, kernel the subgroup ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma of the elements that an irrep Γ\Gamma of the family maps to the identity, image the group that its matrices form, isomorphic to 𝟤𝖢∞/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{2C_{\infty }}/\ker\Gamma, and its character on each component, in the angle ω\omega of the lift. The vector families, on which 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} is the identity, are those of 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, read on these classes through the covering map: a point ω\omega of an arc covers the rotation angle ϕ=ω\phi = \omega modulo 2π2\pi (vector_families); the kernel of one is the preimage of its kernel there, its image and its matrices are those of 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}. The spinor families, on which 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} is −1-1, follow below the rule, one for every half-integer n=12,32,…n = \tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{3}{2}, \dots; the image of one is the group of its characters, turning the complex plane, 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}. As 2n2n is odd, the kernel of a spinor instance nn is a section (Conventions, section 27). The boxed family, of one irrep, is the determinant character of 𝖢∞\mathsf{C_{\infty}}, whose character is det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g} on the elements that cover 𝘨\mathsfit{g}.
