3.1 Subgroup lattice ¶

Conventions: sections 15 and 28. The diagram shows the conjugacy classes of subgroups, each joined to the classes of its maximal subgroups, the orders increasing upward; a normal subgroup is framed, and ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of conjugate subgroups. A subgroup that contains 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} is the preimage of its image in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, named 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H} for the image 𝖧\mathsf{H}; one that does not is a section over its image, named 𝖧\mathsf{H}.
