Conventions: sections 14, 26.2 and 29.4. The kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle at half-integer JJ are the states of section 2.2 (𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} acts as −1-1, 𝘪~\mathsfit{\tilde{i}} as pp on D(J,p)D^{(J,p)}), and a vector is listed by its coefficients eme_m:

(4.3.1) notation and links
Defines
  • ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle the ket of angular momentum JJ and zz component mm; for integer JJ a spherical harmonic
  • eme_m the coefficient of the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle in a basis vector, its amplitude being emFm/tote_m\sqrt{F_m/\mathrm{tot}}
  • FmF_m the weight (J+m)! (J−m)!(J + m)!\,(J - m)! of the ket ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle
  • tot\mathrm{tot} the normalization ∑m∣em∣2Fm\sum_m \lvert e_m\rvert^2 F_m of a basis vector
  • vv a symmetry-adapted vector, a combination of kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle; vμv_\mu, the partner μ\mu of a block
Symbols
  • mm a summation index
  • JJ the angular momentum of an irrep D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}: J=0,12,1,32,…J = 0, \tfrac{1}{2}, 1, \tfrac{3}{2}, \dots
