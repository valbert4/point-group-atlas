3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: subduction_rules in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 16.1 and 29.6. Below is the restriction of D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} to each closed subgroup 𝖧\mathsf{H} of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} (section 3.1) for every half-integer JJ and both parities (subduction_rules). At integer JJ the restriction is that of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, in its section Descent of symmetry. For an axial 𝖧\mathsf{H} the restriction at half-integer JJ is the direct sum, over the kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle, of the terms of the first true case of its rule. For 𝟤𝖢∞𝗏\mathsf{2C_{\infty v}}, 𝟤𝖣∞\mathsf{2D_{\infty }}, 𝟤𝖣∞𝗁\mathsf{2D_{\infty h}} and the preimages of 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}}, 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} and 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}}, the sum runs over the pairs ∣ ±mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,\pm m}\bigr\rangle. In the rules, q=m mod Nq = m \bmod N. However, in the preimages of 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖣N𝖽\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}d}} with NN even and of 𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}h}} and 𝖣N𝗁\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}h}} with NN odd, qq is taken modulo 2N2N, shifted by NN at p=−1p = -1. The map (U,ε)↦U(U, \varepsilon) \mapsto U carries these preimages onto 𝟤𝖢𝟤N\mathsf{2C_{2\mathnormal{N}}} or 𝟤𝖣𝟤N\mathsf{2D_{2\mathnormal{N}}}. An irrep is named by the least JJ at which it occurs in the restriction of D(J,p)D^{(J,p)}, where p=−1p = -1 for the tag u\mathrm{u} and p=+1p = +1 otherwise (Conventions, section 23.2). In the rules of a family, the terms are irreps of the member, labeled as in Conventions, sections 6.1 and 23.2. The terms of a rule whose subgroup contains 𝘪~\mathsfit{\tilde{i}}, which acts on D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} as pp, are written at p=+1p = +1. A section over 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} or 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} (NN odd) is restricted as its preimage, with each spinor irrep renamed by the section’s record (Conventions, section 27). The multiplicity bΛ(J)b_\Lambda(J) of an irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖧\mathsf{H} at p=+1p = +1 is the coefficient of t2Jt^{2J} in its generating function HΛ(t)=∑J≥1/2bΛ(J) t2JH_\Lambda(t) = \sum_{J\ge1/2} b_\Lambda(J)\,t^{2J}. The documents of the subgroups use this definition in their section Multiplicities and allowed mm. At p=−1p = -1 the multiplicity of Λ\Lambda is that of the product of Λ\Lambda and the determinant character of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. In the generating functions, kk is the subscript of an irrep Ek\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{k}, and nn the index of a family of irreps of a continuous subgroup. The document of each preimage except the members with N>12N > 12 lists the restrictions JJ by JJ at both parities, in its section Subduction from 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}.
