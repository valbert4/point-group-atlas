\section{The cover}\label{s:cover}
Conventions: \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, sections \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.20.1}{20.1}, \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.21.1}{21.1} and \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.21.2}{21.2}.
\leavevmode\label{n:group.pin.minus}\label{n:group.su2}\label{n:local.pin.sign}\label{n:matrix.covered}\label{n:matrix.lift.su2}\label{n:index.bar}$\grp{2C_{7v}}$ is the preimage of the point group $\grp{C_{7v}}$ in $\grp{Pin^{-}(3)} = \grp{SU(2)} \dirprod \{+1, -1\}$, whose element $(U, \varepsilon)$ covers $\varepsilon R(U) \in \grp{O(3)}$, $R(U)$ the rotation that $U$ covers: it is the double group of $\grp{C_{7v}}$, with the $2 \cdot 14 = 28$ elements $(g, 0) = (U(g), \det g)$ and $(g, 1) = (-U(g), \det g)$, $g \in \grp{C_{7v}}$, $U(g)$ the canonical lift of $g$ to $\grp{SU(2)}$ (\href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, section \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.21.1}{21.1}). \label{n:function.cocycle}With $U(h)\,U(g) = (-1)^{c(g,h)}\,U(gh)$, $gh$ = first $g$, then $h$, the product is
\begin{equation}\label{e:product}(g, b)(h, b') = \bigl(gh,\ b + b' + c(g, h) \bmod 2\bigr).\end{equation}
\leavevmode\label{n:element.named}\label{n:mark.bar}\label{n:mark.lift}$\elt{\barred{E}} = (\elt{E}, 1)$ is central of order 2, and $(g, b) \mapsto g$ is the covering map, with kernel $\{\elt{E}, \elt{\barred{E}}\}$; a barred element $\elt{\barred{X}}$ is $(\elt{X}, 1)$ for the element $\elt{X}$ of $\grp{C_{7v}}$, and $\elt{\lift{i}} = (1, -1)$, the lift of the inversion, is central of order 2.

\section{Symmetry operations}\label{s:elements}
Conventions: \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, sections \href{../../../rotation/CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.4}{4}, \href{../../../rotation/CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.5.1}{5.1} and \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.21}{21}.
\leavevmode\label{n:index.element}\label{n:matrix.element.pin}\label{n:element.g}\label{n:matrix.element}Element $k$ of $\grp{2C_{7v}}$ is the pair $(g, b)$ of the element $g$ of \href{../../../rotation/data/pdf/C7v.pdf}{$\grp{C_{7v}}$} of index $\lfloor k/2\rfloor$ in its document and $b = k - 2\lfloor k/2\rfloor$: $(g, 0)$ is the canonical lift of $g$ and $(g, 1)$ its barred partner (section~\ref{s:cover}); from section~\ref{s:character_table} on, $g$ stands for the element $(g, b)$ of $\grp{2C_{7v}}$, and $\det g$ is its parity. $M(k)$ is the matrix of the element, the transpose of its $\grp{SU(2)}$ part $(-1)^{b}\,U(g)$, and the matrices compose as the matrices $P(g)$ of the elements of $\grp{C_{7v}}$:
\begin{equation}\label{e:mprod}M(k)\,M(k') = M(kk'),\qquad kk' = \text{first } k\text{, then } k'.\end{equation}

\leavevmode\label{n:head.degree}\label{n:head.form}The matrices of the elements on $u$, $v$ form the binary dihedral group of order 28, of type D\textsubscript{9}, whose invariant forms are Klein's [\href{../../../REFERENCES.pdf\#ref.Kle84}{Kle84}], those of degree $2J$ the kets at $J$ that $\grp{2C_{7v}}$ leaves invariant [\href{../../../REFERENCES.pdf\#ref.Vil68}{Vil68}, chapter III, section 2]; the number of independent invariant forms of degree $n$ is the coefficient of $t^n$ in

\section{\texorpdfstring{$jj$}{jj} configurations}\label{s:jj}
Conventions: \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, sections \href{../../../rotation/CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.19}{19} and \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.25.3}{25.3}.
A spinor level is a Kramers level: a pseudoreal spinor irrep alone, a real one twice, a complex-conjugate pair together. $n$ electrons in a level of dimension $d$ fill its exterior power $\Lambda^{n}$, $n = 0, \dots, d$: vector irreps at even $n$, spinor irreps at odd $n$. Every level of $\grp{2C_{7v}}$ has dimension 2: $\Lambda^{1}$ is the level, and $\Lambda^{0}$ and $\Lambda^{2}$ are the trivial irrep (\texttt{jj\symbol{95}configurations}).
