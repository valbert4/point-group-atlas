\section{Class space}\label{s:class_space}
Conventions: \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, sections \href{../../../rotation/CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.16}{16}, \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.21}{21} and \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.29.1}{29.1}.
\leavevmode\label{n:angle.omega}\label{n:angle.psi}\label{n:head.component}\label{n:head.coset}\label{n:head.element}\label{n:matrix.element.pin}A row is a component of the class space of $\grp{2D_{\infty }}$, an arc of classes or an atom, a single class; coset tells whether its elements cover proper or improper elements of \href{../../../rotation/data/pdf/Dinf.pdf}{$\grp{D_{\infty}}$}, element gives the element of $\grp{Pin^{-}(3)}$ at its angle: the lift $\lift{R}_z(\omega) = \cos\tfrac{\omega}{2} - \iunit\sin\tfrac{\omega}{2}\,\pauli{z}$, $0 \le \omega < 4\pi$ and the lifts of the binary rotations at the azimuth $\psi$, $0 \le \psi < 2\pi$ (\href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, section \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.29.1}{29.1}), and $M(g)$ is the matrix of that element, the transpose of its $\grp{SU(2)}$ part, so that the matrices compose as the matrices $P(g)$ of the elements of \href{../../../rotation/data/pdf/Dinf.pdf}{$\grp{D_{\infty}}$} do (\href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, section \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.21.2}{21.2}).
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}\toprule
component & coset & element & $M(g)$\\\midrule
$\comp{\lift{C}(\omega)}$ & proper & $\elt{\lift{C}}(\omega)$ & $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\expe^{-\iunit\omega/2} & 0\\ 0 & \expe^{\iunit\omega/2}\end{smallmatrix}\right)$\\\addlinespace[6pt]
$\comp{\infty\lift{C}_2'}$ & proper & $\elt{\lift{C}_2'}(\psi)$ & $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0 & -\iunit\expe^{\iunit\psi}\\ -\iunit\expe^{-\iunit\psi} & 0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
On an arc, $\elt{\lift{R}_z}(\omega)$ is conjugate to $\elt{\lift{R}_z}(4\pi - \omega)$, so the classes run over $0 \le \omega \le 2\pi$, from $\cls{E}$ through the half turns to $\cls{\barred{E}}$; an atom is one class: conjugation by $\elt{\lift{R}_z}(\pi)$ carries a lift of a binary rotation to the other lift.
The distinguished points are $\cls{E}$ at $\omega = 0$, $\cls{C_2}$ at $\omega = \pi$, $\cls{\barred{E}}$ at $\omega = 2\pi$.
\leavevmode\label{n:local.haar.h}The Haar measure, of total mass 1: $\diff h = \tfrac{1}{2}\,\diff\omega/(2\pi)$ on $\comp{\lift{C}(\omega)}$, of mass $\tfrac{1}{2}$; the atom $\comp{\infty\lift{C}_2'}$ has mass $\tfrac{1}{2}$.

\section{Fundamental domain}\label{s:fundamental_domain}
Conventions: \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, sections \href{../../../rotation/CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.17}{17} and \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.21.3}{21.3}.
\leavevmode\label{n:operator.cosets}An object with symmetry $\grp{2D_{\infty }}$ placed by $\lift{Q} \in \grp{Pin^{-}(3)}$ is the same when placed by $g\lift{Q}$ with $g \in \grp{2D_{\infty }}$: its orientations are the cosets $\grp{2D_{\infty }}\lift{Q} = \{g\lift{Q} : g \in \grp{2D_{\infty }}\}$, the points of the space $\grp{2D_{\infty }}\backslash\grp{Pin^{-}(3)}$. \label{n:operator.volume}Volumes are those of the metric that the covering map carries to that of \href{../../../rotation/data/pdf/O3.pdf}{$\grp{O(3)}$} (\href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, section \href{../../../rotation/CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.17}{17}), so that $\vol\grp{SU(2)} = 16\pi^2$ and $\vol\grp{Pin^{-}(3)} = 32\pi^2$, twice those of $\grp{SO(3)}$ and $\grp{O(3)}$.

\section{\texorpdfstring{$jj$}{jj} configurations}\label{s:jj}
Conventions: \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf}{CONVENTIONS.pdf}, sections \href{../../../rotation/CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.19}{19} and \href{../../CONVENTIONS.pdf\#conv.25.3}{25.3}.
A spinor level is a Kramers level: a pseudoreal spinor irrep alone, a real one twice, a complex-conjugate pair together. $n$ electrons in a level of dimension $d$ fill its exterior power $\Lambda^{n}$, $n = 0, \dots, d$: vector irreps at even $n$, spinor irreps at odd $n$. Every level of $\grp{2D_{\infty }}$ has dimension 2: $\Lambda^{1}$ is the level, and $\Lambda^{0}$ and $\Lambda^{2}$ are the trivial irrep (\texttt{jj\symbol{95}configurations}).
