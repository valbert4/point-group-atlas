A binary form f=∑ici uivn−if = \sum_i c_i\, u^i v^{n-i} of degree nn in the spin kets u=∣ 1/21/2⟩u = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,1/2}\bigr\rangle and v=∣ −1/21/2⟩v = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,-1/2}\bigr\rangle is invariant when kf=fk f = f for every element kk, the elements acting at the parity +1+1 (Conventions, section 26.4); at the parity −1-1 the invariants of odd degree are the semi-invariants of det⁡𝖦\det_{\mathsf{G}} and those of even degree the same. The matrices of the elements on uu, vv form the cyclic group of order 11 about zz, whose invariant forms [Kle84] are the polynomials in uvuv, u11u^{11} and v11v^{11}, with the one relation (uv)11=u11v11(uv)^{11} = u^{11}v^{11}, their number in degree nn the coefficient of tnt^n in H(t)=(1+t11)/((1−t2)(1−t11))H(t) = (1 + t^{11})/((1-t^{2})(1-t^{11})), and the semi-invariants are the sums of monomials uivn−iu^i v^{n-i} on which the elements act alike. A section has no spinor irreps (Conventions, section 27), so its record has no Molien series of spinor irreps: its irreps have dimension 1, and the invariants of one whose values are the mm-th roots of unity are the powers of the mm-th power of its partner.
