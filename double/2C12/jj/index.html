6.2 jjjj configurations ¶

Data: jj_configurations in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 19 and 25.3. A spinor level is a Kramers level: a pseudoreal spinor irrep alone, a real one twice or a complex-conjugate pair together. In a level of dimension dd, nn electrons fill its exterior power Λn\Lambda^{n}, n=0,…,dn = 0, \dots, d: vector irreps at even nn and spinor irreps at odd nn. Every level of 𝟤𝖢𝟣𝟤\mathsf{2C_{12}} has dimension 2: Λ1\Lambda^{1} is the level, and Λ0\Lambda^{0} and Λ2\Lambda^{2} are the trivial irrep (jj_configurations).
