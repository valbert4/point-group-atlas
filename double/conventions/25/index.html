25.1 Subduction from the parent ¶

The irrep D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}, of angular momentum J=0,12,1,…J = 0, \tfrac{1}{2}, 1, \dots and parity p=±1p = \pm 1, is DJ(U)D^{J}(U) on (U,ε)(U, \varepsilon), times ε\varepsilon when p=−1p = -1. At integer JJ it is the irrep of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} pulled back, whose subduction is that of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (integer_J, section 11). subduction_parent gives the multiplicities bΓ(J,p)b_{\Gamma}(J, p) of the spinor irreps at half-integer JJ with 2J≤412J \le 41, for p=+1p = +1 (g) and p=−1p = -1 (u): table[k] at J=k+12J = k + \tfrac{1}{2}, and

(25.1)bΓ(J,p)=residues[(J−12) mod period][Γ]+increment[Γ]⋅⌊(J−12)/period⌋.b_\Gamma(J, p) = \texttt{residues}[(J - \tfrac{1}{2}) \bmod \texttt{period}][\Gamma] + \texttt{increment}[\Gamma] \cdot \lfloor (J - \tfrac{1}{2})/\texttt{period} \rfloor.

25.2 Spin–orbit levels ¶

A term 2S+1Γ{}^{2S+1}\Gamma, the orbital level Γ\Gamma of 𝖦\mathsf{G} with the spin SS (section 19), splits under spin–orbit coupling into the irreps of 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} in (Γ⊗D(S,+1))↓𝟤𝖦(\Gamma \otimes D^{(S,+1)})\downarrow \mathsf{2G}, the spin being even under the inversion: spinor irreps at half-integer SS, vector irreps at integer SS. spin_orbit_levels gives them for S=12,1,…,72S = \tfrac{1}{2}, 1, \dots, \tfrac{7}{2}, the nonzero spins of a d or f configuration (a singlet keeps its level), from the products of section 24 and the multiplicities of section 25.1: twoS_range, and levels, one per vector irrep Γ\Gamma (orbital, orbital_id) and twoS, with the irreps of the restriction (terms: irrep, irrep_id, multiplicity).

25.3 jj configurations ¶

jj_configurations gives the configurations of electrons in a spinor level, the U(N) branching of section 19 along a level of one electron with its spin. A spinor level is a Kramers level: a pseudoreal spinor irrep alone, a real one twice, a complex-conjugate pair together (members, kind, dim), the rule of time reversal on spinors. nn electrons in a level of dimension dd fill its exterior power Λn\Lambda^{n}, n=0,…,dn = 0, \dots, d (powers: n and the multiplicities terms): vector irreps at even nn, spinor irreps at odd nn. Its character follows from the characters of the level on the powers of each element by Newton's identities, and Λd−n\Lambda^{d-n} is Λn⊗Λd\Lambda^{n} \otimes \Lambda^{d}. The continuous preimages give the levels of their instances up to 72\tfrac{7}{2}; in SU2 these are the configurations of nn electrons in the subshells of s, p, d and f electrons.

A vector lists the nonzero coefficients cc of ∑c f s\sum c\, f\, s (function, twoMs, c), up to a positive factor: coprime Gaussian integers when they are Gaussian rationals up to one positive factor, and otherwise divided by their positive rational content.
