28 Subgroup lattices ¶

28.1 The lattice ¶

subgroup_lattice lists every class of subgroups, preimages and sections, as section 15.1 does, with double_class and double_symbol in place of o3_class and o3_schoenflies, and double_classes and double_classes_by_order in place of o3_classes and o3_classes_by_order (counts). It is stored whole: its preimage nodes repeat what the lattice of 𝖦\mathsf{G} gives.

28.2 Subduction along embeddings ¶

subduction_embeddings is that of section 12.2. The rows of the vector irreps along the embedding of a preimage, when they are those of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, are its pointer vector_subduction. The embedding of a section has no rows: preimage_node names the embedding of its preimage, whose rows, with the spinor irreps renamed, are its rows (section 27).

28.3 The lattice of the catalogue ¶

data/double_groups.json (schema "point-group-atlas/double/lattice/1") orders the 112 classes of the catalogue by containment, as point_groups.json orders those of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} (section 15): nodes (each record: id, symbol, kind, order, class "preimage" with covers, or "section" with image), aliases (the four N=1N = 1 names of preimages of another record's class), edges (the covering relations, lower, upper, with a witness: the node or the entry of the larger record that holds the smaller class, or for Pinminus3 an argument), counts and family_rules.
