24 Fusion and Clebsch–Gordan coefficients ¶

fusion and clebsch_gordan hold every product with a spinor factor, by sections 10.1, 10.3 and 10.4 with the irreps of the preimage; the products of two vector irreps are those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (vector_products).

The copies of an irrep Γ\Gamma that occurs more than once in Γ1⊗Γ2\Gamma_1 \otimes \Gamma_2 are separated by exchange, then crossing (section 10.4). A class of several copies, or all of them when neither rule splits them, is ordered by SU(2) parentage. Each factor occurs once in a D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} at its least JJ (then p=+1p = +1 before −1-1), its parent, as a block of section 26.3; a copy then lies in the product of the parents, a sum of components of total angular momentum. The first copy is the one in the components of least total angular momentum, and each next one, orthogonal to those before, in the least that admits one; parent_twoJ is twice that angular momentum, and separation is su2-parent when parentage alone separates the copies. A document lists the blocks of a product when each row of their coefficients fills at most four lines, and names the others.
