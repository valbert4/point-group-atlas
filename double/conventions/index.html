Conventions

This document defines the fields of the records of the double point groups, the closed subgroups of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} up to conjugacy. It is the second of the files of conventions, which are numbered as one: sections 0 to 19 are those of the point groups (rotation/CONVENTIONS.md), whose notation this document uses, with half-integer JJ (section 25.1) and the tags of the spinor irreps (section 23.2), and whose sections it cites by number, and sections 20 to 29 are this document's. A record of this catalogue holds only what no record of the point groups holds (section 20.3), but for its subgroup lattice, which it stores whole (section 28.1).
