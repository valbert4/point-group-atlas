23 Spinor irreps ¶

23.1 Characters ¶

An irrep Γ\Gamma of a preimage is a vector irrep, χΓ(𝘌ˉ)=d\chi_{\Gamma}(\bar{\mathsfit{E}}) = d, or a spinor irrep, χΓ(𝘌ˉ)=−d\chi_{\Gamma}(\bar{\mathsfit{E}}) = -d. The vector irreps are those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} with χΓ(𝘨,b)=χΓ(𝘨)\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}, b) = \chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) (vector_irreps); irreps lists the spinor irreps, with the fields of section 6.2, spinor true, kernel a node of the record's own lattice (section 28.1), and image the group that its matrices form, isomorphic to 𝟤𝖦/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{2G}/\ker\Gamma: 𝖢n\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{n}}} for dimension 1 (n=∣𝟤𝖦∣/∣ker⁡Γ∣n = |\mathsf{2G}| / |\ker\Gamma|, the cyclic group of its characters); for dimension 2 with every matrix in 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)}, the preimage 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H} in 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} that they form, by its order and the largest order of its elements; otherwise the group of the catalogue isomorphic to it, the record's own when Γ\Gamma is faithful and its su2_subgroup when the kernel is a lift of the inversion. A vector irrep's image is that of the irrep of 𝖦\mathsf{G}.

23.2 Labels and ids ¶

A spinor irrep is labeled by its dimension, A (1), E (2), G (4) or I (6), a complex one by the dimension of its complex-conjugate pair as in section 6.1 (E for dimension 1, G for dimension 2), with the subscript the least half-integer JJ at which it occurs in D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} (section 25.1), p=+1p = +1 or, when 𝖦\mathsf{G} holds the inversion, pp the parity of its tag (+1+1 for g, −1-1 for u). When 𝖦\mathsf{G} holds the inversion the label takes the tag g or u of the character ±d\pm d on the lift (1,−1)(1, -1) of the inversion; otherwise, when the two lifts of σ𝘩\mathsfit{\sigma_{h}} are not conjugate, it takes the tag ' or '' of the character ±i d\pm\mathrm{i}\,d on the canonical lift of σ𝘩\mathsfit{\sigma_{h}}. Two complex irreps that would share a label carry the prefixes 1 and 2, 1 with the character of smaller argument in [0,2π)[0, 2\pi) (then of smaller modulus) on the first class where they differ; a complex pair is one irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R}, named by both labels (pair_label). The id writes J=n/2J = n/2 as nh (E1h, 1E3h, G3hg). The spinor irreps follow the tags (g or none before u, ' before ''), then the letter, JJ and the prefix.

23.3 Matrices ¶

The matrices of a spinor irrep obey section 9 in the element order of section 21.2, by the first construction that applies: linear for dimension 1, the character; wigner, the action of section 26.1 at the 2J2J of twoJ and the first parity parity, +1+1 before −1-1, at which the kets of twoM carry the irrep: ∣ ∓JJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,\mp J}\bigr\rangle for dimension 2 of an axial group, all the kets for dimension 2J+12J + 1; twist, the matrices of the spinor irrep from times the one-dimensional irrep by; galois, the matrices of from with the automorphism ζℳ↦ζℳsigma\zeta_{\mathcal{M}} \mapsto \zeta_{\mathcal{M}}^{\texttt{sigma}} applied entrywise, ℳ=\mathcal{M} = conductor, sigma the least that works among those that fix i\mathrm{i} and ζ3\zeta_3, then the least. A document writes the matrices of an irrep, or those of section 21.2, over a common rational factor when that makes the widest of them at least a fifth narrower; each entry is then an integer times a square root times a sum of integer multiples of 1, φ\varphi, i\mathrm{i} and iφ\mathrm{i}\varphi (5\sqrt{5} for 2φ−12\varphi - 1).

23.4 bethe and bilbao ¶

The spinor irreps of the 36 preimages of the groups of section 6.5 carry bethe and bilbao too, their vector irreps those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (vector_irreps); every other spinor irrep has null not-in-source. In both numberings the spinor irreps come after the vector ones; bethe writes the sign of the inversion as in section 6.5, bilbao a bar and no sign, "bar Gamma_5" for Γˉ5\bar{\Gamma}_{5}. The tables of bethe [KDWS63] give a spinor character on the canonical lift (𝘨,0)(\mathsfit{g}, 0) of section 21.1: their unbarred operation is the rotation through an angle in (−π,π](-\pi, \pi] about the axis that they name, or 𝘪\mathsfit{i} times one, the inversion leaving spinors as they are. The double point groups of bilbao [EBW+17] are these preimages element for element: the 2×22 \times 2 matrix of an operation is a lift of its rotation part, the identity for the inversion (the lift (1,−1)(1, -1) of section 20.1), and the operation marked d is the other lift. On its hexagonal tables the matrices are those of [BC72], whose rows and columns run over m=−12,12m = -\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{1}{2}: in the usual order they are lifts in the frame with xx along [120], yy along [100] and zz against the hexagonal axis c, so that 2_001 and m_001 are barred lifts and 2_1-10 and m_1-10 the lifts about the axes that they name. In 𝖣𝟥𝖽\mathsf{D_{3d}} the binary rotations fix the frame of both works, so that their mirrors σ𝘥=𝘪 𝘊𝟤′\mathsfit{\sigma_{d}} = \mathsfit{i}\,\mathsfit{C_{2}'} are the record's (σ𝘥,1)(\mathsfit{\sigma_{d}}, 1), the canonical lifts of 𝘪\mathsfit{i} and of a binary rotation multiplying to a barred mirror there. Read with the opposite orientation of a work's frame, the complex pairs of spinor irreps of 2C2, 2C1h, 2C2h, 2C1v, 2D1, 2D1d, 2C4, 2S4, 2C4h, 2C3, 2S6, 2C6, 2C3h, 2C6h, 2D3, 2C3v, 2D3d, 2T and 2Th would exchange their symbols; the rule of section 6.5 fixes them.
