26 Kets and symmetry-adapted bases ¶

26.1 The action on kets ¶

An element (U,ε)(U, \varepsilon) acts on the kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle of D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} by DJ(U)†D^{J}(U)^{\dagger}, times pp when ε=−1\varepsilon = -1, where DJD^{J} is the Wigner matrix of section 9. At integer JJ with p=(−1)Jp = (-1)^J this action is that of section 4.1 on the spherical harmonics (at the other parity, that action times ε\varepsilon). The element 𝘌ˉ\bar{\mathsfit{E}} multiplies a ket by (−1)2J(-1)^{2J}. Where a function moves with the points (section 4.3), (U,ε)(U, \varepsilon) has the matrix UU, the transpose of matrix, and acts on these kets by DJ(U)D^{J}(U), times pp when ε=−1\varepsilon = -1. In that convention the product of the pairs of section 20.1 applies its right factor first.

Time reversal Θ\varTheta acts on the kets of half-integer JJ as Θ∣ mJ⟩=(−1)J−m∣ −mJ⟩\varTheta \bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle = (-1)^{J-m} \bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle, an antiunitary map with Θ2=−1\varTheta^2 = -1. It replaces each coefficient eme_m of section 14 by (−1)J+m(-1)^{J+m} times the conjugate of e−me_{-m}. With Θ\varTheta adjoined to the preimage, a spinor irrep Λ\Lambda of indicator νFS=−1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = -1, 1 or 0 falls in the case (a), (b) or (c) of section 32.2. Every block of the bases at half-integer JJ, in the records and in the files, is adapted to time reversal by its case:

  1. Case (a): Θvμ=∑νvνNν,μ\varTheta v_\mu = \sum_\nu v_\nu N_{\nu,\mu}. In a finite preimage the matrix NN is d\sqrt{d} times the block of Λ⊗Λ\Lambda \otimes \Lambda into the trivial irrep (section 24), with its row (μ1,μ2)(\mu_1, \mu_2) as row μ1\mu_1 and column μ2\mu_2. In a continuous preimage it is the matrix of Θ\varTheta on the kets of section 29.2.
  2. Case (b): the copies κ\kappa and κ+1\kappa + 1, κ\kappa odd, are a pair v(1)v^{(1)}, v(2)v^{(2)} with Θv(1)=−v(2)N\varTheta v^{(1)} = -v^{(2)} N and Θv(2)=v(1)N\varTheta v^{(2)} = v^{(1)} N.
  3. Case (c): the block of the second irrep of a complex pair at JJ and κ\kappa is Θ\varTheta applied to the block of the first irrep there, partner by partner.

The first nonzero coefficient of partner 1 has the phase form of section 14. This holds in the blocks of case (a), in the first block of each pair and in the blocks of the first irreps of the complex pairs.

26.2 Bases ¶

At integer JJ the bases of subduction_basis are those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (integer_J). At half-integer JJ the field subduction_basis gives the bases as in section 14, with 2J2J and 2m2m in place of JJ and mm (twoJ, twoM, seed_twoM). It gives the closed form of an axial preimage, its blocks for 2J≤212J \le 21, the multiplicities, the generating function in t2Jt^{2J} and the allowed 2m2m.

The closed forms adapt their blocks to time reversal (section 26.1) by these rules, written in cases:

  1. A two-dimensional block ∣ m1J⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m_1}\bigr\rangle, c ∣ −m1J⟩c\,\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m_1}\bigr\rangle has c=±1c = \pm 1, and both partners are multiplied by i\mathrm{i} when c=(−1)J−m1c = (-1)^{J-m_1}.
  2. The block of the second irrep of a complex pair is the image of the first irrep's: the ket (−1)J+m∣ mJ⟩(-1)^{J+m}\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle, or a pair ∣ mJ⟩+s ∣ −mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle + s\,\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle times (−1)J−m(-1)^{J-m} and the conjugate of ss.
  3. In a preimage with 𝟤𝖦=𝖠\mathsf{2G} = \mathsf{A} the kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle and ∣ −mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle, m>0m > 0, of a real irrep give the pair of copies ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle and −(−1)J−m∣ −mJ⟩-(-1)^{J-m}\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-m}\bigr\rangle.

26.3 The exceptional preimages ¶

The bases of 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T}, 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O} and 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I} at half-integer JJ are built as in section 14.2, with the action of section 26.1 at p=+1p = +1 written on the coefficients eme_m of section 14. On these coefficients it has no square roots. As there, the copies of an irrep of case (a) come from the real form of the range, and those of 2G3/2{}^2\mathrm{\scriptsize G}_{3/2} of 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T} are those of 1G3/2{}^1\mathrm{\scriptsize G}_{3/2} under time reversal.

The files data/symmetry_adapted_bases/<2G>/2J<JJJ>.json.gz (2J=1,3,…,1212J = 1, 3, \dots, 121) hold {twoJ, blocks, group, schema} (schema "point-group-atlas/double/symmetry-adapted-basis/1"), with twoJ and twoM in place of J and m and every value in its minimal field (section 8). They are counted in full_set, whose release names the release archive <2G>_2J023-2J121.tar, which holds those above 21. As in section 14.4, a record holds the blocks for 2J≤212J \le 21 (enumerated).

At p=+1p = +1 an element of 𝟤𝖳𝗁\mathsf{2T_{h}}, 𝟤𝖳𝖽\mathsf{2T_{d}}, 𝟤𝖮𝗁\mathsf{2O_{h}} or 𝟤𝖨𝗁\mathsf{2I_{h}} acts as the element of 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T}, 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O}, 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O} or 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I} with the same 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} part. Each spinor irrep of the parent record then has the matrices of one spinor irrep of the derived preimage (computed_from): the derived files are the parent record's, with the irreps relabeled.

26.4 Invariant polynomials ¶

The invariant polynomials of the group are in invariants, which has these fields:

  1. binary (every preimage): the binary forms f=∑ici uivn−if = \sum_i c_i\, u^i v^{n-i} in the spin kets u=∣ 1/21/2⟩u = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,1/2}\bigr\rangle and v=∣ −1/21/2⟩v = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,-1/2}\bigr\rangle, with 𝘬f=f\mathsfit{k} f = f for every element 𝘬\mathsfit{k} acting at the parity +1+1 (section 26.1). The cic_i are the e-coordinates of D(n/2)D^{(n/2)} (section 26.3), in poly, keyed by ii.

    For a finite preimage the matrices on uu, vv form a finite subgroup of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} of order image_order. Its invariant forms are generated by three forms (generators) of degrees d1≤d2≤d3d_1 \le d_2 \le d_3, with one relation of degree 2d32d_3 (relation: for each term the generators' exponents and a coefficient). Their Hilbert series is (1+td3)/((1−td1)(1−td2))(1 + t^{d_3})/((1 - t^{d_1})(1 - t^{d_2})) (hilbert_series). The type of the subgroup is its ADE class (such as "A_5", "D_6", "E_7"), with these degrees:

    1. A and n−1n - 1 for the cyclic group of order nn: 2, nn, nn.
    2. D and N+2N + 2 for the binary dihedral group of order 4N4N: 4, 2N2N, 2N+22N + 2.
    3. E6 for 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T}: 6, 8, 12.
    4. E7 for 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O}: 8, 12, 18.
    5. E8 for 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I}: 12, 20, 30.

    In each degree the generators are the invariant forms orthogonal to the products of those of lower degree, for the Fischer product ⟨uivn−i,uivn−i⟩=i! (n−i)!\langle u^i v^{n-i}, u^i v^{n-i}\rangle = i!\,(n-i)! [Vil68, chapter III, section 2.4]. They form a reduced row echelon basis (the pivot is the least power of uu), in the order of the pivots, each scaled to coprime cyclotomic integers.

    For a one-dimensional irrep χ\chi, the field semi_invariants of binary gives the forms with 𝘬f=χ(𝘬)f\mathsfit{k} f = \chi(\mathsfit{k}) f, a module over the invariant forms, with generators chosen the same way and its series over the same denominator. An irrep with χ(1,−1)=−1\chi(1, -1) = -1 has none and is left out, the inversion's lift (1,−1)(1, -1) fixing every form.

    At the parity −1-1 an element (U,−1)(U, -1) multiplies a form of degree nn by (−1)n(-1)^n more. Thus the invariants of odd degree at this parity are the semi-invariants of the determinant, and those of even degree are the invariants.

    A section's image is cyclic of order nn about zz, and its invariants, which no record stores, are generated by uvuv, unu^n and vnv^n (every form when n=1n = 1). The continuous preimages have the invariants generated by uvuv (2Cinf, 2Cinfh), by (uv)2(uv)^2 (2Cinfv, 2Dinf, 2Dinfh) and the constants (SU2, Pinminus3). They hold image ("U(1)", "N(U(1))", "SU(2)", in the same order) in place of image_order and type, a null relation and no semi_invariants.

  2. molien: for a finite preimage, the Molien series HV(t)=∣𝟤𝖦∣−1∑𝘬1/det⁡(1−tDV(𝘬))H_V(t) = \lvert\mathsf{2G}\rvert^{-1}\sum_{\mathsfit{k}} 1/\det(1 - t D^V(\mathsfit{k})) of each spinor irrep (irreps) and each spinor level of section 25.3 that is not one irrep (levels). Each is written over the denominator of the rule of section 19 (hilbert_series).

    An axial continuous preimage holds the series of its spinor families (families), with the complex pairs of 2Cinf and 2Cinfh (levels). The records SU2 and Pinminus3 hold those of D(J)D^{(J)}, 2J≤72J \le 7 (instances, section 29.4), and their documents set them in t2t^2 over the denominator of that rule. This denominator has as many factors as the dimension of the ring of D(J)D^{(J)}: 2J−22J - 2 for J≥32J \ge \tfrac{3}{2} and none for J=12J = \tfrac{1}{2}.

  3. higher (2O, 2Td, 2Oh, 2I, 2Ih): the invariant polynomials in the partners of each spinor irrep of dimension 2J+1=42J + 1 = 4 or 6, D(J,±1)D^{(J,\pm 1)} restricted. They are written in the kets Xi=uiv2J−iX_i = u^i v^{2J-i}, i=0,…,2Ji = 0, \dots, 2J, on which an element acts as on the forms of degree 2J2J. They are computed with Sage [Sag26] and Singular [DGPS25] (generator/data/double_invariants.json.gz). They depend on the set of matrices only: an irrep of 2Td, 2Oh or 2Ih with the matrices of an irrep of 2O or 2I names its entry (same_as). Each entry without same_as has these fields:

    1. generators: the generators of degree at most degree_cap, chosen in each degree as in binary, with the Fischer product ⟨Xa,Xa⟩=∏iai! (i! (2J−i)!)ai\langle X^a, X^a\rangle = \prod_i a_i!\,\bigl(i!\,(2J-i)!\bigr)^{a_i} and the monomials in descending lexicographic order of their exponents (poly, keyed by the exponents).
    2. complete: whether these generators generate the ring.
    3. primary: the degrees of a homogeneous system of parameters and the generators that form it.
