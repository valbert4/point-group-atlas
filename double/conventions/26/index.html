26 Kets and symmetry-adapted bases ¶

26.1 The action on kets ¶

An element (U,ε)(U, \varepsilon) acts on the kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle of D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} by DJ(U)†D^{J}(U)^{\dagger}, times pp when ε=−1\varepsilon = -1, DJD^{J} the Wigner matrix of section 9. At integer JJ with p=(−1)Jp = (-1)^J this is the action of section 4.1 on the spherical harmonics (at the other parity, that action times ε\varepsilon), and 𝘌ˉ\bar{\mathsfit{E}} multiplies a ket by (−1)2J(-1)^{2J}.

26.2 Bases ¶

subduction_basis gives the bases at half-integer JJ as section 14 gives them at integer JJ, with 2J2J and 2m2m in place of JJ and mm (twoJ, twoM, seed_twoM): the closed form of an axial preimage, its blocks for 2J≤212J \le 21, the multiplicities, the generating function in t2Jt^{2J} and the allowed 2m2m. The bases at integer JJ are those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (integer_J).

26.3 The exceptional preimages ¶

The bases of 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T}, 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O} and 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I} at half-integer JJ are built as those of section 14.2, with the action of section 26.1 at p=+1p = +1 written on the coefficients eme_m of section 14.1, where it has no square roots. The files data/symmetry_adapted_bases/<2G>/2J<JJJ>.json.gz (2J=1,3,…,1212J = 1, 3, \dots, 121, those above 21 in the release archives <2G>_2J023-2J121.tar; full_set counts them and names the archive, release) hold {twoJ, blocks, group, schema} (schema "point-group-atlas/double/symmetry-adapted-basis/1"), with twoJ and twoM in place of J and m and every value at its minimal conductor. At p=+1p = +1 an element of 𝟤𝖳𝗁\mathsf{2T_{h}}, 𝟤𝖳𝖽\mathsf{2T_{d}}, 𝟤𝖮𝗁\mathsf{2O_{h}} or 𝟤𝖨𝗁\mathsf{2I_{h}} acts as the element of 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T}, 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O}, 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O} or 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I} with the same 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} part, where each spinor irrep of the parent has the matrices of one spinor irrep of the derived preimage (computed_from): the derived files are the parent's, the irreps renamed. A record holds the blocks for 2J≤212J \le 21 (enumerated) as section 14.2's records do.

26.4 Invariant polynomials ¶

invariants gives the polynomials that the group leaves invariant. binary (every preimage) holds the binary forms f=∑ici uivn−if = \sum_i c_i\, u^i v^{n-i} in the spin kets u=∣ 1/21/2⟩u = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,1/2}\bigr\rangle and v=∣ −1/21/2⟩v = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,-1/2}\bigr\rangle, with kf=fk f = f for every element kk acting at the parity +1+1 (section 26.1); the cic_i are the e-coordinates of D(n/2)D^{(n/2)} (section 26.3; poly, keyed by ii). For a finite preimage the matrices on uu, vv form a finite subgroup of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} of order image_order, whose type (as "A_5", "D_6", "E_7") is its ADE class: A and n−1n - 1 for the cyclic group of order nn, D and N+2N + 2 for the binary dihedral group of order 4N4N, E6, E7 and E8 for 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T}, 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O} and 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I}. Its invariant forms are generated by three forms (generators) of degrees d1≤d2≤d3d_1 \le d_2 \le d_3 (2, nn, nn; 4, 2N2N, 2N+22N + 2; 6, 8, 12; 8, 12, 18; 12, 20, 30) with one relation of degree 2d32d_3 (relation: per term the exponents of the generators and a coefficient), and their Hilbert series is (1+td3)/((1−td1)(1−td2))(1 + t^{d_3})/((1 - t^{d_1})(1 - t^{d_2})) (hilbert_series). In each degree the generators are the invariant forms orthogonal, for the Fischer product ⟨uivn−i,uivn−i⟩=i! (n−i)!\langle u^i v^{n-i}, u^i v^{n-i}\rangle = i!\,(n-i)! [Vil68, chapter III, section 2.4], to the products of those of lower degree, in reduced echelon form (the pivot the least power of uu) in the order of the pivots, each scaled to coprime cyclotomic integers. semi_invariants: for a one-dimensional irrep χ\chi, the forms with kf=χ(k)fk f = \chi(k) f, a module over the invariant forms, with generators chosen the same way and its series over the same denominator; an irrep with χ(1,−1)=−1\chi(1, -1) = -1 has none, the lift (1,−1)(1, -1) of the inversion fixing every form, and is left out. At the parity −1-1 an element (U,−1)(U, -1) multiplies a form of degree nn by (−1)n(-1)^n more, so that the invariants of odd degree there are the semi-invariants of the determinant, and those of even degree the invariants. A section's image is cyclic of order nn about zz, its invariants generated by uvuv, unu^n and vnv^n (every form when n=1n = 1); the continuous preimages have those of uvuv (2Cinf, 2Cinfh), of (uv)2(uv)^2 (2Cinfv, 2Dinf, 2Dinfh) and the constants (SU2, Pinminus3), their image ("U(1)", "N(U(1))", "SU(2)") in place of image_order and type, with a null relation and no semi_invariants. molien (a finite preimage): the Molien series HV(t)=∣𝟤𝖦∣−1∑k1/det⁡(1−tDV(k))H_V(t) = \lvert\mathsf{2G}\rvert^{-1}\sum_k 1/\det(1 - t D^V(k)) of each spinor irrep (irreps) and each spinor level of section 25.3 that is not one irrep (levels), written over the denominator of the rule of section 19 (hilbert_series). The continuous preimages hold no molien; their documents give the series, for SU2 and Pinminus3 that of D(J)D^{(J)}, 2J≤72J \le 7, as a series in t2t^2 over the denominator of that rule with 2J−22J - 2 factors for J≥32J \ge \tfrac{3}{2} and none for J=12J = \tfrac{1}{2}, the dimension of its ring. higher (2O, 2Td, 2Oh, 2I, 2Ih): the invariant polynomials in the partners of each spinor irrep of dimension 2J+1=42J + 1 = 4 or 6, D(J,±1)D^{(J,\pm 1)} restricted, written in the kets Xi=uiv2J−iX_i = u^i v^{2J-i}, i=0,…,2Ji = 0, \dots, 2J, on which an element acts as on the forms of degree 2J2J: the generators of degree at most degree_cap, chosen in each degree as the binary forms' are, with the Fischer product ⟨Xa,Xa⟩=∏iai! (i! (2J−i)!)ai\langle X^a, X^a\rangle = \prod_i a_i!\,\bigl(i!\,(2J-i)!\bigr)^{a_i} and the monomials in descending lexicographic order of their exponents (poly, keyed by the exponents); primary, the degrees of a homogeneous system of parameters and the generators that form it; and complete, whether those generators generate the ring. The invariants depend on the set of the matrices only: an irrep of 2Td, 2Oh or 2Ih with the matrices of an irrep of 2O or 2I names its entry (same_as). They are computed with Sage [Sag26] and Singular [DGPS25] (generator/data/double_invariants.json.gz).
