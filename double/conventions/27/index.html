27 Sections ¶

A section over 𝖦\mathsf{G} is a subgroup 𝖧={(𝘨,b(𝘨))}\mathsf{H} = \{(\mathsfit{g}, b(\mathsfit{g}))\} of 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} that meets {𝘌,𝘌ˉ}\{\mathsfit{E}, \bar{\mathsfit{E}}\} in 𝘌\mathsfit{E} only. Sections exist over the 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} and the 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} with NN odd, one class over 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} and two over 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}}. The lift of 𝘪\mathsfit{i}, which is central, distinguishes these two classes.

A section's record holds the bits b(𝘨)b(\mathsfit{g}) (lift_bits, the least bit vector of its class), its identification, its lattice and the pointer orbifold (section 21.3). Its representations are those of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. As 𝟤𝖦=𝖧×{𝘌,𝘌ˉ}\mathsf{2G} = \mathsf{H} \times \{\mathsfit{E}, \bar{\mathsfit{E}}\}, each spinor irrep Δ\Delta of 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} restricts to an irrep of 𝖧\mathsf{H}. The multiplicities and bases of 𝖧\mathsf{H} at half-integer JJ, and the rows that restrict an irrep of a larger group to 𝖧\mathsf{H}, are those of 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} with each Δ\Delta so renamed. No record stores them.
