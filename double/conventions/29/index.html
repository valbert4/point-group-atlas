29 Continuous groups ¶

The preimages of the seven continuous groups are 2Cinf, 2Cinfh, 2Cinfv, 2Dinf, 2Dinfh, SU2 (of SO3) and Pinminus3 (of O3), with schema "point-group-atlas/double/group-continuous/1" and domain "continuous". A record holds what section 16 gives for a continuous group, for the spinor families only. The record SU2 also holds the Wigner matrices and the Clebsch–Gordan coefficients at half-integer JJ, and SU2 and Pinminus3 hold the closed subgroups of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} and 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} with their subduction rules.

The vector families, their products, Clebsch–Gordan coefficients, subduction and bases are those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (vector_families, vector_rules, vector_products, integer_J, vector_entry), and a vector family's kernel is the preimage of its kernel in 𝖦\mathsf{G}. The documents set them with the spinor families.

29.1 The class space ¶

The components are those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 16), each covering the component of the same id (covers):

  1. Cphi: the lifts (R~z(ω),+1)(\tilde{R}_{z}(\omega), +1).
  2. iCphi: (R~z(ω),−1)(\tilde{R}_{z}(\omega), -1).
  3. sv: the lifts (R~𝐯(π),−1)(\tilde{R}_{\mathbf{v}}(\pi), -1) of the vertical mirrors, 𝐯=(−sin⁡ψ,cos⁡ψ,0)\mathbf{v} = (-\sin\psi, \cos\psi, 0).
  4. c2p: the lifts (R~𝐯(π),+1)(\tilde{R}_{\mathbf{v}}(\pi), +1) of the horizontal binary rotations, 𝐯=(cos⁡ψ,sin⁡ψ,0)\mathbf{v} = (\cos\psi, \sin\psi, 0).

Here 0≤ψ<2π0 \le \psi < 2\pi (section 21.1): ψ\psi and ψ+π\psi + \pi are the two lifts of one element.

On an arc the coordinate tt (coordinate) gives the lift's angle ω=2πt\omega = 2\pi t (coordinate.omega), and the characters are functions of the half-angle ω/2=πt\omega/2 = \pi t, integer harmonics of ω/2\omega/2 ("var": "psi"). In 2Cinf and 2Cinfh every element is a class, and tt runs over [0,2)[0, 2). In the others R~z(ω)\tilde{R}_{z}(\omega) is conjugate to R~z(4π−ω)\tilde{R}_{z}(4\pi - \omega), and tt runs over [0,1][0, 1], from 𝘌\mathsfit{E} through the half turns (t=12t = \tfrac{1}{2}, C2) to 𝘌ˉ\bar{\mathsfit{E}} (bE). Each atom is one class, conjugation by R~z(π)\tilde{R}_{z}(\pi) carrying one lift of a mirror or of a binary rotation to the other.

The matrix M(𝘨)M(\mathsfit{g}) of the element 𝘨\mathsfit{g} at its angle (matrix) is the transpose of its 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} part as in section 21.2, in the variable x of section 29.2. The Haar measure has total mass 1, the Weyl density 1−cos⁡ω1 - \cos\omega on the arcs of SU2 and Pinminus3 (times 12\tfrac{1}{2} in Pinminus3) and a rational density per dt\mathrm{d}t on the other arcs. The squares of an atom's elements are 𝘌ˉ\bar{\mathsfit{E}} (square: Cphi at t=1t = 1). On the point tt of an arc a vector family of 𝖦\mathsf{G} has its character at ϕ=ω\phi = \omega modulo 2π2\pi, and on an atom its value on that of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (covering_map).

The fundamental_domain names that of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (image), repeats its space and gives the volumes of section 21.3 (volumes): of the group (twice that of 𝖦\mathsf{G}), of the domain (as in 𝖦\mathsf{G}) and of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} (32π232\pi^2). It also names the harmonics of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (harmonics), and the record names the orbit space of 𝖦\mathsf{G} on the directions (orbit_space, section 17.8).

The documents write the characters in ω\omega.

29.2 Spinor families ¶

The field irrep_families[] lists the spinor families as in section 16 for the families of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, with a half-integer index (index: domain "half-odd-integers", min 12\tfrac{1}{2}). The index is nn (the ∣m∣\lvert m\rvert of the kets ∣ ±nn⟩\lvert{}^{n}_{\,\pm n}\rangle that span the irrep) in the axial preimages, and the angular momentum JJ (written j) in SU2 and Pinminus3. The families are these, each with χ(𝘌ˉ)=−d\chi(\bar{\mathsfit{E}}) = -d:

  1. 1E_n and 2E_n: characters e±inω\mathrm{e}^{\pm\mathrm{i}n\omega}, with g or u in 2Cinfh.
  2. E_n: character 2cos⁡(nω)2\cos(n\omega) on Cphi and 0 on the atoms, with g or u in 2Dinfh.
  3. D^(j): character ∑m=−JJeimω\sum_{m=-J}^{J}\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}m\omega}, with D^(j,+) and D^(j,-) in Pinminus3.

A range term of a character may run over half-integer values (from −J-J to JJ in steps of 1). The instances run to n=132n = \tfrac{13}{2} and J=192J = \tfrac{19}{2}, and an instance's id spells nn as in the finite records (E1h, 1E3hg).

The matrices of an E_n are the action of section 26.1 on the kets ∣ −nn⟩\lvert{}^{n}_{\,-n}\rangle, ∣ nn⟩\lvert{}^{n}_{\,n}\rangle at the family's parity pp (−1-1 for a u family and +1+1 otherwise). They are diag⁡(e−inω,einω)\operatorname{diag}(\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}n\omega}, \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}n\omega}) on Cphi and anti-diagonal on the atoms (matrices.entries, in the variable x: ω/2=πt\omega/2 = \pi t on an arc and the azimuth ψ\psi on an atom). A 1E_n is the character on ∣ nn⟩\lvert{}^{n}_{\,n}\rangle, and a 2E_n that on ∣ −nn⟩\lvert{}^{n}_{\,-n}\rangle. The intertwiners of section 29.5 carry an instance, restricted to a finite preimage, into the matrices of that preimage's irreps.

For the instance nn (or JJ), kernel and image are those of section 23.1. As 2n2n is odd, the kernel is a section: C_2n, S_4n (containing 𝘪~\tilde{\mathsfit{i}}) or S_4nb (containing 𝘌ˉ𝘪~\bar{\mathsfit{E}}\tilde{\mathsfit{i}}) in the axial preimages, C_1 in SU2, and C_i or C_ib in Pinminus3. Here 𝘪~\tilde{\mathsfit{i}} is the lift (1,−1)(1, -1) of the inversion. The image is C_inf of the point groups for dimension 1 (the characters), 2D_inf for dimension 2 (the matrices, in 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)}), and SU(2) in SU2 and Pinminus3.

29.3 Fusion and Clebsch–Gordan coefficients ¶

The field fusion gives the family laws of the products with a spinor factor, in the grammar of section 10.2 (index_grammar). In SU2 and Pinminus3 the sums over JJ run over integers for two spinor factors and over half-integers for one.

In an axial preimage, clebsch_gordan gives the couplings as rules (rules) and, for 2Cinfv, 2Dinf and 2Dinfh, as rows (couplings, the factors' kets partner_1 and partner_2). The partner ∣1⟩\lvert 1\rangle is the product of weight −k-k, and ∣2⟩\lvert 2\rangle is ss times the product of weight +k+k. The sign ss is determined by the reference flip, the lift of σ𝘷(0)\mathsfit{\sigma_{v}}(0) in 2Cinfv and of 𝘊𝟤′(0)\mathsfit{C_{2}'}(0) in the others.

For J1≤J2≤10J_1 \le J_2 \le 10 with J1J_1 or J2J_2 half-integer, SU2 holds ⟨J1m1 J2m2∣JM⟩\langle J_1 m_1\, J_2 m_2 \vert J M\rangle (section 10.6), 165 products. They are in one file for each 2J12J_1, data/clebsch_gordan/SU2/<NN>.json with <NN> =2J1= 2J_1 in two digits, 00 to 19 (files, each with its path). Each file holds {schema, group, twoj1, products} (schema "point-group-atlas/double/clebsch-gordan/1"). With the parity rule p=p1p2p = p_1 p_2, Pinminus3 names them.

29.4 Subduction, spin–orbit levels and bases ¶

A continuous record has these fields:

  1. subduction_parent: D(J,p)D^{(J,p)} restricted to the group for half-integer JJ up to 252\tfrac{25}{2} (instances, rule).
  2. spin_orbit_levels: the spin–orbit levels of section 25.2 for the vector instances of index at most 3, with their terms named by their labels and a spinor instance also by its irrep_id. In a linear molecule they are the levels Ω=∣Λ+Σ∣\Omega = \lvert\Lambda + \Sigma\rvert of Hund's case (a), and in an atom (SU2, Pinminus3) the levels J=∣J1−S∣,…,J1+SJ = \lvert J_1 - S\rvert, \dots, J_1 + S of a term of an orbital level of angular momentum J1J_1.

  3. subduction_basis: the bases at half-integer JJ and p=+1p = +1:

    1. In 2Cinf and 2Cinfh each ket is a block: ∣ mJ⟩\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\rangle of 1E_m for m>0m > 0, and (−1)J+m∣ mJ⟩(-1)^{J+m}\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\rangle of 2E_n, n=−mn = -m, for m<0m < 0, the image of the first under time reversal.
    2. In 2Cinfv, 2Dinf and 2Dinfh the block E_n has the partners ∣ −nJ⟩\lvert{}^{J}_{\,-n}\rangle and (−1)J−n∣ nJ⟩(-1)^{J-n}\lvert{}^{J}_{\,n}\rangle.
    3. In SU2 and Pinminus3 the 2J+12J+1 kets are the partners of one block.

    These blocks are adapted to time reversal (section 26.1). It lists the blocks for 2J≤212J \le 21 (enumerated). The multiplicity of a g or u family names both parities, and its generating function and allowed 2m2m are those at the parity that carries it (parity).

  4. invariants: the binary forms of section 26.4 and the Molien series of the spinor irreps (molien). The series are given by family in an axial preimage, with the complex-conjugate pairs of 2Cinf and 2Cinfh (levels), and by instance with 2J≤72J \le 7 in SU2 and Pinminus3.

29.5 Closed subgroups of the axial preimages ¶

The field subduction_subgroups lists the closed subgroups of the axial preimages up to conjugacy. These subgroups are the preimages of those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 16.1, N≤12N \le 12 in finite families) and the sections over its 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} and 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} with NN odd. A subgroup's elements are points of the class space (elements: component, turn). Its normalizer in 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} is the preimage of the normalizer in 𝖦\mathsf{G} of its image. Its centralizer is the preimage of the elements of the centralizer of its image whose lifts commute with every element of the subgroup.

A spinor family's restriction depends on nn only through nn modulo the period LL, the least LL for which the matrices of nn and n+Ln + L agree on the subgroup. The table lists n=12,32,…,L−12n = \tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{3}{2}, \dots, L - \tfrac{1}{2} with the intertwiners (blocks), and the settings are as in section 16.1. A section's entry names that of its preimage (preimage_entry), whose rows, renamed, are its rows (section 27). A continuous subgroup's index, normality and transversal are those of its image (vector_entry), read as the lifts at the same points, and stored (index, normal, class_size).

The rules give the restriction to every member of the families 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, 𝖢N𝗏\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}v}} and 𝖣N\mathsf{D_{\mathnormal{N}}}, by q=n mod Nq = n \bmod N, and reproduce the tables. The field vector_rules names the rules of 𝖦\mathsf{G} for its families, which the documents set with the rows of each vector_entry, under the preimages.

29.6 SU2 and Pinminus3 ¶

The field wigner_d of SU2 gives the matrices dJ(β)d^{J}(\beta) of section 9 at half-integer J≤192J \le \tfrac{19}{2} (tables, twoJ, the formula d_formula), and at integer JJ they are those of SO3 (integer_j). The field subgroup_classes lists the closed subgroups of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} other than itself: the preimages of those of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and the cyclic groups of odd order (sections 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}), with their normalizers and centralizers in 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)}. At half-integer JJ, subduction_rules gives D(J)D^{(J)} restricted to these subgroups. It gives rules for 𝟤𝖢N\mathsf{2C_{\mathnormal{N}}} (each ket ∣ mJ⟩\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\rangle spans the irrep of q=m mod Nq = m \bmod N), 𝟤𝖣N\mathsf{2D_{\mathnormal{N}}} (the pair ∣ ±mJ⟩\lvert{}^{J}_{\,\pm m}\rangle) and the continuous subgroups, with their generating functions in t2Jt^{2J}. For 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T}, 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O}, 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I} it names their records' generating functions.

The record Pinminus3 holds subgroup_classes and subduction_rules in the same way, for D(J,p)D^{(J,p)}. Its subgroup_classes lists the classes of data/double_groups.json other than 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} (section 28.3), the members N≤12N \le 12 with their records' normalizers and centralizers (section 20.4), and the lattice's aliases (aliases). Its subduction_rules has rules at both parities, as in O3 (section 16.1): rules for the continuous subgroups, terms for 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} and rules for the seven families. Each irrep is named by the least JJ at which it occurs (section 23.2), and q is m + N[p = -1] mod 2N in the preimages that (U,ε)↦U(U, \varepsilon) \mapsto U carries onto 𝟤𝖢𝟤N\mathsf{2C_{2\mathnormal{N}}} or 𝟤𝖣𝟤N\mathsf{2D_{2\mathnormal{N}}}, or m mod N otherwise. The tag p(-1)^(m-1/2) primes the irreps of 𝟤𝖢N𝗁\mathsf{2C_{\mathnormal{N}h}} with NN odd. The generating functions count the blocks at p=+1p = +1, as in the members' records, and the exceptional preimages name their records' generating functions (generating_function). A section is restricted as its preimage (sections), and at integer JJ the rules are those of O3 (integer_J). The rules give the restrictions of the members N≤12N \le 12 for 2J≤412J \le 41 at both parities and the irreps of the blocks of their bases.
