22 Conjugacy classes ¶

A class of 𝖦\mathsf{G} lifts to two classes of 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G}, or to one class that contains both lifts, its size doubled. The two are the class of the canonical lifts (𝘨,0)(\mathsfit{g}, 0) and, right after it, the class of the barred partners (id and label prefixed b and bar). The field base_class names the class of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, whose geometry the record does not repeat, and bar, su2_turn, element_order, centralizer_order, power_map, power_maps, inverse_class and ambivalent are those of 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G}.
