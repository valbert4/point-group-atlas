20 The parent and the records ¶

20.1 The parent ¶

𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)=𝖲𝖴(𝟤)×{+1,−1}\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} = \mathsf{SU(2)} \times \{+1, -1\} is the group of the pairs (U,ε)(U, \varepsilon) with the product (U,ε)(V,δ)=(UV,εδ)(U, \varepsilon)(V, \delta) = (UV, \varepsilon\delta); the records multiply in the order of section 4.1, so that the product of (U,ε)(U, \varepsilon) and (V,δ)(V, \delta) in their tables is (VU,εδ)(VU, \varepsilon\delta) (section 21.2). With σ⃗=(σx,σy,σz)\vec{\sigma} = (\sigma_x, \sigma_y, \sigma_z) the Pauli matrices, UU covers the rotation 𝘙(U)\mathsfit{R}(U) for which U (𝐫⋅σ⃗) U†=(𝐫 𝘙(U)⊤)⋅σ⃗U\,(\mathbf{r}\cdot\vec{\sigma})\,U^{\dagger} = (\mathbf{r}\,\mathsfit{R}(U)^{\top})\cdot\vec{\sigma} for every point 𝐫\mathbf{r}, and (U,ε)(U, \varepsilon) covers ε 𝘙(U)∈𝖮(𝟥)\varepsilon\,\mathsfit{R}(U) \in \mathsf{O(3)}. The kernel is {𝘌,𝘌ˉ}\{\mathsfit{E}, \bar{\mathsfit{E}}\}, 𝘌ˉ=(−1,+1)\bar{\mathsfit{E}} = (-1, +1). The lift (1,−1)(1, -1) of the inversion is central and of order 2, and a reflection lifts to two elements of order 4: this is the double group of the spin-1/2 literature [AH11].

20.2 Names ¶

A closed subgroup that holds 𝘌ˉ\bar{\mathsfit{E}} is the preimage 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} of its image 𝖦\mathsf{G} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}; one that does not is a section over its image (section 27). The record of the preimage of G is named 2G (2C3v), and SU2 and Pinminus3 for SO3 and O3; a section keeps the name of its image (C3), the second class over an 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} with NN odd with b appended (S6b). The preimages of the records of one class of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)} (o3_conjugates, section 2.1) are one class of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}. Other works mark the double group of 𝖦\mathsf{G} with a dagger [BC72], a tilde [AH11], a prime [Ham62, Lax74, Tin64] or a left superscript d [EBW+17], and title its tables with the Schoenflies and Hermann–Mauguin symbols of 𝖦\mathsf{G}; the 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} of this catalogue is that of [CS03] for 𝖦\mathsf{G} a group of rotations, which writes 𝟣𝖢N\mathsf{1C_{\mathnormal{N}}} for the section 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}.

20.3 Pointers ¶

A value that a record of the point groups holds is not stored again, but for the subgroup lattice (section 28.1): the record names it by a pointer, the string "<catalogue>/<name>#<JSON pointer>", a JSON pointer (RFC 6901) after #, the empty one naming the whole record ("rotation/C3v#"); the name point_groups stands for the file rotation/data/point_groups.json (section 15). A pointer stands in a field named for what it replaces: cover_of, image, preimage_of, vector_irreps, vector_families, vector_entry, vector_products, vector_rules, vector_subduction, integer_J and integer_j name values of the point groups' records, preimages (in double_groups.json) their lattice's family_rules, and section_of, d, generating_function, table, shells and same_as values of another record of this catalogue.

20.4 The group block ¶

symbol is the Schoenflies symbol, with the prefix 2 for a preimage, and schema is "point-group-atlas/double/group/1" for a preimage and "point-group-atlas/double/section/1" for a section; cover_of (a preimage) or image (a section, which the covering map carries onto 𝖦\mathsf{G} one to one) names the record of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, whose type, family and geometry the record does not repeat; alternative_names lists the other symbols of its class, those of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 2.2) with the prefix 2 for a preimage (𝟤𝖢𝗌\mathsf{2C_{s}} and 𝟤𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsf{2C_{1h}} for 𝟤𝖢𝟣𝗏\mathsf{2C_{1v}}, 2K and Spin(3) for SU2) and as they are, with b for the second, for a section. hermann_mauguin is that of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 2.3) after ^d, the mark of a double group in [EBW+17], for a preimage (^d-43m), and as it is, with b for the second, for a section; conway_smith the name of Conway and Smith [CS03] of a finite subgroup of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)}, 2C_N and 2D_2N (subscripts orders; D1 has C2's), 2T, 2O, 2I for a preimage of rotations and 1C_N for a section 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, else null not-in-source; altmann_T that of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, whose table [AH11] gives the irreps of 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} too. olive, ihrig_golubitsky and orbifold name classes of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, so they are only 𝖦\mathsf{G}'s. order is the number of elements. A preimage has abstract_type (GAP's StructureDescription in the notation of section 2.2, with 𝖰n\mathsf{Q_{\mathnormal{n}}} the generalized quaternion group of order nn, SL(2,q) the group of the 2×22 \times 2 matrices of determinant 1 over the field of qq elements, . an extension and = between two names of one group), gap_smallgroup_id (GAP's IdGroup of its table), su2_subgroup (𝟤𝖦∩𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{2G} \cap \mathsf{SU(2)}), hk, product_form, center and commutator_subgroup, as in section 2.2 but computed in 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G}; a section has section_of, its preimage. normalizer and centralizer are taken in 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}: the normalizer of 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} is the preimage of that of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, and its centralizer the preimage of the elements of the centralizer of 𝖦\mathsf{G} whose lifts commute with every lift of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. A section has the centralizer of its preimage, and as normalizer the preimage of the elements of the normalizer of 𝖦\mathsf{G} whose lifts carry it to itself. The records hold no Cartesian tensors, invariant polynomials in xx, yy, zz, fundamental domains or degenerate levels of section 19 (those of 𝖦\mathsf{G}); they hold the spin–orbitals (section 25.4) and the invariants of the spinor kets (section 26.4).

20.5 Files ¶

The files of this catalogue are in the folder double/, laid out and written as section 18 sets out Rotation's, the literal of the GAP, Wolfram and SymPy files bound to o3d: data/json/<K>.json, the 116 records; data/gap/, data/wolfram/, data/sympy/, data/latex/ and data/pdf/, the records in the other formats; CONVENTIONS.pdf; data/double_groups.json (section 28.3); data/clebsch_gordan/SU2/<NN>.json (section 29.3); data/symmetry_adapted_bases/<2G>/2J<JJJ>.json.gz (section 26.3); data/pdf/numbers.json, the numbers that the PDFs print (section 18); and schema/<format>.schema.json, the JSON Schema of each format, derived from all its files. Their schema is "point-group-atlas/double/<format>/1", <format> one of group, section, group-continuous, lattice, clebsch-gordan, symmetry-adapted-basis, pdf-numbers.
