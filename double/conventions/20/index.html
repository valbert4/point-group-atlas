20 The parent and the records ¶

20.1 The parent ¶

The parent 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)=𝖲𝖴(𝟤)×{+1,−1}\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} = \mathsf{SU(2)} \times \{+1, -1\} is the group of the pairs (U,ε)(U, \varepsilon) with the product (U,ε)(V,δ)=(UV,εδ)(U, \varepsilon)(V, \delta) = (UV, \varepsilon\delta). The records multiply in the order of section 4.1, so the product of (U,ε)(U, \varepsilon) and (V,δ)(V, \delta) in their tables is (VU,εδ)(VU, \varepsilon\delta) (section 21.2). With the Pauli matrices σ⃗=(σx,σy,σz)\vec{\sigma} = (\sigma_x, \sigma_y, \sigma_z), UU covers the rotation 𝘙(U)\mathsfit{R}(U) for which U (𝐫⋅σ⃗) U†=(𝐫 𝘙(U)⊤)⋅σ⃗U\,(\mathbf{r}\cdot\vec{\sigma})\,U^{\dagger} = (\mathbf{r}\,\mathsfit{R}(U)^{\top})\cdot\vec{\sigma} for every point 𝐫\mathbf{r}, and (U,ε)(U, \varepsilon) covers ε 𝘙(U)∈𝖮(𝟥)\varepsilon\,\mathsfit{R}(U) \in \mathsf{O(3)}. The kernel is {𝘌,𝘌ˉ}\{\mathsfit{E}, \bar{\mathsfit{E}}\}, 𝘌ˉ=(−1,+1)\bar{\mathsfit{E}} = (-1, +1). The lift (1,−1)(1, -1) of the inversion is central and of order 2, and a mirror lifts to two elements of order 4: the parent is the double group of the spin-1/2 literature [AH11]. The other double cover of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, in which a mirror lifts to elements of order 2 and the inversion to elements of order 4, is the parent of no record. It is isoclinic to 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} [CCN+85, chapter 6, section 7]: a spinor character of a preimage in it is a spinor character of this catalogue multiplied by i\mathrm{i} on the improper classes.

20.2 Names ¶

A closed subgroup that contains 𝘌ˉ\bar{\mathsfit{E}} is the preimage 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} of its image 𝖦\mathsf{G} in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, and the others are sections over their images (section 27). The record of the preimage of G is named 2G (2C3v), except SU2 for SO3 and Pinminus3 for O3. A section keeps the name of its image (C3), with b appended in the second class over an 𝖲𝟤N\mathsf{S_{2\mathnormal{N}}} with NN odd (S6b). The preimages of the records of one 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy class (o3_conjugates, section 2.1) form one class of subgroups of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}. Other works mark the double group of 𝖦\mathsf{G} with a dagger [BC72], a tilde [AH11], a prime [Ham62, Lax74, Tin64], a star [Gri61, section 6.9] or a left superscript d [EBW+17]. They title its tables with the Schoenflies and Hermann–Mauguin symbols of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. The work [Gri61, section 6.9] also writes R for 𝘌ˉ\bar{\mathsfit{E}} and U_2 x S for 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}, where S contains the identity and the lift (1,−1)(1, -1) of the inversion. For a group 𝖦\mathsf{G} of type I, the 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} of this catalogue is that of [CS03], which writes 𝟣𝖢N\mathsf{1C_{\mathnormal{N}}} for the section 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}.

20.3 Pointers ¶

Pointers are as in section 18.1. A record of this catalogue repeats no value of a record of the point groups and names such a value by a pointer. The exceptions are the subgroup lattice (section 28.1), the space of a continuous fundamental domain (section 29.1) and the index, normal and class_size of a continuous subgroup (section 29.5). The pointer fields, named for what they replace, name these values:

  1. cover_of, image, preimage_of, vector_irreps, vector_families, vector_entry, vector_products, vector_rules, vector_subduction, integer_J, integer_j, orbifold, harmonics and orbit_space: values of the point groups' records.
  2. preimages (in double_groups.json): the family_rules of point_groups.
  3. section_of, d, generating_function, table, shells and same_as: values of another record of this catalogue.

20.4 The group block ¶

A record has these fields:

  1. symbol: the Schoenflies symbol, with the prefix 2 for a preimage.
  2. schema: "point-group-atlas/double/group/1" for a preimage and "point-group-atlas/double/section/1" for a section.
  3. cover_of (a preimage) or image (a section): the record of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. The covering map carries a section onto 𝖦\mathsf{G} one to one. A record of this catalogue repeats none of the type, family and geometry of 𝖦\mathsf{G}.
  4. alternative_names: the other symbols of its class. For a preimage they are the alternative names of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 2.2) with the prefix 2 (𝟤𝖢𝗌\mathsf{2C_{s}} and 𝟤𝖢𝟣𝗁\mathsf{2C_{1h}} for 𝟤𝖢𝟣𝗏\mathsf{2C_{1v}}, 2K and Spin(3) for SU2). For a section they are unchanged, with b for the second.
  5. hermann_mauguin: that of 𝖦\mathsf{G} (section 2.3). For a preimage it has the prefix ^d, the mark of a double group in [EBW+17] (^d-43m). For a section it is unchanged, with b for the second.
  6. altmann_T: that of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, whose table [AH11] also gives the irreps of 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G}.
  7. conway_smith: the name of Conway and Smith [CS03] of a finite subgroup of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)}, or null not-in-source otherwise. A finite preimage of a group of type I has 2C_N, 2D_2N, 2T, 2O or 2I (subscripts are orders, and D1 has the name of C2), and a section 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} has 1C_N.
  8. order: the number of elements.
  9. section_of (a section): its preimage.
  10. normalizer, centralizer: taken in 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}. The normalizer of 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G} is the preimage of that of 𝖦\mathsf{G}, and its centralizer is the preimage of the elements of the centralizer of 𝖦\mathsf{G} whose lifts commute with every lift of 𝖦\mathsf{G}. A section has the centralizer of its preimage, and its normalizer is the preimage of the elements of the normalizer of 𝖦\mathsf{G} whose lifts carry the section to itself.

A preimage adds these fields, as in section 2.2, computed in 𝟤𝖦\mathsf{2G}:

  1. abstract_type: GAP's StructureDescription in the notation of section 2.2, with . for an extension and = between two names of one group. Here 𝖰n\mathsf{Q_{\mathnormal{n}}} is the generalized quaternion group of order nn and SL(2,q) the group of the 2×22 \times 2 matrices of determinant 1 over the field of qq elements.
  2. gap_smallgroup_id: GAP's IdGroup of its table.
  3. su2_subgroup: 𝟤𝖦∩𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{2G} \cap \mathsf{SU(2)}, in place of rotation_subgroup ("2C_3" for 2C3v).
  4. hk, product_form, center and commutator_subgroup.
  5. schur_multiplier and automorphisms, with 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} in place of 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, and without spinor_class and other_classes. In the orbits of irrep ids, the vector irreps (section 23.1) come before the spinor irreps.

Since olive, ihrig_golubitsky and orbifold name 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}-conjugacy classes, a record of this catalogue repeats none of them. The records hold no Cartesian tensors, invariant polynomials in xx, yy, zz or degenerate levels of section 19 (those of 𝖦\mathsf{G}), and only the continuous records hold a fundamental domain (section 29.1). They hold the spin–orbitals (section 25.4) and the invariants of the spinor kets (section 26.4).

20.5 Files ¶

The files of this catalogue are in the folder double/, laid out and written as in section 18:

  1. data/json/<K>.json: the 116 records.
  2. data/gap/, data/wolfram/, data/sympy/, data/latex/ and data/pdf/: the records in the other formats. The literal of the GAP, Wolfram and SymPy files is bound to o3d.
  3. CONVENTIONS.pdf.
  4. data/double_groups.json (section 28.3).
  5. data/clebsch_gordan/SU2/<NN>.json (section 29.3).
  6. data/symmetry_adapted_bases/<2G>/2J<JJJ>.json.gz (section 26.3).
  7. data/pdf/numbers.json: the numbers that the PDFs print (section 18).
  8. schema/<format>.schema.json: the JSON Schema of each format, derived from all its files.

Their schema is "point-group-atlas/double/<format>/1", where <format> is one of group, section, group-continuous, lattice, clebsch-gordan, symmetry-adapted-basis and pdf-numbers.
