Every spinor irrep VV of 𝟤𝖢𝟤𝗁\mathsf{2C_{2h}} has dimension 1, the group of its matrices on uu, vv being cyclic; among the finite groups of this catalogue only 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O}, 𝟤𝖳𝖽\mathsf{2T_{d}}, 𝟤𝖮𝗁\mathsf{2O_{h}}, 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I} and 𝟤𝖨𝗁\mathsf{2I_{h}} have spinor irreps of dimension above 2 [McK80]. The invariants of VV are those of the group of its values: the number of independent invariant polynomials of degree nn in its partner xx is the coefficient of tnt^n in its Molien series HV(t)H_V(t) [Mol97], and when its values are the mm-th roots of unity they are the powers of xmx^{m}, HV(t)=1/(1−tm)H_V(t) = 1/(1 - t^{m}). A level of section 6.2 that is two of them, of partners xx and yy, has the polynomials in xyxy, xmx^{m} and ymy^{m} when they are complex conjugates, HV(t)=(1+tm)/((1−t2)(1−tm))H_V(t) = (1 + t^{m})/((1 - t^{2})(1 - t^{m})).

Binary forms. The matrices of the elements on uu, vv form the cyclic group of order 4, whose invariant forms are Klein’s [Kle84], those of degree 2J2J the kets at JJ that 𝟤𝖢𝟤𝗁\mathsf{2C_{2h}} leaves invariant [Vil68, chapter III, section 2]; the number of independent invariant forms of degree nn is the coefficient of tnt^n in H(t)=(1+t4)/((1−t2)(1−t4))H(t) = (1 + t^{4})/((1-t^{2})(1-t^{4})), and the invariant forms are the polynomials in f1=uvf_1 = uv, f2=v4f_2 = v^{4} and f3=u4f_3 = u^{4}, with the one relation f14−f2f3=0f_{1}^{4} - f_{2}f_{3} = 0. A form with kf=χ(k)fk f = \chi(k) f for a one-dimensional irrep χ\chi is a semi-invariant of χ\chi; those of χ\chi are the sums of the invariant forms times the generators of the table, and have the series Pχ(t)/((1−t2)(1−t4))P_\chi(t)/((1-t^{2})(1-t^{4})), Pχ(t)=ta+tbP_\chi(t) = t^{a} + t^{b} with aa and bb the degrees of the two generators.
