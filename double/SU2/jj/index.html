6.2 jjjj configurations ¶

Data: jj_configurations in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 19 and 25.3. In jjjj coupling, an electron of an atomic shell p\mathrm{p}, d\mathrm{d} or f\mathrm{f} has the angular momentum JJ of one of its two subshells p1/2\mathrm{p}_{1/2}, p3/2\mathrm{p}_{3/2}, d3/2\mathrm{d}_{3/2}, d5/2\mathrm{d}_{5/2}, f5/2\mathrm{f}_{5/2} and f7/2\mathrm{f}_{7/2}. In the subshell JJ, nn electrons fill the level D(J)D^{(J)}, a Kramers level, and their levels are ΛnD(J)\Lambda^{n}D^{(J)}, n=0,…,2J+1n = 0, \dots, 2J + 1. These levels are vector irreps at even nn and spinor irreps at odd nn, and the configurations of nn and of 2J+1−n2J + 1 - n electrons have the same levels. A configuration in both subshells of p\mathrm{p}, d\mathrm{d} or f\mathrm{f} has the products of their powers (section 2.3), and the electronic terms of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} split into these products. The record holds every nn (jj_configurations).
