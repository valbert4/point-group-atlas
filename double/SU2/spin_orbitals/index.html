5.1 Spin–orbitals ¶

Data: spin_orbitals in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: section 25.4. The states of an electron of orbital angular momentum ll and spin 1 ⁣/2\nicefrac{1}{2} in the subshells J=l∓1 ⁣/2J = l \mp \nicefrac{1}{2} are ∣l J m⟩=∑⟨l ml 1 ⁣/2 ms∣J m⟩ ∣l ml⟩∣1 ⁣/2 ms⟩\lvert l\,J\,m\rangle = \sum \langle l\,m_l\,\nicefrac{1}{2}\,m_s \vert J\,m\rangle\,\lvert l\,m_l\rangle\lvert \nicefrac{1}{2}\,m_s\rangle. The coefficients in the sum are those of section 2.4, whose tables put the spin first: ⟨l ml 1 ⁣/2 ms∣J m⟩=(−1)l+1/2−J⟨1 ⁣/2 ms l ml∣J m⟩\langle l\,m_l\,\nicefrac{1}{2}\,m_s \vert J\,m\rangle = (-1)^{l+1/2-J}\langle \nicefrac{1}{2}\,m_s\,l\,m_l \vert J\,m\rangle. The kets ∣l ml⟩\lvert l\,m_l\rangle are those of the spherical harmonics (Conventions, section 4). The states are written in the functions ff of the s\mathrm{s}, p\mathrm{p} and d\mathrm{d} shells of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} (Conventions, section 13) times the spin kets u=∣ 1/21/2⟩u = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,1/2}\bigr\rangle and v=∣ −1/21/2⟩v = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,-1/2}\bigr\rangle, each up to a positive factor. The record also holds the f\mathrm{f} shell (spin_orbitals). A state of odd ll is multiplied by −i-\mathrm{i}, so that every state is adapted to time reversal (Conventions, section 25.4). A row gives the subshell, mm and the state.
