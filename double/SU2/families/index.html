2.1 Families of irreps ¶

Data: character_functions in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 7, 16, 23 and 29.2. A row is a family of irreps, by its label, with its dimension dd, its Frobenius–Schur indicator νFS\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} (real for +1+1 and pseudoreal for −1-1) and the range of the index of its irreps. The kernel ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma is the subgroup of the elements that an irrep Γ\Gamma of the family maps to the identity, and the character of Γ\Gamma at 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is χΓ(𝘨)\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}). The image is the group formed by the irrep’s matrices, isomorphic to 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{SU(2)}/\ker\Gamma. A row also gives the family’s character on each component, in the angle ω\omega of the lift. The vector families, on which 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} is the identity, are those of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} (vector_families), read on these classes through the covering map. A point ω\omega of an arc covers the rotation angle ϕ=ω\phi = \omega modulo 2π2\pi. The image and matrices of a vector family are those of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, and its kernel is the preimage of its kernel in that group. The spinor families, on which 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} is −1-1, follow below the rule, with an instance for every half-integer J=12,32,…J = \tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{3}{2}, \dots. The image of a spinor family is 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)}. The irreps are irreducible over ℂ\mathbb{C}. Over ℝ\mathbb{R} (Conventions, section 7), a real irrep is also an irrep in a basis with real matrices. Read as a real space of dimension 2d2d, a pseudoreal irrep is one irrep over ℝ\mathbb{R} of character 2χΓ2\chi_{\Gamma} and keeps its own label. The determinant character of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, of value det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g} on the elements that cover 𝘨\mathsfit{g}, is D(0)D^{(0)}, the instance J=0J = 0 of D(J)D^{(J)}, and is trivial in a group of type I.
