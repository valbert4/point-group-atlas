3.2 Descent of symmetry ¶

Data: subduction_rules in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 16.1 and 29.6. Below is the restriction of D(J)D^{(J)} to each closed subgroup 𝖧\mathsf{H} of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} (section 3.1) for every half-integer JJ (subduction_rules). At integer JJ the restriction is that of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)}, in its section Descent of symmetry. For an axial 𝖧\mathsf{H} the restriction at half-integer JJ is the direct sum, over the kets ∣ mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,m}\bigr\rangle, of the terms of the first true case of its rule. For 𝟤𝖣∞\mathsf{2D_{\infty }} and 𝟤𝖣N\mathsf{2D_{\mathnormal{N}}}, the sum runs over the pairs ∣ ±mJ⟩\bigl\lvert{}^{J}_{\,\pm m}\bigr\rangle. In the rules, q=m mod Nq = m \bmod N. In the rules of a family, the terms and the irreps of the generating functions are irreps of the member of 𝟤𝖢N\mathsf{2C_{\mathnormal{N}}} or 𝟤𝖣N\mathsf{2D_{\mathnormal{N}}}. They are labeled as in Conventions, section 23.2. A section 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}} (NN odd) is restricted as its preimage 𝟤𝖢N\mathsf{2C_{\mathnormal{N}}}, with each spinor irrep renamed by the section’s record (Conventions, section 27). The multiplicity bΛ(J)b_\Lambda(J) of an irrep Λ\Lambda of 𝖧\mathsf{H} is the coefficient of t2Jt^{2J} in its generating function HΛ(t)=∑J≥1/2bΛ(J) t2JH_\Lambda(t) = \sum_{J\ge1/2} b_\Lambda(J)\,t^{2J}. The generating functions are given for every 𝖧\mathsf{H}, and without a rule for 𝟤𝖳\mathsf{2T}, 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O} and 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I}. In the generating functions, kk is the subscript of an irrep Ek\mathrm{\scriptsize E}_{k}, and nn the index of a family of irreps of a continuous subgroup. The document of each preimage except the members with N>12N > 12 lists the restrictions JJ by JJ, in its section Subduction from 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}.
