2.3 Direct products of irreps ¶

Data: fusion in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 10.2 and 29.3. The record holds a rule for every pair of families with a spinor factor (rules), and these rules take one form. In this form J1J_1 and J2J_2 are the indices of the factors, an integer for a vector family and a half-integer for a spinor family. The square of a spinor irrep (squares) follows, with the antisymmetric part in braces. The products of two vector families are those of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} (vector_rules).
