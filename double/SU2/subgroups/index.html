3.1 Closed subgroups ¶

Data: subgroup_classes in JSON · SymPy · Wolfram Language · GAP

Conventions: sections 16.1 and 29.5. The closed subgroups of 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} up to conjugacy, other than 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} itself: the preimages of the closed subgroups of 𝖲𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{SO(3)} and the cyclic groups of odd order, the sections 𝖢N\mathsf{C_{\mathnormal{N}}}; normalizer and centralizer in 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)}. A row is a class of subgroups (subgrp), with its order, the number of subgroups conjugate to it (conjugates), whether it is normal, and its normalizer and its centralizer: the continuous subgroups, then per family its preimages and its sections, a row whose symbol holds NN standing for every NN of its range, its entries functions of NN, then the exceptional preimages. The record lists the members with N≤12N \le 12 (subgroup_classes). In the diagram, where each group links to its document, a line joins a group to a maximal closed subgroup among the groups of the diagram; the groups that do not lie in 𝖲𝖴(𝟤)\mathsf{SU(2)} are gray.
