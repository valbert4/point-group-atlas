2.1 Irreducible representations ¶

Data: elements, group in JSON · GAP · Wolfram Language · SymPy

Conventions: sections 6, 7 and 23. The character χΓ(𝘨)\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}) of the irrep Γ\Gamma on the class of 𝘨\mathsfit{g} is given in section 2.2. The irreps of 𝖦\mathsf{G} are those of 𝖢𝟣\mathsf{C_{1}}, on the lifts (𝘨,b(𝘨))(\mathsfit{g}, b(\mathsfit{g})). A row is an irrep of dimension dd, with its Frobenius–Schur indicator νFS=∣𝖦∣−1∑𝘨∈𝖦χΓ(𝘨2)\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = |\mathsf{G}|^{-1}\allowbreak\sum_{\mathsfit{g}\in\mathsf{G}}\chi_{\Gamma}(\mathsfit{g}^2), which gives its reality: real (νFS=+1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = +1), pseudoreal (νFS=−1\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = -1) or complex (νFS=0\nu_{\mathrm{FS}} = 0, paired with its conjugate). The column conjugate gives that conjugate (a dash for the others). The kernel ker⁡Γ\ker\Gamma is the subgroup of the elements mapped to the identity, named as in section 3.1. The image is the group formed by the irrep’s matrices, isomorphic to 𝖦/ker⁡Γ\mathsf{G}/\ker\Gamma, and it is that of the irrep of 𝖢𝟣\mathsf{C_{1}}. The irreps are irreducible over ℂ\mathbb{C}. Each is real. Over ℝ\mathbb{R} (Conventions, section 7), a real irrep is also an irrep in a basis with real matrices. The boxed irrep is the determinant character of 𝖢𝟣\mathsf{C_{1}}, of value det⁡𝘨\det \mathsfit{g} on the element (𝘨,b(𝘨))(\mathsfit{g}, b(\mathsfit{g})), and is trivial in a group of type I.
