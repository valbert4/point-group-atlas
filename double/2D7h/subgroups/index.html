3.1 Subgroup lattice ¶

Conventions: sections 15 and 28. The diagram shows the conjugacy classes of subgroups, each joined to the classes of its maximal subgroups, the orders increasing upward; a normal subgroup is framed, and ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of conjugate subgroups. A subgroup that contains 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} is the preimage of its image in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, named 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H} for the image 𝖧\mathsf{H}; one that does not is a section over its image, named 𝖧\mathsf{H}. A gray letter after a symbol tells apart the classes of subgroups with that symbol, which are not conjugate in 𝟤𝖣𝟩𝗁\mathsf{2D_{7h}} (they are conjugate in 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}); the table and the subduction tables of section 3.2 use the same letters. The table lists the classes by order, then by the type of the image, a preimage before a section; subgrp is the representative 𝖧\mathsf{H} of a class of subgroups, order its order, conjugates the number of subgroups in the class and normal whether 𝖧\mathsf{H} is normal; classes lists the classes of 𝟤𝖣𝟩𝗁\mathsf{2D_{7h}}, other than 𝘌\mathsfit{E}, that 𝖧\mathsf{H} meets (a dash for none), and 𝖭 𝟤𝖣𝟩𝗁(𝖧)\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{7h}}}(\mathsf{H}) and 𝖢 𝟤𝖣𝟩𝗁(𝖧)\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{7h}}}(\mathsf{H}) are the classes of its normalizer and its centralizer in 𝟤𝖣𝟩𝗁\mathsf{2D_{7h}} (normalizer, centralizer); a subgroup’s other names in its class of 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)} follow ≅\cong (Conventions, section 20.2).
