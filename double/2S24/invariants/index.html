A binary form f=∑ici uivn−if = \sum_i c_i\, u^i v^{n-i} of degree nn in the spin kets u=∣ 1/21/2⟩u = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,1/2}\bigr\rangle and v=∣ −1/21/2⟩v = \bigl\lvert{}^{1/2}_{\,-1/2}\bigr\rangle is invariant when kf=fk f = f for every element kk, the elements acting at the parity +1+1 (Conventions, section 26.4). At the parity −1-1 the invariants of even degree are the same, and those of odd degree are the semi-invariants of det⁡𝟤𝖲𝟤𝟦\det_{\mathsf{2S_{24}}}. Every spinor irrep VV of 𝟤𝖲𝟤𝟦\mathsf{2S_{24}} has dimension 1, the group of its matrices on uu, vv being cyclic. Among the finite groups of this catalogue, only 𝟤𝖮\mathsf{2O}, 𝟤𝖳𝖽\mathsf{2T_{d}}, 𝟤𝖮𝗁\mathsf{2O_{h}}, 𝟤𝖨\mathsf{2I} and 𝟤𝖨𝗁\mathsf{2I_{h}} have spinor irreps of dimension above 2 [McK80]. The invariants of VV are those of the group of its values. The number of linearly independent invariant polynomials of degree nn in its partner xx is the coefficient of tnt^n in its Molien series HV(t)H_V(t) [Mol97]. When the values are the mm-th roots of unity, the invariants are the powers of xmx^{m}, and HV(t)=1/(1−tm)H_V(t) = 1/(1 - t^{m}). A level of section 6.2 that is two complex-conjugate spinor irreps, of partners xx and yy, has the polynomials in xyxy, xmx^{m} and ymy^{m}, and HV(t)=(1+tm)/((1−t2)(1−tm))H_V(t) = (1 + t^{m})/((1 - t^{2})(1 - t^{m})).

Binary forms. The matrices of the elements on uu, vv form the cyclic group of order 48, whose invariant forms are those of [Kle84]. In degree 2J2J these forms are the kets at JJ that 𝟤𝖲𝟤𝟦\mathsf{2S_{24}} leaves invariant [Vil68, chapter III, section 2]. The number of linearly independent invariant forms of degree nn is the coefficient of tnt^n in H(t)=(1+t48)/((1−t2)(1−t48))H(t) = (1 + t^{48})/((1-t^{2})(1-t^{48})). The invariant forms are the polynomials in f1=uvf_1 = uv, f2=v48f_2 = v^{48} and f3=u48f_3 = u^{48}, with the one relation f148−f2f3=0f_{1}^{48} - f_{2}f_{3} = 0. A form with kf=χ(k)fk f = \chi(k) f for a one-dimensional irrep χ\chi is a semi-invariant of χ\chi, and the table lists every such irrep carried by a form, except the trivial one. The semi-invariants of χ\chi are the sums of the invariant forms times the generators of its row. Their series is Pχ(t)/((1−t2)(1−t48))P_\chi(t)/((1-t^{2})(1-t^{48})), with Pχ(t)=ta+tbP_\chi(t) = t^{a} + t^{b} for the degrees aa and bb of the two generators.
