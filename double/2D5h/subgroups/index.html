3.1 Subgroup lattice ¶

Conventions: sections 15 and 28. The diagram shows the conjugacy classes of subgroups, each joined to the classes of its maximal subgroups, and the orders increase upward. A normal subgroup is framed, and ×c{\times}c is the number c>1c > 1 of conjugate subgroups. A subgroup that contains 𝘌ˉ\mathsfit{\bar{E}} is the preimage of its image in 𝖮(𝟥)\mathsf{O(3)}, named 𝟤𝖧\mathsf{2H} for the image 𝖧\mathsf{H}. Any other subgroup is a section over its image, named 𝖧\mathsf{H}. A gray letter after a symbol distinguishes the classes of subgroups with that symbol, which are not conjugate in 𝟤𝖣𝟧𝗁\mathsf{2D_{5h}} (they are conjugate in 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}). The table and the subduction tables of section 3.2 use the same letters. The table lists the classes by order, then by the type of the image, a preimage before a section. A row gives the representative 𝖧\mathsf{H} of a class of subgroups (subgrp), its order, the number of subgroups in the class (conjugates) and whether 𝖧\mathsf{H} is normal. It also gives the normalizer 𝖭 𝟤𝖣𝟧𝗁(𝖧)\mathsf{N}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{5h}}}(\mathsf{H}) and the centralizer 𝖢 𝟤𝖣𝟧𝗁(𝖧)\mathsf{C}_{\mkern1mu \mathsf{2D_{5h}}}(\mathsf{H}) in 𝟤𝖣𝟧𝗁\mathsf{2D_{5h}}, by their classes (normalizer, centralizer), and the classes of 𝟤𝖣𝟧𝗁\mathsf{2D_{5h}} other than 𝘌\mathsfit{E} met by 𝖧\mathsf{H} (classes, a dash for none). A subgroup’s other names in its 𝖯𝗂𝗇−(𝟥)\mathsf{Pin^{-}(3)}-conjugacy class follow ≅\cong (Conventions, section 20.2).
